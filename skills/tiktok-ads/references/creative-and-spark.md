# Creative and Spark Ads — TikTok

## Native creative heuristics

These are performance patterns, not immutable platform laws:

- Earn the stop in the **first 1–3 seconds** (pattern interrupt, bold claim, direct question, unexpected visual, POV).
- Shoot **vertical 9:16**, favor native/unpolished looks, and use trending audio when brand-safe.
- Assume many viewers watch **sound-off**; burned-in captions / text overlays carry the hook.
- Refresh creatives on a short cycle (often about 7–14 days) because fatigue arrives quickly on For You delivery.
- Test hooks separately from body; UGC-style usually beats glossy studio spots for direct response.
- Place logos and brand statements after the hook earns the stop; show product early enough that drop-off still sees the offer.

Official creative best-practice articles in `sources.md` reinforce native storytelling, captions, and performance creative hygiene — re-open them before citing UI-specific recommendations.

## Spark Ads

From TikTok **About Spark Ads** (verify live):

- Spark Ads run **organic TikTok posts** as ads so likes, comments, shares, and follows can accumulate on the original post.
- You can Spark your own posts or other creators’ posts **with authorization**.
- Authorization duration is **configurable** for the authorization code — choose a duration that matches campaign length; do not invent fixed menus from memory.
- Spec highlights: organic posts keep their native metadata; banned organic posts are rejected; Spark video max length is **10 minutes**; un-authorize before deleting the organic post; each Ads Manager account supports up to **10,000** Spark Ads.
- Prefer Sparking posts that already show organic traction when the goal is efficient learning.

Creation how-tos (Manual/Search vs Smart+) are linked in `sources.md`.
