# Domain Knowledge

## Product-Market Fit (PMF)

Product-market fit is the degree to which a product satisfies strong market demand. A practical early-stage test is the Sean Ellis survey: ask recent active users how they would feel if they could no longer use the product; ≥40% answering "very disappointed" is the classic dependence bar.

## Burn Rate & Default Alive

Burn rate is the monthly rate at which a company consumes cash (negative cash flow). "Default alive" vs "default dead" (Paul Graham) asks whether, at the current growth rate, revenue reaches costs before cash runs out. Runway alone is cash ÷ burn; default alive includes the growth engine.

## Runway

Runway is cash on hand divided by monthly burn. Use it for raise timing and negotiation posture, not as a substitute for the default-alive check.

## Retention & Cohorts

Cohorts group users who started in the same period. Flattening retention cohorts (the curve plateaus rather than sloping to zero) is a stronger PMF signal than cumulative signup growth.
