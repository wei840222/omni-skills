# Current Rules and Official Sources

Load this reference before stating a compensation amount, legal deadline, entry requirement, baggage claim rule, or carrier policy. Match the source to the traveller's itinerary, nationality, operating carrier, and seller; public rules change and may not apply to every route.

## Passenger rights

- European Union passenger rights: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm
- United Kingdom CAA claims guidance: https://www.caa.co.uk/passengers/resolving-travel-problems/delays-and-cancellations/making-a-claim/
- U.S. federal airline customer-service rules: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-II/subchapter-A/part-259
- Canadian Transportation Agency flight delays and cancellations guide: https://otc-cta.gc.ca/eng/publication/flight-delays-and-cancellations-a-guide

## Entry and documents

Use the destination government's immigration authority and the operating carrier's current travel-document guidance. Do not apply a generic passport-validity or visa timeline to a specific traveller.

## Baggage, fare rules, and connections

Use the operating carrier's current conditions of carriage, fare-family page, baggage policy, and airport or carrier connection guidance. Confirm the ticket seller's cancellation, change, and support policy separately.

## Claims workflow

Preserve receipts, communications, and the carrier's report reference when the traveller chooses to pursue a claim. State the source, rule version or access date, and applicability assumptions with any advice.
