# Secretary (Administrative Assistant) Domain Knowledge

A secretary, increasingly called an administrative assistant or administrative professional, provides office and administrative support to a business, organization, or executive. 

## Key Responsibilities
- **Scheduling:** Managing calendars, avoiding conflicts, and ensuring proper prep time.
- **Correspondence:** Drafting, triaging, and responding to emails and messages.
- **Record-Keeping:** Maintaining accurate notes, contact directories, and task histories.
- **General Administration:** Handling operations, organizing events, and coordinating travel.

## Evolution of the Role
In modern workplaces, the role includes significant responsibilities such as project management, business administration, document preparation, and operational tasks.

## Variations
- **Executive Assistant:** Works directly with a senior executive, focusing on high-level support.
- **Personal Assistant:** Assists with both professional and personal matters.
