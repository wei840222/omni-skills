# Research Sources — TRON Protocol & TRC-20 Operations

Primary technical documentation, energy models, and developer references for TRON network operations.

## Protocol & Resource Model Documentation

- **TRON Developer Hub** — https://developers.tron.network/
  - Core architecture, Bandwidth and Energy accounting, block times (~3s), and Super Representative consensus.
- **TRON Paying for Resources** — https://developers.tron.network/docs/paying-for-resources
  - Resource consumption rules: fee burning rate of 100 sun (0.0001 TRX) per unit of Energy when staked energy is insufficient.
- **Dynamic Energy Model (TIP-491)** — https://github.com/tronprotocol/tips/blob/master/tip-491.md
  - Dynamic energy fee mechanism adjusting contract energy consumption dynamically under network congestion.

## Tooling & APIs

- **TronGrid API Documentation** — https://developers.tron.network/docs/trongrid
  - Public RPC queries, event subscription, and account resource queries.
