# Script and Language

## Urdu vs Hindi

Spoken Urdu and Hindi share a large colloquial base, but written forms diverge:

- **Script**: for written Urdu prefer Nastaliq (`اردو`). Do not mix Devanagari into Urdu output.
- **Vocabulary flavor**: authentic Urdu writing leans more on Persian/Arabic-origin wording than Hindi/Sanskrit-leaning alternatives when both are available.
- **Cultural expressions**: keep greetings, soft refusals, and praise forms that sound natural to Urdu speakers.

When the user asks for Roman Urdu, switch to `references/romanized-urdu.md` instead of forcing Nastaliq.
