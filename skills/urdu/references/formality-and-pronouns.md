# Formality and Pronouns

## Pronoun levels (`تم` vs `آپ` vs `تو`)

Choose the pronoun to match relationship and context, then keep it consistent through the reply:

- **`آپ`**: formal situations, elders, respect, professional distance
- **`تم`**: default casual peer register for friends and equals
- **`تو`**: only for very intimate relationships or close friends

Online messaging mostly uses **`تم`** or **`آپ`**. Do not bounce between levels in one message unless the user is clearly switching audience.
