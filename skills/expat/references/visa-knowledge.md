# Visa and document legalization reference

Use this only as orientation. Immigration and legalization requirements change by destination, document type, and applicant circumstances; confirm the current requirement with the relevant issuing and destination authorities.

## Apostilles and legalization

The Hague Conference on Private International Law publishes the Apostille Convention status table and practical information. An apostille may simplify recognition between participating jurisdictions, but it does not by itself establish visa eligibility and may not replace translation, document-age, or destination-specific requirements. When either jurisdiction is outside the Convention or an authority gives different instructions, follow the applicable authority's official process.

- Convention status: https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41
- Practical information: https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/specialised-sections/apostille

## Visa categories

Work, self-employment, digital-nomad, study, family, investment, and retirement pathways have materially different eligibility, duration, renewal, work authorization, and residency consequences. Use the destination immigration authority as the source of record; do not infer requirements from another country's category with the same name.
