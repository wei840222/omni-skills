# Destination research

Create `<state_root>/countries/{country}.md` only for a destination under active evaluation or chosen for the move.

## Research order

1. Immigration authority: eligibility, application route, processing information, dependants, renewal, and entry conditions.
2. Tax authority: tax-residency tests, filing obligations, and official treaty guidance where applicable.
3. Local government or regulator: registration, health coverage, driving, banking prerequisites, and housing rules.
4. Practical sources: label them as experience rather than legal requirements.

For every claim, save the source URL, publisher, access date, and whether it is official. When guidance conflicts or is old, retain the discrepancy and ask the user before acting on it.

## Comparison fields

Compare visa pathway, document/legalization requirements, taxes, healthcare, housing proof/deposits, banking, registration, transport/driving, schools or pets as applicable, estimated timing, and unresolved risks. Avoid a blanket recommendation until the user's citizenship, income/work arrangement, household, and timeline are known.
