# Memory curation background

Personal information management (PIM) concerns acquiring, organizing, maintaining, retrieving, and using information. This skill applies that idea through selective retention, source and confidence labels, periodic review, and user-controlled deletion.

## Sources

- National Institute of Standards and Technology, *NIST Privacy Framework* — privacy risk management supports limiting retained data to a defined purpose and user-approved handling: https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework
- William Jones, *Personal Information Management* (Annual Review of Information Science and Technology) — PIM studies the activities people use to acquire, organize, maintain, retrieve, and use personal information: https://doi.org/10.1002/aris.1440410112
