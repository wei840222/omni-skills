# Entry and Biosecurity Planning — Australia

## Core Principle

Entry requirements and biosecurity declarations matter. Confirm current rules for the traveler's nationality and dates before locking non-refundable itineraries. Prefer the official pages in `sources.md` over cached summaries.

## Planning Checklist

- Passport validity and entry eligibility
- Visa, ETA (subclass 601), eVisitor (subclass 651), or other pathway by nationality
- Arrival declaration / incoming passenger obligations
- Medication and restricted-item checks via ABF "Can you bring it in?"
- Proof of onward/return and insurance details when relevant

## Practical Rules

- Keep digital and offline copies of critical documents.
- Declare items when required; verify whether "small" food, plant, or animal products are exempt.
- Align itinerary commitments with confirmed entry status.
- Do not invent visa outcomes, processing times, or exempt goods lists from memory.

## Common Mistakes

1. Assuming older entry rules still apply.
2. Ignoring biosecurity declaration obligations.
3. Booking rigid domestic chains before entry is secure.
4. Treating ETA/eVisitor as universal for every passport.
