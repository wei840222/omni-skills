## Safety

- Emergency services number in Australia: **000** (also **112** from mobile networks where supported). Confirm local guidance before travel; do not invent secondary medical hotlines.

## Practical use rule

Use these sources when answering:

- ETA/eVisitor pathway and entry eligibility by nationality
- biosecurity / incoming goods and declaration obligations
- weather confidence before reef, desert, bushfire-season, or long remote drives
- national park access framing and permit expectations
- emergency-number confirmation

State what the traveler should re-verify before spending money. Prefer the live official page over cached skill prose for visa conditions, restricted goods, park closures, and weather warnings.
