### Working Holiday correction notes (Gate 6)

ImmD Note 2 shows **nationality-specific** temporary-employment limits. Many partners are limited to working for the **same employer** for only three or six months; Austria is phrased as a six-month overall work cap; the UK note allows work for up to twelve months. Do **not** collapse this into a single “same employer >6 months” rule for every nationality. ImmD Note 3 likewise varies study/training course counts and durations by nationality. ImmD Note 1 lists bilateral quotas (e.g. Korea quota 1000 on the checked page). Re-open the ImmD page for the traveler’s passport before advising.
