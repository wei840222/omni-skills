# Sources — Vancouver

Back to [Vancouver Skill Overview](../SKILL.md).

Use these verified primary sources when transit zones, provincial rental tenancy regulations, municipal services, or coastal weather logistics apply in Vancouver.

## Public Transit & Compass Card

- **TransLink (Metro Vancouver)** — https://www.translink.ca/
  - Unified transit authority operating the automated SkyTrain (Expo, Millennium, and Canada Lines), SeaBus harbour ferries, West Coast Express, and regional buses. Outlines 1-zone, 2-zone, and 3-zone fare structures (weekdays after 6:30 PM and all weekends/holidays are 1-zone throughout), Compass card taps, and contactless payment.

## Provincial Housing & Tenancy Protections

- **BC Residential Tenancy Branch (RTB)** — https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies
  - Statutory authority governing residential landlord-tenant relationships in British Columbia. Details the annual allowable rent increase limit tied to CPI inflation, security deposit rules (maximum half a month's rent), pet deposits, and formal dispute resolution hearings.

## Provincial Administration & Government Gateway

- **Government of British Columbia** — https://www2.gov.bc.ca/
  - Official provincial portal providing healthcare enrollment (Medical Services Plan - MSP), ICBC driver licensing, and regional climate and disaster emergency information.

## Tourism & Regional Recreation

- **Destination Vancouver** — https://www.destinationvancouver.com/
  - Official metropolitan visitor bureau detailing neighborhood profiles (Gastown, Yaletown, West End, Kitsilano), Stanley Park seawall guidelines, and local dining hubs.
- **HelloBC (Destination British Columbia)** — https://www.hellobc.com/
  - Official provincial travel resource for outdoor recreation, North Shore hiking safety, and sea-to-sky corridors.
