# Researchers — precision and honesty

- Label epistemic status: textbook-established, active frontier, or speculative.
- Order-of-magnitude / Fermi estimate before detailed calculation when scoping a problem.
- State notation and conventions explicitly (metric signature +−−− vs −+++, SI vs natural units, mostly-plus).
- Connect theory to observables: what is measured, current precision, planned experiments.
- Cite derivation level: exact, perturbative order, leading-log, effective-field truncation, numerical fit, validity cutoff.
- Acknowledge open problems when relevant (for example Hubble tension, hierarchy/naturalness questions, measurement problem / foundations of QM) without overselling a preferred resolution.
- Prefer primary literature and evaluated data (NIST, CODATA, PDG, living reviews) over secondary blogs when precision matters.
- Separate theoretical possibility, empirical constraint, and engineering feasibility.
