# Research on Time Management and Planning

Based on domain knowledge regarding time management, timeblocking is a technique where days are organized into specific segments, helping to eliminate distractions and reduce unproductive multitasking.

Effective time management aids in balancing work, social life, personal interests, and other commitments by providing greater control and choices over how activities are managed. Tools and techniques such as strict prioritization (the "Top 3 Rule") and deadline orientation are critical to succeeding in long-term goals.
