# Document Craft — Resume vs CV, Structure, Summaries, Bullets

Load when choosing document type, ordering sections, writing summaries, or rewriting experience bullets.

## Resume vs CV

| Aspect | Resume | CV |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Length | Typically 1–2 pages | Follows content; academic CVs may run many pages |
| Purpose | Snapshot tailored to one role | Complete academic/professional history |
| Common use | Industry jobs (US, Canada, many corporate roles) | Academia, research, medicine, many EU/UK applications, grants |
| Update cadence | Customize per application | Append achievements continuously |

Use a **resume** when the posting says "resume", for tech/startups/corporate industry roles, or when a short tailored snapshot is expected.

Use a **CV** for academic/research roles, grants and fellowships, and markets that expect a full curriculum vitae. Confirm with the user when the market mixes terms (some EU "CV" requests are still short industry documents).

## Structure

**Experienced professionals (resume):**

1. Contact info
2. Professional summary
3. Work experience
4. Skills
5. Education

**Recent graduates (resume):**

1. Contact info
2. Professional summary
3. Education
4. Skills
5. Projects / internships

**Academic CV (high level):** contact → education → research/appointments → publications → teaching → grants → service → skills. Expand only sections the target search values.

## Contact block

Include: name, professional email, phone (if used in that market), city + region, LinkedIn, and role-relevant links (GitHub, portfolio, personal site).

Prefer `firstname.lastname@…` style emails. Keep the contact block single-column and text-selectable.

## Professional summary

Formula:

```text
[Title] with [X years] of [domain] experience.
[Key achievement with metrics].
Skilled in [top 3 relevant skills]. Seeking to [goal] at [company type].
```

Example:

```text
Senior Software Engineer with 7 years of backend experience.
Reduced API latency by 40% serving 2M daily users at scale.
Expert in Python, AWS, and distributed systems.
Seeking to lead platform engineering at a growth-stage fintech.
```

Prefer summaries over objective statements that center on personal growth wishes.

## Experience bullets — CAR

**CAR:** Action Verb + Task + Result with Metrics.

Patterns:

```text
# Vague responsibility
Responsible for managing the engineering team

# CAR
Led 8-person engineering team, delivering 3 major features 2 weeks ahead of schedule

# Vague contribution
Helped improve sales

# CAR
Increased enterprise sales by $2.3M (34% YoY) through automated lead scoring

# Vague task
Worked on the checkout system

# CAR
Reduced checkout abandonment from 68% to 41% by redesigning 3-step flow
```

Action-verb banks (examples):

| Leadership | Technical | Growth | Efficiency |
|------------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Led, Directed | Developed, Architected | Increased, Grew | Reduced, Streamlined |
| Managed, Mentored | Engineered, Built | Expanded, Generated | Automated, Optimized |

Guidelines:

- About 3–5 bullets per role; strongest first
- Ask once for missing metrics; leave qualitative if the user cannot supply numbers
- Drop first-person pronouns; start with verbs

## Quantification patterns

| Vague | Quantified |
|-------|------------|
| Managed a team | Managed team of 12 across 3 time zones |
| Improved performance | Reduced load time from 4s to 800ms |
| Increased revenue | Generated $1.2M ARR from new product line |
| Handled support | Resolved 50+ tickets/week with 98% satisfaction |

Metric types: revenue ($), percentage (%), time saved, team size, users/customers, scale (requests/sec), satisfaction scores.

## Common weak patterns → positive targets

| Weak pattern | Positive target |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Responsibility list | Achievement with evidence |
| Passive "Responsible for…" openings | Verb-led CAR bullets |
| Missing metrics | User-sourced numbers or explicit unknown |
| One generic document for every posting | Tailor keywords and ordering to the JD |
| Typos / inconsistent formatting | Spell-check + consistent dates/punctuation |
| Unprofessional email | Professional address |
| Image-only PDF | Text-layer PDF or DOCX |
| Overlong industry resume | Condense early roles; keep 1–2 pages for most industry targets |
