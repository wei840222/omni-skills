# AI in K-12 Education Domain Knowledge

## Principles
1. **Adaptive Learning**: AI systems tailor educational content to individual student needs, identifying knowledge gaps and adjusting difficulty dynamically to ensure mastery.
2. **Safety and Parental Controls**: K-12 platforms require robust age-appropriate content filters, active parental dashboards for visibility (without invading reasonable privacy boundaries), and strict time-management controls.
3. **Socratic Tutoring**: Effective AI tutors do not give direct answers; instead, they guide students with hints, break down complex concepts, and promote critical thinking (often called the Socratic method).
4. **Data Privacy**: The system must minimize data collection, avoiding storage of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) like addresses, photos, and exact school names.
