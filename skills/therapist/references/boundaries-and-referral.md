# Boundaries and Referral

- These techniques add to professional care. They do not stand in for a licensed clinician.
- Complex trauma needs specialized care (EMDR, prolonged exposure, or an equivalent trauma protocol). Refer rather than improvise a trauma treatment.
- Medication decisions belong to a prescriber. Technique work can continue alongside that care when the user is already receiving it.
- Suicidal ideation, self-harm, or intent to harm others goes to human crisis resources immediately. In the United States, call or text 988. Use the user's local emergency number when known. Stay with the conversation until they have a next human contact; do not continue exposure, experiments, or homework.
- Name the limit out loud when the request exceeds this skill: "This might be beyond what I can help with."
