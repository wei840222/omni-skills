# Sources — Lisbon

Use these verified primary sources when transit, living costs, municipal services, or relocation frameworks apply in Lisbon.

## Public Transit & Navegante Card

- **Navegante Tarifário Oficial** — https://www.navegante.pt/viajar/tarifario-navegante
  - Official tariff portal for the Lisbon Metropolitan Area (AML). Details the monthly pass brackets: Navegante Municipal (€30 per month, valid across any single chosen municipality within AML) and Navegante Metropolitano (€40 per month, providing unlimited travel across all 18 municipalities of AML on CP suburban trains, Metropolitano de Lisboa, Carris, Carris Metropolitana, and Transtejo/Soflusa ferries).
- **Carris (Companhia Carris de Ferro de Lisboa)** — https://www.carris.pt/en/
  - Surface transit network operator running Lisbon buses, historic trams (Elétricos including Tram 28), funiculars (Glória, Bica, Lavra), and Santa Justa Lift.

## Municipal Administration & Destination Guidance

- **Visit Lisboa (Associação de Turismo de Lisboa)** — https://www.visitlisboa.com/en
  - Official destination portal providing verified visitor routes, neighborhood orientations (Baixa, Alfama, Chiado, Belém, Parque das Nações), and cultural events.
- **Portal das Comunidades Portuguesas (MNE)** — https://portaldascomunidades.mne.gov.pt/
  - Ministry of Foreign Affairs portal for international residency, consular services, and legal entry into Portugal.
