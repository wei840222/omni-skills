# Pricing Methodologies & Industry Benchmarks

This document records the foundational research literature, mathematical models, and industry benchmark sources utilized in pricing and monetization analysis.

## Foundational Pricing Methodologies

### Willingness-to-Pay Research
- **Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity Meter (PSM)**:
  - Source: Van Westendorp, P. (1976). *NSS-Price Sensitivity Meter (PSM) — A new approach to study consumer perception of price*. Proceedings of the ESOMAR Congress.
  - Methodology: Four standard survey questions determining Point of Marginal Cheapness (PMC), Point of Marginal Expensiveness (PME), Indifference Price Point (IPP), and Optimal Price Point (OPP).
- **Gabor-Granger Pricing Model**:
  - Source: Gabor, A., & Granger, C. W. J. (1966). *Price as an Indicator of Quality: Report on an Enquiry*. Economica, 33(129), 43-70.
  - Methodology: Sequential price-point acceptance testing to map revenue-maximizing demand curves.
- **Conjoint Analysis / Discrete Choice Modeling**:
  - Source: Green, P. E., & Rao, V. R. (1971). *Conjoint Measurement for Quantifying Judgmental Data*. Journal of Marketing Research.
  - Multi-attribute trade-off modeling for feature packaging and willingness-to-pay elasticity.

## SaaS Monetization & Packaging Frameworks

- **Simon-Kucher & Partners**:
  - Simon, H. (2015). *Confessions of the Pricing Man: How Price Affects Everything*. Springer.
  - Framework: Value-based pricing, segmentation fences, and margin preservation.
- **Paddle / ProfitWell SaaS Pricing Benchmarks**:
  - Official resource: [https://www.paddle.com/resources](https://www.paddle.com/resources)
  - Empirical metrics: Annual contract value (ACV), expansion revenue, cohort churn, and discount elasticity across B2B software verticals.
- **OpenView SaaS Benchmarks**:
  - Product-Led Growth (PLG) pricing, usage-based pricing metrics, and pay-as-you-go vs commit structures.
