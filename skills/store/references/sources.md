- **NRF / retail ops orientation on inventory accuracy** — cycle counts beat rare wall-to-wall counts for fast movers; accuracy underpins replenishment and shrink diagnosis — https://nrf.com/

## Labor, traffic, and conversion

- **NRF retail operations / workforce themes** — labor must follow traffic and conversion opportunity, not equal-hour habit — https://nrf.com/research
- **Harvard Business Review on retail labor productivity** — staffing quality and timing affect conversion and service recovery — https://hbr.org/topic/subject/retail-industry

## Shrink, cash, and loss prevention

- **NRF National Retail Security Survey hub** — shrink is multi-cause (external theft, internal, process failure); log and investigate rather than label “unknown” — https://nrf.com/research/national-retail-security-survey
- **Loss-prevention process hygiene** — dual control on cash, documented variance, high-value visibility — treat as operating controls, not optional admin
