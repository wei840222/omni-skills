# Floor Operations

## Service Coverage

- Put the strongest seller or quickest operator where customer decisions actually happen.
- Protect the queue and fitting-room bottlenecks before adding low-value tasks.
- Move people with traffic, adapting zones dynamically to match the day.

## Task Timing

| Moment | Best Tasks |
|--------|------------|
| Quiet open | recovery, refill, price checks |
| Build-up period | queue prep, role assignment, promo reset |
| Peak trading | service, queue control, quick replenishment only |
| Slowdown | deeper refill, returns, stockroom cleanup |
| Last hour | closing prep, notes, next-day staging |

## When the Floor Feels Chaotic

1. Reduce priorities to service, queue, cash, and visible stock gaps.
2. Delay non-urgent admin and cosmetic work.
3. Put one person in charge of live decisions.
4. Reassign breaks only if customer service or safety is at risk.

## Hard Rules

- Run deep stock tasks only during off-peak hours to protect service.
- Ensure all staff members align on shared priorities set by the shift leader.
- Keep the entrance and top table fresh and impactful to drive conversion.
- Verify productivity by checking conversion and basket size alongside overall traffic.
