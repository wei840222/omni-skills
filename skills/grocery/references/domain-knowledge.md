# Grocery Domain Knowledge

Use this reference only when checking food storage, date labels, or allergens for a stated restriction. A package’s current ingredient list and allergy statement override any general rule here.

## Date Labels and Storage

- “Best if used by” quality dates are not, by themselves, a safety date for most foods. Follow the label and keep perishable foods refrigerated promptly.
- Discard food with unexpected spoilage signs. When a safety decision depends on storage history that is unknown, ask the user rather than assert that it is safe.
- Use refrigerator and freezer storage guidance from FoodSafety.gov for category-specific timing.

## Allergen Checks

- Ask for the current ingredient label when a packaged item may conflict with a stored restriction.
- In the United States, major allergens include milk, egg, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, and sesame; regional labelling rules may differ.
- Do not treat a generic ingredient name or a prior purchase as proof that a new product is suitable.
