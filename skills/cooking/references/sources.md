# Sources — cooking

Gate 6 research anchors. Re-verify before citing jurisdiction-specific legal minima.

## Food safety and temperatures

- [USDA FSIS safe temperature chart](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) — poultry, ground meat, whole cuts, leftovers, and rest guidance.
- [FDA Food Code overview](https://www.fda.gov/food/retail-food-protection/fda-food-code) — retail danger-zone and holding context when household rules need a public-health frame.

## Heat, browning, and carryover

- [Serious Eats — meat temperatures and carryover cooking](https://www.seriouseats.com/the-food-lab-meat-temperatures-and-carryover-cooking) — pull temperature vs serve temperature.
- [Modernist Cuisine — science of browning](https://modernistcuisine.com/mc/the-science-of-browning/) — Maillard temperature band vs steaming at 100°C.

## Practice notes

- Prefer thermometer + weight percentages over brand-specific spoon volume.
- Distinguish **safety minimums** (USDA/FDA style) from **quality targets** (medium-rare steak, dark-meat collagen conversion).
- Household `doneness_policy` and `safety_posture` may choose time-temperature pasteurization equivalents; state who bears the risk.
