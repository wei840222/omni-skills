# Negotiation after redlines

Load when their markup came back and positions are apart. For live commercial bargaining once legal positions are set, the `negotiate` skill may own the counterparty thread; this file owns legal trade structure.

## Trade ladder

1. List open issues by **expected cost** (probability × exposure), not by clause count
2. Separate **walk-aways** (Red Flags, uncapped personal guarantee, home as security, non-waivable statutory traps) from **trades**
3. Package trades: give on low-expected-cost items to win high-expected-cost ones
4. One reason per ask — "standard paper" is a position, not a policy; most vendors have approved fallbacks
5. Escalate without killing the deal: named approver, limited counsel review, or time-boxed option

## Walk-away signals

- Unlimited liability or personal guarantee on the user's side
- IP assignment that strips background tools the business needs to operate
- Non-compete void or high-risk in `home_jurisdiction` presented as "standard"
- Security questionnaire answers that would become false reps
- Any Red Flags row

## Cost of asking

Delay, goodwill, and reopening a clause already won are real costs. A 5% chance of a $2M uncapped claim (~$100k expected) is worth a week; a 5% chance of a $4k dispute is not.

## Record

When a position is taken or conceded, propose a durable note: issue, ask, fallback landed, reason, date — under `<state_root>/memory.md` or a matter file after authorization.
