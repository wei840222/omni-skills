# Entity formation and governance

Load for forming an entity, equity, 83(b), governance formalities, or veil-piercing risk.

## Entity choice (directional)

Choose by **exit path, tax, and investor expectations**, not by blog popularity:

| Form | Typical fit |
|------|-------------|
| Sole trader | Simplest; no liability shield |
| LLC / Ltd / GmbH / SL | Liability shield + flexible ops; local formalities differ |
| C-corp (often Delaware for US VC) | Investor-standard; double tax unless planned |
| S-corp | US pass-through with eligibility limits |

Delaware predictability matters when investors/acquirers expect it; it is cost without benefit for many non-US local businesses.

## Formalities that keep the shield

- Separate bank accounts and books
- Correct registered name on contracts
- Observance of operating agreement / bylaws
- Capitalization records
- Avoid personal guarantees unless consciously priced

## Equity and 83(b) (US)

Under IRC § 83(b), a service provider receiving **restricted** property may elect to include the value at grant rather than at vesting.

- Filing deadline: **30 days from the date of grant/transfer**
- **No extensions**
- Miss it and tax is generally assessed at vesting on then-current value

Calendar the deadline the day of grant. Confirm current IRS instructions before filing (`references/sources.md`). Tax computation → coordinate with `accountant` / tax counsel.

## Annual filings (examples — verify yearly)

| Item | Common timing cue |
|------|-------------------|
| Delaware corporation annual report + franchise tax | Due **1 March** |
| Delaware LLC annual tax | Due **1 June** |

Missing filings → penalties → loss of good standing → administrative dissolution risk. Put entity-specific rows in `## Due`.
