# Intellectual property

Load for trademark, copyright, patent timing, trade secret, open source, or AI-output questions.

## What exists automatically vs needs filing

| Right | Automatic? | Filing / clock |
|-------|------------|----------------|
| Copyright | Yes on fixation (Berne) | Registration helps remedies (e.g. US statutory damages/fees) |
| Trade secret | Yes if secrecy + value + measures | Lost by disclosure; no registration |
| Trademark | Common-law use rights limited | Registration strengthens nationwide/priority rights |
| Patent | No — filing required | Public disclosure can destroy novelty |

## Patent disclosure clocks

- **United States:** generally a **1-year** grace period after certain inventor-originated public disclosures (35 U.S.C. § 102) — confirm current USPTO guidance for the fact pattern
- **Most other jurisdictions:** absolute novelty — disclosure before filing destroys patentability there

File before the announcement when international rights matter.

## Trademark maintenance (US examples)

- Section 8 declaration of use generally between years **5 and 6** after registration
- Renewal generally every **10 years**
- Missing maintenance can cancel the registration and reopen the queue

Verify current USPTO timelines before calendaring (`references/sources.md`).

## Open source

Licence obligations travel with distribution and sometimes with internal SaaS use (AGPL-class). Track inbound licences, copyleft triggers, and attribution files. Do not advise "just copy the code" when a copyleft licence would require reciprocal source offer.

## AI output

Ownership and infringement risk depend on jurisdiction, training data, and contract allocation. Prefer express assignment/work-product language with vendors; do not assume model output is automatically free to register as the user's copyright everywhere.

## Practical order

1. Which right is needed for the business goal?
2. Has there been public disclosure?
3. Filing deadline and venue
4. Contract allocation (assignment, licence back, moral rights waivers where valid)
