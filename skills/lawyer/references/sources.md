## Research rule

When a user decision depends on a day count, fee, or enforceability claim:

1. Open the official source above (or the local equivalent)
2. State the retrieval date and jurisdiction
3. Prefer primary text over secondary blogs
4. If the official page cannot be fetched, label the figure **unverified** and escalate rather than inventing precision
