# Red Flags and escalation

Load before high-stakes answers. Anything in this table suspends normal protocols: stop drafting, route to a licensed practitioner in the relevant jurisdiction, and write the handover.

## Red Flags table

| Signal (observable) | What it suggests | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A document names a court, a case number, a docket, or a service of process | Litigation has started; response clock often 20–30 days | Counsel today; calendar the response date first |
| A letter from a regulator, tax authority, or law enforcement | Statutory deadlines; answering badly is worse than late | Counsel before any reply; preserve everything |
| The words subpoena, warrant, injunction, cease trading, or criminal | Exposure is not commercial | Counsel immediately; say nothing to the other side |
| A limitation period, filing date, or bar date within 60 days | The right disappears on that date | Compute the date, put it in `## Due`, get counsel inside the window |
| Immigration, family, personal injury, criminal, or insolvency facts | Specialist domains where general commercial reasoning is wrong | Route to a specialist; do not draft |
| Issuing shares, options, tokens, or anything sold as an investment | Securities law attaches to the offer, not only the sale | Securities counsel before the first investor conversation |
| Firing someone who complained, is on leave, or is in a protected category | Retaliation / discrimination exposure | Employment counsel before the conversation |
| Personal data of children, health, biometrics, or a suspected breach | Statutory clocks in hours (GDPR Art. 33: 72 hours from awareness) | Start the clock, preserve logs, counsel and DPO in parallel |
| Unlimited liability, personal guarantee, or the user's home as security | Downside not bounded by the business | Do not recommend signing; counsel review is the minimum |
| Value above `signature_authority_usd`, or a term longer than 3 years | Beyond the range where a mistake is affordable | Named approver plus counsel review before signature |

## Handover shape (Rule 6)

Never bare "consult a lawyer". Write:

1. Jurisdiction and forum
2. Documents and where they live (pointers, not secret values)
3. Hard deadline with counting unit
4. Specific decision needed
5. Budget / fee structure to ask for
6. Facts only — no litigation strategy in skill memory when privilege matters

## UPL line

This skill produces business drafts and structured legal information for human review. It does not create an attorney-client relationship, appear in court, or file anything on the user's behalf.
