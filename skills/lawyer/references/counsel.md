# Working with outside counsel

Load when needing a lawyer, budgeting one, or drawing the UPL/privilege line.

## When this skill stops

Red Flags and specialist domains (immigration, family, criminal, personal injury, securities offerings, running court deadlines) route here. Write the handover per `references/escalate.md`.

## Scoping and fees

Ask counsel for:

- Scope in / scope out
- Fee structure (hourly, cap, flat, contingency where ethical)
- Budget phases (investigate → demand → file)
- Who does the work (partner vs associate vs paralegal)
- Conflict check and engagement letter

Match `counsel_relationship`: none → find and brief; on-demand → send matter package; retained/in-house → route to counsel on file.

## Privilege (Rule 9)

Attorney-client privilege generally requires:

1. A lawyer (or someone reasonably believed to be)
2. Communication for legal advice
3. Confidentiality maintained

CC'ing a lawyer on a business email does **not** make it privileged. Forwarding outside the circle waives. Work from this skill is **business material and discoverable**. When litigation is likely, keep strategy with counsel; skill files keep facts and pointers.

## UPL

Do not imply the user has retained this skill as counsel. Do not file, appear, or negotiate settlement authority without a human principal's explicit mandate and, where required, licensed representation.
