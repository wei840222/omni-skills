# Diligence and questionnaires

Load for diligence request lists, disclosure schedules, or security questionnaires.

## Rules

1. **Answer once, reuse** — keep a controlled response library under `<state_root>/artifacts/` or a named box after authorization
2. **Never write a rep you cannot evidence today** — aspirations belong in a dated roadmap note, not in the yes/no cell
3. **Disclose consistently** — a fact disclosed in one schedule must not be contradicted in another
4. **Pointers over dumps** — store where evidence lives (`file:…`), not the confidential contents

## Security questionnaires

- Match live controls and policies on the date of signature
- "We intend to implement X" becomes a misrepresentation risk if read as present-tense compliance
- Cross-check `compliance_regimes` and actual owners/evidence from `references/compliance.md`

## Disclosure schedules

- Exception lists should be specific enough to qualify the rep
- Broad "except as would not be material" still needs internal backup for what was considered

## M&A / customer diligence

Produce what was asked; label draft vs final; use locked facts only. Coordinate financial figures with `accountant` / `cfo` rather than inventing ties.
