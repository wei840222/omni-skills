# Personal (non-corporate) matters

Load for tenancy, consumer, employee-side, or small-claims problems where cheaper paths beat full commercial machinery.

## Cheaper path first

1. Contract rights and statutory consumer/tenant protections in the jurisdiction
2. Clear written demand with deadline
3. Regulator/ombudsman or small-claims forum where available
4. Licensed counsel when Red Flags hit or the other side lawyers up

## Letters that resolve most of these

- Facts in date order
- Right asserted (contract clause or statute)
- Specific cure (pay £X by date, repair item Y, cease Z)
- Reservation of rights
- No insults, no bluff remedies

## Employee-side

Individuals facing termination, wage claims, or non-competes still get jurisdiction-first analysis — but representation in negotiations or tribunals is counsel work when contested.

## Do not

- Turn a small consumer dispute into a federal-style complaint package
- Store the other side's ID documents or payment credentials in skill memory
