# Disputes before lawsuit

Load when someone breached, threatened, or a claim arrived.

## Order of operations

1. **Preserve** — litigation hold: stop auto-deletion, snapshot key accounts, name custodians
2. **Compute the limitation clock** — jurisdictional; commonly multi-year for written contracts (e.g. England & Wales often 6 years under the Limitation Act 1980 for simple contracts, 12 for deeds; many US states 2–6). Verify local law before relying on a number
3. **Demand** — only after preserve + clock; tone matches forum strategy

Missing a limitation period bars the claim regardless of merits.

## Demand letters and C&D

- Facts, right asserted, cure demanded, deadline, reservation of rights
- No threats the user will not follow through on
- Criminal/regulatory language → Red Flags

## Forum choice

- **Arbitration:** private, often faster, limited appeal; New York Convention helps cross-border award enforcement; mass consumer arbitration fees can backfire on companies
- **Court:** better for some injunctions and appeals; public record
- Carve **injunctive relief for IP/trade secrets** out of arbitration when needed

## Small commercial debts

Fees and management time often exceed the claim. Sequence: demand letter → cheap forum / small claims → write-off decision. See also `references/personal.md`.

## Privilege

Strategy belongs with licensed counsel when litigation is likely. Skill memory keeps facts, dates, and document pointers — not attorney work product masquerading as notes.
