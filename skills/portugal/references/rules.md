# Rules

## Core Rules

### 1. Specific Over Generic
Do not say “try pastéis de nata in Lisbon.” Prefer: “Manteigaria in Chiado, Rua do Loreto 2—€1.30, eat standing within ~30 seconds of leaving the oven.”

### 2. Local Perspective
Prefer what locals actually do over brochure rituals:
- Pastéis de Belém queue → Manteigaria or Aloma for many locals
- Bairro Alto dinner prices → Santos or Príncipe Real for calmer meals
- Tram 28 as experience → walk Alfama or consider quieter lines when the goal is transport
- Sangria default → vinho verde or ginjinha when appropriate

### 3. Regional Differences

| Region | Key difference |
|--------|----------------|
| Lisboa | Petiscos, ginjinha, fado in Alfama, dense tourist traps on main squares |
| Porto | Francesinha culture, Port caves, more reserved pace, Ribeira premium pricing |
| Alentejo | Slow pace, porco preto, wine country, cork landscapes |
| Algarve | Beach-resort coast, seafood/cataplana, sharp tourist vs local town split |
| Douro | Wine valley, quintas, scenic rail/road |
| Madeira | Subtropical, poncha, levadas, rocky coast more than sand beaches |
| Azores | Green volcanic islands, whale watching, Cozido das Furnas logistics |

### 4. Timing Is Everything
- Lunch often 12:30–15:00
- Dinner commonly 20:00+; many kitchens quiet before ~19:30
- August: Lisbon empties toward beaches; book coastal lodging early
- Sunday: more closures outside big cities
- Fado: often starts late (~21:30+)
- Shops: some still close mid-afternoon outside malls

### 5. Flag Tourist Traps Explicitly
Call out avoidable patterns:
- Menu-photo restaurants on Praça do Comércio / Rua Augusta seafood markups
- Hawker “traditional fado” door pitches
- Canned-sardine “authentic souvenir” upsell
- Guilt-trip “free” walking tours
- Paying inflated pastel prices at the most famous Belém queue when the goal is the pastry itself

## Common Traps

- 45-minute Belém queue when the goal is a warm pastel, not the ritual
- Treating Tram 28 as efficient transit instead of a crowded sightseeing hop
- Defaulting to Ribeira waterfront restaurants in Porto for every meal
- Last-minute August beach lodging
- US-style 15–20% tipping expectations (rounding up / modest tip is common)
- Renting a car for central Lisbon lodging days
- Expecting warm swimming-pool Atlantic water in Lisbon-area beaches year-round
