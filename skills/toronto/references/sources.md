# Sources — Toronto

Back to [Toronto Skill Overview](../SKILL.md).

Use these verified primary sources when transit fares, provincial tenancy rights, municipal regulations, or winter urban logistics apply in Toronto.

## Municipal Governance & Urban Infrastructure

- **City of Toronto** — https://www.toronto.ca/
  - Official municipal administration hub providing civic bylaws, property taxes, waste management, PATH underground walkway network mapping, and cold-weather emergency warming centres.

## Public Transit & PRESTO Card

- **Toronto Transit Commission (TTC)** — https://www.ttc.ca/
  - Canada's largest transit agency operating the Toronto subway (Lines 1, 2, 4), streetcar network, and bus routes. Features open payment (contactless tap via credit/debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay) with 2-hour transfer windows.
- **PRESTO Card** — https://www.prestocard.ca/en/
  - Regional smartcard fare collection system across the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area (GTHA), including TTC, GO Transit, UP Express, and regional buses with the Ontario One Fare program.

## Housing & Tenancy Protections

- **Government of Ontario — Renting in Ontario & Landlord and Tenant Board (LTB)** — https://www.ontario.ca/page/renting-ontario-your-rights
  - Statutory guidelines under the Residential Tenancies Act (RTA). Outlines provincial annual rent increase guidelines for rent-controlled units (first occupied for residential purposes prior to November 15, 2018), standard lease agreements, and eviction dispute resolution through the LTB.

## Provincial Destination Guidance

- **Destination Ontario** — https://www.destinationontario.com/en-ca
  - Official provincial tourism portal providing neighborhood itineraries (Downtown, Annex, Kensington Market, Distillery District) and regional day-trip options (Niagara Falls, Prince Edward County).
