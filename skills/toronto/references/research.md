# Toronto General Knowledge

Toronto is the most populous city in Canada and the capital city of the province of Ontario. It is located on the northwestern shore of Lake Ontario.
As of the 2021 census, Toronto had a population of 2,794,356, making it the fourth-most populous city in North America. The Greater Toronto Area (GTA) has a population of over 6.7 million.

It is renowned as one of the most multicultural and cosmopolitan cities in the world. Approximately half of its residents were born outside of Canada, and over 200 ethnic origins are represented, with more than 160 languages spoken.

Toronto serves as Canada's primary economic engine and financial center, housing the Toronto Stock Exchange and the operational headquarters of Canada's five largest banks. It also boasts a highly diversified economy with major strengths in technology, design, financial services, life sciences, education, arts, fashion, aerospace, environmental innovation, food services, and tourism.
