## Corrections vs naive folklore

- **GTD is not a single app.** It is a capture/clarify workflow; tool choice is secondary.
- **Eisenhower is a filter, not a score.** It ranks attention; it does not estimate hours.
- **Inbox Zero means processed, not “zero stored mail forever.”** Archive/defer still counts as processed.
- **Back-to-back calendars are not proof of productivity.** Buffer and focus blocks are load-bearing, not optional polish.
- **Assistant autonomy still stops at external side effects.** Draft freely; send only with approval.

## Out of scope facts

Do not invent employer-specific PTO rules, mailbox retention policies, or calendar product limits as universal constants. State the mechanism and confirm against the user’s tools and org policy.
