# Research Notes and Sources

## Terminology and scope

- **Nick Bostrom, *Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies*** — https://global.oup.com/academic/product/superintelligence-9780198739838
  - Uses the term “superintelligence” for intellect that greatly exceeds human cognitive performance across virtually all domains of interest. This skill uses the label only as a reasoning-workflow name; it does not claim such capability.
- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Artificial Intelligence”** — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/artificial-intelligence/
  - Distinguishes philosophical and technical questions around artificial intelligence. Treat broad-capability claims as conceptual context, not an operational guarantee.

## Reasoning and decision support

- **NASA, “Systems Engineering Handbook” (SP-2016-6105 Rev2)** — https://www.nasa.gov/reference/nasa-systems-engineering-handbook/
  - Supports explicit requirements, trade studies, and verification in complex systems work; use it as a check against treating assumptions as requirements.
- **NIST, “AI Risk Management Framework 1.0”** — https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1
  - Supports documenting uncertainty, context, and risk in AI-assisted decisions; communicate confidence and preserve human authorization for consequential actions.
