# Sources — Rome

Official and primary references used to ground Rome guidance. Prefer these over secondary blogs when facts change. Re-check the linked page before quoting a live fee, deadline, eligibility rule, fare, or timetable.

## City and government

- Roma Capitale (City of Rome) portal: https://www.comune.roma.it/
- Italian government portal (Italia.it / amministrazione): https://www.italia.it/en
- Ministero dell'Interno: https://www.interno.gov.it/it
- Polizia di Stato: https://www.poliziadistato.it/
- Questura di Roma orientation via Polizia di Stato local pages: https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/questure

## Visas, residence, and work (informational)

- Ministero degli Affari Esteri — Visas for Italy: https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/home/en
- Immigration portal (Immigrazione / Ministero dell'Interno context): https://www.interno.gov.it/it/temi/immigrazione-e-asilo
- INPS (social security): https://www.inps.it/
- Agenzia delle Entrate (tax codes / codice fiscale): https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/web/english
- EU freedom of movement overview: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/residence/index_en.htm

Treat visa, tax-residency, and work-authorization guidance as orientation only. Licensed immigration counsel owns filings.

## Transport

- ATAC Roma (tickets, metro, bus, tram): https://www.atac.roma.it/
- Trenitalia (regional and national rail): https://www.trenitalia.com/
- Aeroporti di Roma (Fiumicino FCO / Ciampino CIA): https://www.adr.it/web/aeroporti-di-roma-en/
- Roma Servizi per la Mobilità / mobility context: https://romamobilita.it/

## Housing, cost, and daily life

- ISTAT official statistics: https://www.istat.it/en/
- Numbeo and listing portals are secondary market snapshots only; prefer ISTAT and current listing checks for planning ranges.
- Major lodging/listing portals for live rent snapshots (Idealista, Immobiliare.it, Airbnb, Booking) — treat as market quotes, not official stats.

## Visitor, culture, and attractions

- Turismo Roma / official visitor portal: https://www.turismoroma.it/en
- Colosseo archaeological park / Parco archeologico del Colosseo: https://colosseo.it/en/
- Musei Vaticani: https://www.museivaticani.va/
- Ministry of Culture (MiC) museums and sites orientation: https://cultura.gov.it/

## Health and emergencies

- Italian National Health Service (SSN) orientation via Ministero della Salute: https://www.salute.gov.it/
- European emergency number 112 context: https://ec.europa.eu/digital-strategy/our-policies/112-european-emergency-number_en

## Weather and seasonality

- Aeronautica Militare / MeteoAM: https://www.meteoam.it/
- Regional civil protection alerts when severe weather or crowd controls matter: start from national/regional protection portals rather than social media.

## Usage notes

- Package figures (rents, salaries, fares, meal prices) are planning estimates dated in the skill body; verify before booking, immigration, payment, or same-day safety decisions.
- Prefer Italian primary sources over generic “Europe travel” blogs when rules, ZTL, museum tickets, or residence procedures are at stake.
- When multi-city Italy routing dominates, load `italy` / `travel` rather than stretching Rome-only depth.
