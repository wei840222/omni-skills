# Sources — temu skill

Gate 6 research anchors. Prefer primary Temu Help / policy pages and re-verify region-specific shipping, return, and buyer-protection claims before quoting hard numbers.

## Marketplace and product pages

- [Temu homepage](https://www.temu.com/) — live catalog, deal rotation, and region storefront entry
- Product detail page (SKU-specific) — primary evidence for price, warehouse hints, material claims, and size charts
- Seller/store page on Temu — store age proxies, sales volume, and assortment breadth

## Shipping and delivery

- [Temu Help Center](https://www.temu.com/meu-help-center.html) — locate regional shipping articles from the live Help Center
- Checkout shipping estimate for the signed-in address — authoritative ETA and free-shipping threshold for that order
- Carrier tracking linked from the order page — post-purchase fulfillment evidence

## Returns, refunds, and buyer protection

- [Temu Return & refund Help](https://www.temu.com/meu-help-center.html) — return windows, exclusions, and return-shipping rules by region
- In-app/order dispute flow — documentation expectations for damaged, wrong, or not-as-described items
- Order protection countdown on the order detail page — act before protection expires

## Pricing and promotions (secondary)

- Product page strikethrough / "original" price — marketing reference only; not a market comparable
- Coupon and lightning-deal surfaces in the Temu app/web cart — validity windows and stacking rules change frequently
- Cross-check the same SKU/image on AliExpress, Amazon, and eBay for total-cost reality

## Review and quality signals (secondary)

- Buyer photo reviews on the product page — best available signal for color, size, material, and construction
- 1-star and 3–4 star photo reviews — defect clusters and measurement feedback
- Reverse image search of hero photos — bait-and-switch / stock-photo detection aid

## Notes for maintainers

- Shipping days, free-shipping thresholds, return windows, and coupon rules change by region and promotion; never hard-code a single global SLA without checking live pages and checkout.
- Brand-name deep discounts remain counterfeit-risk by default even when a listing looks "official."
- High-risk categories (brand goods, electronics, cosmetics, medical devices, car parts, supplements) need stronger evidence thresholds than decor/accessories.
