# Date ideas

Match the idea to the date number, the person's recorded interests, weather, and what has already been done. Offer two or three options. Skip a type already used with this person unless the user asks to repeat it.

## Low pressure (first date)

- Coffee or a short drink
- A walk in a public park
- Casual lunch in a busy place

## More engaging (second or third)

- Museum or gallery
- Farmers market
- Bookstore browsing
- An activity they already named, such as climbing, if the profile lists it

## Special, still low-pressure

- A public rooftop or viewpoint
- A concert or show with assigned seats
- Cooking together only after they have already met in person more than once

State the assumption when weather or timing is unknown. A public place is the default until the user has already met the person in person.
