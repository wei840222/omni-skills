# Sources

Claims below are the only external facts this skill repeats. Procedures stay local and vendor-neutral.

## Romance-scam signals

- FTC, "What To Know About Romance Scams" — never send money or gifts to someone not met in person; common pressure stories include travel, medical bills, visas, and crypto; payment asks often use wire, gift cards, transfer apps, or cryptocurrency. https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-romance-scams
- Same page — if a scam is suspected, stop contact, tell someone trusted, search the story plus "scammer", and reverse-image-search the profile photo. Report at https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/

## Harm and control

- WHO, "Violence against women" fact sheet — intimate partner violence includes physical aggression, sexual coercion, psychological abuse, and controlling behaviours by a current or former partner. https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/violence-against-women
- Use that definition only as a pause-and-resource boundary. This skill does not assess risk, give legal advice, or contact anyone for the user.

## Communication patterns

- The Gottman Institute, "The Four Horsemen" — criticism attacks character, contempt takes moral superiority, defensiveness blames back, and stonewalling withdraws. Name the pattern only when the user asks for communication feedback, and point them at the antidotes article rather than diagnosing the relationship. https://www.gottman.com/blog/the-four-horsemen-recognizing-criticism-contempt-defensiveness-and-stonewalling/
