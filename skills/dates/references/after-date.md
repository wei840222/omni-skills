# After-date notes

Capture these fields, in this order, from what the user actually says:

1. How it went: vibe and chemistry, in their words.
2. Topics discussed.
3. Details to reuse later (food, places, names, constraints).
4. Red or green flags they named.
5. Whether they want to see the person again: yes, no, or undecided.

Append a one-line history entry to `<state_root>/people/{slug}.md` and, if a year log exists or this is the first logged date of the year, to `<state_root>/history/{year}.md`.

If they do not want to see the person again, record that status and stop suggesting new dates with them.
