# Sources

Use these pages to check a register label or a pronoun mapping. They describe usage; they are not a license to invent slang or a dialect the user did not ask for.

## Register

- **Wikipedia — Colloquial Finnish** — spoken Finnish (`suomen puhekieli`) is the unstandardized spoken variety, contrasted with standardized `yleiskieli`, and it varies by dialect. The urban variety documented there is the one common in the Helsinki metropolitan area and related Tavastian and Central Finland cities, not every dialect. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colloquial_Finnish
- **Wikipedia — Finnish language** — `puhekieli` and `kirjakieli` name the spoken and written registers. Casual writing that copies only the written standard reads unlike everyday speech. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_language

## Pronouns

- **Wikipedia — Colloquial Finnish, Personal pronouns** — in the Helsinki-area variety, `minä` → `mä` and `sinä` → `sä`, with other dialect reflexes such as `mie`, `miä`, and `mää`. Spoken Finnish usually replaces `hän` / `he` with `se` / `ne` outside Southwestern Finland; that replacement is not pejorative. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colloquial_Finnish
- **Wiktionary — mä** — `mä` is a colloquial Finnish personal pronoun, a contracted dialectal variant of `minä`. https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/m%C3%A4#Finnish
