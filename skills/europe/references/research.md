# Research Sources

- The official EU portal: Your Europe (https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/index_en.htm)
- Schengen rules: European Commission Migration and Home Affairs (https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen-borders-and-visa/schengen-area_en)
- EU immigration portal: (https://immigration-portal.ec.europa.eu/)
- EU passenger rights: (https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/index_en.htm)

These sources cover the core framework for Europe basics, travel rights, borders, living, working, and studying in the EU.
