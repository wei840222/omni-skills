# Sources

Cite these pages when repeating a ratio, a prevalence figure, or a token-format claim. Re-measure any hex pair; do not copy a ratio from memory.

## WCAG 2.2 contrast

- W3C, WCAG 2.2 Recommendation — normative criteria index: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
- Understanding SC 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), Level AA — 4.5:1 text, 3:1 large text: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html
- Understanding SC 1.4.6 Contrast (Enhanced), Level AAA — 7:1 text, 4.5:1 large text: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-enhanced.html
- Understanding SC 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast, Level AA — 3:1 for UI components and meaningful graphics: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html
- Understanding SC 1.4.1 Use of Color, Level A — color is not the only visual means of conveying information: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html

## Color-vision deficiency

- NIH National Eye Institute, Color Blindness — about 1 in 12 men: https://www.nei.nih.gov/learn-about-eye-health/eye-conditions-and-diseases/color-blindness
- Colour Blind Awareness — about 1 in 12 men (8%) and 1 in 200 women: https://www.colourblindawareness.org/colour-blindness/

## Tokens and dark surfaces

- Design Tokens Community Group, DTCG format 2025.10 — portable token file shape: https://www.designtokens.org/tr/drafts/format/
- Material Design 3, Color system overview — tonal surfaces instead of shadow-only elevation: https://m3.material.io/styles/color/system/overview

Hex ratios in `references/contrast-ratios.md` and `references/palette-design.md` were computed with the WCAG relative-luminance formula against the cited criteria. They are not copied from a third-party contrast blog.
