# Official Sources Map — Vibe Marketing

Use these primary or high-authority sources when verifying experimentation practice, automation product claims, AI-disclosure expectations, and marketing measurement guidance. Prefer full URLs in PR notes. Re-open live pages before asserting current pricing, plan limits, or UI labels—vendor pages change.

## How to use these sources in-session

- When stating **tool pricing or seat limits**, open the live vendor pricing page first; skill prose is not the system of record.
- When advising **A/B winners**, separate early CTR signals from statistical confidence; cite Optimizely/Google/Meta docs for product-specific experiment features.
- When **AI-generated ads or endorsements** ship publicly, check FTC endorsement guides and applicable EU AI Act transparency expectations before claiming "no disclosure needed".
- Never paste customer PII, ad-account secrets, or private analytics exports into examples.
