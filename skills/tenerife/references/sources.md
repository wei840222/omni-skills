# Sources — Tenerife

Use these verified primary sources when island routing, taxation (IGIC/ZEC), microclimates, or public services apply in Tenerife.

## Tax, Special Economic Zone (ZEC), and Fiscal Regime

- **ZEC Ventajas Fiscales (Official Tax Benefits Portal)** — https://canariaszec.gob.es/la-zec/ventajas/
  - Official statutory benefits portal explicitly specifying the reduced 4% corporate income tax rate (Impuesto sobre Sociedades) for qualifying companies operating within the Canary Islands Special Zone (Zona Especial Canaria), compared to the European average of ~23%.
- **Gobierno de Canarias — Agencia Tributaria Canaria (Tributos & IGIC)** — https://www.gobiernodecanarias.org/tributos/
  - Official tax administration portal for the Canary Islands General Indirect Tax (IGIC). Outlines the statutory rate structure: zero rate (0%), reduced rates (3% and 5%), general rate (7%), increased rates (9.5% and 15%), and special rate (20%). Note: There is no statutory 4% IGIC bracket.
- **Agencia Tributaria (AEAT Spanish State Tax Agency)** — https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/
  - Spanish national tax authority administering personal income tax (IRPF), non-resident income tax (IRNR), and VAT/IGIC cross-border exemptions.

## Official Tourism and Island Orientation

- **Turismo de Tenerife (Official Destination Hub)** — https://www.webtenerife.com/
  - Island tourism board covering microclimate variations (warm and dry south coast vs greener, cloudier north coast), hiking safety, and Teide National Park access.
- **Cabildo de Tenerife (Island Government Portal)** — https://www.tenerife.es/
  - Insular government portal overseeing public roads, bus infrastructure, civil protection advisories, and nature conservation.

## Transit and Urban Mobility

- **Metrotenerife (Tranvía de Tenerife)** — https://www.metrotenerife.com/
  - Tramway operator connecting Santa Cruz de Tenerife and San Cristóbal de La Laguna.

- **Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)** — https://www.ine.es/

## Weather and Severe Alerts

- **AEMET (Agencia Estatal de Meteorología)** — https://www.aemet.es/
  - Official meteorological forecasts and alerts for Canary Islands microclimates, high-altitude mountain conditions, and Calima (Saharan dust haze) events.
