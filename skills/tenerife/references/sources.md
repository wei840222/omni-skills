# Research sources (Gate 6)

Orientation sources for `tenerife`. Re-open the live page before stating a
current Digital Nomad Visa income floor, IGIC/ZEC rate, bus fare, cable-car
ticket, rent median, or emergency procedure as fact. This package teaches
**island routing heuristics**, not personalized immigration or tax advice.

Last verified during handoff: 2026-10-03.

## Official tourism and island orientation

- **Turismo de Tenerife** — official destination hub — https://www.webtenerife.com/
- **Cabildo de Tenerife** — island government portal — https://www.tenerife.es/
- **Spain.info — Canary Islands / Tenerife** — national tourism orientation — https://www.spain.info/en/destination/tenerife/
- **Parque Nacional del Teide** — park access and safety orientation — https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/parques-nacionales-oapn/red-parques-nacionales/parques-nacionales/teide.html
- **Teleférico del Teide** — cable car operations (confirm tickets/hours live) — https://www.telefericoteide.com/

## Immigration, NIE, and remote-work visa orientation

- **Ministerio del Interior — Extranjería** — foreigner procedures entry — https://www.interior.gob.es/opencms/es/servicios-al-ciudadano/tramites-y-gestiones/extranjeria/
- **Sede Electrónica Administraciones Públicas — Extranjería** — appointment and form orientation — https://sede.administracionespublicas.gob.es/pagina/index/directorio/info/extranjeria
- **Incluirme / official guidance mirrors vary by consulate** — always prefer the consulate page for the traveler's passport country when filing
- **Spain Digital Nomad / telework residence** — confirm current legal basis and income multiples on the live government page before quoting € floors (commonly discussed as a multiple of SMI; **do not hard-code SMI from memory**)
- **EU free movement (EU/EEA/Swiss)** — Your Europe residence orientation — https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/residence/index_en.htm

## Tax and Canary special regime (orientation only)

- **Agencia Tributaria** — national tax authority — https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/
- **ZEC — Zona Especial Canaria (official)** — regime overview and eligibility — https://canariaszec.com/
- **Gobierno de Canarias — IGIC** — Canary indirect tax orientation — https://www.gobiernodecanarias.org/hacienda/
- Treat 4% ZEC corporate rate and IGIC standard rate tables inside skill notes as
  **orientation**. Company setup needs a gestoría / tax advisor and current BOE text.

## Notes for agents

1. Rent and coworking bands in this package are **directional**. Prefer live Idealista / operator pages when the user is budgeting.
2. Digital Nomad Visa income floors track SMI multiples and change; never copy a euro floor into a filing packet without the current official HTML/PDF for that date and nationality.
3. Teide cable car and Masca access sell out or close for weather — open operator pages same day.
4. ZEC/IGIC content is educational. Do not present package text as a binding tax opinion.
