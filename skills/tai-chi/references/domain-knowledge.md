# Tai Chi Practice Guidelines and Foundations

Tai Chi is a mind-body practice that originated in China as a martial art but has evolved into a widely recognized form of gentle exercise. It is often referred to as "meditation in motion."

## Health and Balance Benefits
Research from leading health institutions supports the benefits of Tai Chi for balance, flexibility, and muscle strength. It has been shown to reduce the risk of falls in older adults and improve overall physical and mental well-being. The emphasis on slow, controlled movements combined with deep breathing helps engage the core and stabilize posture.

## Core Principles (Root, Shift, Breathe)
- **Rooting:** Establishing a strong, stable foundation connected to the ground. This involves a slight bend in the knees and maintaining an upright posture (head over pelvis).
- **Weight Shifting:** Moving weight smoothly and continuously between the legs. The control required in shifting weight without locking joints is central to balance improvement.
- **Breathing:** Coordinating deep, relaxed breaths with the physical movements.
