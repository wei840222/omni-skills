# Grammar — Chinese Grammar Mechanics

Use this reference when revising Chinese grammar, word order, or measure words. Match the target variant and register; examples describe common Mandarin patterns rather than an exhaustive grammar.

## 的、地、得

| Form | Typical role | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 的 | modifier before a noun | 红色的车 |
| 地 | adverbial modifier before a verb | 认真地学习 |
| 得 | complement after a verb or adjective | 跑得快 |

Use the word that follows as the first check:

- A noun normally takes 的: 高兴的孩子。
- A verb may take 地: 高兴地说。 In informal writing, some adverbial 地 is omitted when the sentence remains clear.
- A complement takes 得: 说得很清楚。

Read the full phrase before changing it: style, fixed expressions, and the target publication can affect a borderline case.

## 了

`了` can mark a completed action or a changed state.

- Verb `了`: 我买了书。
- Sentence-final `了`: 下雨了。
- Both functions can coexist: 我吃了饭了。

For a completed past action, use 没/没有 + verb rather than 没/没有 + verb + 了:

- 我没吃饭。
- 我没看那本书。
- 我不饿了 is also valid because final 了 marks a changed state.

Choose the placement from the intended meaning, not from a mechanical ban on two instances:

- 我昨天去了超市买东西。
- 我昨天去超市买东西了。
- 我昨天去了超市。

All three are grammatical, with different focus.

## 把 and 被

Use 把 when a specific object is acted on and the result, destination, or outcome matters:

- 请把门关上。
- 他把作业做完了。

Use 被 when the receiver of an action is the topic. An agent may be named when it matters, or left implicit when it is unknown or irrelevant:

- 窗户被风吹开了。
- 我被老师批评了。
- 我被批评了。
- 他受到了批评。

Prefer an active sentence when it states the action more directly:

- 我们解决了这个问题。

## 是……的

Use 是……的 to emphasize a detail of a completed action—such as time, place, manner, or agent:

- 我是昨天来的。
- 他是在北京学的中文。
- 我是坐飞机去北京的。
- 我是去北京的。

In casual speech, 是 may be omitted, but 的 remains:

- 我昨天来的。

## Word order and Europeanised phrasing

Chinese often reads more naturally when the action arrives early and long modifiers are split into clauses.

| Less direct | More direct |
| --- | --- |
| 我们进行讨论 | 我们讨论 |
| 对他的批评引起了争议 | 批评他引起了争议 |
| 这个问题被我们解决了 | 我们解决了这个问题 |
| 这是一个非常重要的关于环境保护的决定 | 这个关于环境保护的决定非常重要 |

These are revision options, not universal substitutions. Retain a formal construction when the genre, legal meaning, or established terminology requires it.

## Measure words

Use a measure word when counting a noun. Select it by convention and register.

| Measure word | Typical use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 个 | general items and people | 一个人 |
| 条 | long or narrow items | 一条消息、一条鱼 |
| 张 | flat items | 一张票、一张纸 |
| 本 | books | 一本书 |
| 件 | clothes or matters | 一件衣服、一件事 |
| 辆 | vehicles | 一辆车 |
| 台 | machines | 一台电脑 |
| 位 | respectful reference to a person | 一位老师 |
| 名 | formal count of people | 一名学生 |

## Revision sweep

Before delivering a grammar revision:

1. Read each changed sentence in context and preserve its intended meaning.
2. Check 的、地、得 by syntactic role, not pronunciation.
3. Check aspect and negation before changing 了.
4. Check whether 把 or 被 changes focus or responsibility.
5. Replace a generic 个 only when a conventional measure word is clearly better.
6. Read the final text aloud in the target register.
