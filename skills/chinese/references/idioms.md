# 成语 and 俗语 Guide

成语 (four-character idioms) and 俗语 (colloquial sayings) add cultural depth but are easily misused.

---

## Frequency Ceiling

**One 成语 per paragraph at most.** Three in a paragraph reads as 公文 filler or a student showing off.

| Context | Appropriate density |
|---------|-------------------|
| 公文, formal report | 1-2 per page |
| Business email | 0-1 per message |
| 小红书, 公众号 | 1-2 per post |
| WeChat chat | Rarely; slang preferred |
| Academic paper | Domain-specific terms, not 成语 |

---

## The 12 Most-Misused 成语

These are commonly used with wrong meanings:

| 成语 | Common misuse | Correct meaning |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| 空穴来风 | "無中生有" | 有根據的傳聞（有洞穴才有風） |
| 差强人意 | "很差" | 大致還能接受（勉強滿意） |
| 首当其冲 | "首先" | 最先受到攻擊或遭受災難 |
| 万人空巷 | "巷子空了" | 轟動一時的盛況（人都出來了） |
| 炙手可热 | "很熱門" | 權勢大（貶義，非褒義） |
| 一文不名 | "不值錢" | 一文錢都沒有（窮） |
| 目无全牛 | "沒有全局觀" | 技藝精湛（看到細節而非整體） |
| 七月流火 | "天氣熱" | 天氣轉涼（農曆七月，大火星西沉） |
| 不刊之论 | "不能刊登" | 不可修改的正確言論 |
| 下里巴人 | "低俗" | 通俗文藝（非貶義） |
| 洛阳纸贵 | "紙很貴" | 作品風行一時 |
| 曾几何时 | "曾經" | 時間過去沒多久 |

---

## When to Use 成语

**Use when:**
- The 成语 precisely captures the situation
- The register is formal or literary
- The audience will recognize it (age, education)

**Avoid when:**
- A plain phrase works equally well
- The audience is young or international
- You're stacking multiple 成语 together
- The meaning is approximate rather than exact

---

## 俗语 (Colloquial Sayings)

俗语 are more conversational than 成语:

| 俗语 | Meaning | Register |
|------|---------|----------|
| 吃一堑长一智 | Learn from mistakes | Casual |
| 好事不出门，坏事传千里 | Bad news travels fast | Casual |
| 三个臭皮匠，顶个诸葛亮 | Collective wisdom beats individual | Casual |
| 当局者迷，旁观者清 | Involved parties lose perspective | Neutral |
| 己所不欲，勿施于人 | Don't do to others what you dislike | Neutral/formal |

---

## Register Appropriateness

| Register | 成语 | 俗语 |
|----------|------|------|
| 极正式 (公文) | Yes, sparingly | No |
| 正式 (business) | Yes | Rarely |
| 客气 | Occasionally | Occasionally |
| 日常 | Rarely | Yes |
| 亲近 | Rarely | Yes |

---

## Testing a 成语

Before using a 成语, verify:

1. **Meaning**: Does it actually mean what you think? (Check the 12 misused above)
2. **Register**: Is it appropriate for this audience?
3. **Frequency**: Have you used one recently in this text?
4. **Alternatives**: Would a plain phrase be clearer?

If any answer is uncertain, use the plain phrase instead.
