# Politeness and Face in Chinese

Navigating refusal, apology, gratitude, and social harmony.

---

## The Refusal Ladder

Chinese refusal is indirect. Direct "不" is rare outside close relationships.

| Rung | Phrase | When to use |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 (softest) | 可能有点困难 | Vague difficulty, leaves room |
| 2 | 我看看/我考虑一下 | Delay, implies likely no |
| 3 | 恐怕不太方便 | Polite impossibility |
| 4 | 实在不好意思，这次可能不行 | Apologetic refusal |
| 5 | 这个我真的帮不了 | Direct, for repeated requests |
| 6 (hardest) | 不行/做不到 | Blunt, close relationships only |

### Refusal strategy
1. **Acknowledge** the request's validity
2. **Express regret** (不好意思/恐怕)
3. **Give reason** (brief, not over-explained)
4. **Offer alternative** (if possible)

### Example
```
A: 能帮我周末加班吗？
B: 哎呀，周末家里有点事（reason），恐怕不太行（refusal）。
   要不你问问小王？（alternative）
```

## Apology Weight

Different apologies for different situations:

| Phrase | Weight | When to use |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| 不好意思 | Light | Minor inconvenience, bumping into someone |
| 对不起 | Medium | Genuine mistake, letting someone down |
| 抱歉 | Medium-formal | Professional context |
| 非常抱歉 | Heavy | Serious error |
| 深表歉意 | Very heavy | Formal, official |

### Rules
- **不好意思** is the most common; use it for small things
- **对不起** carries more weight; don't overuse it
- **抱歉** is safer in professional contexts
- Match the apology to the offense weight

### Over-apologizing
Too much apology makes the other person uncomfortable:

**Too much:**
> 对不起对不起，真的太抱歉了，都是我的错，非常抱歉…

**Better:**
> 不好意思，下次注意。

## Accepting Compliments

Chinese culture deflects compliments rather than accepting directly.

| Response | When to use |
|----------|-------------|
| 哪里哪里 | Traditional modest deflection |
| 没有没有 | Casual deflection |
| 还行吧 | Casual, downplaying |
| 谢谢 | Increasingly acceptable, especially among younger people |
| 你过奖了 | Formal modest deflection |
| 托您的福 | Deflecting to the complimenter's influence |

### Rules
- **Don't** just say 谢谢 and leave it (too abrupt in traditional contexts)
- **Don't** agree enthusiastically (显得不谦虚)
- **Do** deflect modestly, then optionally redirect

### Example
```
A: 你中文说得真好！
B: 哪里哪里，还差得远呢。/ 谢谢，还在学。
```

## Asking a Favour

### Strategy
1. **Establish relationship** (if not close)
2. **Explain situation** briefly
3. **Make request** indirect (能不能/可不可以)
4. **Acknowledge burden** (麻烦你了/不好意思)
5. **Offer reciprocity** (下次我请你/有什么需要帮忙的)

### Example
```
A: 小王，在忙吗？（establish）
   我这边遇到个问题，搞了半天没弄好（situation）。
   你能不能帮我看看？（request）
   麻烦你了，改天请你吃饭（reciprocity）。
```

### Indirect request patterns
- 能不能麻烦你…
- 可不可以帮我…
- 你方便的话…
- 不知道你有没有时间…

## Giving Face (给面子)

In group settings, preserve others' dignity:

### Do
- Praise publicly, criticize privately
- Acknowledge others' contributions
- Use titles (王总, 李老师)
- Let senior people speak first
- Attribute good ideas to others (刚才王总说的很对)

### Avoid
- Correcting someone publicly
- Taking credit in front of their boss
- Making someone admit they're wrong
- Interrupting senior people
- Joking at someone's expense in group

## Face-Saving Phrases

| Situation | Phrase |
|-----------|--------|
| Someone made a mistake | 没事，小问题 |
| Someone is embarrassed | 这有什么，我也经常这样 |
| You disagree | 你说的有道理，不过… |
| You need to correct | 可能我理解有误，是不是… |
| Someone can't do something | 这个确实有难度 |

## Register and Politeness

| Register | Refusal | Apology | Request |
|----------|---------|---------|---------|
| 极正式 | 恕难从命 | 深表歉意 | 恳请 |
| 正式 | 恐怕不太方便 | 非常抱歉 | 能否麻烦您 |
| 客气 | 可能有点困难 | 不好意思 | 能不能麻烦你 |
| 日常 | 这次可能不行 | 不好意思 | 帮我看看 |
| 亲近 | 不行/做不到 | 对不起啦 | 帮个忙 |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Refusing too directly** — 不行/不要 without softening
2. **Over-apologizing** — makes the other person uncomfortable
3. **Accepting compliments directly** — 谢谢 without deflection
4. **Asking favours without reciprocity** — 帮我做这个 (no 麻烦你了)
5. **Criticizing in public** — loses face for the other person
6. **Mismatched weight** — 深表歉意 for a small bump, 不好意思 for a major error
