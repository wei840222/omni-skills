# AI Tells — Fingerprint Detection in Chinese Text

AI-generated Chinese has recognizable patterns. This reference helps identify and eliminate them.

---

## Structural Tells

### Essay Scaffolding

AI loves three-part structures:

- "首先…其次…最后…" (Firstly…secondly…finally…)
- "一方面…另一方面…" (On one hand…on the other hand…)
- Numbered lists for everything (1. 2. 3. even in casual chat)

**Natural pattern**: Vary sentence shape only when it fits the channel. Keep the point easy to find; a fragment or a conversational opener should carry real tone or context rather than simulated messiness.

### Uniform Paragraph Length

AI writes paragraphs of similar length (3-5 sentences each). Humans vary: one-sentence paragraphs, then a long rambling one, then a fragment.

**Fix**: Let paragraph breaks follow the message's meaning and channel. Vary rhythm where it improves readability; do not pad or split solely to imitate a person.

### Perfect Topic Sentences

AI may make every paragraph sound like an essay section. Conversational Chinese can begin with context, but the reader should still be able to identify the point.

**Natural pattern**: "上周请假的事，我想补充一下后续安排。"

## Lexical Tells

### Overused Transition Words

| AI-typical | Human alternative |
|-----------|-------------------|
| 值得注意的是 | (just say it directly) |
| 需要指出的是 | (omit, or use 其实/说实话) |
| 毋庸置疑 | 肯定/没得说 |
| 综上所述 | 所以/总之 |
| 与此同时 | 另外/还有 |
| 换言之 | 就是说/说白了 |

### Hedge Phrases

AI hedges excessively:

- "在一定程度上" (to some extent)
- "从某种意义上说" (in a sense)
- "可以说" (it can be said)

**Human pattern**: Humans are more direct. "这事有点麻烦" not "这事在一定程度上存在复杂性".

### Formal Vocabulary in Casual Contexts

AI uses书面语 (written language) in chat:

- "进行购买" → 买
- "予以拒绝" → 拒了/没同意
- "具备能力" → 能/会

**Rule**: If it's chat, use spoken forms. 进行/予以/具备 are for reports, not WeChat.

## Formatting Tells

### Bold in Chat

AI bolds key terms in casual messages:

> 这个**方案**的核心是**优化流程**

Humans rarely bold in WeChat. If emphasis is needed, they use:

- 这个方案的核心是优化流程 (just say it)
- 这个方案的核心是「优化流程」(brackets for emphasis)
- 这个方案的核心是优化流程！！！(exclamation marks)

### Bullet Points in Conversation

AI converts conversational flow into bullet lists. Humans write in paragraphs or stream-of-consciousness.

**Exception**: Work summaries, meeting notes — bullets are fine there.

### Emoji Overuse or Misplacement

AI either:

- Uses no emojis (too formal)
- Sprinkles them randomly (🎉✨🚀)
- Uses them at the end of every sentence

**Human pattern**: Emojis appear at emotional peaks, not as decoration. One emoji at the end of a funny message, not after every line.

## Sweep Procedure

Before finalizing any Chinese output, run this check:

### 1. Structure Check

- [ ] Are there "首先/其次/最后" patterns? → Break them up
- [ ] Are all paragraphs similar length? → Vary deliberately
- [ ] Does the structure fit the channel rather than reading like a generic essay? → Keep the point clear while varying rhythm where useful

### 2. Vocabulary Check

- [ ] Search for: 值得注意/需要指出/毋庸置疑/综上所述 → Replace or delete
- [ ] Search for: 在一定程度上/从某种意义上 → Delete or make direct
- [ ] Check 进行/予以/具备 in casual contexts → Replace with spoken forms

### 3. Formatting Check

- [ ] Is this chat? → Remove bold, reduce bullets
- [ ] Are emojis present? → Check placement (emotional peaks, not decoration)
- [ ] Is punctuation perfect? → Humans drop final 。, use ！more, repeat characters (哈哈哈)

### 4. Tone Check

- [ ] Read aloud — does it sound like a person talking?
- [ ] Are there hedging phrases that add nothing? → Cut them
- [ ] Is the register consistent? (Don't mix 您 and 你, don't mix 购买 and 买)

## Examples

### AI-typical

> 首先，我们需要明确目标。其次，值得注意的是，资源有限。最后，综上所述，建议采取分阶段实施策略。

### Human rewrite

> 目标先定一下。资源就这么多，别想一步到位，分几步走吧。

---

### AI-typical

> 这个方案在一定程度上可以优化流程，但需要指出的是，成本较高。

### Human rewrite

> 这方案能优化流程，但贵。

---

## Key Principle

AI writes like it's being graded. Humans write to communicate. The goal is communication, not perfection.
