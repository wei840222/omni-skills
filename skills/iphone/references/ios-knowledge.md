# iOS Domain Knowledge

This reference provides domain knowledge regarding iOS behavior related to battery, storage, and privacy optimization.

## Battery Optimization

- **Optimized Battery Charging**: Learns the user's daily charging routine and can delay charging past 80% until the phone is likely to be needed, helping reduce battery aging.
- **Low Power Mode**: Reduces or affects features like 5G (except for video streaming), Auto-Lock (defaults to 30 seconds), display brightness, display refresh rate (limited up to 60 Hz on Pro models), iCloud Photos, automatic downloads, email fetch, and background app refresh.

## Storage Optimization

- **Offload Unused Apps**: Automatically removes unused apps but keeps their documents and data. Reinstalling the app will place the data back.
- **Optimize iPhone Storage (Photos)**: Keeps smaller, space-saving photos and videos on the device while all original, full-resolution versions are stored in iCloud.

## Privacy Optimization

- **App Tracking Transparency (ATT)**: Requires apps to get the user's permission before tracking their data across apps or websites owned by other companies.
- **Location Services**: Can be set to "Never", "Ask Next Time Or When I Share", "While Using the App", or "Always". Can also toggle "Precise Location" on or off for specific apps.
