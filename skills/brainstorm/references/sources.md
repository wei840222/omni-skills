input; skill text must still match verified guidance.

## SCAMPER and structured ideation

- **IxDF — What is SCAMPER?** — definition of Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Rearrange. https://ixdf.org/literature/topics/scamper
- **IxDF — How to use the best ideation methods: SCAMPER** — step-by-step SCAMPER provocations; credits Alex Osborn's checklist and Bob Eberle's mnemonic; Modify includes Magnify/Minify. https://ixdf.org/literature/article/learn-how-to-use-the-best-ideation-methods-scamper
- **Wikipedia — SCAMPER** — historical overview of Eberle's mnemonic and Osborn inspiration (secondary). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCAMPER
