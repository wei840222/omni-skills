
## Register Checks and Sources

- Prefer the recipient's requested formality over a blanket casual default. In unfamiliar or professional contexts, use `Εσείς`; use `Εσύ` for peers and established casual conversation.
- Greeklish is a user- and channel-dependent choice, not a substitute for Greek script. Keep one writing system per short message unless the user supplies mixed-script text to preserve.
- Treat slang and profanity as opt-in: mirror the user's tone, avoid escalating it, and provide a neutral alternative when the audience is unclear.
