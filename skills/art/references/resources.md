# Educational resources

Curated starting points. Prefer official project homes. Availability and pricing change — verify before purchase advice.

## Foundational drawing

- **Drawabox** — structured free lessons on form, boxes, and spatial reasoning: https://drawabox.com/
- **Proko** — figure, portrait, and anatomy lesson library (free + paid tiers): https://www.proko.com/

## Digital painting foundations

- **Ctrl+Paint** — free digital painting fundamentals library: https://www.ctrlpaint.com/
- **Krita** — free/open-source professional painting application: https://krita.org/
- **Krita documentation** — tools and workflow reference: https://docs.krita.org/

## Museums / looking practice (appreciation)

- **Google Arts & Culture** — high-resolution public art browsing: https://artsandculture.google.com/
- Use museum collection sites for documented provenance and artist intent when making historical claims.

## How to recommend

1. Match resource to the named medium and level.
2. Give one primary resource + one optional alternate — not a dump of ten links.
3. Pair every curriculum link with a **this-week drill** so links do not replace practice.
4. For paid tools/courses, state free alternatives first unless the user already standardized on a stack.
