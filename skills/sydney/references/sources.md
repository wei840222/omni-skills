# Sources — Sydney

Back to [Sydney Skill Overview](../SKILL.md).

Use these verified primary sources when transit fares, rental bond deposits, civic regulations, or federal healthcare frameworks apply in Sydney.

## Municipal Governance & City Life

- **City of Sydney** — https://www.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/
  - Local government authority for central Sydney managing civic amenities, planning permits, waste recycling, sustainability programs, and local community facilities.

## Public Transit & Opal Card

- **Transport for NSW** — https://transportnsw.info/
  - Unified transport authority coordinating Sydney Trains, Metro, Buses, Light Rail, and Sydney Ferries.
- **Opal Card** — https://opal.com.au/
  - Smartcard ticketing and contactless payment system across the Opal network. Specifies daily travel caps, weekly travel caps, off-peak discount hours (30% discount), and weekend caps.

## Rental Tenancy & Bond Lodgement

- **NSW Fair Trading (Rental Bonds Online)** — https://www.fairtrading.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-property/renting/rental-bonds-online
  - NSW government agency safeguarding tenant and landlord rights. Mandates Rental Bonds Online (RBO) escrow for the statutory maximum 4-week rental bond, condition reports, and dispute resolution via NCAT.

## State Destination Guidance & Federal Services

- **Destination NSW** — https://www.destinationnsw.com.au/
  - Official NSW state tourism agency providing coastal walk guidance (Bondi to Coogee), harbour cruise options, and blue mountain day-trip logistics.
- **Australian Government Services Portal** — https://www.australia.gov.au/
  - Federal gateway for government assistance, Medicare eligibility orientation for permanent residents and reciprocal health care agreement (RHCA) holders, and ATO taxation rules.
