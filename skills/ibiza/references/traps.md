# Ibiza-Specific Traps

- Booking far from planned nightlife and underestimating 02:00-06:00 transport friction.
- Pre-book beaches and clubs during peak season rather than assuming easy walk-in.
- Account for legal tourist tax and extra accommodation charges.
- Calculate total trip cost realistically regardless of flight price.
- Include traffic buffers for cross-island schedules.
- Ensure compliance and deposit clarity before renting vehicles or housing.
- Use only licensed transfers or party offers.
- Check business schedules beforehand during the off-season.
- Ensure visa status matches seasonal-job assumptions.
- Obtain adequate insurance for emergency medical scenarios.
