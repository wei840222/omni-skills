# Ibiza Domain Knowledge and Sources

Use these sources when mutable Ibiza guidance matters. Prefer official portals for visas, tax, transit, traffic controls, and tourism claims; use market indexes only as dated benchmarks.

## Visas, Residency, and Telework

- **ETIAS official portal** — activation status and who should apply via https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias_en
- **ETIAS how it works** — rollout timing and process overview via https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias/how-it-works_en
- **Spain consular services hub** — national visa and consular entry points via https://www.exteriores.gob.es/en/ServiciosAlCiudadano/Paginas/Servicios-consulares.aspx
- **Spain UGE portal** — large-company / telework residence routes entry via https://www.inclusion.gob.es/web/unidadgrandesempresas/

Confirm ETIAS activation status and telework eligibility on official pages before presenting a hard requirement or timeline. Prefer the UGE hub plus consular pages over stale deep links.

## Tourism Tax and Local Regulation

- **Balearic sustainable tourism tax legal text (BOE)** — ITS overnight tourist-stay tax framework via https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2016-3744
- **Consell Insular d'Eivissa** — island institutional portal via https://www.conselldeivissa.es/
- **Ibiza official tourism portal** — destination and practical guidance via https://ibiza.travel/

## Transport, Airport, and Mobility Controls

- **AENA statistics portal** — passenger and flight statistics entry via https://www.aena.es/es/estadisticas/inicio.html
- **AENA Ibiza airport page** — airport operations orientation via https://www.aena.es/es/ibiza.html
- **Ibiza getting-around / mobility guidance** — island mobility orientation and peak-season constraints via https://ibiza.travel/organiza-tu-viaje/moverse-por-la-isla/
- **Ibiza how-to-arrive guidance** — arrival orientation via https://ibiza.travel/organiza-tu-viaje/como-llegar/

Treat summer vehicle caps, ferry-vehicle rules, and road congestion as operational constraints that need a current-season check.

## Cost, Housing, and Market Benchmarks

- **idealista rent index (Eivissa)** — municipal rent level benchmarks via https://www.idealista.com/sala-de-prensa/informes-precio-vivienda/alquiler/baleares/eivissa-illes/
- **Numbeo cost of living (Ibiza)** — comparative living-cost ranges via https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Ibiza
- **Numbeo rent (Ibiza)** — comparative rent ranges via https://www.numbeo.com/property-investment/in/Ibiza

Quote rent and meal figures with month/year context. Market pages are benchmarks, not booking guarantees.

- **Hospital Can Misses** — main island hospital orientation via https://www.hospitalcanmisses.es/
- **AEMET** — Spanish weather agency via https://www.aemet.es/en/portada
- **US travel advisory (Spain)** — country-level advisory context via https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/spain-travel-advisory.html
- **UK FCDO travel advice (Spain)** — country-level travel advice via https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/spain

## Obsolete / Fragile Claims to Re-check

- Exact rent bands, club prices, taxi fares, and meal benchmarks in package references are estimates and must be revalidated before quoting as current.
- ETIAS activation, Schengen 90/180 application, and telework-residence eligibility are time-sensitive.
- Peak-season traffic restrictions and vehicle-cap measures change by year; verify before advising ferry-vehicle or car-rental plans.
- Airport passenger/flight totals should be taken from AENA statistics with the relevant year labeled.
