# Shanghai Visa and Residence Guide (2026)

Use this as orientation, not legal advice. China sets and enforces the rule for each nationality. Re-check `references/sources.md` and the traveller's consulate before filing or booking.

## What to verify first

| Fact | Current check |
| --- | --- |
| British citizen, standard passport | Visa-free up to 30 days until 31 December 2026 for tourism, business visits, family or friend visits, or transit. Paid work, journalism, study, and stays over 30 days need a visa |
| Other nationalities | Do not copy the British rule. Open the Chinese consulate page for that passport |
| Passport | At least 6 months validity after arrival and 2 blank pages for a British citizen. Confirm the equivalent rule for other passports |
| Work | Z visa plus a valid work permit. A tourist, business-visit, or visa-free entry does not allow salaried work |
| Registration | Register the place of residence with the local Public Security Bureau within 24 hours of arrival, and again after an address change. Hotels usually register guests at check-in |

Source for the British snapshot: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/china/entry-requirements (still current at 24 September 2026).

## Common visa routes

| Route | Typical purpose | Work allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa-free window | Short tourism, family visit, transit, or business meetings, where the nationality qualifies | No salaried work |
| L visa | Tourism when visa-free does not apply | No |
| M visa | Business visits and meetings | No salaried local employment |
| Z visa + work permit | Formal employment | Yes, in the permitted role and location |
| X1/X2 visa | Study | Only within the school and permit terms |
| S/Q family routes | Family reunion or visit | Usually no direct employment |

## Work route in practice

Treat the timeline as a planning band, not a promise. The employer and the local exit-entry office own the dates.

| Step | Typical owner | What to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work authorization notice | Employer | Role, location, and name spelling match the passport |
| Z visa issued abroad | Applicant | Category and entries before travel |
| Entry, medical check, residence registration | Applicant | 24-hour registration if the first stay is not a hotel |
| Work permit card and residence permit | Employer + applicant | Location matches the actual workplace |

If the employer or city changes after arrival, confirm whether a new permit is required before starting the new role.

## Documents often requested

- Passport with enough validity and blank pages.
- Employer documents and a signed contract.
- Education and experience proof, authenticated when the office requires it.
- A criminal-record check for the relevant jurisdiction.
- A medical exam from a designated channel.
- The Shanghai address-registration record.

## Compliance habits

1. Track permit expiry at 90, 60, and 30 days.
2. Register again after every address change.
3. Keep digital and paper copies of approvals in the user's own storage, not in this skill package.
4. Name one HR owner for each renewal.
5. Call 12367 before or immediately after renewing a passport while in China.
