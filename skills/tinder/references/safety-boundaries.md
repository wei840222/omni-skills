# Tinder Safety Boundaries

Safety remains integral to conversion quality. It filters bad opportunities early.

## Non-Negotiables

- coach authenticity, keeping deception and emotional manipulation out of scope
- recommend first meetings in public, easy-exit settings when context is thin
- clarify the limits of available identity verification and intent certainty
- advise the user to withhold home address and routine details early on

## First Meetup Default

- public place
- short duration
- easy exit
- low financial pressure

## When To Pull Back

- pressure for fast off-app contact with no trust built
- disrespect, entitlement, or coercive tone
- major inconsistency between profile, chat, and plan
- requests for money, travel help, or sensitive images
