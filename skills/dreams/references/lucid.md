# Lucid dreaming practice

Load only when the user wants lucidity techniques, dream-sign training, or to log a lucid episode. Practice aids recall and awareness; they are not medical treatment.

State file: `<state_root>/lucid/techniques.md` (create on first lucidity work).

## Core techniques (keep short)

### Reality checks (daytime)

- Look at hands and count fingers
- Read text, look away, read again
- Try to push a finger through the opposite palm
- Ask “Am I dreaming?” and genuinely test

Do a few checks daily, especially on dream-sign moments (mirrors, flying cues, dead phones, impossible text).

### MILD (Mnemonic Induction of Lucid Dreams)

1. Set an intention while falling asleep: “Next time I dream, I will notice I am dreaming.”
2. Replay a recent dream and pick the moment awareness could have sparked.
3. Repeat the intention calmly; return to the replay if the mind wanders.

### WBTB (Wake Back To Bed) — optional advanced

1. Sleep ~4–6 hours, wake for 15–45 minutes with soft light.
2. Read one page of dream notes or do a few reality checks.
3. Return to sleep with MILD intention.
4. Skip WBTB if the user is sleep-deprived, ill, or has unstable sleep; route chronic sleep loss to `sleep`.

### Dream-sign list

Maintain a short personal list inside `techniques.md` from the journal (flying, teeth, school hallways, etc.). Check those cues in waking life.

## Logging lucid episodes

Append to `techniques.md` or the night’s journal entry:

```markdown
## Lucid log — YYYY-MM-DD
- Cue that triggered awareness:
- Stability tactics used (spin, touch, verbal stabilize):
- What you did while lucid:
- Length / clarity (brief sketch):
- Next tweak:
```

## Safety and scope

- Stop or downshift techniques that wreck next-day function.
- Sleep paralysis, dream enactment with injury risk, or trauma nightmare floods: supportive stance, no forced induction; point to clinical sleep care / `sleep` skill boundaries.
- Do not promise controllable lucid dreams or therapeutic outcomes.
