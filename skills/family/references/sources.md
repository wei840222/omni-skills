# Sources — family

Gate 6 research anchors for multi-member household coordination, privacy-minimizing shared notes, and care logistics. Prefer public-health and standards guidance over influencer systems. Re-verify before stating jurisdiction-specific legal or clinical claims.

## Household operations and task ownership

- **Getting Things Done overview (official)** — capture → clarify → organize → reflect → engage loop for inbox and next-action discipline via https://gettingthingsdone.com/what-is-gtd/
- **NIST IR 8286D — Integrating Cybersecurity and Enterprise Risk Management** — owner/accountability framing useful for household risk handoffs via https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8286/d/final
- **GOV.UK — Looking after children and young people** — practical safeguarding posture when minors are in scope via https://www.gov.uk/browse/childcare-parenting

## Privacy minimization and data hygiene

- **NIST Privacy Framework 1.0** — Identify-Govern-Control-Communicate-Protect functions for private-by-default family notes via https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework
- **ICO — Data protection and privacy in family settings (UK)** — minimum-necessary sharing mindset for household data via https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/home-schooling/
- **FTC — Protecting Personal Information: A Guide for Business** — practical retention and access-control basics adaptable to household files via https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business

## Care coordination without clinical overreach

- **AHRQ — Care Coordination** — handoff clarity, owner, and closed-loop follow-up patterns via https://www.ahrq.gov/ncepcr/care/coordination.html
- **CDC — Caring for Children** — public-health entry points; clinical decisions remain with professionals via https://www.cdc.gov/childrensmentalhealth/index.html
- **WHO — Standards for improving quality of care for children and young adolescents** — escalation and quality framing, not DIY diagnosis via https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241511513

## School and logistics interfaces

- **US Department of Education — Family Educational Rights** — parent/guardian access boundaries around education records via https://www.ed.gov/laws-and-policy/laws-related-to-higher-education/ferpa
- **CDC — Healthy Schools** — school health logistics context when coordinating forms and attendance constraints via https://www.cdc.gov/healthyschools/index.htm

## Practice notes for this skill

- Keep medical, legal, and custodial authority with responsible adults and licensed professionals.
- Promote private facts into shared files only when another person must act.
- Prefer compact operating files (`weekly-plan`, calendar, inbox, owner/fallback) over transcript dumps.
- When minors or dependent adults are involved, identify decision authority before proposing plans that assume it.
