# Sources — events

Gate 6 research anchors for personal event logging, gathering ops, and deadline hygiene. Prefer primary producer or standards pages over random listicles. Re-verify dates, bag policies, and registration rules per venue before presenting them as fact.

## Personal knowledge and calendar practice

- [Getting Things Done — overview of trusted systems](https://gettingthingsdone.com/what-is-gtd/) — framing for capturing commitments into an external system instead of chat memory.
- [ICALENDAR RFC 5545](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc5545) — industry vocabulary for events, DTSTART/DTEND, and recurrence when bridging to real calendars.
- [Apple Calendar User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/calendar/welcome/mac) — consumer calendar behaviors relevant when handing off to `apple-calendar-macos`.

## Live events, tickets, and venue logistics

- [Eventbrite Organizer / attendee help hub](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/) — common registration, ticket transfer, and order-confirmation patterns.
- [Ticketmaster Help](https://help.ticketmaster.com/) — confirmation numbers, entry rules, and transfer caveats as attendee-facing norms (verify per event).
- [FIFA / major-stadium bag policy examples via venue pages](https://www.msg.com/madison-square-garden) — treat each venue's published policy as authoritative; do not generalize one arena's rules to all.

## Hosting and guest management

- [Emily Post — invitations & guest list etiquette overview](https://emilypost.com/advice) — RSVP follow-up norms without inventing social obligations the user rejected.
- [CDC gathering / community guidance index](https://www.cdc.gov/) — only when the user asks about health constraints for gatherings; cite the specific page retrieved, not memory.

## Practice notes for this skill

- Confirmation IDs and doors times are operational facts; symbolic or emotional write-ups belong in `journal`.
- Recurrence (birthdays, annual conferences) is a light annual list here, not a full RRULE engine — upgrade to calendar skill when the user needs device-level recurrence.
- Never paste live card data into markdown; order/confirmation tokens only.
- When citing venue bag or entry rules, open the venue's current page rather than reusing last year's notes.
