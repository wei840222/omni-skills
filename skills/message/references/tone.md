# Tone and Context Matching

## Fundamental rule

The same person may need different tones on different platforms:

- Professional on email / LinkedIn-class channels
- Casual on Discord / Telegram friend chats
- Brief on X
- Warm on WhatsApp

Read recent history on that channel before assuming.

## Tone spectrum

### Formal (client/investor email, LinkedIn)

- Full sentences and standard grammar
- Professional greeting: "Hi María,"
- Structure: context → ask → close
- Signature with title when external
- Emojis absent or minimal

### Semi-formal (work Slack, professional Discord)

- Complete sentences, relaxed grammar
- Short greeting or none
- Light emoji (👍, ✅) when the room uses them
- Stay inside the thread when one exists

### Casual (friends, gaming Discord, personal Telegram)

- Fragments and lowercase are fine
- Emojis and reactions expected
- Inside jokes only when already established
- Brevity is valid ("lol k")

## Context that changes the draft

### Recent events

- After layoffs or grief in the community, keep a solemn register
- Acknowledge outages before marketing or celebration
- Match the room's current sentiment

### Relationship history

- First message: slightly more formal
- Long history: match the established pattern
- After conflict: measured warmth, not forced cheer

### Time of day

- Morning work hours: slightly more formal for external mail
- Late night: brief unless the relationship is already night-owl casual

## User style matching

### Copy

- Typical message length
- Emoji density (including zero)
- Greeting/closing habits
- Response cadence

### Avoid

- Stock phrases the user never writes
- Emoji they never use
- Being faster or warmer than their baseline
- Signing personal texts with their full name

## Relationship types

### Investors / board

- Always formal
- Draft for review
- Precise language; no casual hedging that hides risk

### Enterprise clients

- Professional, clear, low jargon
- Prefer durable written record

### Startup-to-startup clients

- Often casual — still check history first
- Match their energy after you see it

### Team

- Match team culture
- Thread on shared channels

### Friends / family

- Casual to very casual
- Voice notes only with user authorization and human recording

## Red-flag tone mirrors

| Their signal | Your mirror |
|--------------|-------------|
| "Dear [Name]" | Formal reply |
| "lol" / "bruh" | Casual register OK |
| Full punctuation + full sentences | Match formality |
| Emojis present | Reciprocate lightly |
| No greeting | Skip greeting |
