## MEDDIC / MEDDPICC

- MEDDICC — MEDDIC / MEDDPICC methodology and process (element list; MEDDIC → MEDDICC +Competition → MEDDPICC +Paper Process; PTC origin)
  — https://meddicc.com/meddpicc-sales-methodology-and-process
  Takeaway: Official public materials list Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Paper Process, Implicate/Identify Pain, Champion, Competition; MEDDPICC is described as the complete modern form via Paper Process on top of Competition.

## BANT

- HubSpot Blog — How I use BANT to qualify prospects (definitions; budget beyond dollar amount; buying committee; how not to over-disqualify)
  — https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/bant
  Takeaway: BANT is Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline; use it to structure early qualification while still mapping stakeholders and spend patterns.

## Pipeline coverage

- Salesforce Blog — Why the 3x Pipeline Coverage Rule in Sales Is Broken (Jack Keenan; coverage depends on win rate and cycle length; worked formula)
  — https://www.salesforce.com/blog/pipeline-coverage/
  Takeaway: Fixed 3× quota is only valid under narrow win-rate/cycle assumptions; compute pipeline need from quota, revenue win rate, and cycle days.

## Challenger

- Wikipedia — *The Challenger Sale* (Dixon & Adamson, Portfolio/Penguin, 2011; complex B2B; challenger profile; relationship-only method insufficient)
  — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Challenger_Sale
  Takeaway: Teach, tailor, and take control of the customer conversation for complex solutions; do not read as “relationships do not matter.”
