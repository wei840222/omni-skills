# Qualification Frameworks — B2B

Load when choosing a qualification method, scoring element gaps, or coaching discovery. Prefer primary definitions below; map labels to the user’s CRM fields without inventing universal score thresholds.

## MEDDIC family (enterprise default)

MEDDIC originated at PTC (Dick Dunkel / Jack Napoli, 1990s) and remains the base enterprise checklist. Naming variants on the official MEDDICC materials:

| Name | Elements |
|------|----------|
| **MEDDIC** | Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identify Pain, Champion |
| **MEDDICC** | MEDDIC + **Competition** |
| **MEDDPICC** | MEDDICC + **Paper Process** (most complete for complex modern B2B) |

Some enablement materials brand the pain element as **Implicate the Pain** to stress quantified business impact; treat that as the same `I` pillar, not a ninth letter beyond MEDDPICC’s eight.

| Element | Key question | At-risk signal |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Metrics** | Which numbers must improve, by how much, by when? | Vague “efficiency” with no baseline or target |
| **Economic Buyer** | Who has overall authority on the buying decision? | “My manager decides” with no path to that person |
| **Decision Criteria** | What principles and requirements will they use to choose? | Criteria unknown or refused |
| **Decision Process** | What steps and owners lead to a decision? | No timeline or process map |
| **Paper Process** | After decision intent, what legal/procurement/security steps remain? | Late stage with zero paperwork motion |
| **Identify / Implicate Pain** | What specific problem hurts, and what is the cost of inaction? | “Just exploring” with no consequence narrative |
| **Champion** | Who sells internally when you are absent? | No one will spend political capital |
| **Competition** | Who else is evaluated, including status quo / build-in-house? | Blind to alternatives until final round |

Use MEDDIC early to qualify; keep it live through the full cycle so GTM partners share one language.

## BANT (early prioritization, often SMB)

| Element | Question |
|---------|----------|
| **Budget** | Is commercial capacity realistic (amount, source, flexibility)? |
| **Authority** | Are you speaking with someone on the decision path? |
| **Need** | Is there a real problem your offer can address? |
| **Timeline** | When must a decision or implementation happen? |

**How to use:** structure early discovery and rank effort. **How not to use:** hard-disqualify on the first incomplete budget answer—probe spend patterns, prior investments, and the buying committee first (HubSpot BANT guidance).

## SPIN selling (discovery conversation)

| Phase | Purpose | Example prompt |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Situation** | Context | “How do you handle X today?” |
| **Problem** | Surface difficulties | “What is hardest about that?” |
| **Implication** | Expand cost of inaction | “If this continues for two quarters, what breaks?” |
| **Need-payoff** | Buyer states value | “If that were fixed, what would it free up?” |

SPIN is a questioning sequence, not a replacement for MEDDIC evidence.

## Gap selling

```text
Current state → Desired state → Gap
   problems        outcomes     your offer addresses the gap
```

Anchor talk tracks on the gap and success metrics, not a feature inventory.

## Challenger sale

From Dixon & Adamson (*The Challenger Sale*, 2011): for complex B2B solutions, pure relationship-building is often insufficient. High performers tend to:

1. **Teach** — bring a novel commercial insight
2. **Tailor** — connect that insight to the customer’s political and economic reality
3. **Take control** — guide the buying process constructively

Use Challenger behaviors **with** trust and multi-threading. “Take control” means leading the process and reframing value, not ignoring stakeholders or burning the relationship.

## Framework selection heuristic

| Context | Start with |
|---------|------------|
| Complex enterprise, multi-stakeholder | MEDDPICC element scorecard |
| Early SDR qualification / simpler motion | BANT to prioritize, then deepen pain/authority |
| Discovery call structure | SPIN or Gap questions |
| Insight-led deal strategy | Challenger teach/tailor/take-control plan + MEDDIC proof |
