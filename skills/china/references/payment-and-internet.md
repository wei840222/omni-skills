# Payments and Internet Constraints — China

## Core Reality

Daily travel quality depends on payment readiness (Alipay / WeChat Pay) and internet accessibility. International plastic cards are mainly useful at international hotels and selected high-end merchants. QR payments dominate everyday spending. See `references/sources.md` before asserting current foreign-card support details.

## Practical Setup

- **Payment apps:** Install **Alipay** and **WeChat** before arrival and set up both. Merchant acceptance still differs by vendor.
- **Foreign cards:** Current tourist flows commonly support linking selected international cards, but product names and eligibility change. Complete passport real-name verification and a small live payment test before relying on either app.
- **Cash backup:** Carry a modest amount of RMB in small bills (for example about ¥500). Cash remains legal tender, yet small vendors may struggle to make change.

## Internet and App Strategy

- **Great Firewall:** Google, Meta, WhatsApp, X, and Instagram are typically blocked on mainland Wi-Fi and local SIMs.
- **Preferred visitor path:** Use international roaming or a travel eSIM that exits mainland routing. Treat third-party VPNs as optional, user-owned risk rather than a default recommendation.
- **Maps and translation:** Prefer Apple Maps, Amap/Gaode, or Baidu Maps over Google Maps for mainland routing. Download offline maps and offline translation packs before arrival; WeChat/Alipay also provide built-in translation helpers.

## Required Precautions

1. Verify both Alipay and WeChat with a passport and a tiny live charge before travel day.
2. Navigate with Apple Maps, Amap/Gaode, or Baidu Maps rather than Google Maps.
3. Secure roaming or an outbound-routing travel eSIM before depending on Western apps.
