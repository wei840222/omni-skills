# Research Sources (Gate 6)

Use these primary or official public sources before asserting current entry, payment, or connectivity rules. This skill provides planning baselines, not live immigration or payment advice.

## Entry, visa-free travel, and TWOV

- **National Immigration Administration of China (English)** — official entry, exit, and transit policy portal: https://en.nia.gov.cn/
- **National Immigration Administration — 144-hour visa-free transit overview** — confirm eligible nationalities, ports, and regional movement limits before advising TWOV itineraries: https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147625/n147717/c162185/content.html
- **China Visa Application Service Center** — tourist/business visa documentation baselines when visa-free or TWOV does not apply: https://www.visaforchina.cn/

## Payments and local apps

- **Alipay — Tourists / international card guidance** — verify current foreign-card binding and tourist payment flows before arrival: https://www.alipay.com/
- **WeChat Pay — International users** — verify current foreign-card support and merchant coverage assumptions: https://pay.weixin.qq.com/
- **People's Bank of China / cash remains legal tender** — keep a small RMB cash backup; do not claim cash is unusable nationwide without local context.

## Transport and trip logistics

- **China Railway 12306** — official high-speed and conventional rail booking channel; confirm schedules and seat availability live: https://www.12306.cn/
- **Civil Aviation Administration of China** — aviation disruption and passenger-rights baseline: http://www.caac.gov.cn/

## Connectivity and maps

- **Apple Maps / Amap (Gaode) / Baidu Maps** — prefer locally accurate mapping over Google Maps for mainland routing; verify offline pack availability before advising.
- Travel eSIM or international roaming that exits mainland routing is the preferred Great Firewall bypass path for visitors; treat third-party VPN recommendations as optional and user-owned risk.
