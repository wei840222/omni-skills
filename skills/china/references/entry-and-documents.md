# Entry and Documents Planning — China

## Core Principle

Entry and transit rules change. Treat every nationality list, TWOV window, and permitted-city cluster as a live fact: confirm it against the National Immigration Administration (or the traveler's consulate) before booking non-refundable segments. See `references/sources.md`.

## Planning Checklist

- **Passport:** Plan for at least 6 months remaining validity and blank visa pages; airlines and border officers may apply stricter local rules.
- **Visa-free entry:** China has expanded unilateral visa-free programs for selected nationalities and durations. Do not quote a country list from memory—look up the traveler's passport against the current NIA announcement.
- **Transit Without Visa (TWOV):** 24 / 72 / 144-hour TWOV options exist only for eligible nationalities, designated ports, and limited regional clusters. Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan usually count as a third region for the A→China→C routing test, but the permitted mainland movement area is still port-specific.
- **Tourist (L) visa:** If visa-free or TWOV does not clearly apply, apply for an L visa with flight and hotel evidence before travel.
- **Temporary residence registration:** Foreigners generally must register within 24 hours. Hotels usually handle this; private stays and some short-term rentals may require police-station or WeChat mini-program registration.

## Practical Rules

- Keep digital and offline copies of the passport data page, visa/entry evidence, and hotel bookings.
- Keep a screenshot of the return or onward ticket; airlines and immigration often check it.
- Keep the itinerary inside the geographic limits of the chosen TWOV cluster. Crossing into a non-permitted province is a policy violation, not a soft suggestion.

## Required Precautions

1. Confirm current eligibility before relying on border-zone or Hainan arrival shortcuts.
2. For 144-hour TWOV, enforce a strict A → China → C itinerary and stay inside the published cluster for that entry port.
3. Before booking a private stay, verify the host can legally accept foreigners and complete registration.
