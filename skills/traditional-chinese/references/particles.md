# Casual Particles

Use particles sparingly and only when they suit the target region, relationship, and register.

| Particle | Typical effect | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 啊／呀 | Softens or warms a statement | `謝謝呀。` |
| 呢 | Invites a follow-up or asks “what about …?” | `你呢？` |
| 吧 | Signals a suggestion or uncertainty | `我們走吧。` |
| 嘛 | Marks something as obvious or already understood | `本來嘛。` |
| 喔／哦 | Acknowledges information | `知道了喔。` |
| 耶 | Expresses excitement; especially natural in Taiwan | `好耶！` |
| 囉 | Marks a completed action or a light conclusion | `吃完囉。` |
