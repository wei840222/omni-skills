# Dubai Domain Research

- **Population**: As of 2025, Dubai's population stands at 4 million, 92% of whom are expatriates.
- **Economy**: Centres on trade, tourism, aviation, financial services, and real estate.
- **Tourism**: In 2024, Dubai was the seventh most-visited city globally.
- **Airport**: Dubai International Airport (DXB) is the world's busiest airport for international passenger traffic, handling over 92 million passengers in 2024.
