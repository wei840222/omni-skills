# Official Sources & Technical Specifications

This document records the official governmental and immigration portals for Dubai and the United Arab Emirates.

## Official UAE & Dubai Government Portals

- **The Official Portal of the UAE Government**: [https://u.ae/en](https://u.ae/en)
  - Federal government portal providing official entry visa categories, Green Visa, and Golden Visa 10-year residency guidelines.
- **UAE Corporate Tax Regime**: [https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/finance-and-investment/taxation/corporate-tax](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/finance-and-investment/taxation/corporate-tax)
  - Federal corporate tax structure: 0% tax rate on taxable net profits up to AED 375,000, and a standard 9% rate on profits exceeding AED 375,000.
- **Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security (ICP)**: [https://icp.gov.ae/en/](https://icp.gov.ae/en/)
  - Emirates ID application procedures, federal entry permits, visa status tracking, and digital residency records.
- **General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs Dubai (GDRFA)**: [https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en](https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en)
  - Dubai-specific entry permits, residency issuance and renewal, Amer center service standards, and smart gate registration.
