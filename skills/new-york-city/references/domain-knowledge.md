# Current NYC Operations Reference

Use this reference when a user needs a current operational fact rather than a durable planning framework. Re-check the linked primary source before quoting a fare, toll, service pattern, airport route, or policy because these details change.

## Congestion Relief Zone tolling

- The Congestion Relief Zone covers Manhattan south of and including 60th Street. Before estimating a driving, taxi, or ride-hail trip through the zone, verify the current toll schedule and exemptions on the MTA page.
- Treat a trip's route, time, vehicle type, and potential exemptions as decision inputs. Compare public transit where it meets the user's luggage, accessibility, and timing needs.

## OMNY and transit fares

- Use the OMNY official site for current contactless-payment acceptance and fare-capping rules. Tell users to use the same card or device for each eligible ride when fare-capping behavior matters.
- For a route recommendation, pair fare guidance with current service notices from MTA; payment acceptance does not prove a route is operating normally.

## Airport transfers

- For JFK, compare the official AirTrain and rail/subway options against luggage, arrival time, transfer tolerance, and service alerts. For LaGuardia, use the official airport ground-transportation page; it has no direct rail station.
- If a planned rail, bus, or airport-transfer leg is disrupted, use the responsible airport or MTA page to choose the next viable route rather than promising a fixed travel time.

## Primary sources

- **LaGuardia Airport public transportation** — official bus and ground-transport access information: https://www.laguardiaairport.com/to-from-airport/public-transportation
