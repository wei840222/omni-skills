## Chief Data Officer (CDO) Research

### Definition and Role
A Chief Data Officer (CDO) is accountable for enterprise data strategy, governance, quality, and value creation. The role has shifted from compliance-centric stewardship toward enabling analytics, AI readiness, and data products that map to business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- **Data Strategy:** Align data investments to revenue, cost, and risk priorities.
- **Data Governance:** Define ownership, classification, quality thresholds, and access models that teams actually follow.
- **Analytics Platforms:** Choose BI and semantic-layer approaches that support governed self-service.
- **AI/ML Readiness:** Treat data quality and feature availability as the ceiling for model value.
- **Privacy and Compliance:** Embed privacy-by-design and subject-rights workflows before scaling collection.
