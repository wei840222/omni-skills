## Execution Pitfalls to Mitigate

- Scope creep — mitigate by governing critical data subsets iteratively
- Tool distraction — mitigate by defining business problems before selecting technology
- Low adoption — mitigate by building reports tied to active business processes
- Compliance debt — mitigate by implementing privacy reviews in the design phase
- Storage bloat — mitigate by collecting data with a specific, planned use case
