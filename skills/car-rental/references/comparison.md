# Rent vs Lease vs Buy — Car Rental

Load before recommending lease, subscription, or purchase instead of a short
rental.

## Decision framework

### When to rent (days to weeks)

**Best for:** travel and vacations; occasional needs; testing before buying;
temporary shop loaners; special occasions.

**Advantages:** maximum flexibility; low commitment; insurance often bundled;
different car each time; maintenance included.

**Trade-offs:** highest daily cost; paperwork each booking; age limits;
credit-card holds.

### When to lease (months to years)

**Best for:** temporary relocation (3–24 months); business use with local tax
benefits; testing a lifestyle before buying; preferring predictable monthly
cost and included maintenance; wanting a newer car every few years.

**Advantages:** lower monthly cost than rolling short rentals; maintenance
often included; predictable expenses; gap coverage commonly included.

**Trade-offs:** mileage caps (often 10–15k km/year); early termination fees; no
ownership at end; wear-and-tear charges; condition requirements.

### When to buy

**Best for:** permanent need; high annual mileage; customization; 5+ year keep
horizon; building equity.

**Advantages:** no mileage caps; asset ownership; free customization; no
payments after payoff; can sell anytime.

**Trade-offs:** highest upfront cost; depreciation risk; maintenance and
insurance responsibility; lower flexibility.

## Break-even calculations

### Rental vs lease

**Rule of thumb:** continuous need beyond ~2–3 months usually favors lease or
subscription over repeating short rentals.

**Example (illustrative only — recompute with live quotes):**

- Rental: €40/day ≈ €1,200/month
- Lease: €400/month + €1,000 deposit
- Rough crossover: ~2 months of continuous use

Also weigh: rental insurance bundling, lease insurance requirements, deposit
opportunity cost, and flexibility value.

### Lease vs buy

**Rule of thumb:** keeping 5+ years usually favors buying on total cost.

**3-year mid-size sedan illustration (replace with live numbers):**

| Option | 3-year sketch |
|--------|---------------|
| Lease | €400 × 36 = €14,400 |
| Buy financed | Purchase − expected resale + interest |
| Buy cash | Purchase − expected resale |

Also weigh maintenance on owned cars, depreciation uncertainty, capital
opportunity cost, and flexibility.

## Special situations

### Company / business use

Lease is often tax-advantageous (local rules): deductible payments, possible
VAT recovery, predictable fleet cost. Confirm with the user's accountant.

### Expat / relocation

Prefer lease for known duration; subscription when duration is uncertain;
check local vs international operators and re-export rules.

### Testing before buying

1. Rent the exact model for a weekend.
2. If interested, lease 3–6 months.
3. Buy only with confidence from that trial.

### Seasonal needs

- Summer cottage: rent per visit.
- Winter-only car: consider a 6-month lease.
- Compare annual totals both ways before locking in.
