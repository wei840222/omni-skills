# Onboarding Skill Research Sources

## Activation and time-to-value

- **Amplitude — What is user activation?** — defines activation as the first aha / value moment and ties it to retention lift — https://amplitude.com/blog/user-activation
- **Mixpanel — Activation metric guide** — operationalizes signup → key action conversion and time-to-value measurement — https://mixpanel.com/blog/activation-metric/
- **Reforge — Activation** — frames activation as the bridge between acquisition and retention loops — https://www.reforge.com/guides/activation

## Signup friction and progressive profiling

- **Baymard Institute — Checkout & form UX research** — documents field-count friction and progressive disclosure patterns applicable to signup — https://baymard.com/blog
- **Nielsen Norman Group — Web form design** — shorter forms and deferred optional fields reduce abandonment — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-form-design/

## Empty states, checklists, and first-run UX

- **Nielsen Norman Group — Empty states** — empty screens need explanation, example, and a single next action — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-design/
- **Intercom — Onboarding checklists** — checklist psychology, quick wins, and progress persistence — https://www.intercom.com/blog/product-tours-onboarding-checklists/
- **Appcues — Product tour best practices** — keep tours short (few steps), contextual, and skippable — https://www.appcues.com/blog/product-tour-best-practices

## Lifecycle email after signup

- **Customer.io — Onboarding email sequences** — welcome → reminder → proof → win-back timing patterns — https://customer.io/blog/onboarding-email-sequence/
- **Iterable — Lifecycle messaging** — stop or branch sequences when the activation event fires — https://iterable.com/blog/

## Metrics and cohort context

- **Amplitude — Retention analysis** — compare activated vs non-activated cohorts on D1/D7 retention — https://amplitude.com/blog/retention-analysis
- **Mixpanel — Retention reports** — cohort retention linked to early activation events — https://mixpanel.com/blog/retention-report/
