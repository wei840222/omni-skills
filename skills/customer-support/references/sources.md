## Effort and loyalty (research)

- **HBR — Stop Trying to Delight Your Customers** (Dixon, Freeman, Toman; July–August 2010)  
  https://hbr.org/2010/07/stop-trying-to-delight-your-customers  
  Extracted: large-scale contact-center/self-service study; loyalty improves more by reducing customer effort than by theatrical “delight”; common frictions include re-explain, channel switch, transfer, repeat contact; tactics — head off next issues, address emotion, minimize channel switching, learn from disgruntled customers, focus on problem solving not speed; introduces Customer Effort Score framing as loyalty-relevant.

## Contact quality metrics (industry definitions)

- **Call Centre Helper — Ultimate guide to first call resolution**  
  https://www.callcentrehelper.com/the-ultimate-guide-to-first-call-resolution-105108.htm  
  Extracted: FCR framed as a core contact-centre KPI; guidance emphasizes complete resolution quality and improvement practice rather than a single universal magic number for every operation.
- **Qualtrics — What is CSAT and how do you measure it?**  
  https://www.qualtrics.com/experience-management/customer/what-is-csat/  
  Extracted: CSAT is a defined satisfaction metric from customer feedback; commonly asked as overall satisfaction with goods/service; often scored on a 1–5 scale and reported as the percentage of satisfied/very satisfied (top-box) responses.

## Identity and authentication orientation

- **NIST SP 800-63B — Digital Identity Guidelines (Authentication and Lifecycle Management)**  
  https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-3/sp800-63b.html  
  Extracted: authentication proves control of authenticators; memorized secrets are “something you know” with length/blacklist guidance; verifier impersonation/phishing resistance requirements for higher assurance; session management and authenticator lifecycle considerations. Use as orientation for **not** collecting passwords in clear chat and preferring channel-bound recovery—not as a full IdP implementation guide.

## Clear language and accessible help

- **Plain language guide series (Digital.gov / plainlanguage.gov)**  
  https://www.plainlanguage.gov/guidelines/  
  Extracted: plain language is content the audience can understand efficiently; U.S. Plain Writing Act framing for public-facing clarity; guides cover principles, writing, design, and testing for understanding.
- **U.S. DOJ — Guidance on Web Accessibility and the ADA**  
  https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/  
  Extracted: why web accessibility matters; examples of barriers; when ADA requires accessible web content; high-level how-to orientation for making web content usable by people with disabilities. Use when support replies link to help centers or status pages.
