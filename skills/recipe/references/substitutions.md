# Substitutions — recipe

Substitute by **function**, not by name: bind, leaven, brown, thicken, acidify, fat, sweetness.

## Rules
- Check `<state_root>/health/profile.md` first.
- Name the allergen / constraint and the swap explicitly.
- Mark every uncooked substitution `untested`.
- After swapping, re-check leavening and liquid balance (`vetting.md`).
- Do not silent-substitute allergens even when `diet` prefers a pattern.
