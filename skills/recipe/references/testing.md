# Testing — recipe

## After a cook
1. Write a make-log row in `<state_root>/recipe/made/<year>.md`:
   date | recipe | rating | change | outcome.
2. Promote a repeated successful change into the recipe's Variations.
3. Only then remove an `untested` mark from a scale or substitution.
4. Record kitchen facts that changed a number (oven offset, salt brand) in `memory.md`.
