## Oven, fan, and altitude practice

- Conventional → fan/convection: reduce setting by about 20 °C (35 °F) **or** shorten time ~25%, not both — treat as kitchen practice, confirm against the appliance manual.
- Boiling-point drop with altitude is approximately 1 °C per 300 m; above ~1000 m, increase liquid slightly and watch leavening/sugar in baked goods.

## Food safety adjacent reads

- Prefer thermometer finish temperatures and dated source attribution over smell tests when adapting cook times after a scale.
- Allergen handling stays in the shared health box; never silent-substitute.

## Practice notes

- Prefer grams for anything whose ratio can break (batters, doughs, custards, brines, cures).
- Preserve `## Original` numbers before any conversion.
- Mark every uncooked scale/substitution `untested` until the make log promotes it.
