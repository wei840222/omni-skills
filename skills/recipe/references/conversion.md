# Conversion — recipe

## Volume → mass
Use per-ingredient density, never a universal cup factor. Anchors (US cup, spoon-and-level unless noted):

| Ingredient | g / US cup | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| All-purpose flour | 120 | Dip-and-sweep ~142 |
| Granulated sugar | 200 | |
| Brown sugar (packed) | 213 | |
| Butter | 227 | US stick = 113 g |
| Honey | 340 | |
| Water / milk | 237 | ~1 g/ml |

1 US cup = 236.6 ml. Metric cups (250 ml) and Japanese cups (200 ml) must be recorded in `## Original`.

## Temperature
- °F → °C: `(F - 32) × 5/9`
- Fan/convection: −20 °C (35 °F) **or** ~25% less time, not both
- Gas mark: read exact table values; do not interpolate between uneven steps

## Altitude (above ~1000 m)
- Boiling point drops ~1 °C per 300 m
- Slightly more liquid; watch sugar and leavener in baked goods
