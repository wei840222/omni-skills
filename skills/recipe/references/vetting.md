# Vetting — recipe

Sanity-check a recipe before first cook or after a heavy rewrite.

## Checks
- Hydration vs flour for doughs/batters (flag extremes).
- Salt as % of relevant base (pot dish vs dough).
- Chemical leavener band vs flour weight.
- Fat and liquid enough for the method claimed.
- Time vs temperature plausible for thickness/pan size.
- Allergen conflict vs `<state_root>/health/profile.md`.

If a check fails, say which ratio looks off and what evidence would confirm it. Do not silently “fix” heirloom numbers — preserve them in `## Original` and propose a tested variant beside them.
