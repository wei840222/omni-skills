# Scaling — recipe

1. Compute `factor = target ÷ base` (servings or pan area).
2. Multiply every mass by `factor`.
3. Correct non-linear terms from `SKILL.md` → What Does Not Scale.
4. Recompute pan area if the tin changes; keep depth when possible.
5. Restate finish as internal temperature or visual cue — never multiply clock time blindly.
6. Mark the scaled file/variant `untested` until cooked.
