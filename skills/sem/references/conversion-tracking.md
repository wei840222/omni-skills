# Conversion Tracking

- Define primary conversion actions around the business outcomes used for bidding and reporting; keep secondary actions available for observation when helpful.
- Assign conversion values that reflect the relative business value of different outcomes.
- Set a qualified-call duration when phone calls are a conversion action so short misdials and spam do not represent the desired outcome.
- Interpret modeled and attribution-dependent metrics as estimates, and compare measurement settings before drawing a performance conclusion.
