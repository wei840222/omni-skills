# Scaling Paid Search

- Use impression-share and budget diagnostics to distinguish budget constraints from limited eligible demand.
- Expand only after defining the efficiency-versus-volume trade-off and the conversion measurement that will judge it.
- Localize targeting, ads, landing pages, budgets, and measurement for each market rather than treating international expansion as a location-only change.
- Validate imported or duplicated campaigns on the destination platform before relying on their performance data.
