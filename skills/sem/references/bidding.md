# Bidding

- Verify that conversion actions, values, and campaign goals represent the outcome the bidding strategy should optimize before changing a conversion-based strategy.
- Select a bidding strategy that matches the campaign objective, then use target CPA or target ROAS only when its target is realistic for the available conversion data and business economics.
- Change one material bidding control at a time and compare results after the learning period shown in the account.

## Recovery

- If a new efficiency target sharply reduces traffic or spend, relax the target toward recent attainable performance and recheck conversion measurement before making another bid-strategy change.
- If automated bidding spends in an unexpected pattern, verify budget, targets, conversion goals, and recent account changes before switching strategies.
