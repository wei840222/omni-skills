# Keyword Strategy

- Select keyword match types for the query coverage and control required by the campaign objective.
- Review search-term reports on a recurring cadence; add irrelevant queries as negative keywords at the campaign or ad-group scope that matches the exclusion.
- Group closely related keywords with ads and landing pages that serve the same intent.
- Treat competitor-brand targeting as a deliberate policy and budget decision; confirm applicable trademark and platform policies before launching.
