# Platform Automation

- Review automated recommendations against the campaign objective, measurement design, and budget before applying them.
- Use regular campaign types when the required reporting or controls are unavailable in a simplified campaign type.
- Treat Performance Max as a separate, goal-based campaign type and evaluate its impact with the measurement design appropriate to the account.
- Use scripts and rules only when their trigger, scope, rollback path, and owner are explicit.

## Recovery

- If an automated change has an unwanted effect, use account change history to identify the change, restore the intended configuration, and document the guardrail before re-enabling automation.
- If a Performance Max campaign is not serving as expected, inspect eligibility, policy status, budget, bidding targets, assets, and conversion goals before changing audience signals.
