## Game Mechanics and Collection Management

- **Set Collection**: A mechanic where players collect sets of items (like games themselves) and manage resources or tokens to optimize their playtime and tracking.
- **Backlog Management**: Managing unplayed or unfinished games, which represents a core behavior for personal gaming systems.
- **Video Game Preservation**: Collecting physical and digital games to ensure their legacy.
