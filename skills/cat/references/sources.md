# Official Sources Map - Cat

Use these primary sources when verifying triage thresholds, toxic exposures, stress handling, and care logistics. Prefer the full URL in PR notes and user-facing citations.

## Emergency and clinical triage

- **ASPCA Animal Poison Control** — toxin exposure escalation and why chat cannot replace poison-control dosing guidance via https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/animal-poison-control
- **ASPCA: Signs Your Pet Needs Emergency Care** — breathing distress, collapse, trauma, and nonproductive retching as emergency signals via https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/general-pet-care/signs-your-pet-needs-emergency-care
- **Cornell Feline Health Center: Emergency Care** — feline emergency presentation framing and urgency language via https://www.vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/emergency-care
- **AAHA: How to Handle a Pet Emergency** — rapid assessment priorities and when to transport immediately via https://www.aaha.org/resources/how-to-handle-a-pet-emergency/

## Urinary obstruction and litter signals

- **Cornell Feline Health Center: Feline Lower Urinary Tract Disease** — male-cat obstruction risk, litter straining, and same-day urgency via https://www.vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/feline-lower-urinary-tract-disease
- **Cornell Feline Health Center: Litter Box Problems** — litter avoidance as medical/stress signal rather than defiance via https://www.vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/litter-box-problems

## Behavior, environment, and multi-cat stress

- **AVMA: Cat Behavior Problems** — environment-first behavior work and when professional help is warranted via https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare/cat-behavior-problems
- **International Cat Care: Scratching** — redirect scratching with better options at the target location via https://icatcare.org/advice/scratching/
- **International Cat Care: Multi-cat households** — territory, resources, and gradual introduction logic via https://icatcare.org/advice/multi-cat-households/
- **American Association of Feline Practitioners (AAFP) behavior resources** — feline-friendly handling and stress reduction principles via https://catvets.com/guidelines/practice-guidelines/behavior/

## Travel, carrier, and sitter logistics

- **AVMA: Traveling with Your Pet** — carrier acclimation, paperwork, and trip stress reduction via https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare/traveling-your-pet
- **International Cat Care: Cats and carriers** — train the carrier on calm days, not only before vet visits via https://icatcare.org/advice/cats-and-carriers/
- **ASPCA: Pet Care When You Travel** — sitter handoff basics and continuity of routine via https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/general-pet-care/pet-care-when-you-travel

## Nutrition and appetite decline

- **Cornell Feline Health Center: Feeding Your Cat** — appetite change as an early deterioration signal via https://www.vet.cornell.edu/departments-centers-and-institutes/cornell-feline-health-center/health-information/feline-health-topics/feeding-your-cat
- **AAHA Nutritional Assessment Guidelines** — structured nutrition review without inventing therapeutic diets via https://www.aaha.org/resources/2021-aaha-nutrition-and-weight-management-guidelines-for-dogs-and-cats/

## How to use these sources in-session

- Cite the matching source group when a claim is clinical, toxicological, or time-sensitive.
- Keep user guidance portable: escalate to a licensed veterinarian or poison control for diagnosis and dosing.
- If a local regulation, clinic policy, or product label matters, verify that primary document before treating a secondary summary as final.
