## Administration options

| Option | Where | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| TOEFL iBT (test center) | Authorized center | Computer-delivered; scratch paper provided |
| TOEFL iBT Home Edition | Home | Same test family; live human proctor; room/device requirements apply |

Allow about **two hours** total. Directions time is separate. Adaptive delivery can change exact item counts and section duration.

## Current section map (from 21 Jan 2026)

| Section | Approx. base time | Items (approx.) | Task types |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------|------------|
| Reading | 30 min | 50 | Complete the Words; Read in Daily Life; Read an Academic Passage |
| Listening | 29 min | 47 | Listen and Choose a Response; Listen to a Conversation; Listen to an Announcement; Listen to an Academic Talk |
| Writing | 23 min | 12 | Build a Sentence; Write an Email; Write for an Academic Discussion |
| Speaking | 8 min | 11 | Listen and Repeat; Take an Interview |
| **Total** | ~2 hours | — | Four section scores + overall |

### Reading

- Measures campus-life and academic reading with short, focused texts (not long multi-passage textbook blocks).
- **Complete the Words** — restore partially missing words; vocabulary + context.
- **Read in Daily Life** — notices, messages, short informational texts; main idea, detail, implication.
- **Read an Academic Passage** — short course-like passage; main idea, support, key vocabulary.
- All needed information is in the text; no topic background knowledge required.

### Listening

- Short clips instead of long multi-question lecture sets.
- **Listen and Choose a Response** — choose the best reply; intent and spoken patterns.
- **Listen to a Conversation** — campus-life dialogue; main idea, detail, intent, implication.
- **Listen to an Announcement** — purpose, key facts, next actions.
- **Listen to an Academic Talk** — short professor/expert talk; organization, detail, inference, idiomatic vocabulary.
- Note-taking is allowed. Accents may include North America, UK, New Zealand, or Australia.

### Writing

- Typed responses; clarity and task fit over perfect first drafts.
- **Build a Sentence** — arrange words/phrases into a grammatical sentence or question.
- **Write an Email** — request, inform, or propose a solution in academic/social context.
- **Write for an Academic Discussion** — classroom-forum post that states and supports a position and engages other views.

### Speaking

- Spoken into a microphone; concise structured tasks.
- **Listen and Repeat** — exact repetition for processing + intelligibility.
- **Take an Interview** — simulated academic/campus interview; experiences and opinions at a natural pace.

### Scoring eligibility note

If the test is abandoned before completing at least one Reading item, one Listening item, one Speaking task, and one Writing task, ETS will not score the test.

## Score scales

### Primary scale (tests on/after 21 Jan 2026)

- Four section scores and one overall score on a **1–6** scale in half-point steps.
- Overall = average of the four sections, rounded to the nearest half band (example: 5.125 → 5.0; 5.25 → 5.5).
- Designed for clearer CEFR alignment.

### Transition / legacy comparable scale

- For a **two-year transition** after January 2026, reports also include a **comparable overall 0–120** score (midpoint of the corresponding total range).
- Many institutions still publish cutoffs in 0–120 form while they migrate. Always check the program page and present both scales when the user only has one.

### Illustrative 1–6 ↔ 0–30/0–120 bands (ETS comparison table)

Open the live ETS “Understanding Your Scores” page before quoting fine-grained band edges not listed above.

### Score meaning boundaries

- ETS sets **no pass/fail** score. Each school or agency sets its own minimums and section floors.
- Centralized scoring only (never at the test center).
- Writing assistance software is **not** permitted.

## MyBest® scores

- Score reports include **MyBest** (superscores): best section results combined across eligible tests in the validity window.
- Helpful when a school accepts superscores; **many programs still require a single sitting**.
- Never assume MyBest is accepted — verify the target program.
