## The Real Problem

Write Bengali for the recipient and channel: warm, direct conversation rather than automatically elevated prose. Naturalness comes from audience fit, not a guarantee that writing will evade an AI detector.

## Formality Default

Lean casual for known friends and peers; use respectful, clear language for unfamiliar, professional, institutional, or explicitly formal audiences. Modern চলিত (chalita/cholito) forms work in both casual and formal writing; grammatical variety and social formality are separate choices.

## তুমি vs আপনি vs তুই

Choose address before drafting and keep its verb agreement:
- আপনি: respectful or unfamiliar address; আপনি কেমন আছেন?
- তুমি: familiar peer address; তুমি কেমন আছো?
- তুই: established intimate address; তুই কেমন আছিস? It can sound contemptuous outside that relationship.

For an unknown recipient, default to আপনি or an impersonal sentence. Use তুমি when the relationship or supplied sample supports it; being online alone does not establish intimacy. Reserve তুই for an explicit request or a clear existing pattern.

## Regional Variation

Bangladesh and West Bengal have vocabulary and pronunciation variation, with overlapping usage. Follow the requested region or a supplied sample. মোবাইল and ফোন are usable lexical choices, not exclusive country labels. If region is unknown, choose broadly understandable standard Bengali; ask once only when a specific dialect materially changes the task. Community or religion cannot be inferred from location.

## Particles & Softeners

Use particles for their conversational role, not as a quota:
- না: tag or emphasis in context; also ordinary negation
- তো: emphasis or conversational connection
- কি: question marker in context
- যে: emphasis or clause connection in context
- নাকি: alternative or confirmation-seeking question

## Fillers & Flow

Optional conversational choices:
- মানে, আচ্ছা, তাহলে
- কী যেন, ওই যে
- আসলে, সত্যি
- এইটা, সেইটা: colloquial demonstratives; match the supplied voice

Keep factual messages direct; insert a filler only where it helps the speaker's flow.

## Expressiveness

Match the source sentiment and intensity rather than replacing every ordinary word:
- ভালো → দারুণ, অসাধারণ; মাথা নষ্ট only for clearly supported colloquial hyperbole
- খারাপ → বাজে, জঘন্য when the stronger criticism is intended
- অনেক → প্রচুর, খুবই when the quantity or intensity still fits

## Common Expressions

Choose expressions that fit the speech act:
- ঠিক আছে, আচ্ছা, হ্যাঁ হ্যাঁ
- কোনো সমস্যা নেই
- সত্যি?, কী বলছো?
- বাহ!, দারুণ!, মজা!

## Reactions

Preserve the speaker's actual reaction:
- সত্যি?, কী বলছো?, আরে!
- বাহ!, অসাম!, দারুণ! — loanword slang fits only a compatible casual voice
- আরে বাবা!, কী সর্বনাশ! — surprise or alarm, not a default congratulation
- হাহাহা in friendly text where laughter fits

## The "Native Test"

Read the draft aloud for audience fit: consistent address and verbs, appropriate region and intensity, and unchanged names, numbers, dates, commitments, negation, and uncertainty. If it sounds stiff in a casual exchange, shorten the phrasing; if it is overfamiliar, raise the address level. This check is editorial judgment, not native-speaker certification.

Read `references/sources.md` when verifying register, pronoun agreement, or regional claims. It supplies supplemental evidence, not a second copy of these operating rules.
