# Bengali register and usage sources

Consult for a register, agreement, or regional claim that needs verification. These are domain references rather than guarantees about every speaker's preferences. Use a user's sample as the authority for their own voice.

## Register and pronoun agreement

- **Banglapedia: Bangla Language**, Mohammad Daniul Huq and Pabitra Sarkar, article last edited 2021-06-18: https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Bangla_Language
  - “Forms of language” distinguishes sadhu from chalita using shortened pronouns and verbs, and describes standard colloquial Bengali in speaking, writing, literature, and media. Chalita is not synonymous with socially informal address.
  - “Morphology” distinguishes ordinary তুমি, familiar তুই, and honorific আপনি, with different personal verb endings. Familiar forms can also be contemptuous. Selecting an unfamiliar-recipient honorific fallback is this skill's conservative editorial policy.
  - The article documents historical language contact and loanwords. It does not establish that every current writer mixes English or requires casual slang.

## Regional variation and evidence limits

- **Banglapedia: Bangla Language**: https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Bangla_Language
  - “Forms of language” describes lexical variation and expressly notes overlap and exceptions. Country, community, and religion do not determine a speaker's vocabulary on their own.
- **Omniglot: Bengali alphabet, pronunciation and language**: https://www.omniglot.com/writing/bengali.htm
  - “Bengali dialects” independently corroborates pronunciation and vocabulary variation between Bangladesh and India. It does not support an exclusive মোবাইল/ফোন country map.

Both sources are descriptive secondary references. Regional word lists, native-speaker frequency claims, and narrow dialect prescriptions need additional relevant evidence or a supplied speaker sample. When that evidence is unavailable, use broadly understandable standard Bengali and name the uncertainty when it materially affects the draft. Historical population figures and administrative claims from these pages are outside this skill's writing task.
