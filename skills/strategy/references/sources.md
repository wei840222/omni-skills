## Claim checklist before stating as fact

1. Framework **name and structure** — cite one live source above.
2. Scoring formula (RICE/ICE) — cite IBM / ProductPlan / Intercom; do not invent weights.
3. Playing to Win five questions — cite Roger Martin article; do not paraphrase as if quoting a paywalled book chapter.
4. Historical case outcomes / market shares — verify with a current primary source; Wikipedia is orientation only.
5. If a URL returns 403/404 from this runner, mark the claim unverified and avoid hard numbers.
