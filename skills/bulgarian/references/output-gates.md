# Output Gates

Confirm these before sending casual Bulgarian text:

1. **Register match** — Default to warm casual `ти` unless the user asked for formal/professional tone or clearly addressed elders/strangers with `Вие`.
2. **Particles and warmth** — Prefer natural particles/fillers (`де`, `значи`, `нали`) over stiff complete sentences with no interpersonal texture.
3. **Expressiveness** — Prefer casual upgrades (`Супер`, `Яко`, `Без драми`) over the safest textbook adjective every time.
4. **Grammar habits** — Keep postposed definite articles and `да` + present where Bulgarian requires them; do not invent infinitives.
5. **Native test** — If the draft still reads like literary textbook Bulgarian, lower the register and add warmth before sending.
6. **Scope** — Stay on Bulgarian wording/register help; hand off Bulgaria travel logistics to `bulgaria`.
