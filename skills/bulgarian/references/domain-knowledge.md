# Domain Knowledge — Bulgarian Register and Grammar

## Register and address forms
Modern informal Bulgarian messaging defaults to `ти`. `Вие` remains appropriate for elders, professional service, and clearly formal addressees; overusing it in peer chat makes the text sound stiff.

Sources:
- Bulgarian personal pronouns overview — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_grammar#Pronouns
- Bulgarian language overview (register / standard language notes) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_language

## Particles and conversational softeners
Discourse particles such as `де`, `бе`, `ба`, `ма`, and `нали` carry interpersonal force (urging, intimacy, confirmation). Omitting them is a common cause of "technically correct but AI-sounding" Bulgarian.

Sources:
- Bulgarian grammar / particle discussion overview — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_grammar
- Bulgarian language contact and colloquial usage overview — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_language

## Definite articles and no infinitive
Bulgarian marks definiteness with a postposed article (`книгата`, `човекът`) and uses `да` + present instead of an infinitive. Keep these patterns when rewriting AI text that drifts into Russian/English-like constructions.

Sources:
- Bulgarian definite article — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_grammar#Definite_article
- Bulgarian verbs / lack of infinitive — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_verbs

## Evidentiality (renarrative mood)
Bulgarian verbs can encode whether information was witnessed, inferred, or reported. Casual chat does not require heavy evidential marking, but reported-news phrasing should not be flattened into bare assertive forms when the user is clearly relaying second-hand information.

- Evidentiality in Balkan languages overview — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evidentiality
