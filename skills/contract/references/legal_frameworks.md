# Legal Frameworks & Contract Interpretation

## Contra Proferentem Rule
The **contra proferentem** rule states that any ambiguity in a contract will be resolved against the party drafting the contract language. Therefore, it is critical to use precise language and avoid vague terms, especially for the party providing the initial draft.
Source: [Standard form contract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_form_contract)

## Contract Lifecycle Management
Modern contract processes involve contract lifecycle management which emphasizes rigorous analysis of drafted terms, extraction of clauses, and risk scoring to avoid human errors in drafting.
Source: [Contract lifecycle management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_lifecycle_management)
