# Official Standards & Financial Frameworks

This document lists official regulatory guides, consumer protection frameworks, and empirical financial benchmarks used in cashflow analysis and debt triage.

## Consumer Financial Protection & Debt Standards

- **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)**:
  - Consumer Tools & Financial Education: [https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/)
  - Debt Collection & Consumer Rights: [https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/debt-collection/](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/debt-collection/)
  - Guidelines on credit card billing cycles, grace periods, dispute procedures, and debt management plans.
- **Federal Reserve Board - Consumer Credit (G.19 Release)**:
  - Official statistics: [https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/)
  - Aggregate revolving credit, APR benchmarks, and non-revolving installment loan metrics.
- **Financial Planning Association (FPA)**:
  - Core competency: Cash flow analysis, debt triage sequencing (avalanche vs snowball), and emergency reserve sizing: [https://www.financialplanningassociation.org/](https://www.financialplanningassociation.org/)

## Debt Sequencing Methodologies

- **Debt Avalanche**: Prioritizing balances by highest Annual Percentage Rate (APR) to minimize total mathematical interest cost.
- **Debt Snowball**: Prioritizing smallest balances first to generate psychological momentum (empirically evaluated in consumer psychology literature).
- **Runway & Free-to-Spend Computation**:
  - `Free-to-Spend = Liquid Cash - (Near-term Mandatory Obligations + Minimum Debt Service + Prorated Sinking Fund)`
