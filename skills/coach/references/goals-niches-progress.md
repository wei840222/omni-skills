# Goals, niches, teams, progress

## Goal design

- Split **outcome** (not fully controllable) from **process** (controllable weekly behaviors).
- Borrowed or vague goals: ask for the goal behind the goal; make it observable.
- Planning chunk: `horizon_days` (default 90). Write the review date into `## Due`.
- A second reset of the same goal is evidence the person-level read may be more true than the stated goal — still work the stated goal first.

## Niches (load only the relevant slice)

| Niche | Coaching emphasis |
|-------|-------------------|
| Executive | Stakeholder map, decision quality, political reality without gossip |
| Career | Role experiments, narrative, offer decisions (pair with `career` when analysis-heavy) |
| Business / startup | Constraints, runway, founder bottlenecks; hybrid advice often required |
| Health | Behavior design inside medical advice from clinicians — never prescribe treatment |
| Life | Values, roles, energy; watch dependency |
| Creative | Ship cadence, inner critic, scope of "done" |
| ADHD | Externalize executive function: body doubling, same-day starts, environment design, short horizons — willpower structures add shame |
| Sports / performance | Process goals, recovery, competition anxiety boundaries |
| Financial | Behavior around money with licensed-advisor referral for products |
| Academic | Writing/research blocks, advisor dynamics |
| Manager-as-coach | Separate power conversations: coach the how, decide the what, never pretend both are pure coaching (pair with `management`) |

## Teams and groups

- Design air time explicitly; quiet members get structured turns.
- Confidentiality is **inside the group** and has stated limits.
- Sponsor reporting: agree what leaves the room at intake.
- 360 debriefs: themes over quotes; no surprise individual exposure.

## Progress and endings

- Measure baseline vs now on the **same scale**; prefer leading indicators the client controls.
- Engagement states exit conditions at intake: session count, independence criteria, review date.
- Dependency that feels like success (they keep booking, stop growing) is a failure mode — re-contract or end.
- Accountability product test: check-ins should get **less** frequent over a healthy engagement, not more.
