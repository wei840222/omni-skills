# Intake, ethics, and safety

## Starting a client (advise mode)

Order matters:

1. **Chemistry call** (~20 minutes) — fit only, not a free coaching session
2. **Intake** — goals, constraints, prior attempts, logistics
3. **Agreement** — scope, cadence, fees, cancellation, confidentiality limits, exit
4. **Baseline** — one shared scale you will re-measure

### Three-way contracting

When someone pays and someone else is coached: write what the sponsor gets, what stays private, and how progress is reported. Do this before deep work.

### Confidentiality limits

State limits in one sentence at intake **before** disclosures: harm to self/others, mandated reporting where applicable, and any sponsor-visible metrics. Promising unlimited secrecy destroys the relationship the one time it must break.

## Coaching vs adjacent crafts

| Craft | Client question | Deliverable | Switch when |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|-------------|
| Coaching | "What should I do?" about their life | Their answer, explicit and committed | Default |
| Consulting | "What is the right answer?" | Your recommendation | Missing non-intuitable facts — name the switch |
| Mentoring | "How did you do it?" | Your path as one data point | They want precedent, not a decision |
| Training | "Teach me the skill" | Curriculum and practice | Gap is capability, not action |
| Therapy | "Why am I like this?" | Clinical treatment | Any Red Flag, or work is about pain not a goal |
| Managing | "What do you need me to do?" | Direction with authority | You hold power over them |

## Red flags (suspend coaching protocols)

This is not a coachable goal. Warm handoff to a clinician or emergency service — not a cleverer question. Coaching *alongside* treatment can be fine; coaching *instead of* it is the harm.

| Observable signal | Suspicion | Action |
|-------------------|-----------|--------|
| Statements about ending life, self-harm, or a plan | Suicide risk | Stop coaching. Local emergency services or crisis line now; stay present while help is contacted |
| Talk of harming another person | Risk to third party | Stop; emergency services; confidentiality limits were stated at intake |
| Cannot function: not eating/sleeping for days, not leaving home | Possible acute episode | Refer to physician/clinician; hold the coaching goal |
| Panic, flashbacks, dissociation in session | Trauma response | Stop that line; ground; close early; refer |
| Escalating substance use as the path through the goal | Dependence | Specialist referral; accountability structures can worsen this |
| Disclosure of abuse, current or ongoing | Safety | Specialist support; mandated report where required — say so, do not promise secrecy |
| Same crisis every session; no goal survives | Clinical need | End or pause coaching explicitly and refer |
| Minor or vulnerable adult without guardian consent | Safeguarding | Do not start |

**Record only** the fact and date of a referral in `clients/<name>.md` or `## Boundaries` in `memory.md` — never the disclosure content.

Crisis pointers (verify locally): IASP suicidal thoughts resources; regional emergency numbers (112 / 911 / 999); US 988 Lifeline where applicable. Full URLs in `sources.md`.

## Dual relationships and power

- Do not coach your own direct report as if you had no authority — separate the conversations and name which one this is.
- Dual relationships (friend, business partner, romantic interest) → decline or refer.
