# Practice business and coach craft

## Pricing and packages

- Sell the **outcome and the container** (sessions, cadence, between-session access), not the hour.
- Hourly pricing caps income at time and trains clients to buy inputs.
- Free "sample sessions" that are real coaching give away the product; use a short chemistry call about fit instead.
- Sliding scale, cancellation window, caseload ceiling, corporate vs private rates belong in `config.yaml` / practice notes once chosen.

## Capacity

- Caseload ceiling is a safety control against working harder than every client.
- If you work harder than the client twice, name it; then re-contract or end (`craft` failure mode).

## No-shows and churn

- Cancellation policy stated at agreement time.
- After repeated no-shows, redesign container before moralizing commitment.
- Exit interviews beat silent churn — ask what would have made the engagement complete.

## Recording and supervision

- Recording improves craft and measures talk ratio; it also changes what clients say, especially with sponsors.
- Record only with explicit consent and a deletion date; never for the sponsor.
- Supervision and credential hours support the coach; they are not a substitute for client outcomes.

## Credentials

- Credentials signal model adherence and help corporate buyers filter.
- They do not by themselves predict client outcomes; some high-earning niche coaches have none.
- Treat credentials as **market access**, not proof of competence.

## Expert tensions (stay practical)

| Tension | Practical resolution |
|---------|----------------------|
| Pure coaching vs hybrid advising | Frontier is information: if they cannot know it, name switch + give it; if they can reason to it, ask |
| Accountability as product vs dependency | Healthy engagements reduce external check-in frequency over time |
| Goal-first vs person-first | Work stated goal; second reset of same goal invites person-level read |
| Recording | Development-only, consented, timed deletion |
| Credentialing | Market access filter, not outcome guarantee |
