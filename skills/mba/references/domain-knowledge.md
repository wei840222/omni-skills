# Master of Business Administration (MBA) Core Concepts

## 1. Accounting and Financial Management
- **Financial Statements**: Balance Sheets, Income Statements, and Cash Flow Statements.
- **Managerial Accounting**: Cost behavior, break-even analysis, budgeting, and performance measurement.
- **Corporate Finance**: Capital budgeting (NPV, IRR), cost of capital (WACC), capital structure, and dividend policy.

## 2. Marketing Management
- **The 4 Ps**: Product, Price, Place (Distribution), and Promotion.
- **Market Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning (STP)**.
- **Consumer Behavior**: Understanding the psychological and social factors that influence buying decisions.
- **Brand Management**: Building and maintaining brand equity.

## 3. Operations and Supply Chain Management
- **Process Analysis**: Throughput, bottleneck analysis, and capacity planning.
- **Inventory Management**: Economic Order Quantity (EOQ), Just-in-Time (JIT), and safety stock.
- **Quality Management**: Six Sigma, Total Quality Management (TQM), and statistical process control.
- **Supply Chain Strategy**: Sourcing, distribution networks, and logistics.

## 4. Organizational Behavior and Human Resources
- **Leadership Styles**: Transformational, transactional, and situational leadership.
- **Motivation Theories**: Maslow's Hierarchy, Expectancy Theory, Equity Theory.
- **Group Dynamics**: Team formation, conflict resolution, and communication.
- **Human Resource Management**: Recruitment, performance appraisal, compensation, and training.

## 5. Strategic Management
- **Competitive Advantage**: Porter's Generic Strategies (Cost Leadership, Differentiation, Focus).
- **Industry Analysis**: Porter's Five Forces framework.
- **Internal Analysis**: VRIO Framework (Value, Rarity, Imitability, Organization) and Core Competencies.
- **Corporate Strategy**: Diversification, Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A), and Strategic Alliances.

## 6. Economics (Micro & Macro)
- **Microeconomics**: Supply and demand, elasticity, market structures (perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly), and pricing strategies.
- **Macroeconomics**: GDP, inflation, unemployment, fiscal and monetary policy, and international trade.

## 7. Data Analytics and Quantitative Methods
- **Descriptive and Inferential Statistics**: Hypothesis testing, regression analysis.
- **Decision Trees and Expected Value**: Making decisions under uncertainty.
- **Optimization**: Linear programming and resource allocation.
