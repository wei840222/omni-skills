# Domain Knowledge Sources

Authoritative sources used to verify CIO guidance on IT strategy, vendor management, architecture governance, and digital transformation.

## IT strategy and governance

- [Information technology management - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_technology_management) — IT management scope covering strategy, operations, and governance boundaries.
- [ITIL Foundation overview (Axelos)](https://www.axelos.com/certifications/itil-service-management/itil-4-foundation) — service-management vocabulary for operations, change, and continual improvement.
- [TOGAF Standard overview (The Open Group)](https://www.opengroup.org/togaf) — enterprise-architecture decision framing for standards, ADRs, and portfolio trade-offs.

## Cloud migration and modernization

- [AWS Migration Strategies (the 6 Rs)](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/large-migration-guide/migration-strategies.html) — Rehost/Replatform/Repurchase/Refactor/Retire/Retain taxonomy used in transformation playbooks.
- [Martin Fowler: Strangler Fig Application](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/StranglerFigApplication.html) — incremental legacy replacement pattern for modernization without big-bang cutovers.

## Vendor and architecture decisions

- [Architecture Decision Records guidance](https://adr.github.io/) — lightweight ADR template and decision-history practice reflected in architecture.md.
