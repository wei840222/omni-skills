## Operational heuristics (scoped claims)

- **~85% success band (Rule 4):** treat as a measurable training target (last ~20 attempts), not a universal law. Prefer the learner's measured hit rate over citing a single derivation.
- **8–12 reviews per new item / day at ~0.90 retention:** operational steady-state budget aligned with mature SRS queues and FSRS desired-retention workload guidance; verify against the learner's live tool docs.
- **Deliberate practice debate:** keep the operational core (defined sub-skill, above-comfort difficulty, fast feedback, corrected repetition) without claiming it explains all expertise across domains.
