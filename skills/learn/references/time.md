# Time — Fitting Learning into a Real Week

Load for short sessions, overloaded plans, or streak debates.

## Size to `weekly_hours`

Plans built for imaginary hours fail in month two and get misread as lack of talent. Cut scope until the range from Rule 8 fits the declared budget.

## Short sessions (≤20 minutes)

Drop the hard block. Keep warm retrieval + production. A short retrieving session beats a long reading session.

## Streaks

Streaks build habit and invite fake sessions. Track a minimum *quality* dose (e.g. 10 minutes retrieval + one production artifact) instead of a binary day, and record it under sessions in memory.
