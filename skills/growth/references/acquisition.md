# Acquisition, paid, experiments

## Channel portfolio

- Keep **two or three** channel tests live; more splits attention, creative quality, and attribution.
- Score candidates by CAC, volume ceiling, and time-to-signal.
- Before spend starts, write a **kill number** and **kill date** covering at least one full conversion cycle.

## Unit economics

- `payback_months = CAC ÷ (monthly ARPA × gross_margin)`
- Example: CAC 300, ARPA 60, margin 0.8 → 300 ÷ 48 ≈ 6.3 months.
- Scale only when payback ≤ `target_cac_payback_months` **and** the same payback roughly holds after a **2×** spend increase (CAC usually rises with volume).
- 3:1 LTV:CAC is underwriting shorthand, not a cash-timing law.

## Paid specifics

- Prefer paid to **test messaging/demand**, not to **fake retention**.
- Track paid CAC separately from blended CAC.
- Watch creative fatigue, bid/budget mechanics, and incrementality (hold-out/geo when affordable).

## Experiments

- Pre-register primary metric, horizon, and sample size before shipping.
- Fixed-horizon significance is invalid if you stop when the chart looks good; use a sequential method if peeking is required.
- ICE/RICE ranks the backlog; one owner per surface; avoid ten interacting tests on the same surface.

## Referrals and lifecycle (summary)

- Referral programs after retention flattens and advocacy exists; double-sided incentive, clear trigger, attribution, fraud controls.
- Lifecycle maps by **user state**, not calendar spam; respect frequency ceilings, deliverability, and consent (`privacy_regime`).

## Monetization motion

- Self-serve: activation → paywall/trial design → expansion.
- Sales-assisted: motion fit by ACV, PQL definition, hand-off rules.
- Discounting to hit a quarter trains wait-behaviour and pollutes LTV in the measured cohort.
