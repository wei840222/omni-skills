# Growth loops

## Definition

A loop's output must feed its own input. A funnel with better branding is not a loop.

`output ≈ f(conversion_at_each_step, cycle_time)`

Halving time from value → invite often beats a modest lift in invite acceptance because the exponent is `t ÷ cycle_time`.

## Selection

1. Name the loop in one sentence (who does what that creates the next user/usage).
2. Measure conversion at each step and the **cycle time**.
3. Find the stuck step; fix that step before adding campaigns on top.
4. Prefer one primary loop until it closes reliably.

## Model notes

- Content/media: production must outrun traffic-per-unit decay.
- Marketplace: liquidity and the constrained side dominate cold start.
- Consumer apps: install-to-first-value gap usually dominates viral coefficients.
