# Sources — dhgate skill

Gate 6 research anchors. Prefer primary DHgate Help / buyer-protection pages and re-verify region-specific shipping, return, and dispute claims before quoting hard numbers.

## Marketplace and product pages

- [DHgate homepage](https://www.dhgate.com/) — live catalog, wholesale listings, and storefront entry
- Product detail page (SKU-specific) — primary evidence for price, MOQ, material claims, pack quantity, and shipping options
- Seller/store page on DHgate — store age proxies, transaction history, and assortment breadth

## Shipping, tracking, and delivery

- [DHgate Help Center](https://help.dhgate.com/) — locate regional shipping and tracking articles from the live Help Center
- Checkout shipping estimate for the signed-in destination — authoritative method, cost, and ETA for that order
- Carrier tracking linked from the order page — post-purchase fulfillment evidence after dispatch

## Returns, refunds, and buyer protection

- [DHgate buyer protection / dispute Help](https://help.dhgate.com/) — return windows, dispute stages, and evidence expectations by region
- In-app/order dispute flow — documentation expectations for damaged, wrong, incomplete, or not-as-described items
- Order protection countdown on the order detail page — act before protection expires

## Pricing and total-cost context (secondary)

- Product page list / discount price — marketing reference only; recompute landed cost before a buy verdict
- Coupon and bulk-price surfaces in cart/checkout — validity windows and MOQ rules change frequently
- Cross-check the same SKU/image on AliExpress, Temu, Amazon, and local retail for total-cost reality

## Authenticity and compliance signals (secondary)

- Seller replies confirming branded vs generic / unbranded positioning
- Packaging and logo claims on the listing vs photo evidence
- Destination-country customs and restricted-goods guidance for the user's region (user-provided or official government pages)

## Notes for maintainers

- Tracking lag, free-shipping thresholds, return windows, and dispute SLAs change by region and promotion; never hard-code a single global SLA without checking live pages and checkout.
- Brand-name deep discounts remain counterfeit-risk by default even when a listing looks "official."
- High-risk categories (brand goods, electronics, cosmetics, medical devices, toys, supplements) need stronger evidence thresholds than unbranded home goods.
