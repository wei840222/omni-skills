# Current-Market Verification — Stock Market

Read this before making a claim about a live price, intraday move, current news, an earnings date, a macro release, or a briefing for "today."

## Evidence record

For each current claim, record the instrument or event, source, retrieval time with timezone, and whether the value is delayed, indicative, or final. Keep source facts separate from the analysis they support.

Use a source appropriate to the claim:

- issuer investor-relations materials or regulatory filings for company results and announced events;
- the issuing government or central-bank calendar for macro releases and policy events;
- a market-data source that identifies its exchange, delay, and timestamp for prices and volume.

If a source cannot be verified, treat the value as unavailable and supply a briefing or research template with the fields left open. Treat an undated value as a hypothesis rather than a current-market claim.

## Order mechanics

This skill does not place orders. When discussing order types, explain the applicable broker and market rules. The SEC's Investor.gov notes that a market order does not guarantee execution price, and a stop order becomes a market order when its stop price is reached. Model gap risk separately from the planned invalidation.
