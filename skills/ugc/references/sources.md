## How to use in-session

- When coaching **#ad / gifted / paid** disclosures, open the FTC endorsement guides first.
- When stating **platform creative rules or ad products**, open the live TikTok/Meta/Google/YouTube help page for that product.
- When quoting **hook-rate or CPA benchmarks**, label them as directional diagnostics unless the user supplies account data.
- Never paste creator contracts, whitelisting tokens, or ad-account secrets into examples.
