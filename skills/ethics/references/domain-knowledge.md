# Ethics Domain Knowledge

Verified primary / reference sources that back the moral-reasoning guidance in this skill.

## Core Frameworks and Orientation
- **Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Ethics** — survey of normative ethics branches and applied ethics entry points via https://iep.utm.edu/ethics/
- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Metaethics** — moral realism vs expressivism and what normative claims can mean via https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/metaethics/

## Population Ethics and Identity
- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Population Ethics** — Mere Addition Paradox / Repugnant Conclusion framing via https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/population-ethics/
- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Personal Identity** — continuity, fission, and teletransportation edge cases via https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/identity-personal/
- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — The Nonidentity Problem** — person-affecting views under future-person contingency via https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nonidentity-problem/

## Teaching and Classroom Practice
- **Association for Practical and Professional Ethics — Intercollegiate Ethics Bowl** — case-first competitive ethics pedagogy via https://appe-ethics.org/ethics-bowl/
- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Moral Relativism** — classroom "who's to say?" objections and responses via https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/moral-relativism/

These sources support Gate 6 domain accuracy for beginners, students, researchers, and teachers without inventing statute/version claims.
