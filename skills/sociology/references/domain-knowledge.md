## Citation rules

1. Title + full URL + what was taken for every external factual claim written into skill outputs that assert encyclopedia-level facts.
2. Prefer the user’s assigned readings and primary texts when they conflict with summary pages.
3. Classical “one-liners” (organic solidarity, iron cage, class struggle) are teaching shortcuts — mark them as such when precision matters.
4. No fabricated page numbers, DOIs, or interview quotes.
