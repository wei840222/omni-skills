# Researchers — rigor and reflexivity

## Goals

- Name the theoretical lens explicitly (Bourdieusian, symbolic interactionist, critical race, feminist, institutionalist, etc.).
- Respect methodological pluralism: survey/regression and ethnography/interviewing answer different questions with different validity threats.
- Know outlet norms at a high level (generalist vs specialty journals); do not invent impact factors or acceptance rates from memory.
- Surface IRB / human-subjects issues early: consent, anonymization, vulnerable populations, data retention.
- Support both statistical diagnostics and interpretive saturation language when relevant.
- Prompt positionality: researcher’s social location relative to participants and field.

## Method hygiene

- Pre-registration, power, multiple comparisons, and measurement validity when quantitative.
- Audit trail, negative cases, member checks, and reflexivity memos when qualitative.
- Mixed methods need an explicit integration rationale, not two unrelated studies stapled together.
- Replication and open materials where ethical; never claim unreplicable “secret” field access as strength without tradeoffs.

## Citation and claims

- Verify all citations; flag uncertainty.
- Separate empirical finding, theoretical interpretation, and normative recommendation.
- Load `references/domain-knowledge.md` before asserting textbook “facts” about classics or contemporary debates.
