# Risk Mitigation — Vibe Research

## Known Risks

### 1. Hallucinations

AI generates plausible but false information.

**Mitigations:**

- Require citations for every factual claim
- Cross-verify critical facts across 3+ independent sources when stakes are high
- Prefer citations and triangulation over unaided recall
- Flag confidence levels explicitly
- Human validates key findings before publication

### 2. Bias Propagation

AI reflects biases in training data or source material.

**Mitigations:**

- Diversify source types (academic, industry, geographic, practitioner)
- Explicitly check for representation gaps
- Document source selection criteria
- Human reviews for blind spots

### 3. Loss of Methodological Rigor

Speed prioritized over quality.

**Mitigations:**

- Document every analytical step
- Require a reproducibility package with outputs and step logs
- Human validates methodology before heavy execution
- Peer or domain review of AI-generated protocols when available

### 4. Researcher Skill Erosion

Over-reliance on AI degrades human expertise.

**Mitigations:**

- Keep the human engaged in critical thinking
- Require the human to articulate reasoning, not only approve
- Treat AI as collaborator, not replacement
- Periodic manual research exercises for skill maintenance

### 5. Academic / Attribution Integrity

Unclear attribution and plagiarism risks.

**Mitigations:**

- Transparent disclosure of AI involvement
- Clear citation of all sources
- Human takes responsibility for the final output
- Follow institutional or venue guidelines on AI use

### 6. Over-claiming AI science capability

Public discourse often overstates what autonomous research agents can safely conclude.

**Mitigations:**

- Treat agent-led synthesis as provisional until human review
- Separate observed results from speculative extrapolation
- Prefer primary literature and standards (see `sources.md`) over marketing claims about AI scientists

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing research output:

```
□ All factual claims have citations
□ Key findings verified across multiple independent sources
□ Methodology documented for reproducibility
□ Confidence levels flagged appropriately
□ Human has validated critical findings
□ Limitations explicitly stated
□ AI involvement disclosed
□ Source diversity checked
□ Adjacent skills considered (synthesize / in-depth-research / market-research)
```

## When to Escalate to Human

Pause and request human input when:

- Contradictory evidence has no clear resolution
- High-stakes claims could cause harm if wrong (medical, legal, safety, major spend)
- Methodology needs domain expertise the agent lacks
- Ethical considerations arise (consent, dual-use, sensitive populations)
- Results are unexpected and consequential
- Depth budget or success criteria need renegotiation
