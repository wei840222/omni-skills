# Sync, conflicts, and backups

Use for conflicted copies, multi-device edits, missing edits, and backup drills.

## Conflict handling

1. Prefer the platform's conflict marker or a newly created conflicted copy over silent last-write-wins.
2. Merge by section; keep both unique commitments/decisions.
3. After merge, delete or archive the loser deliberately and say how many files changed.

## Backup posture

Aim for 3-2-1: three copies, two media, one offsite/offline. A backup that has never been restored is a hope, not a plan — restore one note on the backup cadence recorded in `## Due`.
