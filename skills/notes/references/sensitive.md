# Sensitive notes

Use when deciding whether something should be written down, and what to strip first.

## Record test

Write it only if a future reader (including someone who was not in the room, HR, or legal) would still need the decision, evidence, or commitment.

## Keep out

- Speculation about people, health, and pay in meeting/interview notes
- Credentials, PINs, recovery codes, wifi passwords — replace with pointers
- Third-party personal details that are not required for the decision record

## Split vaults

Merge work and personal only when policy allows. Employer policy, client NDA, or discovery risk are reasons to split vaults/platforms.
