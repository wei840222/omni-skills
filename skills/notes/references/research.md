# Research notes

Use for books, papers, courses, and highlight imports.

## Required fields

Source with locator, verbatim quotes in blockquotes, and your claim in your own words.

## Procedure

1. Separate quote from interpretation.
2. Prefer atomic claims when they will be reused; keep long-form when the reading path matters.
3. Record the source locator strongly enough to re-find the passage.

## Template

```markdown
# <Claim title>
source: <title / URL / DOI>
locator: <page|timestamp|section>
date: YYYY-MM-DD

> Verbatim quote

## My claim
...
```
