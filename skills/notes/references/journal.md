# Journal and reviews

Use for daily notes, weekly reviews, and monthly rollups of personal or work reflection that still need retrieval later.

## Required fields

Date, what happened, what it means, tomorrow's top three (or the review's equivalent).

## Cadence

- Driven by `review_cadence` / `review_day` in `config.yaml`.
- Every accepted cadence becomes a `## Due` row in `<state_root>/notes/memory.md`.
- Journal practice prompts themselves belong to the `journal` skill; this file owns storage shape and review artifacts.

## Template

```markdown
# YYYY-MM-DD
## What happened
...
## What it means
...
## Tomorrow
1.
2.
3.
```
