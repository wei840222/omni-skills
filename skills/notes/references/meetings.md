# Meeting notes

Use for meetings, 1-on-1s, interviews, retros, and client calls when the artifact is the note (not facilitation).

## Required fields by subtype

| Subtype | Required |
|---|---|
| Meeting | Date, attendees, absentees, decisions, actions |
| 1-on-1 | Carry-forward from last time, their topics, actions on both sides |
| Interview | Evidence and quotes separated from assessment |
| Retro | Timeline, contributing factors, actions — never a person as the cause |

## Procedure

1. Capture live when possible; clean structure after.
2. Keep decisions and commitments verbatim; summarize the rest.
3. Put durable decisions into a decision note and link from the meeting note (one fact, one home).
4. Write attendees/action owners into `<state_root>/contacts/contacts.md` as pointers only.
5. Keep speculation about people, health, and pay out of the record (`references/sensitive.md`).

date: YYYY-MM-DD

...
