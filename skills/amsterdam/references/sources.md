# Research sources (Gate 6)

Orientation sources for `amsterdam`. Re-open the live page before stating a current IND salary threshold, OV fare, museum ticket, rent median, or emergency procedure as fact. This package teaches **city routing heuristics**, not personalized immigration or tax advice.

## City and visitor orientation

- **I amsterdam — Visit** — official visitor hub for attractions, practical tips, and events — https://www.iamsterdam.com/en
- **I amsterdam — Getting around** — transport orientation for visitors — https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/travel-staying/getting-around
- **City of Amsterdam (Gemeente)** — municipal services hub — https://www.amsterdam.nl/en/
- **Amsterdam.nl — Registration / moving** — BSN and moving orientation entry — https://www.amsterdam.nl/en/civil-affairs/

## Immigration, work, and 30% ruling

- **IND — Residence permits** — Immigration and Naturalisation Service main entry — https://ind.nl/en
- **IND — Highly skilled migrant** — HSM orientation (confirm current salary thresholds on the live page) — https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/highly-skilled-migrant
- **IND — EU Blue Card** — alternative skilled work route orientation — https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/eu-blue-card
- **Business.gov.nl — 30% facility** — tax ruling orientation for incoming employees — https://business.gov.nl/regulation/30-percent-facility/
- **Belastingdienst — 30% facility** — tax authority page; open current rules before quoting percentages or eligibility windows — https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/individuals/living_and_working/working_in_another_country_temporarily/you_are_coming_to_work_or_study_in_the_netherlands/30_percent_facility/

## Notes for agents
