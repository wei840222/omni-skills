# Research sources (Gate 6)

Primary orientation sources for `negotiate`. Re-check the live page before repeating a numeric benchmark, consumer-protection rule, or employment claim. This package does **not** assert a single global "fair" price or salary figure.

## Negotiation craft (BATNA, process)

- **Harvard PON — Top Negotiation Skills** — process skills orientation (preparation, listening, options) — https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/top-10-negotiation-skills/
- **Harvard PON — BATNA tag index** — Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement framing; open a specific BATNA article from this index before quoting tactics as doctrine — https://www.pon.harvard.edu/tag/batna/

## Professional / compensation orientation

- **U.S. DOL — Wages topic hub** — federal wage-and-hour orientation; not a salary-band oracle — https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/wages
- **U.S. DOL WHD — Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)** — wage-hour baseline when employment classification or overtime framing appears in a professional deal — https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa
- **U.S. EEOC — Prohibited Employment Policies/Practices** — illegal interview/offer topics and discriminatory practice orientation (U.S.-centric; localize elsewhere) — https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices

## Consumer / marketplace risk

- **U.S. DOJ — Mass Marketing Fraud** — fraud pattern orientation for pressure, advance-fee, and impersonation schemes that show up in remote deal threads — https://www.justice.gov/criminal/criminal-fraud/mass-marketing-fraud
- **CFPB — Consumer tools hub** — consumer financial product orientation when payment plans, deposits, or credit terms enter a deal — https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/

## Domain / naming industry (buying track)

- **ICANN — Name Collision resources** — domain technical-risk orientation; not a secondary-market price guide — https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/name-collision-2013-12-06-en

## Evidence limits

- No universal percentage anchor (for example "always open at 50%") is asserted as law; category files give heuristics only.
- Salary, domain, and P2P comps are jurisdiction- and listing-specific; require principal-provided comps or a fresh primary source before stating a number as fact.
- Marketplace protection rules differ by platform; prefer the live platform help center for hold, shipping, and payment-protection claims.
