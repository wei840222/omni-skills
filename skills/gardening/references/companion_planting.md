# Companion Planting

Companion planting is the polycultural practice in horticulture and small-scale agriculture of co-planting different crops to provide a measure of weed control, pest control, habitat for beneficial insects, or other mutual benefits.

## Common Combinations
- **Tomatoes and Basil**: Basil is said to repel pests like tomato hornworms and aphids, and may improve the flavor of tomatoes.
- **Three Sisters (Corn, Beans, Squash)**: Corn provides a stalk for beans to climb; beans fix nitrogen in the soil; squash leaves shade the ground, retaining moisture and preventing weeds.
- **Marigolds**: Often planted with various vegetables to deter nematodes and some above-ground pests.

## Spatial Considerations
- Plant alliums (onions, garlic) in separate beds from legumes (peas, beans) to ensure optimal growth for both.
