# Sources

Checked 2026-09-25. These pages support the numeric claims kept in the playbooks. They are orientation, not a substitute for counsel or a current filing quote.

## Privacy penalties

- GDPR Article 83 — administrative fines up to 20 000 000 EUR, or up to 4% of total worldwide annual turnover of the preceding financial year, for the infringements listed in paragraphs 5 and 6. https://gdpr-info.eu/art-83-gdpr/
- California Civil Code section 1798.155 — CCPA civil penalties of $2,500 per violation, or $7,500 per intentional violation (and per violation involving the personal information of consumers the business knows are under 16). https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1798.155.

## Patent terms

- USPTO Patent essentials — utility and plant patents last up to 20 years from the first non-provisional filing date; a design patent lasts 15 years from grant; utility patents need scheduled maintenance fees to stay in force. Issuance cost is counsel- and fee-schedule-specific, so this package does not quote a single budget. https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/essentials
