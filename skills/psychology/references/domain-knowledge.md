# Domain Knowledge and Verifiable Facts

## The WEIRD Psychology Phenomenon
- **Definition:** WEIRD stands for Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, and Democratic.
- **Fact:** First identified by researchers (e.g., Henrich, Heine, Norenzayan), WEIRD subjects were historically overrepresented in psychological research, often assuming the findings were universally applicable.
- **Application in Practice:** When evaluating research, it is essential to consider the sample demographic. Psychological phenomena in WEIRD populations may not generalize globally.

## The Replication Crisis
- **Definition:** Also known as the reproducibility crisis, it refers to widespread failures in reproducing published scientific results.
- **Relevance:** It heavily impacted social psychology and medicine, leading to shifts in scientific culture, publishing, and stricter methodological rigor.
- **Application in Practice:** Contested concepts like ego depletion, Stanford Prison experiment findings, and some priming studies require honest acknowledgment. Emphasize methodological robustness (e.g., sample size, pre-registration) in contemporary research.

## DSM-5-TR (Text Revision)
- **Status:** The latest edition of the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders by the American Psychiatric Association, published in 2022.
- **Application in Practice:** Ensure conceptual diagnostic linkages explicitly refer to criteria provided in the DSM-5-TR, recognizing its role as a principal authority for psychiatric diagnoses in the US and global alignment efforts (like ICD-11).
