# Sources — Bar Exam

Official and primary references used to ground US Bar Exam prep guidance. Prefer these over secondary blogs when cut scores, formats, calendars, or transfer rules change. Re-check the linked page before quoting a live deadline, fee, eligibility rule, or jurisdiction list.

## National / multistate authorities

- National Conference of Bar Examiners (NCBE): https://www.ncbex.org/
- NCBE — Uniform Bar Examination (UBE): https://www.ncbex.org/exams/ube
- NCBE — Multistate Bar Examination (MBE): https://www.ncbex.org/exams/mbe
- NCBE — Multistate Essay Examination (MEE): https://www.ncbex.org/exams/mee
- NCBE — Multistate Performance Test (MPT): https://www.ncbex.org/exams/mpt
- NCBE — MBE subject matter outline / prep materials portal: https://www.ncbex.org/exams/mbe/preparing
- NCBE — jurisdiction information and score services entry points: https://www.ncbex.org/jurisdiction-information

## Score transfer and jurisdiction boards (examples)

UBE cut scores and portability are set by admitting jurisdictions, not solely by NCBE. Always confirm the destination board before relying on a transfer plan:

- New York State Board of Law Examiners: https://www.nybarexam.org/
- District of Columbia Court of Appeals Committee on Admissions: https://www.dccourts.gov/court-of-appeals/committee-on-admissions
- State Bar of California (non-UBE format reference): https://www.calbar.ca.gov/Admissions/Examinations
- Florida Board of Bar Examiners: https://www.floridabarexam.org/
- National Conference of Bar Examiners score services overview: https://www.ncbex.org/scores

Treat commercial prep vendors (Barbri, Themis, Kaplan, etc.) as study-method context only. Official format, scoring, and eligibility rules come from NCBE and the admitting jurisdiction.

## Scope notes

- Package tables for UBE weights (MBE 50% / MEE 30% / MPT 20%) and common cut-score bands are planning defaults; verify before high-stakes scheduling.
- California, Louisiana, Florida, and other non-UBE or hybrid formats require the local board page, not UBE defaults.
- This skill coaches exam technique. It is not legal advice and does not replace a licensed attorney or official board determination.
