# Content Operations — Help Center

Sustain quality with an editorial system tied to support outcomes, not page views alone.

## Article standard

Every published article should include:

- Problem statement in the customer’s language
- Ordered resolution steps
- Edge cases and known limitations
- Escalation path when self-service fails
- Last reviewed date and named owner

## Editorial workflow

| Stage | Owner | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | Subject expert | Technically accurate content |
| Review | Support lead | Clear language, correct tags, safe scope |
| Publish | Help center owner | Search metadata and links validated |
| Monitor | Ops analyst | Performance metrics captured |

## Weekly metrics review

Track and discuss:

- Deflection rate by category
- Top unresolved search queries
- Articles with high exit-without-helpful rates
- Escalations that published docs should have prevented
- Article freshness against the review cadence

## Update triggers (project policy defaults)

Refresh or rewrite when:

- Product behavior changes
- The same ticket issue appears **3+ times in a week**
- A search-miss pattern persists for **2 weeks**
- SLA breaches cluster around one category

Record priority changes and owners in `<state_root>/memory.md` or the content inventory after consent.
