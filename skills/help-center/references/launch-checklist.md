# Launch Checklist — Help Center

Use before public or company-wide rollout. Success bands are **project policy**—set numeric targets with the user and store them in `<state_root>/memory.md`.

## Readiness

- [ ] Provider or custom-stack decision documented with scores and rejected options
- [ ] Taxonomy approved by support and product stakeholders
- [ ] Top ticket intents covered (default planning bar: **top 20**, adjustable)
- [ ] Escalation routes tested from docs failure to ticket workflow
- [ ] Redirect map validated for migrated public URLs

## Quality controls

- [ ] Search relevance tested with real support queries
- [ ] Broken-link scan completed with zero critical failures on launch paths
- [ ] Article owners assigned for every category
- [ ] Review cadence scheduled (monthly or quarterly)

## Go-live operations

- [ ] Monitoring for deflection and search misses enabled
- [ ] On-call owner assigned for launch week
- [ ] Rollback plan documented and rehearsed
- [ ] Launch communication sent to support and customer-facing teams

## Success criteria (first 30 days, project policy)

- [ ] Deflection rate meets the agreed target band
- [ ] First response time improves or stays stable for documented intents
- [ ] Escalation noise decreases for issues covered by published articles

Log go-live notes and exceptions in `<state_root>/rollout-log.md` after consent.
