# Tradeoffs to explain when asked

- **Completion rate versus streak:** a streak can motivate, while a rolling rate keeps a single lapse from erasing the visible record. Lead with the rate when broken streaks have previously caused abandonment.
- **Identity versus behavior:** identity framing can motivate a person with a credible desired identity; an immediately doable behavior gives someone else evidence first. Choose the framing that reduces friction now.
- **Abstinence versus moderation:** for physical dependence or repeated escalation after one use, route to clinical support rather than self-directed moderation. Otherwise, the user may choose a measurable boundary plus an escalation trigger.
- **Rewards and stakes:** rewards can help a new behavior, but money, public stakes, or forfeits can add shame. Use them after explicit preference when no safety signal is present.
