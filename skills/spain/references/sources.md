# Official Sources & Technical Specifications

This document records the official governmental, tourism, and administrative portals for Spain.

## Official Spanish Government & Tourism Portals

- **Spain Official Tourism Portal (Turespaña)**: [https://www.spain.info/en/](https://www.spain.info/en/)
  - Official national tourism portal detailing destinations, cultural heritage monuments, national parks, transport connections, and regional gastronomy.
- **Ministry of Foreign Affairs, European Union and Cooperation**: [https://www.exteriores.gob.es/en/Paginas/index.aspx](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/en/Paginas/index.aspx)
  - Official institutional portal of the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs, covering foreign policy, international cooperation, and diplomatic missions directory.
- **Portal de Administración Electrónica (General Administration of the State)**: [https://administracion.gob.es/](https://administracion.gob.es/)
  - Official public administration portal for residency registration (Empadronamiento), NIE (Foreigner Identity Number) issuance, and civil procedures.
