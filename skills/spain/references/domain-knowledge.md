# Tourism in Spain

Tourism in Spain is a major component of the national economy. Domestic and international tourism combined accounted for a significant portion of Spain's GDP. Popular regions include Catalonia, Andalusia, and Madrid. Popular attractions include the Sagrada Familia in Barcelona, the Alhambra in Granada, and the Prado Museum in Madrid.
Spain is also renowned for its beaches (e.g., Costa del Sol, Balearic Islands), festivals (e.g., La Tomatina, San Fermín), and culinary traditions like tapas, paella, and various regional wines.
