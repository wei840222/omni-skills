# Sources — Berlin

Back to [Berlin Skill Overview](../SKILL.md).

Use these verified primary sources when legal, transit, registration, or housing regulations apply in Berlin.

## Municipal Governance & Registration (Anmeldung)

- **Berlin.de Official City Portal** — https://www.berlin.de/en/
  - Primary municipal portal for public administration, civic guidance, and district administrative services.
- **Service Berlin (Bürgeramt)** — https://service.berlin.de/
  - Registration appointment booking and official requirements for Anmeldung (proof of residence / Wohnungsgeberbestätigung within 14 days of moving in).

## Public Transit & Deutschlandticket

- **BVG Deutschlandticket Portal** — https://www.bvg.de/en/deutschland-ticket
  - Nationwide regional transit subscription ticket priced at €63/month (effective 2025/2026 tariff), valid across all German local public transit (U-Bahn, S-Bahn, buses, trams, and regional trains). Note: The historical €29 Berlin-Abo was officially discontinued on December 10, 2024.
- **Berliner Verkehrsbetriebe (BVG Main Portal)** — https://www.bvg.de/en
  - Operator of U-Bahn, tram, bus, and ferry networks across Berlin. Covers Berlin fare zones (AB, BC, ABC) and single trip validity rules (valid 2 hours in one direction, no round trips).
- **Verkehrsverbund Berlin-Brandenburg (VBB)** — https://www.vbb.de/en/
  - Unified transit authority coordinating regional trains (S-Bahn, Regionalbahn, RE) across Berlin and Brandenburg.

## Housing & Tenancy Law (Mietpreisbremse)

- **German Civil Code § 556d BGB (Rental Brake / Mietpreisbremse)** — https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__556d.html
  - Statutory basis limiting re-letting rents in designated tight housing markets to a maximum of 10% above the local comparative rent (Mietspiegel), subject to statutory exceptions for newly constructed buildings and extensive modernizations.

## Immigration & Skilled Labor (EU Blue Card)

- **Make it in Germany (Federal Government Portal)** — https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card
  - Official federal portal for foreign qualified professionals. Specifies EU Blue Card statutory salary thresholds, fast-track permanent residency pathways (21 or 27 months with German language proficiency), and IT specialist exemptions.
