# Trading Domain Knowledge

## Technical Analysis Fundamentals

Technical analysis is a financial analysis methodology for forecasting the direction of prices through the study of past market data, primarily price and volume. Behavioral economics and quantitative analysis incorporate many of the same tools. Active management relies heavily on technical analysis.

**Core Principles:**
- Market action discounts everything (price reflects available information)
- Prices move in trends (uptrends, downtrends, sideways)
- History tends to repeat itself (human psychology is relatively constant)

**Common Indicators:**
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index):** Measures momentum on a scale of 0 to 100. Over 70 is often considered overbought; under 30 is considered oversold.
- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence):** Trend-following momentum indicator showing the relationship between two moving averages.
- **Moving Averages (SMA, EMA):** Smooth price data to create a trend-following indicator.
- **Bollinger Bands:** Volatility indicator placing bands around a moving average.

## Risk Management & Position Sizing

Proper risk management is often cited as the most critical component of trading success.

- **Risk per trade:** Typically advised to risk only 1-2% of total account equity on any single trade.
- **Position Size Calculation:** Position Size = (Account Value * Risk %) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price)
- **Risk/Reward Ratio:** Compares expected returns to the amount of risk taken. A 1:2 or 1:3 ratio is commonly targeted.

## Trade Execution and Stops

- **Stop Loss:** An order placed with a broker to buy or sell a specific stock once the stock reaches a certain price, designed to limit an investor's loss on a position.
- **Take Profit:** An order specifying the exact price at which to close out an open position for a profit.
