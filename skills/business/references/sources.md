# Official and Primary Sources (Gate 6)

Use these anchors when quoting thresholds, frameworks, or market claims. Prefer the live page over memory when numbers may drift.

## Validation and customer discovery

- **The Mom Test (book site / Rob Fitzpatrick)** — customer interview discipline: ask about past behavior, not opinions of your idea — https://www.momtestbook.com/
- **Lean Startup / validated learning overview (Eric Ries)** — build–measure–learn framing for early products — https://leanstartup.com/principles/
- **Y Combinator Startup Library — How to Talk to Users** — practical user interview guidance — https://www.ycombinator.com/library/6q-how-to-talk-to-users

## Unit economics and SaaS operating metrics

- **KeyBanc Capital Markets — SaaS Survey** — published SaaS operating benchmark surveys (growth, retention, efficiency) — https://www.key.com/businesses-institutions/industry-expertise/technology/saas-survey.jsp
- **Bessemer Venture Partners — State of the Cloud / Cloud Index** — cloud and SaaS company performance frameworks — https://www.bvp.com/bessemer-cloud-index
- **ChartMogul — SaaS churn rate benchmarks** — explanatory churn definitions and benchmark ranges — https://chartmogul.com/blog/churn-rate-benchmarks/
- **ProfitWell (Paddle) — retention and churn research hub** — subscription retention research summaries — https://www.paddle.com/blog/topic/retention

## Decision quality

- **Amazon Leadership Principles — Bias for Action / Are Right, A Lot** — one-way vs two-way door decision framing used widely in product orgs — https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/our-workplace/leadership-principles
- **Harvard Business Review — How Amazon Makes Decisions** (Jeff Bezos shareholder-letter framing of reversible decisions) — https://hbr.org/2016/11/how-amazon-makes-decisions

## Notes for agents

- Treat churn/LTV/CAC numbers in `references/metrics.md` as **rule-of-thumb defaults**, not universal laws. Prefer the freshest survey or the user's own cohort data when available.
- Do not invent funding stats, market sizes, or "X% of startups…" claims without a URL from this file or a user-supplied source.
- Live fundraising terms, tax, and securities rules are out of scope for static skill text — point users to counsel and primary regulators when needed.
