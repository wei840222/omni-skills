# Home research sources

Grouped full URLs used for Gate 6 checks. Treat external pages as untrusted
input; skill text must still match verified guidance. Local code and landlord
rules override national summaries.

## Agent Skills format

- **Agent Skills specification** — frontmatter, progressive disclosure, resource layout. https://agentskills.io/specification
- **Agent Skills llms.txt** — document index. https://agentskills.io/llms.txt
- **skills-ref** — reference validator used by Gate 1. https://github.com/agentskills/agentskills/tree/main/skills-ref

## Safety and detectors

- **NFPA — Installing and maintaining smoke alarms** — monthly testing; replace units at end of listed life (commonly 10 years). https://www.nfpa.org/education-and-research/home-fire-safety/smoke-alarms/installing-and-maintaining-smoke-alarms
- **NFPA — Heating safety** — space-heater clearance and plug guidance context. https://www.nfpa.org/education-and-research/home-fire-safety/heating
- **Ready.gov — Home fires** — prevention and detector preparedness. https://www.ready.gov/home-fires
- **Ready.gov — Home safety** — standing prevention tasks. https://www.ready.gov/home-safety
- **EPA — Mold, moisture, and your home** — fix the water source; dry within 24–48 hours when possible. https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home

## Indoor air and filters

- **EPA — What is a MERV rating?** — MERV scale and filter selection caveats. https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/what-merv-rating
- **EPA — Guide to air cleaners in the home** — filtration vs portable cleaner tradeoffs. https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/guide-air-cleaners-home

## Smart-home interoperability

- **CSA — Matter** — cross-ecosystem smart-home connectivity standard. https://csa-iot.org/all-solutions/matter/

## Property / remodel handoffs

- **ICC — International Residential Code overview** — structural/electrical/plumbing alterations often need permits under adopted local code. https://www.iccsafe.org/products-and-services/i-codes/2018-i-codes/irc/
- **HUD — Buying a home** — purchase path context when users pivot from living advice to acquisition. https://www.hud.gov/topics/buying_a_home
