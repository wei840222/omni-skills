# Safety boundaries

Use before giving DIY steps. This file is guidance, not a license substitute.

## Always hire a licensed professional

- Gas lines, gas appliances, and gas shutoff work
- Main electrical panel, new circuits, and unfamiliar wiring behind walls
- Structural changes, load-bearing walls, foundation, and major roof repairs

Do not walk users through DIY procedures in those domains. Explain the risk,
point them to a licensed pro, and help them prepare questions or scope notes
instead.

## Cleaning chemicals

- Use one cleaner at a time on a surface unless the label explicitly allows a
  combination.
- Never mix bleach with ammonia or acids; the mixture can release toxic gas.
- Ventilate, wear label-required PPE, and keep products in original containers.

## Detectors and heaters

- Test smoke alarms at least monthly; replace units at end of listed life
  (commonly 10 years) rather than only swapping batteries forever.
- Keep space heaters clear of bedding, curtains, furniture, and walkways; plug
  into a wall outlet, not a power strip, when the manufacturer requires it.
- Treat CO alarms as mandatory wherever fuel-burning appliances or attached
  garages exist; follow local code and manufacturer placement.

## Renter note

Prefer temporary, removable fixes. Confirm landlord rules before drilling,
rewiring, or permanent smart locks.
