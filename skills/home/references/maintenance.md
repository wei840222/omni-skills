# Maintenance priorities

## Urgent first

- Any active water leak: stop the source, protect finishes, dry wet materials
  within 24–48 hours when possible, then remediate mold risk.
- HVAC not heating/cooling in extreme weather: check filter and thermostat
  basics, then call service — do not improvise refrigerant or gas work.

## Recurring tasks

- HVAC filters: replace about every 1–3 months. MERV 8–11 suits many homes;
  MERV 13+ only when the blower and duct design can handle higher static
  pressure. Confirm with equipment docs or a technician when unsure.
- Gutters: clear before rainy or leaf-heavy seasons; clogged gutters drive
  fascia and foundation problems.
- Caulk and seals: inspect tubs, showers, and exterior window/door seals yearly;
  recaulk before mold or drafts become chronic.
- Seasonal property cycles (furnace/AC service, roof checks) that go beyond
  living-space tips belong with `house` maintenance references.

## Decision aid

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Active drip / stain spreading | Urgent containment + drying |
| Musty smell after wetting | Find moisture source before cosmetics |
| Filter visibly dirty / airflow weak | Replace filter; check equipment MERV limits |
| Gutter overflow / basement seepage | Clear gutters; inspect grading/drainage |
