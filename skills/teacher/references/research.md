# Research Base & Domain Knowledge

Pedagogical claims in this skill are grounded in cognitive psychology and education research. Prefer these sources over secondary summaries.

## Spaced practice and retrieval
- **Dunlosky et al. (2013), Psychological Science in the Public Interest** — Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1529100612453266
- **Cepeda et al. (2006), Psychological Bulletin** — Distributed practice in verbal recall: https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354
- **Application:** low-stakes retrieval starters; space revisits at roughly 10–20% of the retention interval.

## Cognitive load, worked examples, expertise reversal
- **Kirschner, Sweller & Clark (2006), Educational Psychologist** — Why Minimal Guidance During Instruction Does Not Work: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1207/s15326985ep4102_1
- **Kalyuga et al. / expertise reversal overview** — when worked examples help or hinder: https://doi.org/10.1207/s15326985ep3801_1
- **Application:** explicit instruction and worked examples for novices; fade scaffolds as accuracy rises.

## Working memory capacity
- **Cowan (2010), Current Directions in Psychological Science** — The Magical Mystery Four: How is Working Memory Capacity Limited, and Why?: https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721409359277
- **Application:** present roughly 5–10 minute chunks that keep new element count near four before requiring a response.

## Formative assessment and feedback
- **Wiliam / Black & Wiliam formative assessment tradition** — hinge questions and responsive teaching overview: https://www.dylanwiliam.org/Dylan_Wiliams_website/Papers.html
- **Butler (1988), Journal of Educational Psychology** — Enhancing and undermining intrinsic motivation via grades vs comments: https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.80.4.474
- **Kluger & DeNisi (1996), Psychological Bulletin** — feedback intervention meta-analysis: https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.119.2.254
- **Application:** whole-class response formats; comment-only on formative work; task-focused feedback with time to act.

## Behaviour and wait time
- **Rowe (1986), Journal of Teacher Education** — wait time and questioning: https://doi.org/10.1177/002248718603700110
- **PBIS / OSEP technical assistance** — positive-to-corrective ratio guidance: https://www.pbis.org/
- **Application:** 3–5s wait after questions; teach routines before relying on them; keep corrections least-invasive first.

## Integrity detectors (caution)
- **Liang et al. (2023)** — GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.02819
- **Application:** never accuse from a detector score alone; use process evidence and redesign assessments.
