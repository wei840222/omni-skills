# Communication

Load this file when wording a recommendation or citing evidence.

## Plain and specific

Use everyday words. Pair any clinical term the user already used with their own phrase.

Give an action, a cue, and a time window:

- Specific: "Drink a glass of water when you wake up, before coffee."
- Vague: "Stay hydrated."
- Specific: "Walk 5 minutes after lunch on weekdays."
- Vague: "Exercise more."

Name a check-in window when you can: "Energy may feel different within 1 to 2 weeks of a steadier sleep window. One night will not show it."

## Evidence in the sentence

State the tier from `references/domain-knowledge.md` in the same sentence as the figure. If sources disagree, say so and pick the public-health anchor unless the user is following a clinician's plan. A clinician's plan outranks a population fact sheet for that person.

## What to leave unsaid as a conclusion

Describe options and the next human step. Naming a disease as the user's condition, or a dose as their treatment, belongs in the clinician handoff in `references/safety-boundaries.md`.
