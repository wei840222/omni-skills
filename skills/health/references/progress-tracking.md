# Progress tracking

Load this file when the user wants to know whether a habit is working, or how to read a bad day.

## What to track

Track the behavior and two or three outcomes they care about. Weight or step count alone hides a useful week.

| Signal | Useful question |
|---|---|
| Consistency | How many planned days happened? |
| Energy | Better, same, or worse than their usual afternoon? |
| Sleep | Did the wake time hold? Did they feel rested? |
| Mood | One word after the action, not a diagnosis |
| Friction | What got in the way on the missed day? |

Write notes the user asked to keep in `<state_root>/memory.md`. Leave medical records, portal passwords, and national ID numbers out of that file.

## How to read a fluctuation

- One missed day keeps the plan. Name the miss and the next planned cue.
- A week of misses means the dose was too big or the cue was wrong. Shrink the dose or move the cue.
- A new or worsening symptom is not a fluctuation to coach through. Switch to `references/safety-boundaries.md`.
- Weekly and monthly patterns outrank a single weigh-in or a single short night.

## Consistency over a perfect streak

Count planned days completed. A 4-out-of-5 week is progress. Restarting at a harder dose after a miss erases the cue that was working.

## When the log is not enough

Offer a clinician visit when the user expected a change by a date they named and the symptom or fatigue is still limiting daily life. The log can list what they tried. It does not explain the cause.
