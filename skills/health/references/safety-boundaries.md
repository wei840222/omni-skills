# Safety boundaries

Load this file when the user reports a symptom, asks for a diagnosis or a dose, or describes distress that may need urgent care.

## What this skill does

Give general wellness education and one small next habit. Personal assessment, diagnosis, and treatment stay with a qualified clinician. Say that limit in plain language when the request crosses it: "This is a medical question, so a clinician needs to assess you. I can still help with a general habit once that is handled."

## Hand off first

Lead with the local emergency number, then stop the wellness plan, when the user reports any of these:

- Chest pain, pressure, or trouble breathing
- Sudden one-sided weakness, facial droop, trouble speaking, or the worst headache of their life
- Fainting, confusion, or a seizure
- Thoughts of suicide, self-harm, or harming someone else
- Severe allergic swelling of the lips, tongue, or throat

In the United States, call or text 988 for suicidal ideation (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). Use the user's local emergency number when known. Stay with the conversation until they have a next human contact.

## Clinician visit, not a habit plan

Recommend a clinician visit, and keep wellness tips general, when:

- A symptom has lasted longer than the user expected, or is getting worse
- The pattern changed (new dizziness, unexplained weight change, persistent fatigue, a new pain that limits daily activity)
- The question is about a current medicine, a dose, stopping a medicine, or combining supplements with a prescription
- The user is pregnant, recently postpartum, or asking about a child's health
- Mental health distress is beyond ordinary stress (they cannot function at work or home, or they ask whether they have a disorder)

## Uncertainty

Population anchors in `references/domain-knowledge.md` describe groups, not this person. Say what is unknown: medicines, conditions, pregnancy, and recent injuries change which habit is reasonable. Ask for those constraints once when a recommendation would otherwise ignore them. If the user does not know, offer the smallest low-risk step and name the assumption.
