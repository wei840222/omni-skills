# Veterinary sources and anchors

## Professional guidance and EBVM

- American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA) resources — professional practice and public guidance via https://www.avma.org/
- RCVS Knowledge / EBVM overview — evidence hierarchy and practice application via https://knowledge.rcvs.org.uk/evidence-based-veterinary-medicine/
- ACVIM consensus statements index — specialty consensus on selected conditions via https://www.acvim.org/publications/consensus-statements
- Veterinary Cooperative Oncology Group (VCOG) — oncology response and adverse-event criteria context via https://www.vetcancersociety.org/

## Toxicology and urgent owner guidance

- ASPCA Animal Poison Control — common toxins and when to seek emergency care via https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/animal-poison-control
- Pet Poison Helpline toxin lists — household hazards and species differences via https://www.petpoisonhelpline.com/poisons/
- Merck Veterinary Manual toxicology overview — clinical toxin monographs via https://www.merckvetmanual.com/toxicology

## Teaching anchors preserved from baseline skill

These are teaching/orientation anchors, not standing medical orders. Always re-check current label, formulary, and attending DVM direction before clinical use.

- Chocolate / theobromine owner framing: dark chocolate roughly ~1 oz per 10 lb as a high-concern teaching threshold; milk chocolate lower risk per ounce; always escalate when uncertain.
- Shock fluid teaching anchors often cited in emergency teaching: dog ~90 mL/kg/hr, cat ~60 mL/kg/hr.
- Species heart-rate teaching ranges often cited: feline ~140–220 bpm, canine ~60–140 bpm at rest (context-dependent).
- Hard stops repeatedly emphasized in baseline skill: human NSAIDs for pets; acetaminophen in cats; ivermectin in MDR1-mutant collies; lily exposure in cats.
