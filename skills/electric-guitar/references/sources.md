- [Wikipedia — Guitar picking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitar_picking) — background on plectrum/alternate picking as a right-hand articulation family.
- [Wikipedia — Finger vibrato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finger_vibrato) — fretting-hand vibrato as controlled pitch oscillation rather than random shake.
- [Wikipedia — Palm mute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palm_mute) — right-hand damping used for articulation and noise control.
- [Fender — Learn the CAGED System](https://www.fender.com/articles/play/learn-the-caged-system) — manufacturer lesson framing five open-chord shapes as a movable fretboard map.
- [Wikipedia — Guitar chord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitar_chord) — chord-tone and triad vocabulary context for landing notes on harmony changes.
