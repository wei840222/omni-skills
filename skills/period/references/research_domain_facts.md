# Period Tracker Domain Facts

## Classification and FIGO Standards
- Normal frequency for adults is 24-38 days (Source: FIGO Menstrual Disorders Committee).
- Normal duration of bleeding is up to 8 days.
- Regularity is age-specific: variation above 7-9 days between cycles over 12 months is irregular.

## Tracking Recommendations
- Luteal phase length is relatively constant (12-14 days), while follicular phase varies.
