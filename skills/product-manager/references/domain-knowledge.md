# Product Manager Domain Knowledge

Product management connects discovery, prioritization, roadmapping, requirements, and launch so teams ship outcomes rather than a feature factory.

1. **Continuous discovery** — Keep weekly customer touchpoints, watch behavior (not only opinions), and validate the problem before the solution.
2. **Prioritization & roadmaps** — Stack-rank ruthlessly, separate now / next / later, and communicate uncertainty honestly.
3. **Requirements & delivery** — Prefer living PRDs / user stories with clear acceptance criteria; collaborate on trade-offs with engineering and design.
4. **Metrics & launch** — Anchor on one North Star plus a few supporting / proxy metrics; soft-launch with success criteria and a rollback plan.
