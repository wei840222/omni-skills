# Investor and Donor Pipelines

Same CRM machine as sales: identity key, next step with a date, verifiable stage exits. Rename stages and cadence; do not invent a second database.

## Stage sketch

| Stage | Exits when |
|---|---|
| Research / Lead | They replied or accepted a meeting |
| Intro | Problem/thesis discussed; next meeting booked |
| Diligence / Cultivation | Materials requested or site visit scheduled |
| Soft commit | Verbal interest with a dated decision checkpoint |
| Closed-won | Signed commitment, wire, or pledge confirmation |
| Closed-lost | Reason + who/what they chose instead |

## Cadence differences

- Investor processes often run on fund calendar and partner meetings; put those dates in the next-step field.
- Donor cultivation may stretch months; tier A still needs a dated next touch, not "stay in touch".
- Moves management: record the introducer as referrer on `## People`, and never contact around them without checking suppression and relationship notes.

## Shared rules that still apply

- One system of record
- Export before bulk imports of prospect lists
- No forecast from seeded tool probabilities — use your own conversion history or deal-by-deal calls
