# Sources for CRM operational claims

Use these primary or canonical sources when validating identity handling, pipeline discipline, privacy duties, or tool lock-in claims.

| Topic | Source | Applied guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRM definition and channel aggregation | [Wikipedia: Customer relationship management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customer_relationship_management) | CRM administers customer interactions across channels and depends on usable customer data. |
| Gmail dot-insensitive local parts | [Google Account Help: dots don't matter in Gmail addresses](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/7436150) | Strip dots only for known Gmail domains; other providers may treat dotted local-parts as distinct. |
| GDPR erasure and data-subject rights | [ICO: Right to erasure](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/right-to-erasure/) | Honor deletion requests across copies; suppression/opt-out records may still need to persist. |
| CCPA/CPRA consumer deletion | [California AG: CCPA](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) | Covered businesses must provide deletion and disclosure paths for consumer personal information. |
| Salesforce data export | [Salesforce Help: Export Backup Data](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sf.admin_exportdata.htm&type=1) | Prefer tools that can export objects and related data; lock-in rises when history cannot leave. |
| HubSpot data export | [HubSpot Knowledge Base: Export data](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/accounts/export-data) | Verify contacts, companies, deals, and activity can leave before adopting a hosted CRM. |
| Pipedrive data export | [Pipedrive Help: Export data from Pipedrive](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/export-data-from-pipedrive) | Confirm deal and activity history export before migration planning. |
| MEDDIC qualification framing | [MEDDIC Academy: What is MEDDIC](https://meddic.academy/what-is-meddic/) | Multi-stakeholder enterprise deals benefit from explicit metrics, economic buyer, and decision process fields; light deals do not. |
