# Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Domain Knowledge

Customer relationship management (CRM) is a process in which a business or other organization administers its interactions with customers, typically using data analysis to study large amounts of information. CRM systems compile data from a range of different communication channels, including a company's website, telephone, email, live chat, marketing materials, and more recently, social media.

## Key Benefits

- Improved customer service and retention
- Anticipation of customer needs
- Better marketing based on customer behavior

## Core Concepts

- **Data Hygiene**: Maintaining clean, accurate data through deduplication and standardisation
- **Pipeline Management**: Defining clear stages for deals and tracking their progress through the sales cycle
- **Lead Scoring**: Assigning values to leads based on their interactions and demographics to prioritize follow-ups
