# Sources

Use these pages before repeating a loan ratio, a permit rule, a seasonal task, or a remodel payback figure. They describe common U.S. practice. Local law, lender overlays, and current contractor bids override them.

## Buying

- **HUD — Buying a home** — A purchase path includes financing, inspection, and closing. A visual home inspection does not open walls or replace a specialist sewer, well, septic, or pool check. https://www.hud.gov/topics/buying_a_home
- **NAR — Home inspections** — Keep an inspection contingency unless the buyer has already accepted the unknown. The inspector’s report is the input for repair, credit, or walk-away decisions, not a guarantee of hidden conditions. https://www.nar.realtor/home-inspections
- **USA.gov — Housing help** — Federal housing pages point to loan, rental, and fair-housing programs. They do not replace a lender’s current DTI or PMI overlay. https://www.usa.gov/housing-help

## Maintenance

- **NFPA — Installing and maintaining smoke alarms** — Test smoke alarms at least once a month. Replace the unit when it reaches the end of its listed life, commonly 10 years. https://www.nfpa.org/education-and-research/home-fire-safety/smoke-alarms/installing-and-maintaining-smoke-alarms
- **EPA — A Brief Guide to Mold, Moisture, and Your Home** — Fix the water source first. Dry wet materials within 24–48 hours. Do not treat mold as a smell to cover. https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home
- **Ready.gov — Home safety** — Detector checks, utility shutoffs, and seasonal weather prep are standing prevention, not a one-time project. https://www.ready.gov/home-safety

## Improvements and selling

- **ICC — International Residential Code** — Structural change, most electrical and plumbing alterations, and additions are permit work under the adopted local code. Paint and similar cosmetic work often are not. Confirm with the local building department before starting. https://www.iccsafe.org/products-and-services/i-codes/2018-i-codes/irc/
- **NAR — Remodeling Impact Report** — Cost-recoup figures are market estimates, not a quote. Minor kitchen and curb-appeal work usually recoup more than large upscale additions. Use a current local report, not a memorized percentage. https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/research-reports/remodeling-impact

## Renting

- **HUD — Rental help** — Habitability, fair housing, and deposit rules are local. A lease cannot waive required safety standards. https://www.hud.gov/topics/rental_assistance
- **HUD — State information** — Deposit limits, eviction timelines, and required disclosures come from the state or city, not from a national checklist. https://www.hud.gov/states
