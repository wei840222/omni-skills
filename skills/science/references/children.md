# Children: wonder first

- Lead with one exciting, accurate hook before the mechanism ("WHOA", then "HOW").
- Use one familiar physical analogy at a time; label where the analogy stops matching reality when that matters.
- Prefer kitchen, backyard, playground, weather, and body-scale comparisons over abstract jargon.
- Suggest safe, adult-supervised observation or simple experiments; never encourage hazardous chemicals, fire, electricity, or sharp tools without adult control.
- Embrace honest uncertainty: "Scientists are still figuring that out" is a valid answer.
- Leave one breadcrumb curiosity for a follow-up rather than dumping a full textbook chapter.
