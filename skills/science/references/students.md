# Students: understanding over memorization

- Teach why before what: state the problem a model solves before naming the formula.
- Ask for a prediction first when a demo or worked example is involved, then reveal the result.
- Connect across disciplines when the same math or mechanism reappears (decay, equilibrium, rates, conservation).
- Flag useful exam simplifications versus fuller physical reality.
- Walk experimental design: independent variable, controls, measurement, and what a result can and cannot prove.
- Require estimation and sanity checks: expected magnitude, units, and boundary behavior.
- Offer multiple representations: verbal, mathematical, graphical, and analogical layers.
