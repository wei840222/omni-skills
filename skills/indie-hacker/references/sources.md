# Research Sources — indie-hacker

Gate 6 anchors for solo-founder validation, pricing discovery, distribution compounding, and time protection. Prefer primary operator essays, accelerator guidance, and durable definitions over secondary growth blogs when rules conflict.

## Validation and MVP

- **Y Combinator — How to Validate Your Startup Idea** — customer conversations, willingness-to-pay checks, and early signal discipline via https://www.ycombinator.com/library/4D-how-to-validate-your-startup-idea
- **Wikipedia — Minimum viable product** — MVP as learning vehicle, not a polished mini-product via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_viable_product
- **Wikipedia — Lean startup** — validated learning and build-measure-learn framing via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lean_startup
- **Eric Ries / The Lean Startup summary context** — prefer primary lean definitions above when tactical blogs conflict

## Pricing and willingness to pay

- **Price Intelligently / Paddle research context** — value-based packaging and willingness-to-pay research patterns via https://www.paddle.com/resources/value-based-pricing
- **Stripe — Pricing pages & billing overview** — practical checkout and plan packaging baseline via https://docs.stripe.com/products-prices/overview
- **Lemon Squeezy docs — Getting started** — common solo-founder merchant-of-record alternative via https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/guides/getting-started

## Distribution / build in public

- **Y Combinator — Startup Library distribution essays index** — durable acquisition framing via https://www.ycombinator.com/library
- **Wikipedia — Product-led growth** — PLG vocabulary when the product itself is the primary funnel via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product-led_growth

## Time, focus, and kill decisions

- **Basecamp / Shape Up philosophy (public guide)** — appetite-based scoping and fixed-time variable-scope thinking via https://basecamp.com/shapeup
- **Wikipedia — Opportunity cost** — explicit hour-cost framing for side-project work via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opportunity_cost

## Notes for maintainers

- Re-check YC Library and Stripe/Lemon Squeezy docs before changing validation windows, payment defaults, or kill thresholds.
- Keep skill thresholds (5 interviews, 2-week validation ceiling, ~10–15 h/week side-project budget, 3-month unpaid traction review) labeled as operational defaults, not universal laws.
