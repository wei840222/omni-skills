## Growth Loop Types

### Viral Loops
- **User-generated content** — Users create content, attracts new users (e.g., TikTok, Canva)
- **Invite mechanics** — Incentivized referrals, two-sided rewards (e.g., Dropbox, Airbnb)
- **Network effects** — Product improves as more people join (e.g., Slack, WhatsApp)
- **Social proof** — Usage visible to others triggers curiosity (e.g., "Sent from iPhone")

### Paid Loops
- **CAC < LTV** — Unit economics must work at scale (3:1+ ratio target)
- **Payback period** — How fast you recover acquisition cost (< 12 months for venture-backed)
- **Channel diversification** — Don't depend on one platform (platform risk)
- **Creative velocity** — Fresh ads beat audience fatigue (test 10+ creatives/month)

### Content Loops
- **SEO flywheel** — Content ranks, drives traffic, funds more content
- **Community-generated** — Users create content that attracts users (e.g., Stack Overflow, Reddit)
- **Educational content** — Teaches topic, positions product as solution
- **Template/tool marketing** — Free tools drive signups (e.g., HubSpot Website Grader)

### Sales Loops
- **Referral from customers** — Happy customers introduce new ones
- **Partner channels** — Distribution through aligned businesses
- **Expansion revenue** — Existing customers grow into bigger accounts (land and expand)

## Channel Evaluation

| Channel | Fit Signals | Warning Signs |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Paid social | Visual product, impulse purchases | Long sales cycles, B2B complex |
| SEO | Information-seeking users, evergreen content | Fast-changing product, no search volume |
| Viral | Social product, shareable outcomes | Solo utility, private use cases |
| Sales | High ACV, complex decisions | Low price point, self-serve possible |

## CAC Optimization

1. **Audience refinement** — Tighter targeting, lookalikes from best customers
2. **Creative testing** — Headlines, images, formats, landing pages (test 10+ variants)
3. **Funnel fixes** — Remove friction between click and conversion
4. **Channel timing** — Seasonality, day-of-week, competitive cycles
5. **Attribution clarity** — Know what actually drives conversions (use incrementality testing, not just attribution models)

## Anti-Patterns

- **Vanity channels** — Impressive reach, no conversions
- **Single channel dependency** — Platform changes kill you (iOS ATT, algorithm changes)
- **CAC blindness** — Growing users while losing money per user
- **Attribution games** — Taking credit for organic growth
- **Ignoring payback period** — High CAC is fine if payback is fast

## 🔴 Failure Modes and Recovery

### If CAC is rising but you don't know why:
1. **Check audience fatigue** — Are you running the same creatives for 3+ months? Test 10+ new variants
2. **Check channel saturation** — Is your primary channel hitting diminishing returns? Diversify to 2-3 channels
3. **Check competitive pressure** — Did a competitor enter your market? Differentiate messaging or target underserved segments
4. **Check funnel friction** — Did something break in onboarding or checkout? Audit conversion rates at each step

### If growth loops aren't compounding:
1. **Check loop closure** — Are users actually completing the loop (e.g., inviting others, creating content)? Measure loop completion rate
2. **Check loop velocity** — How fast does the loop cycle? Shorten time-to-value and time-to-share
3. **Check loop quality** — Are new users from the loop retaining? If not, fix onboarding before scaling the loop
4. **Check loop leakage** — Where are users dropping out? Fix the biggest leak before adding more input

### If you can't find a scalable channel:
1. **Check product-channel fit** — Are you forcing a channel that doesn't match your product? (e.g., paid social for complex B2B)
2. **Check ICP clarity** — Do you know exactly who your best customers are? Narrow ICP before scaling spend
3. **Check unit economics** — Is your LTV:CAC ratio < 1:1? Fix retention and expansion before scaling acquisition
4. **Check timing** — Are you scaling too early (pre-PMF)? Get retention signal before spending

## ❌ Anti-Patterns (Do NOT Do These)

❌ **Scaling paid acquisition before fixing retention** — You're paying to fill a leaky bucket. Fix churn first.

❌ **Copying competitor channels** — What works for them may not work for you (different product, audience, stage). Test before committing.

❌ **Optimizing CAC without considering payback period** — $500 CAC is fine if payback is 3 months, terrible if payback is 24 months.

❌ **Depending on a single channel** — Platform risk is real (iOS ATT, algorithm changes, policy updates). Maintain 2-3 channels.

❌ **Using attribution models as truth** — Attribution takes credit for organic growth. Validate with incrementality testing on your biggest channels.

❌ **Ignoring creative velocity** — Ad fatigue is real. Test 10+ creatives per month, refresh winners quarterly.

❌ **Measuring acquisition without measuring quality** — Are new users from channel X retaining better than channel Y? Track cohort quality, not just volume.
