# Nutrition Domain Knowledge

## General WHO/FAO Principles
Healthy diets, according to the WHO and FAO, are defined by four core principles:
1. **Adequate**: Meets our body's energy and essential nutrient requirements to support bodily functions without exceeding them.
2. **Diverse**: Includes various nutritious foods across different groups to secure sufficient nutrients.
3. **Balanced**: Energy from the three primary sources (proteins, fats, and carbohydrates) is balanced to foster a healthy weight, growth, activity, and to prevent disease.
4. **Moderate**: Includes only small quantities (or none) of foods that negatively impact health, such as highly salty and sugary foods.

## Essential Knowledge
- A healthy diet includes fruits, vegetables, and whole grains, and limits or excludes ultra-processed foods and sweetened beverages.
- Vegan diets require additional sources of Vitamin B12.
- A lack of a healthy diet is a major contributor to the global disease burden, often linked to unaffordability, particularly in developing regions.
