# Entry and Documents — United Kingdom

## Fast Decision Tree

1. Confirm whether the traveler needs an ETA, a visitor visa, or another permission for their exact nationality and purpose.
2. Verify passport validity for the whole stay and whether the trip includes the Republic of Ireland as well as the UK.
3. Check whether the user is flying, arriving by train/ferry, or crossing via the Common Travel Area.
4. Only then lock flights, rail passes, and non-refundable lodging.

## Visitor Pathways

| Path | Typical use | Main caution |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| ETA + visitor entry | Many non-visa nationals (for example USA, EU/EEA, Australia, Canada) for tourism/family/business visits | ETA is permission to travel, not admission guarantee |
| Standard Visitor visa | Nationalities that require a visa, or travelers whose purpose does not fit ETA | Processing time can shape the trip window |
| No ETA needed | British/Irish passport holders, people with UK permission to live/work/study, some CTA/airside cases | Confirm the exact exemption before advising someone to skip ETA |

## ETA facts to verify live

Checked against GOV.UK on 2026-10-24 (`https://www.gov.uk/eta`, `https://www.gov.uk/eta/apply`):

- An ETA lets eligible travelers come to the UK, Jersey, Guernsey, or the Isle of Man for up to 6 months for tourism, visiting family, and certain other permitted reasons.
- Application cost is £20 via the official online form or UK ETA app; third-party sites may charge more.
- Decisions usually arrive by email within a day and can take up to 3 working days; wait for confirmation before travel.
- An ETA lasts 2 years or until the passport expires, whichever is sooner, and is linked to the passport used in the application.
- Each traveler needs their own ETA, including babies and children.
- Landside airport transit (passing through UK border control) needs an ETA when the traveler is otherwise in an ETA cohort; airside transit without border control is a different case—confirm with the airline.
- ETA does not allow paid/unpaid work for a UK company outside narrow concessions, living in the UK through frequent successive visits, claiming public funds, or giving notice of marriage/civil partnership.

Always re-check `https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa` for the traveler's nationality and purpose before stating eligibility.

## Passport and proof logic

- Keep the passport valid for the whole stay.
- Carry onward or return travel proof if the route looks open-ended.
- Be ready to explain tourism purpose, lodging plan, and basic funds.
- Identity documents are checked on arrival; ETA or visa status does not replace border admission decisions.

## Ireland and Northern Ireland

- Combining Great Britain, Northern Ireland, and the Republic of Ireland raises entry complexity even when physical border friction looks low.
- The Common Travel Area is a long-standing UK–Ireland–Crown Dependencies arrangement; it does not erase the need for the correct immigration permission for each traveler.
- If the route includes Dublin plus Belfast or vice versa, confirm both UK and Ireland entry logic before spending money.
- People who live in Ireland and enter the UK from Ireland or the Crown Dependencies can fall under different ETA exemption rules—verify on GOV.UK rather than assuming.

## Children and family documents

- Minor travelers need the same core identity pathway as adults (including ETA when required).
- If a child travels with only one parent or with another adult, supporting consent paperwork can reduce friction at carriers and borders.

## Before locking non-refundables

- Entry pathway confirmed for the exact passport and purpose
- ETA or visa status checked where relevant
- Passport validity confirmed for the whole stay
- Ireland/Northern Ireland combination checked if relevant
- Return/onward logic and lodging proof ready
