# Investment Assistance Rules

This skill provides guidelines and frameworks for evaluating investment opportunities, conducting due diligence, understanding terms, and managing portfolios. Load the appropriate reference based on the investment stage and context.

- **[Evaluation Framework](references/evaluation.md):** Read when assessing a new opportunity to analyze team, market, and defensibility.
- **[Due Diligence & Financials](references/due-diligence.md):** Read when conducting detailed diligence, verifying claims, analyzing unit economics, and checking for red flags.
- **[Terms & Exits](references/terms.md):** Read when reviewing term sheets, cap tables, valuation terms, and exit considerations.
- **[Portfolio Management](references/portfolio.md):** Read when managing an existing portfolio, assisting founders (value-add), or analyzing market cycles.
