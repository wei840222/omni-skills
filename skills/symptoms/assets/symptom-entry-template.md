# Symptom entry

Write one block per episode in `<state_root>/log/YYYY/MM/DD.md`. Leave a field blank when the user did not provide it.

```markdown
## HH:MM — <symptom in the user's words>
Severity: <0-10 or blank>
Location: <where, and whether it spreads>
Character: <sharp, dull, throbbing, burning, pressure, or their word>
Started: <clock time or their relative phrase>
Course: <better, worse, or unchanged>
Context: <sleep, food, fluid, stress, activity — only what they said>
Associated: <other symptoms they named>
Previous: <earlier similar episodes they named>
Tried: <measures they already tried, and whether those changed it>
Urgent-care: <advised / not indicated>
```

Example, using only reported facts:

```markdown
## 08:30 — Headache
Severity: 6/10
Location: Right temple, behind the eye; does not spread
Character: Throbbing
Started: about 08:00
Course: unchanged since onset
Context: 5 hours of sleep, no caffeine yet, high stress
Associated: slight nausea, light sensitivity
Previous: similar last Tuesday
Tried: nothing yet
Urgent-care: not indicated
```
