## Practical Use Rule

Use these sources when answering:

- visa, VOA, e-visa, or stay-window questions
- Bali levy and customs arrival steps
- volcano, rain, or sea-condition re-checks before boat or park days
- park permits or conservation fee updates (via official park or ministry pages linked from the tourism portals above)

State what the traveler should re-verify before spending money. Prefer the live official page over cached skill prose for nationality-specific eligibility and fee amounts.
