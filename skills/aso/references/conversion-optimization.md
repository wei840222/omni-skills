# Conversion optimization

Diagnose the funnel before changing copy or creative. Separate acquisition quality, listing comprehension, technical eligibility, and post-install activation instead of treating a single conversion rate as an ASO verdict.

1. Define the cohort and event window.
2. Compare impressions, product-page views, installs, first-open completion, and meaningful activation where available.
3. Segment by platform, country, language, acquisition source, device constraints, and new versus returning users.
4. Form a single reversible hypothesis and test it with a control when possible.
5. Document tradeoffs: an install-rate lift that harms activation, support burden, or policy compliance is not automatically a win.

Use `references/a-b-testing.md` for experimental design and `references/tracking-and-analytics.md` for measurement details.
