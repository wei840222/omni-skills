# Seasonal and event strategy

Use seasonal creative or events only when they match a real, available in-app experience and the release calendar has enough review, localization, and rollback time.

1. Define the audience, event window, user benefit, and owner.
2. Confirm platform eligibility and policy before promising an event in metadata or creative.
3. Freeze localized assets early enough for review and QA; keep a non-seasonal fallback ready.
4. Monitor support load, conversion quality, and policy feedback during the event. Remove or correct expired claims promptly.

Sources:
- Apple, *In-App Events overview*: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events/
- Google Play Console Help, *Promotional content*: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10788710
