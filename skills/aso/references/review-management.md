# Review management

Treat reviews as user feedback, not a ranking-manipulation channel.

- Triage safety, billing, account-access, outage, and reproducible defect reports through the appropriate support or incident path.
- Reply only when authorized, using the platform's current review-response tooling. Acknowledge the issue, avoid personal data, and give a concrete next step.
- Never pressure a user to revise a rating, offer compensation for a positive review, create artificial reviews, or disclose account details publicly.
- Preserve evidence and report reviews only when they violate the platform's applicable policy; disagreement is not by itself a reportable violation.

Sources:
- Apple, *Ratings, reviews, and responses*: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/monitor-ratings-and-reviews/overview-of-ratings-reviews-and-responses
- Google Play policy, *User ratings, reviews, and installs*: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898684
