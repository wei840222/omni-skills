# Android metadata

Use the Play Console Help documentation and current Google Play policy as the source of truth for store-listing fields, translations, and prohibited metadata. Write for the selected language and country rather than translating keywords mechanically.

- Make the app name, short description, and full description accurate, readable, and consistent with the installed product.
- Do not use misleading claims, unsupported superlatives, keyword spam, or content that breaches the Google Play metadata policy.
- Confirm current character limits and which fields are available in Play Console before submitting a change.
- Review localized listings independently: a literal translation can create false claims, cultural errors, or policy risk.

Sources:
- Google Play Console Help, *Create and set up your app*: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152
- Google Play policy, *Metadata policy*: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842
- Google Play Console Help, *Manage translations*: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/3125566
