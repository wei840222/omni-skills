# Localization

Localize the product promise, terminology, screenshots, and support path for the target audience. Translation is one input, not final validation.

- Confirm the app actually supports the language and capability shown in the listing.
- Adapt examples, currencies, legal claims, and imagery only after local review.
- Maintain a locale-by-locale terminology and claim register so marketing copy, screenshots, and in-app text do not diverge.
- Test comprehension with representative users or qualified reviewers where risk or ambiguity is high.

Sources:
- Apple, *Localize app information*: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/localize-app-information
- Google Play Console Help, *Manage translations*: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/3125566
