# Domain Knowledge: Udemy Policies & Best Practices

## Generative AI Policies (2025 Updates)
Udemy maintains strict guidelines regarding the use of Generative AI in course creation:
- **Mandatory Disclosure**: Instructors must disclose the use of Generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Midjourney) in the creation of their course materials. A clear statement should be added to the course description: "This course contains content created with the assistance of AI tools."
- **Human-in-the-Loop Requirement**: Fully automated or purely AI-generated courses are prohibited. AI can be used as an assistant for outlining, scripting, and research, but a human must review, polish, and synthesize the content to ensure high educational quality and accuracy.
- **Visuals**: AI-generated visuals (slides, graphics, thumbnails) are acceptable provided they are high quality, relevant, and do not mislead students.
- **Quality Standards**: Low-quality AI outputs, hallucinatory content, or repetitive/fluff material generated by AI will result in course rejection or removal.

## Pricing Strategies (2025 Trends)
- Udemy frequently runs sitewide sales where most courses are discounted heavily (often to $10-$20).
- Course list prices (e.g., $99.99 or $199.99) are primarily psychological anchors to highlight the value of the discount.
- The pricing tiers generally depend on the course's depth, length (1-3 hours vs 10+ hours), and the instructor's reputation/social proof.

## SEO & Discoverability
- The course title and subtitle must contain the primary search keywords in the first 100 characters.
- Avoid keyword stuffing. Instead, align the language naturally with how a student would query the platform.
- High-quality, readable thumbnails featuring human faces tend to perform better in search results.
