# Research sources

Use these primary or official public sources to verify hiring, screening, and compensation guidance before making process claims. This skill provides recruiting practice education; it is not legal advice for a specific jurisdiction or employer.

## Fair hiring and screening

- U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission — prohibited employment policies and practices: https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices
- U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission — pre-employment inquiries and disability-related questions: https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/pre-employment-inquiries-and-disability
- U.S. Department of Labor — Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures overview: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ofccp/faqs/employee-selection-procedures

## Candidate experience and process design

- Society for Human Resource Management — interview and selection resources: https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/tools/toolkits/interviewing
- Society for Human Resource Management — recruiting and sourcing overview: https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/tools/toolkits/recruiting-internally-externally

## Compensation transparency and equity

- U.S. Department of Labor — Equal Pay Act overview: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/oasam/centers-offices/civil-rights-center/statutes/equal-pay-act
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — understanding job offers and compensation decisions should stay evidence-based rather than relying on prior salary history alone; confirm local salary-history bans before asking: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/

- Agent Skills specification: https://agentskills.io/specification
