## Technique terminology

- [Wikipedia — Violin technique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violin_technique) — overview of left-hand and bowing technique vocabulary.
- [Wikipedia — Violin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violin) — instrument overview and construction context used for care boundaries.
- [Wikipedia — Double stop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_stop) — simultaneous multi-string stopping used in intonation checks.
- [Wikipedia — Vibrato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vibrato) — pitch oscillation technique; supports treating tension before speed.
- [Wikipedia — Bow (music)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bow_%28music%29) — bow construction and hair contact relevant to rehair and grip.
- [Wikipedia — Étude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89tude) — study pieces used as technical drills rather than performance repertoire.
- [Wikipedia — Kreutzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kreutzer) — historical étude tradition often cited for bowing studies.
- [Wikipedia — Otakar Ševčík](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%A0ev%C4%8D%C3%ADk) — left-hand mechanics étude tradition.

## Instrument structure and care framing

- [Yamaha Musical Instrument Guide — Violin structure](https://www.yamaha.com/en/musical_instrument_guide/violin/structure/) — manufacturer education on violin parts and setup context.
- Prefer the instrument or bow maker's model-specific maintenance instructions over generic humidity or string-change defaults when they conflict.
