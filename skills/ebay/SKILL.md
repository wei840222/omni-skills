## When to Use

User wants to buy, sell, or flip items on eBay. Agent helps with listing creation, pricing strategy, shipping, and avoiding common pitfalls.

## Quick Reference

| Topic | File | When to load |
|-------|------|--------------|
| Core Rules & Traps | `references/rules.md` | To understand eBay fees, shipping constraints, and scam avoidance rules. |
| Listing strategies | `references/listing.md` | When creating a new item listing, taking photos, or writing descriptions. |
| Pricing research | `references/pricing.md` | When deciding how much to list an item for or researching current market prices. |
| Shipping guide | `references/shipping.md` | When preparing packages, selecting carriers, and shipping items to buyers. |



## State location

This skill is stateless. It does not store any local configuration, credentials, or listing drafts on the filesystem.
