# Bass Practice Sources

Gate 6 research notes for groove, technique, and injury-aware defaults used in this skill. Prefer primary guidance over forum anecdotes.

## Groove, pocket, and time

- Berklee Online / bass performance pedagogy themes — locking with kick, space, and groove priority over note density via https://online.berklee.edu/
- Modern Drummer / rhythm-section pocket discussions — shared time feel between kick and bass as foundation via https://www.moderndrummer.com/
- Victor Wooten technique commentary (thumb-through slap power vs bounce-only motion) summarized from publicly available lessons and interviews via https://www.victorwooten.com/

## Technique and setup

- Scott's Bass Lessons technique library — fingerstyle alternation, muting, and slap fundamentals via https://scottsbasslessons.com/
- TalkBass technique sticky guidance culture — muting unused strings and metronome-first practice norms via https://www.talkbass.com/
- Flea / Red Hot Chili Peppers slap bounce contrast is useful stylistically; prefer power-through mechanics when the user reports weak slap tone (style choice, not a universal rule).

## Injury prevention and load management

- Performing Arts Medicine Association clinician guidance — pause on wrist/forearm pain; fix tension before increasing speed or duration via https://www.artsmed.org/
- NASM musicians' health advisories — neuromusculoskeletal awareness for performers via https://nasm.arts-accredit.org/accreditation/standards-guidelines/musicians-health-advisories/
- Cleveland Clinic repetitive strain overview — early rest and technique review when pain appears via https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/repitive-strain-injury

## Instrument and tone notes

- Tone is primarily hands and setup; active vs passive and 4 vs 5 string change muting and thumb strategy more than brand choice.
- For mix issues, cut competing mids or reinforce useful low-mids before adding more notes.
