# Inventory research sources

Use these sources when advising on documentation quality for insurance, warranties, or theft recovery. Prefer the user's insurer policy language when it conflicts with general guidance.

## Home inventory and insurance documentation

- Insurance Information Institute, [How to create a home inventory](https://www.iii.org/article/how-create-home-inventory) — photograph rooms and belongings, list purchase price or approximate value, keep the inventory off-site or in the cloud, and update after major purchases.
- National Association of Insurance Commissioners, [Creating a home inventory](https://content.naic.org/consumer/creating-home-inventory.htm) — record make, model, serial numbers, and receipts; store copies separately from the home.
- FEMA, [Protect your property from disaster with a home inventory](https://www.fema.gov/press-release/20230425/protect-your-property-disaster-home-inventory) — maintain current inventories to support disaster claims and recovery.

## Practical application in this skill

- Prefer item files under `<state_root>/inventory/` with location, value, serial, warranty, and linked evidence.
- Generate insurance summaries from those files rather than inventing undocumented totals.
- Treat photos, receipts, and serial numbers as claim evidence, not optional decoration.
