# Sources

Use these pages to check a tú/usted label, a voseo claim, a regional marker, or a fixed expression. They describe common usage; they are not a license to invent slang, intimacy, or a regional voice the user did not ask for.

Live-checked on 2026-10-22.

## Notes on evidence boundaries

- Prefer Wikipedia / Wiktionary / CLDR over blog slang lists when locking tú/usted, voseo, or regional labels.
- Do not invent prices, legal form requirements, or "official" slang bans from memory; verify contested lexical claims against the sources above before stating them as fact.
