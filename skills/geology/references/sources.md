# Geology Sources

Load this file before citing chronostratigraphy, formal epoch status, seismic
or plate-tectonic context, or other time-sensitive geology claims. Prefer the
primary page or chart over memory.

## Authoritative sources (verified reachable in this handoff)

- **International Commission on Stratigraphy (ICS) chart hub** —
  https://stratigraphy.org/chart/ — entry point for the International
  Chronostratigraphic Chart.
- **ICS Chronostratigraphic Chart (2024-12 PDF)** —
  https://stratigraphy.org/ICSchart/ChronostratChart2024-12.pdf — formal
  chart artifact used for unit names and boundaries.
- **ICS home** — https://stratigraphy.org/ — commission context and chart
  updates.
- **USGS This Dynamic Earth** —
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/dynamic.html — agency educational overview
  of plate tectonics and related Earth dynamics.
- **USGS Earthquake Hazards Program** —
  https://earthquake.usgs.gov/ — earthquake and related hazards program hub
  (use for current program context; verify specific map/product pages before
  quoting numbers).
- **Nature news on Anthropocene epoch vote** —
  https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-024-00675-8 — reporting that
  geologists rejected the Anthropocene as a formal epoch after extended
  debate.

## Knowledge updates applied in this refactor

1. **Anthropocene status:** Treat Anthropocene as an active scientific and
   stratigraphic debate. Do not present it as a ratified ICS epoch; Nature’s
   reporting summarizes the rejection of formal epoch status after long
   debate. If a user needs formal units, point to the current ICS chart.
2. **Permian–Triassic boundary age:** High-precision U-Pb zircon work places
   the end-Permian boundary near ~251.9 Ma (commonly cited ~251.902 Ma in
   secondary summaries). Quote a primary paper or chart when the exact digit
   matters; do not invent a tighter precision than the cited source.
3. **Early plate tectonics:** Evidence that plate-tectonic-like recycling
   operated earlier in Earth history (Hadean/Archean discussions) is an active
   research area. Present it as model-dependent interpretation, not as a
   single settled start date.

## Claim hygiene

- Ages need method + uncertainty language.
- Formal chronostratigraphic names follow the current ICS chart, not informal
  media usage.
- Site-specific hazard or resource conclusions need local agency or regulatory
  sources beyond this general skill package.
