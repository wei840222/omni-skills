# CPA Exam Format

Official anchors: `research-sources.md`. Re-check live NASBA/AICPA/Board pages before asserting current policy.

## Core and Discipline Sections

| Section | Name | Duration | Content Focus |
|---------|------|----------|---------------|
| AUD | Auditing and Attestation (Core) | 4 hours | Audit procedures, ethics, reports |
| FAR | Financial Accounting & Reporting (Core) | 4 hours | GAAP, financial statements, transactions |
| REG | Taxation and Regulation (Core) | 4 hours | Tax law, business law, ethics |
| BAR | Business Analysis and Reporting (Discipline) | 4 hours | Advanced accounting, data analytics |
| ISC | Information Systems and Controls (Discipline) | 4 hours | IT governance, security, SOC engagements |
| TCP | Tax Compliance and Planning (Discipline) | 4 hours | Advanced tax strategies, entity planning |

*Note: In 2024, the CPA Evolution initiative replaced the traditional 4-part exam (which included BEC) with a Core + Discipline model. Candidates must pass all 3 Core sections (AUD, FAR, REG) and choose 1 of the 3 Discipline sections (BAR, ISC, TCP).*

## Question Types

### Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
- ~50% of score
- Adaptive difficulty (harder questions = more points)
- First testlet medium difficulty; second testlet adapts based on performance

### Task-Based Simulations (TBS)
- ~50% of score
- Real-world scenarios with research, calculations, written responses
- FAR and REG have more complex simulations



## Scoring

- **Passing score:** 75 (scale of 0-99)
- **Score release:** ~2-3 weeks after testing window closes
- **Score report:** Shows performance by topic area (weaker/comparable/stronger)

## Testing Windows

Continuous testing year-round at Prometric centers. No blackout dates anymore (changed in 2020).

## Credit Window (commonly 30 months)

**Critical:** All 4 sections (3 Core + 1 Discipline) must be passed inside the candidate's Board of Accountancy credit window. Many jurisdictions now use a 30-month rolling window after CPA Evolution; confirm the local Board rule before advising.

Example under a 30-month Board rule:
- Pass FAR: January 1, 2025
- Deadline to pass remaining 2 Cores and 1 Discipline: July 1, 2027
- If the remaining sections are not passed in time, FAR credit expires and must be retaken

## Notice to Schedule (NTS)

- Valid for 6 months after issuance
- Covers sections you applied for
- If unused, must reapply and repay fees
- **Apply only when you are ready to test within the 6-month NTS window**

## Fees (Approximate)

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Application (per section) | $50-100 |
| Exam fee (per section) | $200-250 |
| Ethics exam (some states) | $150-200 |
| License fee | $50-200 |

Total for all 4 sections: ~$1,500-2,500

## Content Blueprints

### FAR (most content-heavy)
- Conceptual Framework & Standards: 25-35%
- Financial Statement Accounts: 30-40%
- Transactions: 20-30%
- Government & NFP: 5-15%

### AUD
- Ethics, Independence: 15-25%
- Assessing Risk: 25-35%
- Performing Procedures: 30-40%
- Forming Conclusions: 10-20%

### REG
- Ethics & Responsibilities: 10-20%
- Business Law: 10-20%
- Federal Taxation: 55-85%

### Disciplines (Choose One)
- **BAR:** Data Analytics, Financial Risk, Advanced Accounting
- **ISC:** Information Security, IT Audits, SOC Engagements
- **TCP:** Advanced Entity Tax Planning, Personal Financial Planning
