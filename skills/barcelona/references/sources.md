# Sources — Barcelona

Official and primary references used to ground Barcelona guidance. Prefer these over secondary blogs when facts change. Re-check the linked page before quoting a live fee, deadline, eligibility rule, fare, or timetable.

## City and government

- Ajuntament de Barcelona (City of Barcelona): https://www.barcelona.cat/
- Generalitat de Catalunya: https://web.gencat.cat/
- Spanish government portal: https://www.lamoncloa.gob.es/lang/en/Paginas/index.aspx
- Mossos d'Esquadra (Catalan police): https://mossos.gencat.cat/
- Policia Nacional orientation: https://www.policia.es/

## Visas, residence, and work (informational)

- Ministerio de Inclusión, Seguridad Social y Migraciones: https://www.inclusion.gob.es/
- Extranjería / immigration procedures overview: https://www.exteriores.gob.es/en/ServiciosAlCiudadano/Paginas/Informacion-para-extranjeros.aspx
- EU freedom of movement overview: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/residence/index_en.htm
- Agencia Tributaria (tax): https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/

Treat visa, tax-residency, and work-authorization guidance as orientation only. Licensed immigration counsel owns filings.
