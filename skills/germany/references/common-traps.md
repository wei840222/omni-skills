# Common Traps

- Plan travel times realistically, factoring in transfer overhead between cities.
- Rely on public transit and rail for stays in major cities like Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, or Cologne.
- Advise users to carry cash for smaller restaurants, kiosks, or market stalls.
- Prepare a specific strategy for meals and essentials when arriving on a Sunday, as many shops are closed.
- Focus on one or two adjacent regions for short trips rather than combining distant areas.
- Treat Christmas markets, Oktoberfest, and major trade fairs as high-demand periods requiring early booking and careful planning.
