# Recent Domain Knowledge

## Deutschlandticket (D-Ticket)
The Deutschlandticket is a monthly ticket for local and regional public transport across Germany. Originally launched in May 2023 at €49 per month, the price increased to €58 in January 2025 and to €63 in January 2026. This ticket is a crucial factor when choosing between rail/transit and car rentals for longer trips or exploring metropolitan regions.

Sources:
- Deutsche Bahn Deutschlandticket overview — https://int.bahn.de/en/offers/regional/deutschland-ticket
- Germany Travel resource guide — https://www.germany.travel/en/information/resource-guide.html

## Entry/Exit System (EES)
The Entry/Exit System (EES) is an automatic electronic monitoring system implemented by the EU at Schengen borders. It became operational on 10 April 2026. It replaces manual passport stamping for non-EU/EFTA citizens and collects biometric data (fingerprints and facial recognition). Its rollout has caused significant wait times at some checkpoints and technical disruptions. Travellers must buffer extra time for airport and border crossings upon arrival/departure from Germany.

- ETIAS official portal — https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias_en
