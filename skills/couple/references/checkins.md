# Relationship Check-ins — Couple

## The State of the Union Meeting

Gottman Institute framework for a weekly 30-60 minute check-in.

### 1. Appreciation (10 mins)
Start by expressing specific gratitude for each other this week. What went well?
* "I noticed and appreciated when you..."
* "I loved it when we..."

### 2. What Went Right (10 mins)
Discuss the good things happening in the relationship. Acknowledge teamwork and positive moments.

### 3. What Went Wrong (20 mins)
Select one issue to discuss. The goal is understanding rather than immediately solving.
* **Speaker**: Use "I" statements ("I feel X about Y"). No blame.
* **Listener**: Take notes. Listen openly without defending. Ask "What do you mean by that?"
* Summarize and validate: "It makes sense that you feel this way because..."

### 4. What Do We Need? (10 mins)
How can we make each other feel loved this coming week?
* "What is one thing I can do to make you feel loved this week?"
* Focus on actionable, small behaviors.
