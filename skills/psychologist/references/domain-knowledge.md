# Psychological support domain knowledge

Load this reference when explaining why a listening move, Psychological First Aid step, or handoff boundary matters. It supports education and emotional first response, not diagnosis or clinical care.

## Psychological First Aid (Look, Listen, Link)

Psychological First Aid (PFA) is a brief, supportive response after distress or crisis exposure. Use it to reduce immediate distress and connect people to practical help, not to force trauma disclosure.

- **Look**: notice safety needs, urgent practical needs, and signs of severe distress without being intrusive.
- **Listen**: approach respectfully, ask about concerns, and listen without pressure or judgment.
- **Link**: connect the person with information, social support, and professional services when needed.

Sources:

- [WHO — Psychological first aid: Guide for field workers](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241548205)
- [IFRC — Psychological First Aid](https://www.ifrc.org/document/psychological-first-aid)

## Active listening and validation

Active listening prioritizes reflection, naming emotions, and open questions before advice. Validation lowers defensiveness and helps the person stay regulated enough to choose a next step.

Sources:

- [American Psychological Association — Listening](https://www.apa.org/topics/listening)
- [SAMHSA — Coping With Stress During Infectious Disease Outbreaks](https://store.samhsa.gov/product/coping-stress-during-infectious-disease-outbreaks/sma14-4885) (supportive listening and adaptive coping framing)

## Crisis boundaries

Expressions of hopelessness, self-harm, or intent to harm others require direct, calm safety inquiry and local crisis resources. Persistent trauma, severe depression, or clinical needs belong with licensed professionals and real-world support networks.

Sources:

- [National Institute of Mental Health — Suicide Prevention](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/suicide-prevention)
- [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/)

## Scope boundary

This skill provides supportive conversation and PFA-style linking. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, guarantee confidentiality, or replace licensed therapy.
