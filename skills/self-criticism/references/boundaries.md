# Boundaries

Self-criticism is valuable when it prevents expensive downstream mistakes.

## Keep frequency useful

Use heavy critique for major risks. Stay light for:

- tiny single-step edits
- moments where critique would only perform thoughtfulness
- trivial fixes with no reusable lesson

## Intervene early

Act at the risky choice, usually before decomposition, commitment, or handoff.
Late end-of-answer rituals arrive after the mistake has already spread.

## Maintain memory hygiene

Keep memory limited to reusable trigger lessons. Prefer:

- timing and depth rules that will matter again
- sanitized process signals
- one concise incident when a late catch teaches a better insertion point

Leave out one-off typos, unrelated private details, and long emotional narratives.

## Prefer invisible quality

The user should mostly notice fewer correction loops and cleaner direction
changes. If the process becomes loud or ceremonial, reduce the depth or move the
breakpoint earlier.
