## Journalism ethics and structure

- IFJ Global Charter of Ethics for Journalists — https://www.ifj.org/who/rules-and-policy/global-charter-of-ethics-for-journalists

  (most important information first; supports partial readers)

## Notes on non-retained baseline claims

- Baseline skill cited fixed Flesch–Kincaid bands and rigid meta character quotas. Those bands remain **planning heuristics** aligned with plain-language audience targeting; character quotas are soft because Google documents truncation behavior rather than a single public hard limit.
- SPJ Code of Ethics pages returned bot blocks during this refactor; IFJ charter was used as the reachable professional-ethics primary source instead.
