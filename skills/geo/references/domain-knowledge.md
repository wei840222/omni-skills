# GEO Domain Knowledge

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) has evolved from traditional SEO. Peer-reviewed and practitioner research highlights specific strategies that influence how LLMs recommend and cite brands.

## Key Research Findings

According to the Princeton / IIT Delhi / Georgia Tech / Allen Institute GEO study and follow-on practitioner guidance:

- **Cite-and-Verify Mechanisms**: Retrieval-augmented and citation-forward systems (for example Perplexity and Bing Chat) explicitly surface sources. Presence in authoritative, citable corpora increases the chance of recommendation and attribution.
- **Quotation Addition**: Adding specific quotes or statistics can improve visibility / citation likelihood by roughly 30–40% in controlled GEO evaluations.
- **Fluency Optimization**: Grammatically precise, logically structured prose is easier for LLMs to parse and weigh heavily.
- **Keyword Stuffing Penalty**: Unlike older SEO tactics, keyword stuffing can actively harm GEO by lowering semantic density and fluency, causing models to downgrade reliability.

## Strategic Shifts

- Move from optimizing only for search crawlers to optimizing for semantic ingestion and citation.
- Prefer high-quality, dense information over long, repetitive articles.
- Treat discussion platforms, technical docs, and independent press as training / retrieval surfaces, not just traffic channels.
