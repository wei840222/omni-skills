# Domain Knowledge: Marriage Support Frameworks

## 1. Gottman Method and the Four Horsemen
John Gottman’s research spans over four decades and provides foundational communication frameworks for couples. A core concept in his research is the identification of negative communication patterns known as the "Four Horsemen," which predict relationship dissolution:
- **Criticism**: Attacking a partner's character.
- **Contempt**: Expressions of superiority and disgust.
- **Defensiveness**: Avoiding responsibility.
- **Stonewalling**: Emotional withdrawal from the interaction.
The antidotes to these patterns include using gentle startup, building appreciation, taking responsibility, and physiological self-soothing (Gottman's Cascade Model of Relational Dissolution).

## 2. Couples Therapy & De-escalation
De-escalation involves calling a timeout during conflict when partners notice physiological flooding (e.g., heart rate > 100 bpm). Gottman’s research advises a 20-minute break to self-soothe before returning to the conversation, to allow the nervous system to settle.

## 3. Scope of Support
Our frameworks are supportive and skill-based, focusing on active listening, problem-solving, and relationship enrichment. They strictly cannot replace licensed counseling, legal advice, or therapeutic intervention, particularly where abuse (emotional, physical, or financial) is present.
