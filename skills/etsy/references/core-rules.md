# Core Rules - Etsy

## 1. Lock context before recommendations

Confirm the listing category, target buyer, shipping region, production model, and current goal before proposing changes.
Without scope, Etsy advice becomes vague and low impact.

## 2. Build keyword clusters, not isolated tags

Create one primary intent cluster and two supporting clusters.
Map those terms across title opening words, tags, and first description lines without repeating identical phrasing.

Etsy search still rewards clear buyer language in the title opening and tags, but cluster design must stay honest: no stuffing, no trademarked brand hijacks, no attributes the product does not have. Prefer exact buyer phrases over high-volume generic terms that pull the wrong shopper.

## 3. Optimize the full listing stack together

Treat title, images, offer clarity, description opening, and shipping promise as one system.
Ensure photos and value proposition are optimized before adjusting tags alone.

## 4. Protect unit economics before growth tactics

Estimate a conservative contribution margin before recommending discount campaigns, sale events, or paid traffic.

Include at minimum:

- Etsy listing and transaction fees (re-check current schedule in `references/sources.md` before quoting numbers)
- payment processing fees
- packaging and shipping cost (or digital delivery cost)
- expected ad / offsite-ads spend if used

Never recommend a steep discount or paid boost when post-fee margin is unknown. If the user cannot supply costs, give a margin worksheet and stop short of a go/no-go on paid growth.

## 5. Run controlled experiments with traceable metrics

Change one major variable per cycle and measure:

- views / impressions
- favorites
- add-to-cart (or equivalent intent signal)
- conversion rate
- revenue per visit when available

Default window: **7 to 14 days**, unless traffic is high enough that a shorter window still yields a fair read. Do not rewrite the title daily; that destroys the baseline.

## 6. Enforce policy and trust constraints

Exclude trademarked terms, unverifiable claims, and misleading delivery promises.
When compliance risk exists, state it explicitly and provide a safer alternative.
Do not coach users to evade Etsy seller policies, IP rules, or prohibited-item rules.

## Etsy growth traps

- Chasing high-volume keywords without buyer fit → more impressions, weaker conversion.
- Rewriting titles every day → unstable learning and no reliable baseline.
- Pricing from competitor screenshots alone → hidden fee structure and margin collapse.
- Ignoring listing photos while tweaking tags → search traffic may improve but sales stay flat.
- Scaling ads before listing fundamentals → expensive traffic with low purchase intent.
- Weekend flash sales without unit-economics check → temporary rank noise, lasting margin damage.
