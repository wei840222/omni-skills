# Sources — personal-trainer skill

Gate 6 research anchors for coaching defaults. Prefer primary reviews; re-verify before citing version-specific numbers. Quantified load math still routes to the `fitness` skill.

## Resistance training volume and progression

- [Schoenfeld BJ, Ogborn D, Krieger JW. Dose-response relationship between weekly resistance training volume and increases in muscle mass (2017)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27433992/) — weekly set volume corridor context for hypertrophy coaching
- [ACSM position stand: Progression models in resistance training for healthy adults (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19204579/) — beginner → intermediate progression framing and recovery spacing

## Protein and recovery context

- [Morton RW et al. Protein supplementation during resistance training (2018)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28698222/) — ~1.6–2.2 g/kg/day protein support for adaptation (coaching default, not meal planning)

## Pain, DOMS, and medical boundaries

- [ACSM guidelines overview / exercise preparticipation](https://www.acsm.org/education-resources/trending-topics-resources/physical-activity-guidelines) — general healthy-adult activity framing; persistent pain or medical red flags remain clinician territory
