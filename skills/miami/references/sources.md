# Sources — Miami

Use these primary sources before treating rents, insurance, hurricane status, transit fares, or safety conditions as decisive facts. Dated skill figures are planning estimates only.

## Official local government

- City of Miami: https://www.miamigov.com/
- Miami-Dade County: https://www.miamidade.gov/
- Miami Beach official site: https://www.miamibeachfl.gov/
- Miami-Dade Emergency Management / hurricane readiness: https://www.miamidade.gov/global/emergency/home.page
- National Hurricane Center: https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/
