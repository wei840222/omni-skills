## Verified claim extracts (2026-10-09 retrieval)

Retrieved via alternate reader when direct Migri HTML returned HTTP 403. Prefer re-opening the live pages above before any filing decision.

### EU Blue Card (`https://migri.fi/en/eu-blue-card`)

- Gross salary at least **EUR 3,937 per month in 2026** (fringe benefits not counted toward the floor).
- Confirmed employment required; work must last **at least six months** (not a fixed one-year contract floor).
- Higher education degree of at least 3 years **or** higher professional skills (typically 5+ years comparable experience).
- First permit electronic fee **530 €** / paper **630 €**; extended electronic **230 €** / paper **430 €** (as shown on the Blue Card page).
- Card may be issued up to 2 years; shorter work → duration of employment + 3 months.

### Specialist (`https://migri.fi/en/specialist`)

- Expert duties requiring special expertise; higher education degree or equivalent special expertise via experience/education.
- Gross salary at least **EUR 3,937 per month in 2026**.
- Same first-permit fee orientation as Blue Card page: electronic **530 €** / paper **630 €**.
- First permit max 2 years, or duration of employment if shorter.

### Student funds (`https://migri.fi/en/income-requirement-for-students`)

- At least **EUR 800 per month** living funds orientation; **EUR 9,600** in account for studies of 1 year or longer (grants may reduce the cash remainder).
- Lower monthly floors when institution provides free accommodation / meals (see live page).

### Student work rights (`https://migri.fi/en/residence-permit-application-for-studies`)

- Paid work average **30 hours per week** (not 25); hours may vary by week if the yearly average stays within the limit.

### Processing fee table 2026 (`https://migri.fi/en/processing-fees-and-payment-methods`)

- First residence permit, no labour market testing group (includes **specialist** and **specialist, EU Blue Card**): paper **630** / electronic **530**.
- First residence permit for studies: paper **750** / electronic **600**.
- Startup / other entrepreneur rows are separate on the decree table — open the live table before quoting.

3. HSL zone fares move; prefer the live HSL page over memory when the user is buying a multi-day or monthly product.
