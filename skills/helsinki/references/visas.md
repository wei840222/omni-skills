# Finland Visas and Residence Permits

> **Freshness:** Permit floors, fees, and work rights change by decree year. Before any filing or non-refundable booking, open `references/sources.md` and the live Migri page for that permit. Package numbers are orientation from 2026-10-09 retrievals, not legal advice.

## Who Needs What

| Status | Short Visit | Living/Working |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| EU/EEA/Swiss | No visa | Register right of residence after longer stay (DVV) |
| Schengen visa nationality | Yes (if required) | Residence permit |
| Visa-free nationality | No visa for short stay (typically 90/180) | Residence permit for longer stay / work |
| Other | Yes | Residence permit |

Confirm short-stay visa need by nationality: https://um.fi/visa-requirement

## EU/EEA Citizens

### Rights
- No visa needed for entry
- Can work and study under free-movement rules
- Longer stays still need registration of residence rights

### Registration
After the short free-movement period, register right of residence:
- At DVV
- Bring ID plus proof of employment, study, or funds as required

### Family Members
Non-EU family may need a residence card based on the EU citizen's rights. Apply via Migri / Enter Finland and re-check current process pages.

## Non-EU Citizens

### Short Visits (Under 90 Days)

Some nationalities need a Schengen visa; others are visa-free for short stays. Tourism entry is **not** a work permit.

### Residence Permits

Required for stays beyond the short-visit rules and for most work.

Common tracks:
- Work-based (specialist, EU Blue Card, other employment, ICT, …)
- Study-based
- Family-based
- Startup entrepreneur
- Other (research, etc.)

## Work-Based Permits

### Specialist Residence Permit

**Most common for tech workers.** Live page: https://migri.fi/en/specialist

**Requirements (Migri page retrieved 2026-10-09):**
- Coming to work in expert duties that require special expertise
- Higher education degree **or** special expertise acquired through work experience / other education (employer verifies expertise when needed)
- Gross salary at least **EUR 3,937 per month in 2026**
- Employer supplements the application with terms of employment (Enter Finland for Employers or paper form)

**Process:**
1. Applicant submits via Enter Finland (preferred) or paper
2. Employer adds terms of employment promptly
3. Migri decides (automation is faster when the employer uses Enter Finland for Employers)

Do **not** invent a TE labour-market test on the specialist track described on the Migri specialist page. Other employment permits may differ — open the matching Migri page.

**Validity orientation:** First permit maximum 2 years, or duration of employment if shorter.

### EU Blue Card

**Highly qualified employment.** Live page: https://migri.fi/en/eu-blue-card

**Requirements (Migri page retrieved 2026-10-09):**
- Confirmed highly qualified employment before applying
- Employment lasting **at least six months**
- Higher education degree of at least 3 years **or** higher professional skills (typically 5+ years comparable experience)
- Gross salary at least **EUR 3,937 per month in 2026** (fringe benefits not counted toward this floor)

**Benefits orientation:**
- EU mobility rules under the Blue Card framework
- Family path per current Migri rules
- Issued up to 2 years; if work is shorter than 2 years, duration of employment + 3 months

**Timeline:** Use Migri processing-times pages; do not treat a fixed “1–3 months” as a guarantee.

### Intra-Company Transfer (ICT)

For transfers within multinational companies (manager, specialist, or trainee). Confirm employment history and maximum validity on the live Migri ICT pages before advising.

## Startup Permit

**For entrepreneurs starting a business in Finland.**

### Requirements orientation
- Innovative business idea evaluated by Business Finland
- Sufficient funds and business plan
- Then residence permit application at Migri

### Process orientation
1. Business Finland evaluation
2. Positive evaluation
3. Migri / Enter Finland application
4. Decision

Open https://migri.fi/en/start-up-entrepreneur and Business Finland pages before quoting funds, validity, or work rights.

## Student Permits

Live orientation: https://migri.fi/en/residence-permit-application-for-studies and https://migri.fi/en/income-requirement-for-students

### Requirements
- Acceptance to a Finnish educational institution
- Health insurance per Migri rules (Kela / EHIC / GHIC may satisfy)
- Living funds: at least **EUR 800 per month**; for studies of **1 year or longer**, typically **EUR 9,600** available when applying (grants may reduce the cash remainder; free accommodation/meals lower the floor — see live page)
- Tuition paid or funds for tuition when applicable

### Process
1. Acceptance letter
2. Apply via Enter Finland / Migri
3. Processing per live Migri times

### Work rights (retrieved 2026-10-09)
- Average **30 hours per week** paid work in any field; weekly hours may vary if the yearly average stays within the limit
- Studies must still progress at a normal pace

### After studies
Post-study job-seeking / switch-to-work paths exist and change — open the current Migri student and job-seeking permit pages before quoting durations (do not hard-code “2 years” without a live check).

## Family Permits

Types include spouse of a Finnish citizen, spouse of a residence-permit holder, children, and some parent cases. Requirements (relationship proof, income, housing) vary by category. Processing can be long — use live Migri family pages and fee tables.

## Application Process

### Where to apply
- **Outside Finland:** Finnish mission / VFS where applicable, and Enter Finland online
- **Inside Finland:** only when legally present; Enter Finland and Migri service points

### Enter Finland
https://enterfinland.fi/eServices — create account, submit, upload, pay, track. Employer terms of employment often complete the file after submission.

### Typical documents
| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Passport | Validity beyond stay as required |
| Photo | Recent, passport-style; often ≤ 6 months |
| Application | Online or paper form for the permit type |
| Employment contract / terms | Work tracks |
| Degree / expertise evidence | Specialist / Blue Card |
| CV | Work history |
| Insurance | When required |
| Proof of funds | Students and some other tracks |
| Fee | Per decree table |

## Processing Times

Treat any fixed month range as historical orientation only. Prefer https://migri.fi/en/check-the-processing-time-of-your-application and category processing-times pages.

Factors: completeness, employer speed on terms of employment, background checks, current workload, and whether the track uses automation.

## Living in Finland During Application

### Can you stay?
Depends on where you applied and current legal status. Do not advise overstaying. Prefer Migri guidance for the user's exact status.

### First permit vs extended
Extended-permit rules and fees differ from first permits. Apply before expiry when renewing; confirm whether presence is allowed while pending on the live page for that permit.

## Path to Permanent Residence and Citizenship

Continuous residence, language, income, and clean-record rules change. Open Migri permanent-residence and citizenship pages before quoting year counts or language levels. Dual citizenship rules also need a live check.

## Practical Tips

### Speed and quality
- Prefer complete online applications
- Have the employer ready to file terms of employment immediately
- Use official fast-track pages only when the user actually qualifies
- Answer Migri requests quickly

### Common failure modes
- Wrong permit type for the job
- Stale salary-floor or fee numbers from blogs
- Missing authentication of foreign documents
- Insufficient student funds proof
- Late extended-permit filing

### After arrival (orientation)
1. Register residence / identity as required (DVV)
2. Address registration
3. Kela where eligible
4. Bank account and tax card (Vero) as needed
