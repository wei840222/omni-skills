# Current United States Sources

Load this reference only when a request needs a current United States tax, credit, investor-protection, or trading-mechanics fact. These sources are examples for U.S. questions; obtain the applicable authority for every other jurisdiction.

Verified 2026-08-08. Check the linked page's publication or update date before relying on a changing number, rule, product term, or threshold.

## Tax and investment-tax rules

- **IRS — Federal income tax rates and brackets**
  - URL: https://www.irs.gov/filing/federal-income-tax-rates-and-brackets
  - Use for: current U.S. federal income-tax brackets by tax year and filing status.
  - Key point: tax brackets apply marginally; the page separates 2025 tables from the linked 2026 adjustments. Confirm the tax year and filing status before quoting a threshold.
- **IRS — Topic no. 409, Capital gains and losses**
  - URL: https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409
  - Use for: U.S. capital-gain/loss basics, holding-period distinctions, and links to controlling publications.
  - Key point: classification and rates depend on facts including holding period and taxable income; use the linked current materials for exceptions.
- **IRS — Publication 550, Investment Income and Expenses**
  - URL: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550
  - Use for: U.S. investment-income topics, including wash-sale rules.
  - Key point: obtain transaction dates, security details, and jurisdiction before applying a tax rule to a user.

## Credit and consumer protection

- **myFICO — What's in my FICO Scores?**
  - URL: https://www.myfico.com/credit-education/whats-in-your-credit-score
  - Use for: the general FICO Score category weights and the caveat that importance differs by profile.
  - Key point: the five general categories are payment history, amounts owed, length of credit history, new credit, and credit mix; the displayed percentages are general-population weights rather than an individual's exact score calculation.
- **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — Credit cards**
  - URL: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/credit-cards/
  - Use for: U.S. credit-card interest, terms, disputes, and consumer-protection questions.
  - Key point: issuer terms and daily interest calculations determine an actual charge; use the statement and applicable CFPB guidance rather than a market-average APR.

## Investing and order mechanics

- **SEC Investor.gov — Introduction to Investing**
  - URL: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing
  - Use for: savings versus investing, horizon, diversification, and investment-risk education.
  - Key point: investments involve risk and do not have a fixed rate of return; label illustrations with their assumptions.
- **SEC — Investor Bulletin: Trading Basics**
  - URL: https://www.sec.gov/investor/pubs/trading101basics.pdf
  - Use for: U.S. market, limit, stop, and stop-limit order mechanics.
  - Key point: a stop price triggers a market order and is not a guaranteed execution price; a limit condition can prevent execution. Confirm a brokerage's available order types and policies.

## Source-use recovery

If an authoritative source is unavailable or conflicts with another source, provide the stable concept, cite the available material, and leave the current legal or numeric conclusion conditional until the applicable regulator, issuer, or original study is resolved.
