## Consumer contracts, deposits, and refunds

- **Federal Trade Commission (FTC)** — consumer protection home; start here before generalizing deposit/refund advice.
  - https://www.ftc.gov/
- **FTC Consumer site** — shopping and consumer-rights guidance.
  - https://consumer.ftc.gov/
- **FTC Consumer — Shopping and donating** — consumer purchase framing when evaluating large vendor commitments.
  - https://consumer.ftc.gov/shopping-and-donating
- **USA.gov** — official US government consumer and family-services entry points (jurisdiction still must be named).
  - https://www.usa.gov/

## Contingency and day-of resilience

- **Ready.gov — Make a plan** — household emergency planning patterns useful for weather, venue, and travel contingencies (adapt; not wedding-specific law).
  - https://www.ready.gov/plan
- **Ready.gov — Be informed** — hazard awareness inputs for outdoor or multi-site events.
  - https://www.ready.gov/be-informed

## Culture and etiquette boundaries

Etiquette, gift norms, and ceremony roles are culture- and family-specific. Do not universalize US-centric guest, gift, or seating norms. When the user names a culture or jurisdiction, prefer that community's current guidance and mark every assumption explicitly. For international travel components, open the relevant government travel advisory pages in-session rather than caching stale country lists here.
