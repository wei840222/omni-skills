# Official Sources & Platform Specifications

This document records the official documentation and platform guidelines for Suno music generation workflows.

## Official Platform Resources

- **Suno Official Portal**: [https://suno.com/](https://suno.com/)
  - Explore page, model release announcements, and web generation workspace.
- **Suno Help Center**: [https://help.suno.com/](https://help.suno.com/)
  - FAQ covering subscription tiers (Free, Pro, Premier), commercial rights ownership, and credit usage policies.

## Generation Conventions & Model Capabilities

- **Model Versions**:
  - v3.5 / v4: Supports up to 4-minute full song generations in a single prompt, enhanced vocal clarity, and advanced song structure adherence.
  - v3: Baseline model with 2-minute initial clip generation and extension chaining.
- **Structural Meta Tags**:
  - Valid functional tags recognized by the audio parser: `[Intro]`, `[Verse]`, `[Pre-Chorus]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, `[Guitar Solo]`, `[Outro]`, `[End]`.
- **Audio Deliverables**:
  - Standard download format: MP3 (192-320 kbps).
  - High-resolution lossless WAV downloads and stem separation (vocals/instrumentals) available on paid plans.
