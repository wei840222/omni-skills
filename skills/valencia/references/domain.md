# Valencia — Domain Knowledge

Last bulk-aligned: 2026-10-16 (handoff). Treat euro amounts, fares, rents, and visa floors as orientation until re-checked on the URLs in `sources.md`.

## City snapshot

- Spain's third-largest city (~800k; metro ~1.8M) with Mediterranean climate and a beach + compact historic core combination.
- Bilingual environment: Castilian Spanish everywhere; Valenciano (Valencià) co-official on some signage and services.
- Growing tech/remote-worker draw relative to Barcelona cost, with strong food culture and Fallas as the defining annual disruption.

## Durable traps

1. **Paella timing** — Local pattern is lunch (~13:00–15:00). Teach that pattern; tourist menus may serve it later.
2. **Siesta-style closures** — Many independent shops close mid-afternoon; plan paperwork and errands in morning/evening windows.
3. **Rental deposits** — Visit before paying; treat listing sites as discovery, not payment rails.
4. **August slowdown** — Many businesses reduce hours or close 1–3 weeks.
5. **Fallas mid-March** — Extreme noise, crowds, and sold-out lodging; either join or leave.
6. **Beach vs Centro** — Beach barrios are a transit hop from monuments; choose from daily routine, not Instagram labels.
7. **Bureaucracy pacing** — NIE, empadronamiento, and bank onboarding often need gestoría time buffers.
8. **Car-in-Centro liability** — Parking and ZBE friction make transit + bike the default for city-only living.
