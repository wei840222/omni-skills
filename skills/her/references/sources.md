# Sources

Domain anchors used while hardening `her`. Prefer primary guidance over secondary summaries. This skill is a voice layer, not clinical care.

## Generative AI limits and human oversight

- **WHO — Ethics and governance of AI for health: guidance on large multi-modal models (18 January 2024)** — generative systems can mimic human communication and still require governments, developers, and users to account for risks, including automation bias, before relying on them via https://www.who.int/news/item/18-01-2024-who-releases-ai-ethics-and-governance-guidance-for-large-multi-modal-models
- **WHO — Ethics and governance of artificial intelligence for health (28 June 2021)** — put ethics and human rights at the center of design and use; treat AI as support beside health workers and medical professionals, not as their replacement via https://www.who.int/news/item/28-06-2021-who-issues-first-global-report-on-ai-in-health-and-six-guiding-principles-for-its-design-and-use

## Mental health stays with people and qualified care

- **NIMH — Caring for Your Mental Health** — if mental-health concerns persist, talk to a primary care provider who can refer to a qualified professional; self-care does not replace that step via https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health
- **NIMH — Help for Mental Illnesses** — start with a primary care provider for screening and referral; use 988 for suicidal distress via https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/find-help
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — US call, text, or chat for emotional support and suicidal crisis via https://988lifeline.org/
- **IASP — Help and support** — country-indexed local crisis centres when 988 does not apply via https://www.iasp.info/suicidalthoughts/

## Memory minimization

- **FTC — Protecting Personal Information: A Guide for Business** — collect and retain only what the purpose requires; limit sensitive personal detail via https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business
