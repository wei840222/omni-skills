# Research sources (Gate 6)

Full URLs used while verifying OKR craft, scoring conventions, and adjacent-system contrasts. Prefer these over model memory for attributions.

## Primary OKR lineage and practice

- **Intel / Andy Grove — High Output Management (overview)** — factory/measurement mindset that later OKR practice draws from — https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/history/historic-contributions.html
- **John Doerr — Measure What Matters (site)** — popularized OKRs; commit vs aspirational framing and Google adoption narrative — https://www.whatmatters.com/
- **What Matters — OKR scoring and the 0.7 aspirational convention** — 0–1 scoring; aspirational success near 0.7 rather than forced 1.0 — https://www.whatmatters.com/faqs/how-to-grade-okrs-with-the-google-system-and-the-simpler-way
- **What Matters — OKRs and compensation** — guidance against tightly coupling stretch OKRs to bonuses — https://www.whatmatters.com/faqs/okrs-and-compensation
- **Google re:Work — Guide to OKRs** — public Google/re:Work OKR guide (set fewer goals, grade transparently, separate from performance bonuses) — https://rework.withgoogle.com/intl/en/guides/set-goals-with-okrs/

## Cadence, alignment, and failure modes

- **What Matters — How to write OKRs** — qualitative objectives vs measurable key results; avoid task lists as KRs — https://www.whatmatters.com/faqs/how-to-write-okrs-examples
- **What Matters — OKR examples and anti-patterns** — common failure modes (too many OKRs, output metrics, set-and-forget) — https://www.whatmatters.com/articles/how-to-write-effective-okrs
- **Harvard Business Review — Having Trouble with Your Strategy? Then Map It (Kaplan/Norton context)** — strategy maps / measurement alignment adjacent to KPI vs initiative confusion — https://hbr.org/2000/09/having-trouble-with-your-strategy-then-map-it

## Adjacent systems (contrast, not substitutes)

- **SMART goals (University of California performance toolkit explainer)** — goal hygiene checklist, not a scored quarterly system — https://www.ucop.edu/local-human-resources/_files/performance-appraisal/How%20to%20write%20SMART%20Goals%20v2.pdf
- **EOS Worldwide — Rocks** — quarterly priorities in EOS; often commit-oriented and less numerically scored than OKRs — https://www.eosworldwide.com/blog/rocks-eos
- **Salesforce V2MOM overview** — Vision, Values, Methods, Obstacles, Measures narrative alignment tool — https://www.salesforce.com/blog/how-to-create-alignment-within-your-company/

## Notes on claim classes

- Scoring thresholds (0.7 green for aspirational, 0–1 scale) are **convention from OKR practice literature**, not a universal ISO metric.
- Comp-decoupling is a **recommended default for stretch culture**; some enterprises intentionally couple commit goals to review — label the trade-off rather than inventing a single law.
- Historical Intel/Google attributions should stay high-level unless quoting a specific edition page; link primary public guides above when pressed for sources.
