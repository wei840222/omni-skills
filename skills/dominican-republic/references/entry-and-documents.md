# Entry and Documents

## What To Confirm First

- Whether the traveler is visa-free or needs a visa from Dominican foreign affairs.
- Whether arrival is by commercial flight, because the electronic entry-and-exit form applies there.
- Passport-validity rule for the traveler's nationality, including the temporary tourist exception.
- A round-trip air ticket for foreign air passengers who are not Dominican nationals or residents.
- Stay length relative to 30 days, because a longer stay can add a departure fee.

## Core Rules

- Commercial airline passengers, foreign and Dominican, complete the official free e-ticket before presenting documents at the airline counter, inbound and outbound. Private flights, non-commercial vessels, ferries, and cruise ships use the physical forms instead.
- The 72-hour lead time is gone. Fill the e-ticket once flight details exist, even months ahead, and finish it before the airport counter. A family of up to seven can share one form and one QR; more than seven needs another form. Customs is adults only.
- Foreign air passengers need a round-trip ticket into and out of the Dominican Republic. Dominican nationals and foreigners residing in the Dominican Republic are exempt. Airlines enforce this before boarding.
- The baseline tourist passport rule is six months of validity. Through 31 December 2026, nationals of the European Union, the United Kingdom, Canada, the United States, Brazil, Chile, Argentina, Colombia, and Ecuador may enter for tourism on a passport that stays valid through their stay and departure. Everyone else still follows the six-month rule unless a live official page says otherwise.
- A visa applicant's passport still needs at least six months of validity. Travelers who hold a valid United States, Canada, United Kingdom, or Schengen visa, or legal residence in one of those places, can be admitted without a separate Dominican tourist visa. Confirm the traveler's nationality on the live foreign-ministry page before non-refundable bookings.
- Stays past 30 days can add a sliding departure fee based on total length of stay. Pay it online before departure or at airport immigration after check-in. The separate US$20 departure tax is already inside the airline fare.
- COVID vaccination cards, PCR tests, and antigen tests are not routine entry requirements. Do not revive them as a checklist.

## Practical Planning Advice

- Save a PDF or screenshot of the e-ticket QR, plus the application code, before reaching the airport. Use the code to edit the form later.
- Keep passport, hotel address (province, municipality, and sector for a rental), and the return ticket easy to show offline.
- If the stay may pass 30 days, budget the extra stay fee and open the live migration fee page before quoting an amount.
- If the user has a complex routing pattern, frame the entry step as a travel-day task, not an afterthought.

## Safe Output Pattern

For entry questions, answer in this order:
1. Whether this looks like a standard tourist pathway or a visa-check case
2. E-ticket requirement for commercial air travel, including family-form limits
3. Passport rule for that nationality, including whether the 2026 tourist exception applies
4. Round-trip ticket and the 30-day stay-fee boundary
5. Official source to re-check before payment
