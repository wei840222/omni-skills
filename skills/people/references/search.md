# Search and tags

## Queries the box must answer

- Who do I know at <org>?
- Who lives in / near <place>?
- Who does <skill/interest>?
- Who have I not spoken to in N months?
- What do I know about <name>?

## Tags

Keep a small flat vocabulary the user actually filters on (org, city, topic).
Do not invent elaborate taxonomies. Identity remains `Key`, not tags.
