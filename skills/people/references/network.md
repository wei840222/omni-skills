# Network and weak ties

Weak ties matter for information and opportunities; this box still is not a CRM.

## Do

- Keep one context line and last contact for orbit ties
- Track open favors/intros as open loops until closed
- Separate reconnect from ask by at least one exchange after long silence

## Do not

- Turn reciprocity into a running scoreboard
- Pipeline-stage people who are not commercial deals (use `crm`)
- Automate outreach sequences from this skill
