# Interactions

## What counts as contact

Counts: meeting, call, meaningful text/email thread, long voice note exchange.
Does not count: thinking about them, seeing a social post, liking a photo.

## Log shape

One line per real interaction:

`YYYY-MM-DD | <name> | <channel> | <one changed thing> | next:<optional>`

Update the contacts row `Last contact` to that date in the same turn.
Append longer notes only on the person file when the line would exceed ~160 characters.
