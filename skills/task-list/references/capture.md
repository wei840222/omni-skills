# Capture and clarification

## Capture first

Accept raw language such as “remind me,” “I need to,” “waiting on,” “someday,” and “not this week.” Split a brain dump into separate Inbox records, preserving the original wording until clarification changes execution.

## Clarify only material ambiguity

Ask one concise question when a date might mean due versus start, an outcome needs a project, a task may be waiting on someone else, or an edit would complete, delete, or regenerate a record. Infer an explicit date, obvious waiting language, an existing project name, or a clear Someday instruction.

## Title rule

Use a visible action that begins with a verb and can advance in one focused block. Keep context in the task note when shortening the title would lose meaning.

Examples:

- “Need to send Marta the draft by Friday” → `Send draft to Marta` with a due date.
- “Waiting on legal before I can ship contract” → Waiting record with owner, blocker, and a follow-up date if known.
- “Someday redesign landing page” → Someday, not Today.
