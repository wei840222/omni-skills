# Sources — Paris

Back to [Paris Skill Overview](../SKILL.md).

Use these verified primary sources when transit ticketing, administrative procedures, district security, or cultural etiquette apply in Paris.

## Municipal Governance & District Orientation

- **Ville de Paris (Official City Portal)** — https://www.paris.fr/
  - Municipal portal for Paris administration, district mairie services, local bylaws, cycling networks (Plan Vélo), and public green spaces.

## Public Administration & Housing Aid (CAF)

- **Service-Public France** — https://www.service-public.fr/
  - Official national portal for public administrative rights and procedures. Details residency formal declaration, tenant rights (bail d'habitation, dépôt de garantie), and CAF housing allowances (APL - Aide Personnalisée au Logement).

## National Rail & Regional Transport

- **SNCF (Société Nationale des Chemins de fer Français)** — https://www.sncf.com/en
  - National rail operator running high-speed TGV, Intercités, and Transilien regional suburban lines connecting Paris to outer Île-de-France and international hubs.

## Tourism & Visitor Guidance

- **France.fr (Atout France Official Tourism Portal)** — https://www.france.fr/en
  - National agency for tourism development offering cultural guidelines, museum opening conventions, safety advisories, and hospitality etiquette (e.g., standard polite greetings: *Bonjour*, *Merci*, *Au revoir*).
