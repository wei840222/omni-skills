# Owner pay

Load when owners take money out, put money in, or pay personal expenses on business accounts.

## Entity-shaped treatments

| Entity (typical) | Taking money out | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sole trader | Owner draw (equity), not expense | Profit is taxed on the return regardless of draws |
| Partnership / multi-member LLC (tax partnership) | Distributions per agreement; guaranteed payments if applicable | Keep capital accounts coherent |
| S-corp style | Reasonable compensation (payroll) + distributions | Distributions without reasonable wage are a common exam flag |
| C-corp style | Salary (payroll) and/or dividends | Dividends are not an expense; double-tax aware |
| Nonprofit | Compensation only under policy and law | No owner draws |

## Personal on the business card

Dr owner draw (or receivable if to be repaid) / Cr cash — **not** an operating expense.

## Business paid personally

Dr expense or asset / Cr owner contribution (or Cr owner payable if reimbursing).

## Reasonable compensation (directional)

When the entity type requires wages for owner-employees, compensation should match role, time, qualifications, and market data. Document the basis. This skill drafts the bookkeeping entries; compensation reasonableness opinions belong to a tax professional when contested.

## Failure recovery

| Trigger | First fix | Still failing |
|---|---|---|
| Owner spend in P&L | Reclass to draw/contribution | Restate periods if material and open |
| S-corp distributions only | Quantify wages missing | Escalate compensation design |
| Mixed personal/business account | Split every line; open receivable/draw | Separate accounts going forward |
