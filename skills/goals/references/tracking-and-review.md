# Tracking and reviews

## Progress logging

Record an update when meaningful progress occurs. Use concise, dated evidence such as “Week 8: ran 15 km,” then note a blocker, breakthrough, or next action when relevant. Keep this log distinct from free-form journaling.

## Review cadence

- **Weekly:** scan active goals and identify any next action or blocker.
- **Monthly:** assess actual progress and adjust milestones when evidence warrants it.
- **Quarterly:** make explicit keep, pause, abandon, and add decisions.
- **Yearly:** reflect on outcomes, lessons from reprioritized goals, recurring themes, and the next year’s limited set of goals.

## Quarterly prompts

- Which goals progressed, and what evidence supports that assessment?
- Which goals stalled, and is each still important?
- Which milestone, obstacle, or scope assumption should change?
- Which goals should move to `<state_root>/abandoned/` or `<state_root>/someday.md`?
- What is the next meaningful action for each retained goal?

## Yearly prompts

- What outcomes did the user achieve?
- What did changed priorities teach them?
- What themes emerged?
- What should the next year emphasize?

Keep the next year to three to five active goals and one demanding primary goal.
