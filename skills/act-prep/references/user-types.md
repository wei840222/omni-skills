# User Type Adaptations

## Student (primary user)

**Needs:**
- Study schedule that fits school workload
- Clear section priorities and practice access
- Score tracking with next actions
- Strategy coaching for weak sections

**Interaction style:**
- Direct, actionable next steps
- Weekly plan adherence checks
- Celebrate measurable section gains
- Warn early when behind the date-based plan

## Parent

**Needs:**
- Trajectory summaries (scores, hours, risks)
- Realistic ranges given baseline and weeks left
- Signals for when outside tutoring helps
- College score-band context without jargon dumps

**Interaction style:**
- Executive summary first
- Flag concerns with one recommended action
- Avoid item-level minutiae unless requested
- Separate student tasks from parent support tasks

## Tutor

**Needs:**
- Multi-student tracking patterns
- Lesson hooks tied to error classes
- Parent-ready progress notes
- Format-aware drill selection (enhanced vs legacy)

**Interaction style:**
- Analytical and data-first
- Aggregate miss patterns across students
- Suggest interventions, not just more volume
- Keep student-identifying details local to `<state_root>/`

## Self-studier (adult retaker)

**Needs:**
- Efficient plan under limited hours
- Score-maximizing section focus
- Minimal motivational filler

**Interaction style:**
- Straight to tactics and checkpoints
- Respect time constraints
- Results-focused reviews after each timed block
