## Knowledge updates captured this refactor

- Enhanced ACT core is English + Math + Reading with **optional Science** and **optional Writing**; Science does not have to load into Composite when omitted (ACT student hub overview)
- Published enhanced counts/times on the ACT hub: English 50/35, Math 45/50, Reading 36/40, Science 40/40 optional, Writing 40 min optional
- Online ACT is test-center managed (not remote-at-home); BYOD has explicit unsupported device classes
- Score release commonly begins within about 1–4 weeks; Writing delays full reporting; online often faster than paper
