# ACT Exam Structure

> Confirm the examinee's actual registration (paper vs online; Science/Writing add-ons) before locking a study plan. ACT publishes the current overview on the official test page; treat third-party summaries as secondary.

## Enhanced ACT core (current official overview)

ACT currently describes the core multiple-choice battery as English, Math, and Reading. **Science and Writing are optional add-ons and do not affect the Composite score** when omitted.

| Section | Questions | Time | Notes |
|---------|-----------|------|-------|
| English | 50 | 35 min | Revising/editing short texts and essays |
| Math | 45 | 50 min | Through beginning of grade-12 math; calculator allowed per ACT policy |
| Reading | 36 | 40 min | Close reading and multi-source reasoning |
| Science (optional) | 40 | 40 min | Interpretation/analysis; optional add-on |
| Writing (optional) | 1 prompt | 40 min | Optional essay add-on |

**Planning implication:** If target colleges do **not** require Science, do not spend scarce weeks maximizing Science at the expense of English/Math/Reading. If they do require or strongly prefer Science, treat it as a full scored section in the plan.

## Legacy four-section paper timings (still useful for older materials)

Some commercial books and older practice sets still use the pre-enhancement layout. Label those materials explicitly when used:

| Section | Questions | Time | Pace guide |
|---------|-----------|------|------------|
| English | 75 | 45 min | ~36 sec/question |
| Math | 60 | 60 min | ~60 sec/question |
| Reading | 40 | 35 min | ~52 sec/question |
| Science | 40 | 35 min | ~52 sec/question |
| Writing | 1 essay | 40 min | Optional |

**Total (legacy full battery):** about 2h 55min without Writing; about 3h 35min with Writing.

## Scoring

- **Section scores:** 1–36 each reported section
- **Composite:** average of the scored multiple-choice sections ACT includes for that administration (rounded). Do not invent a composite formula that forces optional Science when the student omitted it
- **Writing:** separate domain-based score; does not change Composite
- **Superscore:** many colleges consider best section scores across dates—verify each target school
- **National ranks / percentiles:** use ACT's current national-ranks tables rather than memorized cut lines

## Delivery modes

- **Paper** and **online (test-center)** options exist; online is **not** an at-home remote exam for national testing
- Online may be BYOD or center-provided device; personal Chromebooks, Windows S Mode, iPads, and tablets are commonly unsupported for BYOD—check current ACT device guidance
- Scratch paper: provided/collected for online; paper examinees annotate the booklet per center rules
- Calculator: permitted calculator for Math; online also exposes a built-in calculator app

## Registration and logistics anchors

- National test dates are published on ACT registration pages (commonly seven national dates across the year; local availability varies, e.g. July centers)
- Registration and late-change windows are fee-sensitive; format changes are typically locked after the late deadline
- Scores: most multiple-choice scores appear in MyACT within roughly 1–4 weeks; online often releases earlier than paper; Writing delays full reporting until essay scoring finishes

## Critical timing facts for coaching

- English remains high item-count relative to time—efficiency beats rereading whole passages
- Reading rewards passage budgets; abandon sunk-cost rereads
- Science (when taken) is primarily data interpretation, not content recall
- Math rewards early accuracy; hard late items are not worth more than easy early ones
