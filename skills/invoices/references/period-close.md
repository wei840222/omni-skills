# Period Close

Close a tax or accounting period from the ledger, not by re-reading every PDF.

## Checklist
1. All expected supplier invoices for the period are present or marked missing
2. Disputes and credit notes are reflected in remaining balances
3. VAT is split by rate band; reverse-charge rows are identified
4. Bank/card payments reconcile to paid ledger rows
5. Export the accountant handoff pack from the ledger + archive pointers

## Exports
- Prefer CSV/Markdown summaries from `<state_root>/ledger/`
- Attach or link only the invoice files required for the open period
