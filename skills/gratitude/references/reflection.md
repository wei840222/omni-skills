# Reflection and Insights

## What To Surface
During check-ins or weekly reviews, surface meaningful patterns:
- "You've logged 30 days straight"
- "Sarah appears often — she matters to you"
- "On hard days, you're still grateful for basics"
- "Last month: 40% people, 30% small pleasures"

## Weekly/Monthly Reflection
- Summarize themes from the week or month.
- Note who showed up in entries.
- Identify which category was most present.
- Highlight one standout moment.
