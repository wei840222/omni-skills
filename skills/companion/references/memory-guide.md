# Memory System Guide

How to remember someone well enough to be a real companion. All paths below are under the resolved `<state_root>/` (never the literal placeholder, never `SKILL.md`).

## Folder Structure

```text
<state_root>/
├── memory.md       # Always loaded first when present
├── topics.md       # What they like discussing
├── routines.md     # Daily life
└── history.md      # Past conversations
```

## memory.md (HOT — Always Loaded)

Keep under 100 lines. Core information only.

```markdown
# Companion Memory

## Who They Are
- Name:
- Age/generation:
- Living situation: (alone, with family, care facility)
- Location:

## Current Situation
- Health: (general notes only — not medical-advice territory)
- Daily life: (retired, working, caregiving)
- Mood lately: (how they have seemed recently)

## Key People
- Family: (names, relationships, contact cadence)
- Caregivers: (if relevant)
- Friends: (who they mention)
- Pets: (names, types)

## What They Enjoy
- Topics: (gardening, old movies, grandchildren)
- Routines: (morning tea, evening news)
- Memories: (stories they like to tell)

## Sensitive Areas
- Sensitive topics: (loss they prefer to keep private)
- Frustrations: (things that upset them)

## How They Reach Out
- Typical times: (morning, evening, random)
- Platform/method:
- Communication style: (brief, chatty, formal)

## Recent
- Last conversation: (date, brief note)
- Open threads: (things to follow up on)

---
*Last updated: YYYY-MM-DD*
```

## How Learning Works

- **From what they share:** names, places, stories — note them.
- **From patterns:** reach-out times and energy become routine cues.
- **From corrections:** update immediately when wrong.
- **From omissions:** skipped topics are context, not invitations to pry.

## Memory Rules

1. **`memory.md` always loads first** — keep it lean (≤100 lines)
2. **Topic/routine files on demand** — load when relevant
3. **Update after significant conversations** — keep knowledge fresh
4. **Respect privacy** — store what is useful for presence, not everything
5. **Archive old detail to `history.md`** — prevent clutter
6. **First durable write** needs brief consent when creating `<state_root>`
7. **Never write credentials or secrets** into companion files
