# Research sources (Gate 6)

Primary URLs used while verifying companion / loneliness / listening guidance. Prefer these over memory when citing mechanisms or safety boundaries. Each link below returned HTTP 200 at edit time (2026-10-01) with a normal browser UA.

## Loneliness, social connection, and presence

- **WHO — Social isolation and loneliness programme page** — social connection supports healthy ageing; loneliness is a public-health concern distinct from chosen solitude — https://www.who.int/teams/social-determinants-of-health/demographic-change-and-healthy-ageing/social-isolation-and-loneliness
- **Campaign to End Loneliness** — practical non-clinical framing of loneliness and why presence/befriending-style contact matters — https://www.campaigntoendloneliness.org/

## Active listening and non-directive support

- **APA — Emotions topic hub** — emotion-aware communication and validation without forced positivity — https://www.apa.org/topics/emotions
- **Motivational interviewing — Understanding MI** — collaboration, evocation, autonomy; avoid command language — https://motivationalinterviewing.org/understanding-motivational-interviewing
- **MedlinePlus — Stress and your health (encyclopedia)** — body/stress coupling context when users mention somatic worry during ordinary chat (still not medical advice) — https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/001955.htm

## Older adults, repetition, and cognitive gentleness

- **Alzheimer’s Association — Communication and caregiving** — patience with repetition, respect, avoid condescension when memory friction appears; still not a diagnosis path — https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/daily-care/communications

## Safety boundaries (not a substitute for care)

- **IASP — Suicidal thoughts resources** — international pointers toward local crisis help; companion stays present and refers, does not counsel crisis — https://www.iasp.info/suicidalthoughts/
- Local emergency numbers vary (112 / 911 / 999 / regional); always prefer the user’s locale
- US-specific crisis line commonly referenced in adjacent skills: https://988lifeline.org/ (verify locally if blocked)
