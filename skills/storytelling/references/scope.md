# Scope

## This skill covers

- narrative strategy for clarity, persuasion, and memorability
- story logic, evidence placement, pacing, and adaptation
- iterative drafting with explicit quality checks

## Route or pause when

- Fabricated facts or testimonials would be required to finish the story — mark gaps instead
- Claimed outcomes exceed available evidence — reduce the claim or gather proof
- Domain, legal, medical, or financial accuracy needs specialist review the user has not supplied
- The real need is sentence polish without narrative structure (`writing`), sales conversion copy (`copywriting`), or calendar/funnel planning alone (`content-marketing`)
