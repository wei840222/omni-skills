# Room-specific planning prompts

Use this reference after collecting dimensions, fixed elements, access constraints, and user needs. Treat the prompts as planning heuristics; verify local code, accessibility requirements, appliance manuals, and professional guidance before physical work.

## Living room

- Map conversation, circulation, media, storage, and daylight zones before selecting furniture.
- Check that seating, rug, and table dimensions support the intended use without blocking paths.
- Use product dimensions rather than generic sofa-size labels.

## Kitchen

- Map storage, preparation, cooking, cleaning, and appliance-door zones.
- Confirm appliance specifications, ventilation, electrical, plumbing, and clearances with the relevant professional and manufacturer before fixing a layout.
- Maintain accessible, safe routes for the household’s actual use.

## Bedroom

- Check bed, door, closet, window, and bedside-clearance needs together.
- Ask about sleep, storage, privacy, temperature, and accessibility requirements.
- Confirm window, egress, electrical, and anchoring decisions with applicable local requirements and product instructions.

## Bathroom

- Identify moisture exposure, waterproofing, ventilation, fixture clearances, and cleaning needs.
- Route plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, structural, and accessibility execution to qualified professionals and local requirements.

## Home office

- Map focused work, video calls, daylight, screen glare, acoustics, cable routing, and ergonomics.
- Use product specifications and the user’s work pattern before selecting desk, chair, storage, or lighting.

## Commercial office

- Identify occupancy, work modes, privacy, meeting needs, accessibility, fire/egress requirements, and lease constraints.
- Engage qualified design, code, and building-management professionals before committing to construction or occupancy changes.
