# Measurements and preliminary estimates

Use this reference for planning estimates. Verify dimensions on site, product specifications, local code, manufacturer instructions, and installer allowances before ordering or construction.

## Material quantities

- **Flooring:** `area = length × width`. Add a waste allowance that matches the product, layout, and installer guidance; diagonal or complex patterns usually require more than a straight layout. Divide by the manufacturer’s stated coverage per box and round up.
- **Wall tile:** `wall area = perimeter × tile height − openings`. Apply a pattern- and cut-dependent waste allowance, then use the manufacturer’s coverage data.
- **Paint:** Calculate paintable wall area, subtract openings, identify coats and substrate condition, then use the selected paint manufacturer’s stated coverage. Dark-to-light changes, texture, repair work, and absorption can increase coverage needs.

State the formula, assumptions, and a range when inputs are uncertain.

## Budget allocation

Use the user’s total budget, local quotes, and priorities to build a category plan. Reserve a clearly labeled contingency for unknown conditions, delivery, assembly, disposal, permits, and change orders. Cost ranges vary by region and project scope; obtain current local quotes before treating an estimate as a budget commitment.

## Furniture and access fit

1. Obtain the item’s packed and assembled dimensions and weight from its manufacturer.
2. Measure the narrowest access point and every turn along the delivery route.
3. Compare the item’s required orientation against door, stair, elevator, and hallway geometry.
4. Confirm delivery, assembly, wall anchoring, and return constraints before ordering.

## Circulation and room use

Use the user’s actual movement paths, mobility needs, and furniture dimensions to model clearance. If accessibility needs or regulated clearances apply, load `references/domain.md` and verify the jurisdictional standard with the appropriate professional or authority.

## Hidden-cost checklist

Include applicable costs for measurement, delivery, upper-floor access, assembly, installation, substrate preparation, electrical work, disposal, permits, taxes, and returns. Mark each figure as a local quote, a vendor estimate, or a planning assumption.
