# Official Sources & Regulatory References

This document records the primary official regulatory and educational authorities for investing principles, account types, and fiduciary standards.

## Regulatory & Government Authorities

### United States
- **U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) - Investor.gov**:
  - Official investor education portal: [https://www.investor.gov/](https://www.investor.gov/)
  - Introduction to investing, asset classes, compound interest calculations, and fee analyzers.
- **Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA)**:
  - Official investor education: [https://www.finra.org/investors](https://www.finra.org/investors)
  - Broker and firm verification (BrokerCheck): [https://brokercheck.finra.org/](https://brokercheck.finra.org/)
  - Risk disclosure and margin trading rules.
- **Internal Revenue Service (IRS)**:
  - Retirement Plans Community: [https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans)
  - Contribution limits for 401(k), Traditional IRA, Roth IRA, and HSA plans (updated annually under cost-of-living adjustments).

### International Standards & Non-US Equivalents
- **UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) & HM Government**:
  - FCA Consumer Hub & Retail Protection: [https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers)
  - FCA InvestSmart Retail Investor Hub: [https://www.fca.org.uk/investsmart](https://www.fca.org.uk/investsmart)
  - UK Government Individual Savings Accounts (ISAs): [https://www.gov.uk/individual-savings-accounts](https://www.gov.uk/individual-savings-accounts)
- **Canada Revenue Agency (CRA)**: [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-20800-rrsp-deduction.html](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-20800-rrsp-deduction.html) (RRSP and TFSA rules).
- **Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) - Moneysmart**: [https://moneysmart.gov.au/](https://moneysmart.gov.au/) (Superannuation and investment education).

## Core Principles & Empirical Benchmarks

- **Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT)**: Markowitz, Harry (1952). "Portfolio Selection", *The Journal of Finance*.
- **The Bogleheads Guide to Investing / Index Investing**:
  - Bogle Financial Markets Research Center: [https://www.boglecenter.net/](https://www.boglecenter.net/)
  - Focus on broad market indexing, expense ratio minimization, and tax-efficient asset location.
