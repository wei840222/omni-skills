# Research sources

Use these sources to verify the domain claims that shape the skill's safety and context guidance. They are evidence records, not operating instructions.

## Burnout and clinical boundary

- World Health Organization, "Burn-out an occupational phenomenon: International Classification of Diseases." https://www.who.int/mental_health/evidence/burn-out/en/
  - Key point: ICD-11 treats burnout as an occupational phenomenon and describes exhaustion, mental distance or cynicism, and reduced professional efficacy. The skill therefore avoids clinical diagnosis and routes persistent or severe symptoms to appropriate support.

## ADHD care boundary

- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, "Treatment of ADHD." https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/treatment/index.html
  - Key point: treatment planning may include behavioral interventions, medication, and educational supports. The skill offers only low-risk planning accommodations and leaves assessment or treatment decisions to qualified care.

## Student learning methods

- Dunlosky et al., "Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques." https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/journals/pspi/learning-techniques.html
  - Key point: practice testing and distributed practice have high utility across learning conditions. The student context routes users toward retrieval and spaced study rather than rereading alone.
