# Sources — parenting

Gate 6 research anchors for everyday parenting coaching. Prefer primary pediatric and public-health guidance over influencer rules. Re-verify before stating time-sensitive clinical claims.

## Development and behavior

- **CDC — Positive Parenting Tips** — age-banded expectations and practical tips via https://www.cdc.gov/child-development/positive-parenting-tips/index.html
- **CDC — Child Development** — milestone framing and when to talk with a professional via https://www.cdc.gov/child-development/index.html
- **AAP — HealthyChildren behavior & discipline hub** — parent-facing guidance on limits and alternatives via https://www.healthychildren.org/English/family-life/family-dynamics/communication-discipline/Pages/default.aspx
- **AAP — Ages & Stages** — developmental context for expectations via https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/Pages/default.aspx

## Sleep

- **AAP / HealthyChildren — Healthy Sleep Habits** — safe routines and common issues via https://www.healthychildren.org/English/healthy-living/sleep/Pages/default.aspx
- **CDC — Sleep and Sleep Disorders (Children)** — population sleep guidance entry point via https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/index.html
- **AAP safe sleep overview** — infant sleep safety boundaries via https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/sleep/Pages/default.aspx

## Screens and media

- **AAP — Media and Children** — family media plan framing rather than one-size bans via https://www.healthychildren.org/English/family-life/Media/Pages/default.aspx
- **AAP family media plan tool** — collaborative household rules via https://www.healthychildren.org/English/fmp/Pages/MediaPlan.aspx

## Mental health, burnout, referral

- **CDC — Children’s Mental Health** — when concerns need professional evaluation via https://www.cdc.gov/children-mental-health/index.html
- **WHO — Mental health of children** — global framing; professional care remains primary via https://www.who.int/health-topics/mental-health
- **IASP — Find a crisis centre** — local crisis routing when safety is in doubt via https://www.iasp.info/suicidalthoughts/

## Practice notes for this skill

- Ask age and context before quoting any age-banded tip.
- Present sleep-training methods as options with tradeoffs; do not brand-push.
- Treat screen guidance as context-sensitive family media planning, not moral purity.
- Clinical, legal, and child-protection actions leave this skill immediately.
