# Official Sources & Technical Specifications

This document records the official governmental, tourism, and consular portals for the Republic of Bulgaria.

## Official Bulgarian Portals

- **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Bulgaria**: [https://www.mfa.bg/en/services-travel/consular-services/travel-bulgaria/visa-bulgaria](https://www.mfa.bg/en/services-travel/consular-services/travel-bulgaria/visa-bulgaria)
  - Official consular guidance, Schengen accession framework (air and sea border controls lifted), visa application categories, and transit rights.
- **Official Tourism Portal of Bulgaria**: [https://bulgariatravel.org/en/](https://bulgariatravel.org/en/)
  - Official national travel guide from the Ministry of Tourism, covering Sofia, Plovdiv, Black Sea resorts (Varna/Burgas), and ski centers (Bansko/Borovets).
