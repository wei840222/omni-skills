# Slovak Domain Knowledge

Use this file for register, particles, expressiveness, reactions, and delivery checks while drafting Slovak. It describes common conversational patterns; it is not a license to invent intimacy, slang, or Czech forms the user did not ask for.

## Formality Default

Default AI register is often too high. Casual Slovak is warm and direct. Unless explicitly formal: lean casual among peers. Prefer `Ahoj` over `Dobrý deň`, and `Hej` over stiff `Áno`, when the relationship supports it.

## Ty vs Vy

Critical distinction:

- **Vy**: strangers initially, elderly, professional or institutional contexts
- **Ty**: friends, peers, internet, casual channels
- Slovak internet writing is almost entirely **ty**
- Overusing **vy** among peers sounds stiff; inventing **ty** for unfamiliar formal recipients is a Required failure

Keep address agreement consistent for the whole draft.

## Particles & Softeners

Use particles for conversational role, not as a quota:

- **No**: filler / "well" (`No, neviem`)
- **Tak**: transitional "so"
- **Však**: "right?" / contrastive "but"
- **Proste**: "simply" / "just"
- **Ako**: filler "like"

## Fillers & Flow

Optional conversational choices:

- No, tak, však
- Ako, proste, vlastne
- Vieš, počuj
- Akože, v pohode

Keep factual messages direct; insert a filler only where it helps the speaker's flow.

## Expressiveness

Match the source sentiment rather than replacing every ordinary word:

- Dobré → Super, Skvelé, Hustý, Bomba when enthusiasm is intended
- Zlé → Hrozné, Otrasné; stronger vulgar intensifiers only when the source clearly wants them
- Veľmi → Mega, Fakt, Strašne when intensity still fits

## Common Expressions

Choose expressions that fit the speech act:

- V pohode, Jasné, Super
- Nie je za čo, Pohoda
- Fakt?, Vážne?, No jasné
- Paráda, Pecka

## Reactions

Preserve the speaker's actual reaction:

- Fakt?, Vážne?, Čo?
- No teda!, Hustý!, Super!
- Bomba!, Skvelé!, Pecka!
- Light `haha` / `lol` in chat when the channel already uses them

## Slovak vs Czech

Similar but distinct:

- Different vocabulary for some everyday words
- Different endings and patterns
- Maintain strict separation—Slovaks notice mixing
- If the target is Czech, stop and load `czech` instead

## Delivery check

Before sending:

1. Confirm one ty/vy choice and matching greetings or verb forms.
2. Confirm the draft is Slovak, not a Czech blend.
3. Confirm particles/fillers are optional seasoning, not a stuffed checklist.
4. Confirm facts, names, numbers, and uncertainty from the source survived.
5. If the draft still reads like stiff textbook Slovak for a peer chat, add one natural particle or a warmer word choice—not a slang pile-up.

The goal is audience-fit Slovak, not a guarantee that writing will evade an AI detector.
