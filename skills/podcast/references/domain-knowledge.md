# Podcast Domain Knowledge — Gate 6 Sources

Primary sources used to refresh monetization, discovery, and production guidance during the `podcast` refactor. Prefer these over undated blog claims when numbers drift.

## Monetization / CPM

- **IAB Podcast Measurement Technical Guidelines** — standardized download/listen metrics sponsors rely on for CPM math — https://iabtechlab.com/standards/podcast-measurement-guidelines/
- **IAB / Edison Podcast Landscape** — industry scale and advertising context for host-read vs produced inventory — https://www.iab.com/insights/the-podcast-landscape/
- **Sounds Profitable / industry CPM ranges** — cross-check host-read vs pre-produced mid-roll CPM bands used in `growth.md` ($18–50 headline CPM; host-read often higher) — https://soundsprofitable.com/

## Discovery / SEO / Clips

- **Apple Podcasts Connect help** — show/episode metadata and discovery surfaces — https://podcasters.apple.com/support/
- **YouTube Help: podcasts on YouTube** — video-podcast discovery and RSS/video pairing notes — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13563063
- **Spotify for Podcasters** — distribution and audience tools — https://podcasters.spotify.com/support

## Production / AI voice ethics

- **FTC Endorsement Guides** — disclosure expectations when synthetic or sponsored content could mislead listeners — https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
- **Recording/consent baseline** — only clone or synthesize a real person's voice with explicit permission; label AI-generated speech in show notes when used.

## How this skill uses the sources

- `references/growth.md` CPM bands ($18–50 overall; host-read $25–50, pre-produced $15–25) are planning heuristics, not guaranteed rates — re-verify against current IAB/seller cards before quoting a deal.
- `references/ai-generation.md` consent + labeling rules follow disclosure norms above.
- Episode planning structure remains skill-local in `references/episodes.md` and `references/formats.md`.
