# Sources - Mindfulness

Verified external references for domain claims used by this skill. Prefer these
URLs when stating research language, public-health framing, or crisis routing.
Do not invent prevalence, effect sizes, or clinical indications from memory.

## Agent Skills format

- [Agent Skills Specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) — package
  frontmatter, resource layout, and validator expectations for this repository.

## Public-health and clinical context (coaching boundary)

- [NHS — Mindfulness](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/tips-and-support/mindfulness/) —
  public self-help framing; mindfulness as a practice people can try, not a
  replacement for clinical care.
- [APA — Mindfulness](https://www.apa.org/topics/mindfulness) — overview of
  mindfulness as attention training; keep claims descriptive, not diagnostic.
- [NCCIH — Meditation and Mindfulness: What You Need To Know](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/meditation-and-mindfulness-what-you-need-to-know) —
  evidence summary and safety notes for meditation/mindfulness practices.
- [NIMH — Caring for Your Mental Health](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health) —
  general mental-health self-care context; crisis and professional-help routing.
- [NCBI Bookshelf — Mindfulness overview (NBK568733)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK568733/) —
  secondary clinical literature overview; use cautiously and without inventing
  dosing claims.

## Safety language used by this skill

- Keep the skill in coaching/tracking scope (NHS/APA/NCCIH framing).
- If the user reports self-harm risk, crisis, or severe escalating distress,
  stop routine coaching and direct immediate emergency or professional support
  (NIMH caring-for-your-mental-health guidance as the public routing reference).
- Do not present short guided scripts as therapy, diagnosis, or a cure.
