the EU AI Act timeline (commonly cited from August 2026). Re-check the overview
page before stating an enforcement date as settled law for a specific use case.

a dead FTC URL — verify the current FTC business-guidance page at ask time.

## Marketplace fees

Platform fee pages are frequently bot-blocked (403/404 from automation). Before
stating Upwork/Fiverr take rates, open the live fee/terms page in a browser and
record the observed all-in commission with a date in `<state_root>/memory.md`.

## Claim checklist

1. Classification / IR35 — cite GOV.UK or IRS pages above; do not invent tests.
2. Statutory late-payment interest — cite GOV.UK or EUR-Lex; recompute rates live.
3. AI disclosure dates — cite EU AI Act overview/Article 50; mark uncertain dates.
4. Copyright transfer — cite Circular 30; require written assignment language.
5. Marketplace % fees — verify live; never copy stale numbers from this file.
