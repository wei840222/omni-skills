# Research Sources and Claim Inventory

Research completed 2026-08-21. Use the linked primary sources for platform policy and compliance decisions; validate business-specific conversion, retention, and price assumptions with the product's own data.

## App-store policy

- **Apple, “App Store Small Business Program”** — eligible developers with up to US$1M in prior-calendar-year proceeds may receive a 15% rate; eligibility includes associated accounts and can change after the threshold. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/
- **Google Play Help, “Service fees”** — fees vary by program, transaction type, market, and install cohort; the page documents regional changes effective June 30, 2026. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en

## Creator disclosures

- **U.S. Federal Trade Commission, “Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers”** — material connections need clear, conspicuous disclosure with the endorsement; use simple language and place it with the endorsement. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers

## Measurement and experimentation

- **NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook, “What are the elements of a good experimental design?”** — define objectives, factors, responses, and analysis before collecting data. https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section1/pri11.htm

## Research-based operating rule

For changing prices, paywalls, offers, or lifecycle messages: write a hypothesis, select one primary metric and a guardrail, test one material change at a time, and retain the decision with its observed result. When platform policy or law applies, consult the current authoritative rule and qualified local advice before publication.
