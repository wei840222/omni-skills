# Current Policy and Benchmark Checks

This guide keeps policy-sensitive facts out of pricing heuristics. Read it before estimating net receipts or publishing creator sponsorship content. Re-check the linked authoritative page at the time of the decision because programs, fees, and regional rules change.

## Store receipts

1. Identify the storefront, customer market, product type, billing path, developer program, and associated accounts.
2. Retrieve the current fee rule from the relevant official policy.
3. Calculate net receipts using the applicable fee, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, and other variable costs; record the date and policy URL with the model.
4. If eligibility or a billing path is unclear, use a range and obtain platform or qualified professional confirmation before committing a public price.

### Apple App Store

Apple’s Small Business Program states that eligible developers with up to US$1M in prior-calendar-year proceeds can receive a 15% commission rate. Associated accounts and current-year proceeds affect eligibility. Read the current program terms before modelling receipts:

- https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/

### Google Play

Google Play’s service fees vary by program, transaction type, market, and—in some regions—install cohort. The official fee page includes regional changes effective June 30, 2026; retrieve the applicable schedule rather than applying one global percentage:

- https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en

## Sponsorship and endorsement disclosure

For content that affects U.S. consumers, the FTC says a material relationship with a brand should be clearly disclosed with the endorsement. Place the disclosure where the audience can notice and understand it, use plain language such as “ad” or “sponsored,” and use the same language as the endorsement. Other jurisdictions can impose additional rules.

- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers

## Experimentation baseline

Treat public conversion, CPM, and churn figures as context rather than promises. Establish a product-specific baseline, one primary outcome, and at least one customer-impact guardrail before deciding whether to retain a change. The research source record and claim classes are in `references/research-sources.md`.
