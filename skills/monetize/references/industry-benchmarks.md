# Industry Benchmarks (Updated 2026)

## Platform Commissions & Policies

### Apple App Store
- **Standard Commission**: 30%
- **Small Business Program**: 15% (for developers making <$1M/year)
- **External Links**: As of 2025 court rulings in *Epic Games v. Apple*, restrictions and commissions on external payment links were removed (currently pending appeal as of mid-2025/2026).
