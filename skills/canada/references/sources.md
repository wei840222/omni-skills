# Sources — Canada

Official and primary references for Canadian travel logistics. Prefer these over secondary blogs when booking windows, entry rules, park access, fares, or safety guidance change. Re-check the linked page before quoting a live fee, deadline, eligibility rule, or timetable.

## Entry, visas, and border

- Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html
- Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA): https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta.html
- Visit Canada overview: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada.html
- Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA): https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/

Treat visa, eTA, and entry guidance as orientation only. The traveler owns the filing; licensed counsel owns contested cases.

## National parks and Rockies logistics

- Parks Canada: https://parks.canada.ca/
- Banff National Park: https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff
- Jasper National Park: https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper
- Lake Louise / Moraine Lake shuttle and access updates: start from Parks Canada Banff pages and seasonal alerts
- Parks Canada reservation system (camping / selected experiences): https://reservation.pc.gc.ca/

Peak-season parking and shuttle rules change; verify the current Parks Canada notice before promising car access at popular lakes.

- Environment and Climate Change Canada weather: https://weather.gc.ca/
- Canadian Avalanche Association (when backcountry winter travel matters): https://www.avalanche.ca/
- Emergency readiness orientation via Government of Canada: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/policing/emergencies.html
- European-style local emergency number in Canada is **911**

## Usage notes

- Distance and season dominate Canada itineraries; treat multi-province loops as multi-trip designs unless the user has long duration and budget.
- Park shuttle, camping, and lodging inventory are time-sensitive in July–August; confirm live Parks Canada and lodging inventory before locking a Rockies day plan.
- Prefer official tourism boards and Parks Canada over generic listicles when trail conditions, wildfire closures, or ferry schedules matter.
- When the user needs multi-country trip systems, deeper cuisine workflows, or language production, hand off to related skills rather than stretching this package.
