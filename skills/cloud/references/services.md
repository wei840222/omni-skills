# Cloud Services Comparison

Prices and quotas change. Treat the tables below as orientation only; confirm
the live pages listed in `references/sources.md` before quoting a number to a
user.

## Orientation tiers (consumer, personal plans)

| Service | Typical free tier | Common paid steps (confirm live) |
|---------|-------------------|----------------------------------|
| iCloud+ | 5 GB | 50 GB / 200 GB / 2 TB (+ larger family tiers) |
| Google One / Drive | 15 GB shared with Gmail + Photos | 100 GB / 2 TB and higher |
| Dropbox | 2 GB | Plus / Family multi-TB plans; free tier has device limits |
| OneDrive | 5 GB | 100 GB standalone; Microsoft 365 often includes 1 TB |

## Feature orientation

| Feature | iCloud | Google Drive | Dropbox | OneDrive |
|---------|--------|--------------|---------|----------|
| Photo auto-backup | Yes | Yes (Photos) | Yes | Yes |
| Offline access | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| File versioning | Limited window | Revisions | Version history (plan-dependent) | Version history |
| Strongest native fit | Apple | Android / Chrome | Mixed OS | Windows / M365 |

## Best for

**iCloud** — Apple-only households; zero-setup Continuity; Family Sharing with
other Apple IDs.

**Google Drive** — Gmail users (quota is shared); Android photo backup; Docs /
Sheets collaboration.

**Dropbox** — mixed Windows/Mac/phone fleets; simple shared folders; teams that
already standardize on Dropbox links.

**OneDrive** — Windows desktops; Microsoft 365 subscribers who already pay for
1 TB; Office desktop workflows.

## Switching services

1. Download everything first (provider export or folder download).
2. Verify local copies open.
3. Upload to the new primary service.
4. Keep the old account read-only until one successful restore test.
5. Cancel the redundant paid plan only after the restore test.

Export anchors:

- iCloud: [icloud.com](https://www.icloud.com)
- Google: [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com) (Drive)
- Dropbox: folder download from [dropbox.com](https://www.dropbox.com)
- OneDrive: folder download from [onedrive.com](https://onedrive.live.com)

## Hidden gotchas

- **Google:** free 15 GB includes Gmail attachments and Photos.
- **iCloud:** device backups and Messages in iCloud consume the same quota.
- **Dropbox:** free plans enforce a low device limit; confirm current limit on
  the plans page before promising multi-device free use.
- **OneDrive:** personal vs work/school tenants look similar in the shell but
  follow different admin policies.
