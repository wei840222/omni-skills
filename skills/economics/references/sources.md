# Economics Sources

Load this file before citing macro series, official outlooks, identification
debates, or other time-sensitive economics claims. Prefer the primary page over
memory.

## Authoritative sources (verified reachable in this handoff)

- **American Economic Association — What is Economics?** —
  https://www.aeaweb.org/resources/students/what-is-economics — student-facing
  framing of the discipline and career paths.
- **American Economic Association home** —
  https://www.aeaweb.org/ — journals, resources, and professional hub.
- **NBER home** — https://www.nber.org/ — working-paper and research hub for
  identification and applied micro/macro debates.
- **FRED (St. Louis Fed)** — https://fred.stlouisfed.org/ — downloadable
  official and curated macro series; use series pages for dated figures.
- **IMF World Economic Outlook hub** —
  https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WEO — multilateral outlook and forecast
  releases (verify the specific issue page before quoting a vintage).
- **Federal Reserve — Monetary Policy** —
  https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy.htm — policy framework and
  related publications hub.
- **Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences** —
  https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/ — prize archive for
  landmark contributions (cite the specific prize year page when naming a
  result).
- **Our World in Data — Economic Growth** —
  https://ourworldindata.org/economic-growth — long-run growth charts with
  downloadable data and source notes.

## Knowledge updates applied in this refactor

1. **Opportunity cost framing:** Keep beginner explanations choice-based (what
   is given up), not money-first. AEA’s student framing treats economics as the
   study of choices under scarcity; reinforce that before introducing markets.
2. **IV / LATE external validity:** Instrumental-variable estimates (for
   example compulsory-schooling reforms) identify local average treatment
   effects for compliers. Do not present a single IV return-to-education number
   as the population average without stating the complier population and
   citing the paper.
3. **Macro figures are vintage-sensitive:** GDP, CPI, unemployment, and WEO
   forecasts change with revisions. Quote FRED series pages, Fed monetary-policy
   materials, or a dated IMF WEO release rather than memorized round numbers.
4. **Positive vs normative:** Policy recommendations always smuggle welfare
   weights. Label efficiency claims separately from distributional judgments.

## Claim hygiene

- Series values need the series id/page, units, and observation date.
- Identification claims need design + assumptions + external-validity limits.
- Personalized investment, tax filing, or trade directives are out of scope;
  route to `financial-literacy`, `invest`, or `trading`.
- Site-specific regulatory or legal conclusions need jurisdiction-primary
  sources beyond this general skill package.
