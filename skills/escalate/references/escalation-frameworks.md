# Escalation Frameworks and Delegation

When deciding whether to act or escalate, refer to standard models of delegation and responsibility, such as the Responsibility Assignment Matrix (RACI).

## RACI Matrix
The RACI matrix describes participation by various roles in completing tasks or deliverables for a project or business process. It is useful in clarifying roles and responsibilities.

*   **Responsible (R):** Those who do the work to complete the task.
*   **Accountable (A):** The one ultimately answerable for the correct and thorough completion of the deliverable or task.
*   **Consulted (C):** Those whose opinions are sought, typically subject matter experts.
*   **Informed (I):** Those who are kept up-to-date on progress, often only on completion.

*Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix*

## Conflict Escalation
Escalation occurs when a situation moves to a state of higher intensity or scope. In systems, distinguishing between issues that can be handled autonomously (local edits, drafts) and those requiring higher-level intervention (external deployment, public communication) prevents escalation of commitment and ensures proper oversight.
