# Current-source guide

Use a dated primary source before making a claim that can change by jurisdiction, broker, or market conditions. These sources support the stable education in `SKILL.md`; they do not supply live prices, account-specific terms, or personal advice.

## Order mechanics and diversification

- **SEC Investor.gov, Types of Orders** — explains market, limit, and stop orders, including that a stop order becomes a market order when triggered: <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/how-stock-markets-work/types-orders>
- **SEC Investor.gov, Asset Allocation and Diversification** — explains diversification and its limits: <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/getting-started/asset-allocation>

## Margin and day-trading rules

- **FINRA, Day Trading** — use for current United States day-trading education and links to applicable rules: <https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investment-products/stocks/day-trading>
- **FINRA Rule 4210, Margin Requirements** — use for current rule text rather than relying on a remembered threshold or proposal: <https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/4210>

## Broker-specific terms

Broker order handling, margin requirements, fees, and liquidation policies can be stricter than general education. Use the user's broker documentation and an as-of date before calculating an account-specific scenario.
