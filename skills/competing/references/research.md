# Competitive Analysis & Performance Research

## After-Action Reviews (AARs)
The U.S. Army's After-Action Review (AAR) framework is the gold standard for performance analysis. It focuses on four questions:
1. What was supposed to happen?
2. What actually happened?
3. Why was there a difference?
4. What can be learned from it?

## Loss Analysis & Deliberate Practice
Deliberate practice requires focused attention with the specific goal of improving performance. Key components include:
- Objective measurement of performance.
- Immediate, specific feedback.
- Focus on fixing weaknesses, not just practicing strengths.
