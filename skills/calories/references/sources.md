## Practice notes retained from domain review

- Treat nutrition labels as best-available single sources with legal tolerance, not exact ground truth.
- Prefer measured TDEE from 14+ clean log days over formula TDEE once the window is valid.
- Cap unsupervised deficit targets at the 1200 kcal/day (women) / 1500 kcal/day (men) floors and suspend protocols on Red Flags.
