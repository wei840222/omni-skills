# Shein Quality Assessment Signals

## Fabric and Material

- **100% Polyester**: Often feels cheap, lacks breathability (poor for summer), prone to static. Check finish (e.g., "satin-like" vs. plain weave).
- **Cotton Blends**: Generally offer better comfort and durability than pure synthetics. Look for at least 50% cotton.
- **Viscose/Rayon**: Good drape and breathability, but prone to wrinkling and shrinking if not washed carefully.
- **Knitwear**: Check if it's acrylic (can pill quickly) or a better blend. Chunky knits are usually safer bets than fine knits.
- **Denim**: "Rigid denim" (100% cotton) often feels stiffer and higher quality but lacks stretch. "Stretch denim" is more forgiving but may lose shape over time. Check thickness in review photos.

## Construction Indicators

- **Lining**: Check if the description mentions lining. Unlined dresses and skirts, especially lighter colors, are often sheer.
- **Seams and Stitching**: Look at close-up product photos. Uneven stitching, loose threads, or puckered seams are red flags.
- **Hardware**: Zippers, buttons, and buckles often reveal quality. Plastic zippers or flimsy buttons are common on cheaper items.
- **Pattern Matching**: On printed items (stripes, plaids), check if the pattern aligns at the seams. Misaligned patterns indicate lower manufacturing standards.

## Photographic Evidence

- **Seller Photos**: More photos from different angles (front, back, details, fabric texture) usually indicate a more confident seller.
- **Review Photos**: These are crucial. Compare the fabric drape, color accuracy, and overall look on real people versus the highly styled seller photos.
- **Video Reviews**: If available, videos provide the best sense of fabric movement and true color under normal lighting.

## Brand Tiers (Shein Sub-brands)

- **MOTF**: Positioned as premium, uses better fabrics (silk blends, higher quality synthetics), tailored fits.
- **DAZY**: Korean style, often better construction but heavily skewed towards Asian sizing (runs very small).
- **ROMWE**: Often lower price point and fast-fashion trendy, quality can be hit or miss.
- **Shein Curve/Fit+**: Generally decent quality, focused on fit and structure for plus sizes.
