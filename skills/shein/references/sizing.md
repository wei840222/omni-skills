# Shein Size Conversion Guide

## Understanding Shein Sizing

Shein sizes generally run smaller than standard US/EU sizing, often aligning more closely with Asian sizing charts. The most reliable method is always using exact measurements rather than letter sizes.

## Shoes Sizing

Shein shoe sizes are listed in EUR sizing. 
- EU 36 ≈ US 6
- EU 37 ≈ US 6.5
- EU 38 ≈ US 7.5
- EU 39 ≈ US 8.5
- EU 40 ≈ US 9
- EU 41 ≈ US 10

*Note: Shein shoes often run narrow. For wide feet, consider sizing up or looking for wide-fit specific styles.*

## Key Recommendations

1. **Always check the specific item's size chart**: Rely primarily on exact measurements.
2. **Read reviews for fit feedback**: Many users state if an item runs "true to size," "small," or "large." Look for reviewers with similar body measurements.
3. **Check fabric composition**: Items with high spandex/elastane content offer more stretch and flexibility in sizing than 100% cotton or polyester.
