# News, Press, and Wire Copy

Scope: news articles, press releases, wire copy. Sources: inverted pyramid + BLUF (`domain-knowledge.md`).

## Reading strategy

- Payload is usually in the **first 1–2 paragraphs**.
- Cut ending background before cutting the lead.
- Separate issuer claims from verified facts; keep attribution.

## Must retain

- Who / what / when / where needed to act
- Numbers and named parties
- Hedged or contested claims with their attribution
- Corrections or "developing" status if present

## Traps

- Writing a headline-only summary that drops the lede's constraints
- Treating press-release voice as independent verification
