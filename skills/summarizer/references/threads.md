# Email, Chat, Tickets, and Comment Threads

Scope: email threads, Slack/Discord channels, issue comments, support tickets.

## Reading order

- Email: cause often at the bottom; newest on top — read bottom-up, write top-down.
- Chat channels: diffuse; reorganize by topic. Pure chronology is usually unreadable.
- Tickets: status + blocker + owner matter more than full reply archaeology.

## Procedure

1. Deduplicate quoted replies and repeated stacks.
2. Build a topic map; collapse pure ACK/noise.
3. Name who is blocked on whom.
4. State cut-off timestamp for live threads.
5. Preserve decisions, asks, and commitments with owners.

## Traps

- Summarizing a live thread without a cut-off
- Keeping every social nicety while dropping the blocker
- Collapsing multi-party disagreement into false consensus
