# Meetings, Calls, and Standups

Scope: transcripts and notes from meetings, calls, interviews, standups, and workshops. Method selection is in `techniques.md`; action-item fidelity is Core Rule 8 in `SKILL.md`.

## Reading order

1. Read the **last 20% first** (decisions and wrap-up). Lead-first is the worst prior for spoken meetings.
2. Scan for commitments: owner language, dates, "I will", "can you", "by Friday".
3. Then fill context from earlier discussion only as needed to make decisions intelligible.

## Output shape

```
Header: source, date/time, attendees if material, level + word count
Decided: ...
Open: ...
Actions: <owner> — <verb phrase> — <date>
Omitted: <material cuts, if any>
```

## Rules

- Every action item needs **owner + verb + date**. Missing any one → move to Open, not Actions.
- Do not allocate space by airtime. Weight by decision authority and evidence offered.
- Preserve dissent: one objection inside an hour of agreement still survives, or the omission note names it.
- Relative dates ("next Tuesday") resolve against the meeting date, or keep verbatim plus that date.
- Filler, logistics, and repeated status with no delta compress hard; decisions and blockers do not.

## Traps

| Trap | Fix |
|------|-----|
| Agenda-order recap | Reorder by reader consequence; decisions lead |
| Ownerless "next steps" | Demote to Open until owner+date exist |
| Loudest-speaker bias | Prefer decided text and named owners over monologue |
