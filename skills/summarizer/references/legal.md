# Contracts, Policy, and Regulation

Scope: contracts, terms, policies, statutes, and compliance text. Default mode: extractive or hybrid with spans (`techniques.md`).

## Where the deal lives

- Definitions, termination, liability, payment, exhibits/schedules often carry more payload than body prose.
- Absences matter: missing cap, missing exit, missing governing law can be the finding.

## Must retain

- Obligations (who must do what)
- Money, caps, penalties, fee shifts
- Dates, renewal, notice windows → material deadlines become `## Due` candidates
- Exit / termination / remedy paths
- Conditions precedent and dependencies
- Defined terms that change ordinary meaning

## Output discipline

- Prefer quoted or near-quoted spans for operative clauses.
- Label "not stated in the source" rather than inferring a missing protection.
- Do not give legal advice disguised as summary; compression only.
