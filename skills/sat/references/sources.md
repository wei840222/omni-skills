## Knowledge updates captured this refactor

- Digital SAT is Bluebook-delivered with multistage adaptive modules for both Reading and Writing and Math (Understanding Scores PDF; College Board adaptive-testing explainer)
- Planning structure retained from prior skill + secondary corroboration: RW 54Q / 64 min (2×32), Math 44Q / 70 min (2×35), scored length 2h 14min plus inter-section break
- Section scores 200–800; total 400–1600; no wrong-answer penalty framing in official scoring materials
- Built-in Desmos calculator available for all Math items; approved handheld calculators also allowed per College Board Math guidance
- Score Choice and superscoring remain school-specific—verify per target rather than assuming universal rules
- Legacy paper-SAT timing/content must not drive Digital SAT plans
