# Digital SAT Exam Format

Verify unstable numbers against `sources.md` before locking a plan or quoting fees.

## Structure (Digital SAT / Bluebook)

| Section | Modules | Time | Questions |
|---------|---------|------|-----------|
| Reading and Writing | 2 equal-length stages | 64 min total (32 min each) | 54 |
| Math | 2 equal-length stages | 70 min total (35 min each) | 44 |
| **Scored total** | 4 modules | **2h 14min** (+ ~10 min break between RW and Math) | **98** |

Delivered in the College Board **Bluebook** app. Students may also use an approved handheld calculator; Bluebook includes a built-in **Desmos** graphing calculator for all Math questions.

## Adaptive testing (MST)

The SAT Suite uses multistage adaptive testing:

- Each section has two separately timed modules.
- Module 1 mixes easy, medium, and hard items.
- Module 2 difficulty routes from Module 1 performance (harder or easier path).
- Stronger Module 1 performance unlocks a higher score ceiling on that section.

**Implication:** Pace Module 1 carefully. Do not burn time early, and do not rush careless misses that route you to the easier Module 2.

## Scoring

| Score | Range |
|-------|-------|
| Reading and Writing | 200–800 |
| Math | 200–800 |
| **Total** | **400–1600** (sum of section scores) |

- No wrong-answer penalty; unanswered items cannot score points—finish the module.
- College Board also reports content-domain insights and percentiles on official reports (see Understanding Scores PDF in `sources.md`).
- Many colleges **superscore** (best section across dates); some require all sittings. Confirm per school (`colleges.md`).

## Reading and Writing content domains

Official reports describe four RW domains (approximate shares vary by form; treat percentages as planning aids, not exact quotas):

| Domain | Typical focus |
|--------|---------------|
| Craft and Structure | Words in context, text structure, purpose, cross-text |
| Information and Ideas | Central ideas, details, inferences, evidence, data graphics |
| Standard English Conventions | Boundaries, form/structure/sense (grammar & punctuation) |
| Expression of Ideas | Rhetorical synthesis, transitions, effectiveness |

Passages are short (about 25–150 words) with one question per passage/stimulus.

## Math content domains

| Domain | Typical topics |
|--------|----------------|
| Algebra | Linear equations, systems, functions |
| Advanced Math | Quadratics, polynomials, exponentials, nonlinear |
| Problem-Solving and Data Analysis | Ratios, percentages, statistics, quantitative evidence |
| Geometry and Trigonometry | Area/volume, triangles, circles, basic trig |

Calculator allowed on **all** Math questions.

## Registration and score timing (planning defaults)

- Register via College Board student account / My SAT flows; weekend dates commonly include Aug, Oct, Nov, Dec, Mar, May, Jun (confirm current calendar).
- Registration deadlines are typically about a month before the test; late windows may exist with fees.
- Weekend scores commonly release on the order of ~2 weeks after the test date—always check the published score-release calendar.

Do not invent fee amounts or exact deadline clocks from memory; open the registration and score-release pages in `sources.md`.
