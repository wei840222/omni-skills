# Latvian language sources

Use these pages to check a register label, a tu/jūs mapping, a particle gloss, or contested terminology. They describe usage; they are not a license to invent slang or a dialect the user did not ask for.

## Official Latvian language authorities

- **Latviešu valodas aģentūra** — state agency for Latvian language policy, public guidance, and consultations. https://valoda.lv/
- **Valsts valoda** — agency hub for official-language policy, normative documents, and related programs. https://valoda.lv/valsts-valoda/
- **Konsultācijas** — consultation entry points (including person-name rendering and weekly language Q&A). https://valoda.lv/konsultacijas/
- **Par mums** — agency purpose, structure, and service context. https://valoda.lv/par-mums/
- **Māci un mācies** — agency learning portal for materials, levels, and self-study tools. https://maciunmacies.valoda.lv/

## Lexicon and reference

- **Tēzaurs** — large contemporary Latvian lexical resource with headwords, senses, and (when enabled) inflection/corpus views. Autumn 2026 edition advertises 415 514 entries. https://tezaurs.lv/
- **Wikipedia — Latvian language** — overview of Latvian as a Baltic language, orthography, and modern written/spoken norms. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvian_language
- **Wikipedia — T–V distinction** — second-person formality contrasts; Latvian retains a living tu/jūs split unlike universal-du Nordic norms. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T%E2%80%93V_distinction
- **Wiktionary — tu** — Latvian second-person singular informal pronoun. https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/tu#Latvian
- **Wiktionary — jūs** — Latvian second-person plural / polite form. https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/j%C5%ABs#Latvian

## Particles and adjacency

- **Wiktionary — nu** — discourse particle / filler common in casual Latvian. https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/nu#Latvian
- **Wiktionary — jau** — "already" / emphasis particle. https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/jau#Latvian
- **Wiktionary — vai** — interrogative particle. https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/vai#Latvian
- **Wikipedia — Lithuanian language** — sibling Baltic routing when the target is Lithuanian rather than Latvian. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_language
- **Wikipedia — Baltic languages** — family context for Latvian–Lithuanian adjacency without collapsing the two. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltic_languages

## Routing boundaries

- Load `lithuanian`, `russian`, or `polish` when the target language is not Latvian.
- Load `translate` for multi-pair catalogs, ICU/XLIFF workflows, or non-Latvian-first localization jobs.
- Keep this skill on phrasing; it is not a travel, legal, or full language-course product.
