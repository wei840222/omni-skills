# Official Sources & Technical Specifications

This document records the official governmental, immigration, and tourism portals for Romania.

## Official Romanian Portals

- **Inspectoratul General pentru Imigrări (IGI)**: [https://igi.mai.gov.ro/en/](https://igi.mai.gov.ro/en/)
  - Official Romanian immigration framework, Schengen air and maritime entry protocols, residence permits, and Romania Digital Nomad Visa requirements.
- **Romania Tourism Board**: [https://romaniatourism.com/](https://romaniatourism.com/)
  - Official national travel guide, Transylvania cultural monuments, Carpathian mountain safety, and regional transport infrastructure.
