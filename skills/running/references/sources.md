# Sources — running skill

Gate 6 research anchors for endurance-training defaults. Prefer primary reviews; re-verify before citing version-specific numbers or product claims.

## Intensity distribution and heart rate

- [Seiler S. What is best practice for training intensity and duration distribution in endurance athletes? (2010)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20861519/) — polarized / 80–20 intensity-distribution framing for endurance sports
- [Tanaka H, Monahan KD, Seals DR. Age-predicted maximal heart rate revisited (2001)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11153730/) — Tanaka HRmax estimate context vs older `220 − age` heuristics

## Cadence and mechanics

- [Heiderscheit BC et al. Effects of step rate manipulation on joint mechanics (2011)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20581797/) — higher step rate / shorter steps reduce mechanical loading associated with overstride patterns

## Taper and carbohydrate

- [Mujika I, Padilla S. Scientific bases for precompetition tapering strategies (2003)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17465622/) — volume reduction with maintained intensity during taper
- [Burke LM et al. Carbohydrates for training and competition (2011)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21660838/) — duration-linked carbohydrate guidelines for endurance events

## Activity guidelines and coaching boundaries

- [Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans — current guidelines (ODPHP/health.gov)](https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines/current-guidelines) — population activity framing; not a race-plan substitute
- [ACSM physical activity guidelines overview](https://www.acsm.org/education-resources/trending-topics-resources/physical-activity-guidelines) — healthy-adult activity context; persistent pain remains clinician territory
