# Sources - Turkey Skill

Last checked: 2026-09-25

Use these pages before repeating a visa duration, passport-validity rule, airport transfer, museum scope, or emergency number. Refresh visa pathway, airport transport, domestic rail, museum-pass scope, and weather alerts close to departure.

## Entry, visa, and passport validity

- Republic of Türkiye MFA, Visa Information for Foreigners — nationality table, e-Visa scope (tourism or commerce only), and the 60-days-beyond-stay passport rule: https://www.mfa.gov.tr/visa-information-for-foreigners.en.mfa
- MFA, General Information About Turkish Visas — tourist/business visas differ from work and study visas: https://www.mfa.gov.tr/general-information-about-turkish-visas.en.mfa
- MFA, Passport Validity Requirements (Law No. 6458) — passport must expire at least 60 days beyond the visa, e-Visa, visa exemption, or residence duration of stay; a 90-day stay therefore needs 150 days of remaining validity: https://www.mfa.gov.tr/passport-validity-requirements-while-entering-turkey-in-accordance-with-law-on-foreigners-and-international-protection.en.mfa
- Official e-Visa portal — the only application site: https://www.evisa.gov.tr/en/

## Airports and domestic movement

- Istanbul Airport (IST) transportation hub, including urban and intercity options: https://www.istairport.com/en/airport/airport-transportation
- Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) transportation — car, public bus, Havaist, Havabus, taxi, metro, rental: https://www.sabihagokcen.aero/passengers-and-visitors/transport-and-parking/transportation
- TCDD Taşımacılık — domestic rail: https://www.tcddtasimacilik.gov.tr/
- Istanbul Metro: https://www.metro.istanbul/en
- KGM tolls and motorways: https://www.kgm.gov.tr/Sayfalar/KGM/SiteEng/Root/Tolls.aspx

## Museums, weather, and emergencies

- Ministry museums portal (MüzeKart and site list): https://muze.gov.tr/
- Turkish State Meteorological Service city forecasts: https://www.mgm.gov.tr/eng/forecast-cities.aspx
- AFAD disaster and emergency authority: https://en.afad.gov.tr/
- 112 Emergency Call Center: https://112.gov.tr/

- UK FCDO entry requirements for British citizens — visa-free up to 90 days in any 180-day period, and a passport expiry date at least 150 days after arrival: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/turkey/entry-requirements
