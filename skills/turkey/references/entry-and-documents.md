# Entry and Documents - Turkey

Read `sources.md` and the MFA nationality row before stating a visa outcome. Apply only the row for the passport being presented.

## Fast decision tree

1. Identify the passport and travel purpose (tourism/commerce, work, or study).
2. Classify the pathway as visa-free, official e-Visa, or consular/sticker visa.
3. Check passport expiry against the duration of stay, not against a generic "6 months" slogan.
4. Match the passport spelling across flights, hotels, and balloon or tour bookings.
5. Confirm IST versus SAW, or a different arrival and departure pair, before locking the first and last night.

## Visitor pathways

| Path | When it applies | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa-free short stay | The MFA row for that ordinary passport says exempt | Stay length and any 90-in-180 cap on the same row |
| e-Visa | The MFA row sends the traveler to www.evisa.gov.tr | Tourism or commerce only; extra conditions such as a supporting visa |
| Consular / sticker visa | The row says the traveler must apply at a Turkish mission, or the purpose is work or study | Processing time at the mission that covers the traveler |

Official anchors, checked 2026-09-25:

- e-Visa is only for tourism or commerce. Work and study visas come from Turkish embassies or consulates.
- Under Law No. 6458, the passport must remain valid at least 60 days beyond the duration of stay of the visa, e-Visa, visa exemption, or residence permit. MFA's worked example: a 90-day stay needs 150 days of remaining validity at entry.
- Ordinary United States passports are visa-exempt for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. Official United States passports still need a visa. A US tourist stays on the visa-exempt path instead of buying an e-Visa.
- British citizen ordinary passports, per FCDO's reading of Turkish rules, may enter visa-free for up to 90 days in any 180-day period, with a passport expiry date at least 150 days after arrival and at least one blank page.
- People's Republic of China ordinary passports are visa-exempt for up to 90 days in any 180-day period as of 2 January 2026. That date is recent; re-read the MFA row before travel.

## Practical rules

- Use only the official channels in `sources.md`. Aggregator visa blogs drift.
- Solve the pathway before locking non-refundable domestic flights.
- Keep hotel, onward-ticket, and insurance proof easy to surface if an airline or border officer asks.
- If the route continues to Greece or another Schengen country, check that document set separately.

## Booking-name logic

- Match the passport spelling on international flights, domestic flights, and tours.
- If the traveler has more than one passport, anchor the whole Turkey segment to one document set.
- Dual-nationality travelers still get one pathway per passport actually presented at the border.

## Before locking non-refundables

- Entry pathway confirmed for this passport and purpose
- Passport validity checked against the planned duration of stay
- Airport pair confirmed
- Name spelling aligned across bookings
- First and last night logic decided

## Source reminder

Refresh the MFA nationality row and the e-Visa portal close to departure. A rule that was true for a different passport, or true last season, is not evidence for this traveler.
