## Citation rules

1. Pair technique claims with source + scope (student learning research ≠ every novel-reading night).
2. Do not invent page-count laws as universal science; the ~50-page fair-sample quit heuristic is product guidance retained from the pre-refactor skill, not a lab constant.
3. Prefer the user’s goal and constraints over generic “best books” lists.
4. When a URL is blocked or 404 in-environment, record it under failed lookups instead of citing from memory.
