# Bali Visas and Stay Compliance Guide 2026

> Last updated: October 2026 | Verify before purchase: rules can change fast.

## Operating rule

Quote a visa class, duration, fee, or extension path only after opening the current official immigration or e-Visa page for the traveler's nationality and purpose. If that page was not opened in this turn, say exactly what to re-check and do not give a booking-ready legal answer.

Stay permission is not work permission. Tourism entry does not automatically authorize local employment or Indonesian-client business activity.

## Common short-stay reference: Visa on Arrival / B1 family

Official immigration materials commonly describe a Visa on Arrival path used by many eligible visitors for tourism and limited visit purposes. Treat every number below as a **starting checklist**, not a guarantee:

| Item | Typical published reference (re-check live) |
|------|---------------------------------------------|
| Fee | Often published around IDR 500,000 |
| Initial stay | Often up to 30 days |
| Extension | Often one extension path up to an additional 30 days |
| Purpose boundary | Tourism / visit categories only as listed on the live page |

Do not invent a “remote worker VOA”, “default 6-month tourist stay”, or nationality-specific eligibility list from memory.

## Bali Tourist Levy

Separate from visa cost:
- Commonly published at IDR 150,000 per international visitor.
- Pay only through the official Bali tourism levy channel or designated payment points listed by Bali authorities.
- Keep proof; airport and local checks can ask for it.

## Typical Stay Framing (not legal advice)

| Stay Goal | Planning frame |
|-----------|----------------|
| 1-30 days | Short tourist/visit entry if eligible |
| ~31-60 days | Only if the live page still offers an extension path for that entry class |
| Multi-month remote work, study, family move, or local business | Pre-arrange the correct category; do not stretch tourist entry by rumor |

## Compliance Rules

1. Verify activity allowances before using a tourism visa for work or business.
2. Keep digital and printed proof of entry, levy, and payment records.
3. Track expiration dates early; plan extensions or exit with buffer days.
4. Exit before expiry; overstay fines and immigration friction escalate quickly.
5. Name the official URL you checked when giving a final fee or duration.

## Practical Document Checklist

- Passport with sufficient validity for the itinerary.
- Onward/return evidence when requested.
- Accommodation details.
- Funds/means evidence if requested.
- Tourist levy proof for Bali arrivals.

## Risk Traps

- Using forum or agent hearsay as the legal source.
- Confusing remote work for foreign clients with local work permission.
- Assuming every nationality gets the same VOA terms.
- Paying levy or visa fees on unofficial look-alike sites.
