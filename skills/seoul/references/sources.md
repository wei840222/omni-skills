# Sources — Seoul

Back to [Seoul Skill Overview](../SKILL.md).

Use these verified primary sources when transit passes, rental deposit regulations, immigration procedures, or civic services apply in Seoul.

## Municipal Governance & Climate Card

- **Seoul Metropolitan Government (English Portal)** — https://english.seoul.go.kr/
  - Primary municipal administration hub detailing city initiatives, public programs, and the Seoul Climate Card (기후동행카드) transit pass offering unlimited rides on Seoul subway lines, public buses, and Ddareungi public bicycles.

## Urban Subway & Public Transit

- **Seoul Metro** — http://www.seoulmetro.co.kr/en/
  - Operator of Seoul subway Lines 1 through 8. Key reference for fare structures, distance-based surcharges, T-money card compatibility, transfer discount rules, and accessibility facilities.

## Immigration & Alien Registration (ARC)

- **Hi Korea (e-Government for Foreigners)** — https://www.hikorea.go.kr/
  - Official immigration e-services portal for international residents. Covers Alien Registration Card (ARC / Residence Card) appointments, visa status changes, period of stay extensions, and re-entry permits.
- **Korea Immigration Service (Ministry of Justice)** — https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do
  - Statutory guidelines on visa classifications (D-series student, E-series employment, F-series residency, and working holiday).

## Destination Orientation & Cultural Guidance

- **Visit Seoul (Seoul Tourism Organization)** — https://english.visitseoul.net/
  - Official destination guide covering historical districts (Jongno, Bukchon), shopping and nightlife areas (Myeongdong, Hongdae, Gangnam), and seasonal festivals.
