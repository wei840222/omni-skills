# Piano Practice Sources

Gate 6 research notes for pedagogy defaults used in this skill. Prefer primary guidance over forum anecdotes.

## Practice structure and motor learning

- Royal Conservatory of Music, Piano Syllabus — levelled repertoire expectations and progressive difficulty via https://www.rcmusic.com/learning/examinations/academic-resources-and-policies/syllabi-and-related-resources
- Music Teachers National Association (MTNA), Pedagogy resources — structured lesson and practice guidance via https://www.mtna.org/
- Interleaved vs blocked practice in motor skill learning — retention benefit of mixing difficult sections rather than endless single-block repetition via https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4590767/

## Technique and injury prevention

- Performing Arts Medicine Association / related clinician guidance — stop on wrist/forearm pain; address tension and technique before load increases via https://www.artsmed.org/
- NASM musicians' health promotion — hearing, neuromusculoskeletal, and vocal health awareness for performers via https://nasm.arts-accredit.org/accreditation/standards-guidelines/musicians-health-advisories/
- Cleveland Clinic, Repetitive strain / tendinopathy overview — early rest and technique review when pain appears via https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/repitive-strain-injury

## Instrument setup

- Weighted hammer-action keyboards transfer better to acoustic piano technique than light synth actions; confirm key count and action type before advanced repertoire advice.
