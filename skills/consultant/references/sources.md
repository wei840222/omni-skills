# Research Sources - Consultant

Use these sources when refreshing consulting method guidance. Prefer primary methodology pages over secondary summaries.

## Problem structuring and diagnosis

- **MECE principle (McKinsey Explained)** — mutual exclusivity / collective exhaustiveness as a structuring check for issue trees — https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/the-mece-principle
- **Issue trees overview (Case Interview HQ)** — symptom → driver → root-cause layering for ambiguous business problems — https://caseinterview.com/issue-tree
- **Hypothesis-driven problem solving (StrategyU)** — form testable hypotheses before deep analysis — https://strategyu.co/hypothesis-driven-problem-solving/

## Engagement design and scoping

- **Statement of Work essentials (Project Management Institute)** — scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, and decision rights as contract artifacts — https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/statement-work-defining-expectations-6706
- **RACI matrix guidance (Project Management Institute)** — map decision owners, approvers, and operators before recommendations — https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/responsibility-assignment-matrix-raci-model-7526
- **Consulting engagement models (Consultants Mind)** — diagnostic, design, and execution phases as reusable engagement shapes — https://www.consultantsmind.com/

## Decision quality and risk

- **Decision Quality chain (Strategic Decisions Group)** — frame, alternatives, information, values, reasoning, and commitment as decision readiness checks — https://www.sdg.com/thought-leadership/decision-quality/
- **Pre-mortem technique (Harvard Business Review)** — surface failure modes before commitment — https://hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem
- **Cynefin framework overview (Cognitive Edge)** — match analysis depth to problem domain complexity — https://thecynefin.co/about-us/about-cynefin-framework/

## Delivery and execution planning

- **30-60-90 day plan patterns (Harvard Business Review)** — convert recommendations into timed ownership windows — https://hbr.org/2018/06/the-right-way-to-create-a-30-60-90-day-plan
- **OKR guide (What Matters / John Doerr)** — pair recommendations with leading and lagging measures — https://www.whatmatters.com/resources/google-okr-playbook
- **RAID log practice (Association for Project Management)** — risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies as living controls — https://www.apm.org.uk/resources/what-is-project-management/what-is-a-raid-log/

## Notes for Gate 6 maintainers

- Prefer methodology pages with durable URLs over transient blog posts.
- When a recommendation depends on industry benchmarks, cite the primary dataset URL in the engagement file, not only in chat.
- Do not invent financials, timelines, or legal/tax claims; keep claims inside provided evidence and labeled assumptions.
