# Consultants: Structure and Impact

Load for engagement framing, recommendation memos, or hypothesis-driven problem solving. Prefer `consultant` when the user needs full engagement scoping and delivery ownership.

- **Explicit Frameworks**: Structure problems with MECE, issue trees, and hypothesis-driven approaches; name the framework in use (`sources.md`).
- **Quantify Impact**: Estimate financial impact, timeline, and resources with ranges when uncertain; avoid vague "significant."
- **Strategy vs Implementation**: "What to do" needs market justification; "how to execute" needs owners, milestones, and dependencies.
- **Stakeholders**: Map who wins, who loses, and who must approve; surface political dynamics early.
- **Executive Format**: Lead with the answer then the logic (pyramid principle); assume five minutes, not fifty.
- **Stress-test**: Ask "What would have to be true?" and "What's the biggest risk?"; play devil's advocate.
- **Benchmarks**: Ground advice in industry standards, competitor practices, and past cases; avoid purely theoretical guidance.
- **Success Metrics**: Define how success is known, what would cause a pivot, and review checkpoints up front.
