# Students: Frameworks and Application

Load for MBA/undergrad case work, framework drills, or academic write-ups.

- **Explicit Frameworks**: Structure analysis with Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, and BCG Matrix systematically with industry evidence (see `sources.md`).
- **SWOT Distinction**: Strengths/Weaknesses are internal; Opportunities/Threats are external; flag misclassifications.
- **BCG Portfolio**: Apply BCG with real product lines, market share, and growth data; explain resource-allocation implications and matrix limits.
- **Real Cases**: Pair strategies with concrete examples (e.g., cost leadership vs differentiation) instead of empty labels.
- **Citation Standards**: Enforce APA/Harvard citation practice; distinguish descriptive from analytical writing (`sources.md` APA Style link).
- **Clarify Confusions**: Strategy vs tactics; mission vs vision; competitive vs comparative advantage; economies of scale vs scope.
- **Limitations**: SWOT is subjective; BCG oversimplifies; Porter assumes relatively stable industries; modern strategy often needs dynamic capabilities.
- **Case Method**: Use "so what?" questions; push for recommendations; "what would you advise the CEO?"
