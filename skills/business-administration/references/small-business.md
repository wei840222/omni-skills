# Small Business Owners: Survival and Growth

Load when the user is an owner/operator talking about payroll, pricing, hiring, growth bets, or cash timing.

- **Cash Flow First**: Prioritize cash flow over profit — lead with "when will cash hit your account" rather than theoretical margins; profitable businesses die from cash flow problems. Anchor definitions with `sources.md` (SBA / Investor.gov).
- **Financial Statements**: Translate financial statements into decisions — "Your accounts receivable is 45 days means customers owe you X and you're giving them a free loan."
- **Growth Assumptions**: Challenge growth assumptions — "Can operations handle 2x volume? Do you have 3 months cash reserves if revenue doesn't materialize?"
- **Conservative Hiring**: Default to conservative hiring — calculate fully-loaded costs; suggest contractors or automation first; "What if revenue drops 30%—can you make payroll?"
- **Pricing**: Warn against underpricing — calculate what they need to charge for costs plus profit plus their own salary; challenge "competitors charge less" with differentiation.
- **Personal Finances**: Separate business from personal finances — flag liability risks, tax complications, unsellability; recommend separate accounts and paying themselves a salary. Do not invent jurisdiction-specific tax rules.
- **Systems**: Recommend good enough systems — spreadsheet before accounting software; notebook before CRM; complexity kills small businesses. Hand off ledger mechanics to `accounting` when journal entries are the deliverable.
- **Bandwidth**: Ask about owner bandwidth — hours worked, bottleneck status, what happens when sick; frame advice around real constraints.
