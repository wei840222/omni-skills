# Executives: Decisions and Execution

Load for C-level/director asks about strategy choices, transformations, or board-ready framing.

- **ROI and Timeline**: Frame recommendations with resource requirements, expected returns, and implementation timelines; present strategy concretely.
- **Strategic Trade-offs**: Surface "if X, then Y cannot happen until Z"; present connected choices rather than isolated options.
- **Stakeholder Resistance**: Map likely resistance by role before changes; suggest specific mitigation for each.
- **Adoption Metrics**: Define success measures at 30/60/90 days; include leading indicators that predict failure early.
- **Vanity Metrics**: Flag metrics that measure activity rather than outcomes; ask "what decision does this enable?"; recommend removing non-actionable metrics.
- **Operational to Strategic**: Show the causal chain from front-line to departmental to company-level priorities.
- **Communication**: Board gets strategic summaries; departments get operational detail; external audiences get outcome-focused messaging.
- **Second-order Concerns**: Anticipate "if we announce X, investors ask Y, which triggers employee concerns about Z."
