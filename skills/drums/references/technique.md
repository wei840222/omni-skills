# Drum Technique & Strategies

Load before diagnosing form, fills, kit setup, or level-specific mistakes.

## Before advising

- Ask kit — acoustic vs electronic changes rebound, dynamics, and gear advice
- Ask level — notation reading and rudiment vocabulary change the plan
- Ask goals — rock, jazz, session work, and hobby practice diverge

## Practice errors

- Speeding up fills — rushing is the #1 amateur tell; always use a click
- Neglecting weak hand — doubles and left-lead rudiments expose it
- Playing with full power only — train dynamics and ghost notes deliberately
- Skipping rudiments — singles, doubles, paradiddles, flams are vocabulary

## Technique traps

- Death grip — keep a loose grip so fingers manage rebound
- Hitting from the shoulder for everything — wrist for speed, arm for power
- Burying the bass beater — allow rebound for faster doubles
- Throne too low — thighs angled slightly down for free hip motion

## Mistakes by level

**Beginners:** Inconsistent tempo, ignoring hi-hat foot, fills that lose the beat

**Intermediate:** Overplaying, weak left foot, cannot lock with bass

**Advanced:** Stylistic ruts, neglecting brushes, chops prioritized over groove

## Rudiment priority

| Priority | Rudiments | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| Essential | Single, double, paradiddle | Foundation |
| Important | Flams, drags, flamacue | Accents and ornament vocabulary |
| Advanced | Swiss triplets, ratamacues | Speed and hybrid control |

Start clean at **60 BPM**, then add **5 BPM** only when both leads stay relaxed and even.

## Groove vs chops

- Groove pays bills — hired for feel, not fills
- Prefer a simple solid beat over a complex shaky one
- If a fill loses beat 1, it is the wrong fill for the job

## Troubleshooting

- "Fills lose tempo" → practice the fill **into** beat 1 of the next bar with a click
- "Bass doubles weak" → heel-up where appropriate; check beater spring and rebound
- "Can't play quiet" → deliberate *pp* practice and ghost-note drills
- "Hands not synced" → paradiddles at **40 BPM**, even strokes, no tension

## Electronic kits

Prefer mesh heads for stick rebound that transfers to acoustic kits. Rubber pads train compressed rebound and build habits that fight acoustic feel.

## Hearing

Hearing damage is permanent. Default to earplugs around **-15 dB** for practice and live volume; raise protection rather than "getting used to" loud kits.
