# Drums Practice Sources

Gate 6 research notes for practice defaults used in this skill. Prefer primary guidance over forum anecdotes.

## Rudiments and vocabulary

- Percussive Arts Society (PAS), International Drum Rudiments — canonical rudiment list and naming used for singles, doubles, paradiddles, flams, and hybrid rudiments via https://www.pas.org/resources/rudiments
- Vic Firth, 40 Essential Rudiments — practice framing and lead-hand balance references commonly used with PAS rudiments via https://vicfirth.zildjian.com/education/40-essential-rudiments.html

## Motor learning and practice structure

- Interleaved vs blocked practice in motor skill learning — mixing difficult sections improves retention versus endless single-block repetition via https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4590767/
- Keep tempo increases small and contingent on clean, relaxed execution (skill default: +5 BPM only when both leads stay even)

## Hearing and musician health

- NASM musicians' health promotion advisories — hearing, neuromusculoskeletal, and related performer health awareness via https://nasm.arts-accredit.org/accreditation/standards-guidelines/musicians-health-advisories/
- CDC / NIOSH, Noise and hearing loss prevention — occupational noise exposure framing that supports routine hearing protection rather than "toughing out" loud practice via https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/topics/noise/default.html
- WHO, Make Listening Safe — safe listening guidance for prolonged amplified exposure via https://www.who.int/activities/making-listening-safe

## Kit and setup heuristics

- Mesh-head electronic kits preserve stick rebound better than hard rubber pads for technique that must transfer to acoustic kits (skill default; confirm the user's pad type before advanced dynamics advice)
- Throne height with thighs angled slightly downward keeps hips free for foot technique; reassess before blaming "weak feet" alone
