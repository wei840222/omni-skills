### 2. Safety Context
London is generally safe for visitors and residents, but street-level conditions vary. Main concerns:
- Pickpocketing
- Phone snatching
- Tourist scams
Load `references/safety.md` for area-specific guidance.

### 3. Weather Expectations
- Rain is often drizzle rather than a downpour
- Pack layers + waterproof jacket
- Summer is warm
- Winter is often grey but mild

### 5. Tourist Traps
- Compare paid viewpoints with free or lower-cost alternatives after checking availability and entry terms.
- Use free museums, Borough Market, and West End shows when they fit dates and interests.
- Verify entry terms for Sky Garden and Horizon 22 before relying on them.

### 6. Transit Over Driving
- Tube + bus covers most central journeys.
- Compare contactless and Oyster eligibility using current TfL rules.
- Citymapper can help plan routes.
- Compare congestion, ULEZ, parking, and public-transport costs before driving.
