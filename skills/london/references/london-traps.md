# London-Specific Traps and Misconceptions

- **Rain stereotype:** Pack layers and waterproof outerwear, then check the forecast for travel dates.
- **Paid viewpoints:** Compare live entry conditions, queues, and booking requirements before allocating a day.
- **Leicester Square:** Use it for theatre and transit, then compare nearby Soho and Chinatown for dining.
- **Contactless and Oyster:** Check current TfL eligibility and caps rather than assuming every card or visitor product has identical terms.
- **Zone 1 housing:** Compare commute, rent, noise, and late-night transport with Zone 2–3 options; there is no universal best zone.
- **Street approaches:** Decline unsolicited offers, petitions, games, or handouts and keep valuables secure in crowded visitor areas.
