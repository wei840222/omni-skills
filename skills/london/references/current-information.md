# Current Information and Source Checks

Use this reference when an answer depends on a fare, price, availability, visa rule, public-service policy, venue access, safety condition, or another time-sensitive detail. Treat the package's dated ranges and venue examples as planning material, not live quotes.

## Check the Current Source Before a Decisive Answer

| Topic | Authoritative source | What to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Public transport, caps, and service status | Transport for London — https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/ and https://tfl.gov.uk/plan-a-journey/ | Current fare products, caps, routes, disruptions, accessibility, and contactless/Oyster eligibility. |
| Airport transfers | The relevant airport and rail operator | Availability, operating hours, routing, luggage rules, and live prices. |
| Visitor information and attractions | Visit London — https://www.visitlondon.com/ and the venue's official site | Opening hours, timed entry, accessibility, event dates, and admission price. |
| UK visas, work, and immigration | GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk/browse/visas-immigration | Nationality-specific eligibility, salary thresholds, fees, application steps, and processing information. |
| Renting and tenancy rights | GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk/private-renting | Current tenant rights, deposits, and tenancy process. |
| NHS and urgent care | NHS — https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/gps/how-to-register-with-a-gp-surgery/ and https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/urgent-and-emergency-care-services/ | GP registration, urgent-care route, and eligibility details. |
| Weather and severe-weather planning | Met Office — https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/weather/forecast/ | Date-specific forecast, warnings, and travel-impact conditions. |
| Council tax and local services | The relevant London borough's official website | Band, discounts, collection schedules, and local-service rules for the exact address. |

If the official source is unavailable, say that the live value could not be verified. Keep a package figure only as a dated estimate and direct the user to the listed authority; do not present it as a current official price or rule.

## Freshness Classes

- **Time-sensitive:** transport fares and caps, airport pricing, rent, salary, food and hotel prices, tax, visas, NHS eligibility and access, venue admission, service schedules, startup funding, and local business availability.
- **Stable-domain:** neighborhood tradeoffs, broad transit-system roles, common scam patterns, and general itinerary principles. Qualify street-level safety and venue advice when the user needs a high-stakes or same-day decision.

## Practical Answer Pattern

1. Identify dates, nationality or residence status when relevant, budget, location, and accessibility needs.
2. Load the matching topical reference from `SKILL.md`.
3. Verify time-sensitive facts at the source above before a booking, immigration, payment, or safety recommendation.
4. Separate verified facts from estimates and explain any live-information gap.
