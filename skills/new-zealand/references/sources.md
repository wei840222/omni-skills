## Notes for agents

- Treat ferry schedules, hut availability, fees, and visa/biosecurity rules as
  perishable. Open the official URL before stating a current requirement.
- Prefer Immigration NZ, MPI, Customs, NZTA, DOC, Police, and Health NZ pages
  over secondary travel blogs when border, road, or safety rules matter.
- Tourism marketing sites can 403 automated fetchers; still point humans to the
  official brand/region pages when useful, and do not invent live inventory.
