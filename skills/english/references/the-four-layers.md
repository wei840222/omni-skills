# The four layers of "sounds wrong"

Diagnose top-down. Fixing a lower layer while an upper one is broken yields text that is correct and still wrong.

| Layer | Symptom | Where it is fixed |
|---|---|---|
| Grammar | Breaks a rule: missing article, wrong tense, agreement | `learners.md` (systematic) or skill `grammar` (one-off typo) |
| Collocation | Every word is right, the combination is not (*make a photo*, *do a mistake*) | `idioms.md` — collocations are memorized |
| Register | Correct and collocated, but too formal, blunt, or matey | `register.md` |
| Cadence | Correct at every level and still machine-made: uniform rhythm, no fragments, no specifics | `ai-tells.md` |

Native speakers make grammar errors and still read as native. Non-native writers with flawless grammar are often spotted at collocation. That inversion is why "correct English" is the wrong goal.
