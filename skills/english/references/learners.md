# Learners and L1 interference

Fix the **class**, not only the sentence (Core Rule 8). Use `first_language` when set; otherwise infer from the error pattern and state the assumption.

## Article procedure (five steps)

When an article is missing or wrong:

1. Is the noun countable in this sense?
2. Is it singular countable? If yes, it almost always needs *a/an* or *the* (or a determiner).
3. Is the referent identifiable to the reader (unique, previous mention, shared context)? → *the*
4. Is it generic?
   - plural/uncountable generic → usually no article (*Dogs bark*, *Water freezes*)
   - singular countable generic → *a* or *the* depending on meaning (*A shark is a fish* vs *The shark is endangered* as a class in some styles)
5. Fixed expressions and institutions keep their own patterns (*at school*, *in hospital* [GB], *to church*) — do not "logic" them away.

Do not fix articles by ear when the user is systematically wrong; walk the procedure and record the class on second occurrence.

## Prepositions and verb+preposition

Preposition choice after verbs and adjectives is largely collocational. Wrong *in/on/at*, *for/to*, *depend of*, *discuss about* are class errors:

- Teach the chunk: *depend on*, *interested in*, *discuss [topic]* (no *about* in many careful styles), *arrive at/in*, *good at*.
- Store the whole chunk in `## Vocabulary`, not the bare verb.

## High-frequency L1 patterns (illustrative)

| Pattern | Often seen when L1… | Class fix |
|---|---|---|
| *the life is hard*, *the nature is beautiful* | article systems differ (e.g. Slavic, some East Asian) | generic abstract: no *the* |
| *I am knowing*, *I am agreeing* | progressive extended to statives | stative verb list; simple present |
| *I have 25 years* | age expressed with *have* | *I am 25* |
| *I am agree* | adjectival participle pattern | *I agree* |
| *make a photo*, *do a mistake* | collocation transfer | *take a photo*, *make a mistake* |
| *almost people*, *all of employee* | quantifier + noun shape | *almost all people*, *all employees* |
| tense for narratives | aspect systems differ | timeline + simple past vs present perfect contrast for the active variety |

This table is a starting diagnostic map, not a complete typological atlas. Prefer pattern evidence in the user's text over stereotype.

## Procedure when the same error repeats

1. First occurrence: correct in place (mode from `correction_mode`).
2. Second occurrence: name the class in one line, give the rule, append `## Recurring Errors` in `<state_root>/memory.md`.
3. Later sessions: open the journal before generic tips; drill the class (`practice.md`).
