# Register

Match the relationship and channel, not maximum politeness (Core Rule 1).

## Formality ladder

| Rung | Label | Typical channels | Moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intimate | partner, close friend, family chat | Fragments OK; slang OK; almost no hedging |
| 2 | Casual | team chat, known peers | Contractions; light idiom; short ask first |
| 3 | Neutral (default) | general email, docs, mixed audience | Clear ask; one hedge max; concrete specifics |
| 4 | Professional | client, external partner, boss you do not know well | Full sentences; title care; hedge the *ask*, not the whole message |
| 5 | Formal | legal, official, high-stakes ceremony | Full forms; controlled vocabulary; longer mean sentence length |

**One-notch rule:** if their last reply was shorter and less formal than yours, drop one rung. If the text is too casual for the reader, raise one rung only — do not jump 2→5.

## Plain English

Prefer short common words when they carry the same meaning. Cut empty openers ("I am writing to…", "Please be advised that…"). Put the ask or decision early unless the culture or relationship requires soft landing first.

US plain-language guidance stresses simple words and concise sentences for public-facing text; apply that pressure on rungs 2–4 when the reader is busy or international. Keep controlled idiom only when the goal is to persuade a fluent in-group audience.

## Sentence-length bands (per rung)

| Rung | Mean words | Hard ceiling (`max_sentence_words` default 25; rungs 4–5 may raise toward 35) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 8–14 | Split anything past the ceiling |
| 3 | 14–20 with spread ≥ ±7 | Core Rule 3 |
| 4 | 16–24 | Prefer clarity over flourish |
| 5 | 20–30 | Longer clauses OK if structure stays parseable |

Every paragraph needs at least one sentence under 8 words on rungs 1–4.

## Contraction density

Casual/neutral: about one contraction every 2–3 sentences (Core Rule 2). Full forms stay for emphatic negation, formal/legal register, and sentence-final copulas ("Yes, I am").

## Channel cues

| Channel | Default rung bias | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SMS / DM | 1–2 | Fragments and emoji tolerance from user prefs |
| Slack / Teams | 2–3 | Thread context can drop greetings |
| Email external | 3–4 | Subject line carries the ask when possible |
| Report / policy | 4–5 | House style overrides ladder defaults |
