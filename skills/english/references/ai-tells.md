# AI tells and repairs

Cadence failures survive perfect grammar. Diagnose here after Grammar / Collocation / Register are clean (`the-four-layers.md`).

## High-signal tells

| Tell | What it looks like | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Closing summary paragraph | Restates the body with "In conclusion" / "Overall" / "In summary" | Delete the paragraph; end on the action or the last concrete point |
| Uniform sentence length | Three+ consecutive sentences within ~3 words of each other | Force spread: one under 8 words, one compound, one mid-length |
| Uniform paragraph length | Every block is 3–4 lines of similar density | Vary block size; allow a one-line paragraph |
| Abstract nouns without numbers | "several challenges", "in the near future", "a number of stakeholders" | Replace with a count, name, date, or delete |
| Stacked formal connectors | However / Furthermore / Therefore / Moreover chains | Swap to But / So / And / Also / end-position though |
| Empty empathy openers | "I hope this email finds you well", "I wanted to reach out regarding" | Start with the reason or the ask |
| Symmetrical three-item rhetoric | Forced tricolons with no informational need | Keep only load-bearing items |
| Safe corporate verbs | utilize, facilitate, leverage, streamline, unlock | use, help, use, simplify, open — or name the real verb |
| Zero contractions in casual text | 200 words of informal prose with no *'s / 're / 've* | Restore natural contraction density (`register.md`) |
| No specifics | Praise or critique without a named detail | Add one concrete (file, date, metric, quote) |

## Weaker signals (do not overfit)

- Em dash frequency alone — fix the rhythm around punctuation, do not purge dashes by policy.
- Presence of lists — lists are fine when they carry distinct items.
- Polite closings — "Thanks" is not a tell; "Please do not hesitate to contact me should you require any further assistance" is.

## Repair order

1. Cut summary / throat-clearing openers.
2. Break length uniformity (sentences, then paragraphs).
3. Inject one concrete specific.
4. Demote formal connectors and corporate verbs.
5. Re-check register for the actual reader.
