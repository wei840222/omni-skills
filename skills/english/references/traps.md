# Traps

| Trap | Why it fails | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Fixing "sounds wrong" by adding formality | Formality often *causes* the wrong sound | Diagnose the layer first (`the-four-layers.md`) |
| Learning single words instead of collocations | Wrong verb marks non-native more than rare nouns | Store whole chunks in `## Vocabulary` |
| Copying idioms from film/TV | Slang ages quickly; dated slang is a loud tell | Prefer plain wording when freshness is unclear (`idioms.md`) |
| Mixing varieties in one document | Reads as three authors or a TM dump | One variety, one sweep |
| Treating British understatement as literal | "That's interesting" / "with respect" often soften disagreement | `business.md` understatement table |
| Over-apologizing | Multiple apologies transfer discomfort and read junior | One apology, first line, then the fix |
| Deleting every em dash to avoid "AI style" | Frequency alone is a weak signal; uniform rhythm is the tell | Fix cadence and specificity |
| "Native-like" as the only goal past intermediate | Above independent user levels, accent/idiom yield less than precision and collocation | Target intelligibility + collocation (`practice.md`, CEFR-oriented) |
| Correcting spoken English mid-conversation | Interrupts fluency more than the error costs | Batch after, from `## Recurring Errors` |
| Hedging a decision already made | Invites renegotiation of a closed question | State it, then offer the escape hatch |
| Blanket passive ban | Passive is right when actor is unknown, irrelevant, or withheld | Ban only where actor matters |
| Same correction for months with no record | Sessions never compound | Second occurrence → `## Recurring Errors` |
