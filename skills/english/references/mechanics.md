# Mechanics

House rules with variety splits. Defaults below; user Conventions in `config.yaml` win.

## Commas

- **Serial (Oxford) comma:** default on (`oxford_comma: true`). Chicago and APA require the serial comma in series; some UK news styles omit it. Keep it unless a named house style forbids it — absence can create ambiguity; presence rarely does (`sources.md`).
- **Comma splice:** two independent clauses joined by only a comma → period, semicolon, or coordinator.
- **Introductory clauses:** comma after a fronted subordinate clause when it aids scanning; do not fake complexity with comma stacks.

## Hyphens and dashes

- Compound modifiers before a noun: hyphenate when ambiguity exists (*small-business owner*).
- En dash for ranges when the house style uses it; em dash for parenthetical breaks — do not purge em dashes merely to look "less AI" (`ai-tells.md`).
- *email* not *e-mail* unless house style says otherwise.

## Quotes and titles

- Follow `variety` for quote-period order (`varieties.md`).
- Titles of works: follow house style (italics vs quotes). In plain text, *italics* markup or quotes consistently inside one document.
- Nested quotes: alternate double/single per variety convention.

## Capitalization

- Sentence case for most headings unless house style mandates title case.
- Capitalize proper nouns and official names; do not capitalize job titles mid-sentence without house reason.

## Dates, times, numbers, currency

- Prefer unambiguous forms in cross-border text: `7 October 2026` or `2026-10-07`, never bare `07/08/2026`.
- en-US short dates are m/d/y; en-GB d/m/y — state variety when short forms must appear.
- Numbers: spell small integers in running prose when house style expects it; keep numerals for metrics, versions, and comparisons.
- Currency: ISO code or symbol consistently (`USD 40`, `$40`); space per house style.

## Lists

- Parallel structure across items.
- Terminal punctuation: none for short phrase lists; periods for full-sentence lists — one pattern per list.
