# Sources — Madeira

Back to [Madeira Skill Overview](../SKILL.md).

Use these verified primary sources when hiking safety, nomad hubs, regional governance, or climate conditions apply in Madeira.

## Regional Government & Public Administration

- **Governo Regional da Madeira** — https://www.madeira.gov.pt/
  - Autonomous Region of Madeira official government portal covering regional legislation, public services, civil protection advisories, and administrative infrastructure.

## Nature, Levadas & Trail Safety

- **IFCN (Instituto das Florestas e Conservação da Natureza)** — https://ifcn.madeira.gov.pt/
  - Official nature conservation and forestry agency managing classified walking routes (PR trails and Levadas such as PR25 Levada das 25 Fontes, PR9 Levada do Caldeirão Verde, and PR1 Pico do Arieiro). Provides real-time trail status, weather closures, eco-tax regulations, and safety mandates (sturdy footwear, headlamps for tunnels).

## Digital Nomad Community & Innovation

- **Startup Madeira (Digital Nomads Madeira)** — https://startupmadeira.eu/
  - Regional innovation agency pioneering the Ponta do Sol Digital Nomad Village, co-working spaces, community networking hubs, and nomad infrastructure across Madeira and Porto Santo.

## Island Destination Guidance

- **Visit Madeira (Associação de Promoção da Madeira)** — https://visitmadeira.com/en/
  - Official tourism board detailing island microclimates (sunny south coast vs lush rainy north coast), scenic viewpoints (Miradouros), natural volcanic swimming pools (Porto Moniz), and mountain road safety.
