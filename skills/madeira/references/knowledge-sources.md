# Recent Updates & Domain Knowledge

## NHR (Non-Habitual Resident) Tax Regime
The NHR tax regime grants a set of tax exemptions and flat rate taxation for a period of 10 years for qualifying expat professionals, pensioners, and passive income earners in Portugal (including Madeira). It was designed to attract high-value individuals. Recent years have seen changes phasing out broad applicability for new applicants in favor of more targeted incentives.
