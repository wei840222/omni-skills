# Madeira Domain Knowledge and Sources

Use these sources when mutable Madeira guidance matters. Prefer official portals for visas, tax, transit, and tourism claims; use encyclopedia pages only for stable geography context.

## Visas, Residency, and Immigration

- **Portuguese government portal** — entry point for residency and citizen services via https://www.gov.pt/
- **ACM / High Commission for Migration** — migrant and residency orientation pages via https://www.acm.gov.pt/
- **ePortugal** — national service catalogue for residency and citizen procedures via https://eportugal.gov.pt/

Verify current D7 / digital-nomad / registration steps on official portals before presenting eligibility, fees, or timelines. Immigration brand names and form routes have shifted (e.g., SEF → AIMA), so treat older skill wording as historical unless confirmed live.

## Tax Residency and NHR

- **Portal das Finanças** — Portuguese tax authority home for residency and filing via https://www.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/
- **ACM NHR orientation** — public guidance on Non-Habitual Resident framing via https://www.acm.gov.pt/

The historic NHR regime offered multi-year incentives for new tax residents and is described in-skill as being phased out / narrowed for new applicants. Confirm the currently open successor incentive (if any), eligibility windows, and filing path on Portal das Finanças or a licensed adviser before advising an applicant.

## Tourism, Levadas, and Island Logistics

- **Visit Madeira** — destination guidance and activity orientation via https://www.visitmadeira.com/
- **Visit Portugal — Madeira** — national tourism overview via https://www.visitportugal.com/en/destinos/madeira
- **Horários do Funchal** — local bus operator information via https://www.horariosdofunchal.pt/
- **Madeira Airport** — airport operations orientation via https://www.madeiraairport.com/

## Stable Geography Context

- **Wikipedia — Madeira** — island geography and orientation via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeira
- **Wikipedia — Madeira Airport** — challenging approach / wind closure context via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeira_Airport
- **ICAO** — aviation safety orientation (not airport-specific ops) via https://www.icao.int/

## Obsolete / Fragile Claims to Re-check

- Exact rent bands, bus fares, restaurant prices, and coworking rates in `SKILL.md` / cost references are estimates and must be revalidated before quoting as current.
- Visa brand names, NHR successor incentives, and Golden Visa real-estate route status are time-sensitive.
- Mountain weather, airport wind closures, and levada difficulty remain operational traps; pair traps guidance with live webcams / operator notices when a same-day decision depends on them.
