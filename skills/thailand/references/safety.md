# Safety and Risk - Thailand

Checked against FCDO Thailand advice on 24 September 2026 (safety page and entry page updated 18 September 2026). Re-check before a dated trip.

## Risk model

Thailand is manageable for most travelers who keep routine discipline. The highest practical risks are road incidents, legal missteps, and environment-driven exposure: heat, storms, and smoke.

## Top risk domains

1. Road safety, especially motorcycles.
2. Legal exposure from visa status, work activity, speech, and controlled substances.
3. Heat and dehydration.
4. Air-quality events in Bangkok and north Thailand.
5. Water and marine conditions on island routes.
6. The far south and the Cambodia land-border belt, where official travel advice currently restricts non-essential travel.

## Urban safety defaults

- Use licensed transport and app-logged rides.
- Plan late-night transport before drinking. Keep drinks in sight.
- Keep document copies and emergency contacts offline.
- Do not hand over a passport as a rental deposit.

## Legal-risk defaults

- Treat monarchy-related speech and online posts as high legal-risk territory. Avoid commentary rather than testing the boundary.
- Cannabis use in Thailand is restricted to medical purposes with a Thai-issued prescription, limited to a 30-day supply. Recreational use is illegal. Do not buy, possess, or carry cannabis out of Thailand. Export without permission starts at a THB 30,000 per kilogram penalty, with detention if unpaid.
- Keep visa and work status documented. A tourism or visa-exempt stay is not a work permit.
- FCDO advises against all but essential travel to Pattani, Yala, Narathiwat, and the Chana, Thepa, Na Thawi, and Saba Yoi districts of Songkhla, plus the Hat Yai–Padang Besar train line through those provinces.
- FCDO also advises against all but essential travel within 20 km of the Thailand–Cambodia land border. Crossings and some border temples remain closed after 2025 fighting. Do not route a casual trip through that belt.

## Road defaults

- Thailand has a high motorcycle fatality burden. Prefer cars, rail, or licensed ride-hailing when the user does not already hold the correct licence.
- A hired motorcycle must be registered for public-road use, and the rider must hold the licence their insurer requires. By law, wear a helmet.
- For a UK licence, carry the 1968 international driving permit plus the home licence. An unlicensed ride can invalidate insurance.

## Environment

- Check Air4Thai before promising a Chiang Mai or Bangkok outdoor plan in burning season or a high-PM episode.
- Keep a buffer day for ferries and speedboats when the marine forecast is unstable.

## Fraud and scam patterns

- Inflated transport or tour pricing from unverified operators.
- Urgent booking pressure with no cancellation terms.
- Rental and deposit disputes where the paperwork is weak.

## Emergency baseline

- Save Tourist Police and local emergency numbers offline. FCDO lists 191 for an ambulance and 1669 as a local medical number.
- Know the nearest private hospital in each planned base. Private hospitals can be high quality and expensive; they may require a payment guarantee before treatment.
- For island travel, keep a weather-aware way back to the departure city.
