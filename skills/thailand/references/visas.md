# Visa and Legal Stay Strategy - Thailand

## Core principle

Visa planning is a dynamic, nationality-specific system. Open the official page for the user's nationality and travel date before naming a duration, fee, or booking step. Stay permission is not permission to work.

Checked against official pages on 24 September 2026. Re-check before the user pays or travels.

## Current anchors

These are source-backed snapshots, not a substitute for the user's own check:

- **Tourist visa (consular PDF):** THB 1,000 per entry. Single-entry validity is 3 months; multiple-entry validity is 6 months. On arrival, stay does not exceed 60 days. Employment in Thailand is prohibited. The PDF lists evidence of THB 20,000 per person or THB 40,000 per family. Extension or a change of visa type is decided by Immigration, not by this skill.
- **Digital arrival card:** from 1 May 2025, foreign nationals entering by air, land, or sea complete the TDAC online. Registration opens within 3 days before arrival.
- **Visa exemption, British citizen passport, from 15 September 2026:** up to 30 days, tourism only, normally two exemption entries per calendar year. It does not cover business travel, conferences, or meetings. Proof of funds may be requested at THB 10,000 per person or THB 20,000 per family, plus onward or return travel. Overstay is THB 500 per day, capped at THB 20,000, with detention, deportation, or a re-entry ban as further risk.
- **Work, study, retirement, or residence:** obtain the matching visa before travel. Do not plan those activities on exemption or tourist status.
- **LTR:** use the BOI LTR portal for high-potential categories. Confirm the category criteria there; do not invent income thresholds.
- **DTV:** treat it as a named remote-work pathway only after the current consular or e-Visa page lists it for that nationality. Do not quote a 180-day stay or a 5-year validity unless that same official page states it.

## Response pattern

1. Ask nationality, purpose, dates, and whether any work or business activity is planned.
2. Name one or two candidate pathways and the official page that confirms each.
3. State the stay-versus-work boundary and the fact that would invalidate the plan.
4. If the official page was not opened for this answer, label the recommendation as unverified and stop before flights, leases, or fees.
