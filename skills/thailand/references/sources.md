| Consular visa overview | https://consular.mfa.go.th/th/page/cate-7393-general-information?menu=5d68c88b15e39c160c0081e0 | Current public index; replaces the retired `mfa.go.th` publicservice visa URL |

| British-passport entry snapshot | https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/thailand/entry-requirements | Updated 18 September 2026: from 15 September 2026, visa-exempt stay is 30 days, tourism only, normally two entries per calendar year; TDAC from 1 May 2025; overstay THB 500/day up to THB 20,000 |

## Freshness rules

1. Re-open the consular or e-Visa page before any recommendation that changes flights, a lease, or a fee.
2. Use a current Air4Thai reading and TMD forecast for a dated outdoor or island plan.
3. Keep prices as ranges tagged with month and year.
4. Send business and tax execution to a Thai lawyer or licensed accountant before filing.
