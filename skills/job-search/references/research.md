# Research Patterns — Job Search

Load before company diligence, offer comparison, or remote/comp claims.

## Company Due Diligence

### Financial Health
- Funding rounds and runway signals
- News: layoffs, hiring freezes, acquisitions
- Headcount trends (growing vs shrinking)

### Culture Signals
- Filter reviews to the last 6 months
- Average tenure markers
- Leadership turnover on team pages

### Red Flag Checklist
| Check | Red Flag |
|-------|----------|
| Job posting age | >90 days → ghost role or chronic turnover |
| Salary transparency | "DOE" / missing range → lowball risk |
| Interview rounds | >5 rounds → indecisive process |
| Review sentiment | "Great people, bad management" pattern |
| Growth claims | "Hyper-growth" with flat headcount |

## Offer Evaluation

### Salary Research
- Levels.fyi for tech ranges when relevant
- Employer/review salary tools with recency filters
- Anonymous peer comps only as soft signals
- Always verify recency — ranges stale quickly after market shifts

### Total Compensation
Compare total compensation, not base alone:
- Equity: vesting schedule, strike price, 409A freshness
- Benefits: healthcare cost, PTO policy
- Remote policy: truly remote vs flexible hybrid
- Growth: promotion velocity, learning budget

For freshness-sensitive legal or market citations, load `references/sources.md`.
