# Job Search Sources

Load this file before citing discrimination risk, compensation benchmarks, or other freshness-sensitive job-market claims. Prefer the primary page over memory.

## Authoritative sources (verified reachable in this handoff)

- **EEOC prohibited employment policies/practices** —
  https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices —
  official U.S. framing for discriminatory advertising and hiring practices
  (including age-coded language risks such as “young dynamic team”).
- **Levels.fyi compensation explorer** —
  https://www.levels.fyi/ —
  tech total-compensation ranges; always re-check the live page before quoting
  a number because market data moves quickly.
- **Levels.fyi comp landing** —
  https://www.levels.fyi/comp.html —
  alternate entry for role/level compensation browsing.
- **USA.gov jobs and unemployment hub** —
  https://www.usa.gov/jobs-and-unemployment —
  government-facing orientation for job search and unemployment resources.
- **SHRM talent-acquisition news** —
  https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition —
  practitioner coverage of hiring-process norms; use as secondary context, not
  statute.
- **Agent Skills specification** —
  https://agentskills.io/specification —
  format authority for this skill package’s frontmatter and packaging rules.

## Knowledge updates applied in this refactor

1. **Age-coded hiring language:** Treat phrases like “young dynamic team” as a
   discrimination-risk signal and route legal framing to EEOC prohibited
   practices rather than informal culture advice alone.
2. **Compensation freshness:** Prefer Levels.fyi live pages for tech ranges and
   refuse to invent stale multi-year salary bands from memory.
3. **Remote claims:** Keep geographic, tax/legal, and timezone checks as
   first-class diligence; do not equate “flexible” with truly remote without
   evidence from the posting or employer policy.

## Claim hygiene

- Quote market numbers only after opening a current source.
- Separate culture hearsay from legal risk language.
- Region-specific labor rules need local primary sources beyond this package.
