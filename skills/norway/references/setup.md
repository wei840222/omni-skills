# Setup - Norway Travel Guide

Create `<state_root>/data/norway/` on first use and initialize `<state_root>/data/norway/memory.md` from `references/memory-template.md`.

## First Contact

Answer the immediate Norway question first, then capture only the facts that change the route:
- Dates or month range
- Fjords, cities, Arctic winter, hiking, road trip, family, or mixed priorities
- Tolerance for ferries, regional flights, and long drives
- Snow-driving confidence and outdoor comfort
- Budget band and pace
- Whether Svalbard, Lofoten, or a long scenic route is part of the dream plan

## What To Save

Keep durable facts in memory:
- Travel window and date flexibility
- Main corridor under consideration
- Confirmed bases, flights, or car decisions
- Outdoor ambition and weather tolerance
- Mobility needs, traveler mix, and child setup
- Open decisions such as "fjords vs Lofoten" or "train-first vs drive-first"

## Returning Users

Read `<state_root>/data/norway/memory.md`, reuse what is still valid, and ask only what changed:
- Dates
- Region focus
- Bookings already made
- Car, rail, ferry, or flight decisions
- Hiking or aurora priorities

## Quick Start Prompts

**"I want fjords"**
- Ask total days, arrival city, and whether the user wants a rail-and-ferry trip or a self-drive loop.
- Then use `references/bergen-and-western-fjords.md`, `references/fjords-and-scenic-routes.md`, `references/itineraries.md`, and `references/road-trips-and-driving.md`.

**"I want northern lights"**
- Ask month, cloud-risk tolerance, and whether the user is open to flexible plans or just one fixed base.
- Then use `references/tromso-and-arctic-north.md`, `references/weather-and-seasonality.md`, `references/budget-and-costs.md`, and `references/safety-and-emergencies.md`.

**"I need a full Norway route"**
- Ask trip length, arrival airport, and whether the user wants one corridor or accepts internal flights.
- Then use `references/regions.md`, `references/itineraries.md`, `references/transport-domestic.md`, and `references/accommodation.md`.

## Important Notes

- Norway quality comes from fewer bases with stronger logistics, not from attraction count.
- Seasonality changes what is practical more than in many European trips.
- Ferries, scenic roads, and Arctic weather create hidden timing risk even on short distances.
- If the user has no winter-driving experience, default to transit-first or base-first plans.
