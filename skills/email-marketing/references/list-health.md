# List Health And Segmentation

Use this for hygiene, growth quality, and who should receive what.

## Health Metrics

| Metric | Healthy | Warning | Critical |
|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| Open rate | >20% | 10–20% | <10% |
| Click rate | >2% | 1–2% | <1% |
| Bounce rate | <2% | 2–5% | >5% |
| Unsubscribe | <0.5% | 0.5–1% | >1% |
| Spam complaints | <0.1% | 0.1–0.3% | >0.3% |

Open-rate absolute values drift with privacy changes (e.g., Apple MPP); prefer trends, clicks, and complaints together.

## Growth Rules

- Grow organically; never buy lists.
- Prefer confirmed/double opt-in for public signup forms.
- Separate marketing vs transactional consent where required.

## Minimum Viable Segments

| Segment | Definition | Typical use |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| New | Joined recently; still in welcome | Welcome sequence only |
| Engaged | Opened/clicked within ~30 days | Core campaigns |
| Unengaged | No engagement past window | Re-engagement then sunset |
| Customers | Purchased or activated | Onboarding / cross-sell |
| VIP | Highest engagement or LTV | Early access, exclusives |

Tag meaningful clicks to build behavioral profiles instead of relying only on demographics.

## Sunset Policy

1. Identify unengaged cohort (commonly 60–90 days).
2. Send a short re-engagement series with easy preference center.
3. Suppress or remove remaining non-responders to protect reputation.

List size without engagement is a liability, not an asset.
