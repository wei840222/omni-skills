# Question Anti-Patterns

Rewrite the stem; keep the intent.

## Leading questions

**Pattern:** The desired answer is already inside the question.

**Rewrite toward:**

- "How do you feel about the new design?"
- "How did you feel when that happened?"
- "What's your perspective on…"

**Weak stems to rewrite:** "Don't you think…", "Wouldn't you agree…", "How frustrated were you…"

---

## Compound questions

**Pattern:** Several asks in one breath.

**Rewrite toward:** One question → wait → next question.

**Example split:** Meeting reaction, then improvements, then attendance—three turns.

---

## Closed questions too early

**Pattern:** Yes/no while still exploring.

**Rewrite toward:**

- "What was your experience with it?"
- "Tell me about the challenges you faced."

Use closed forms later to pin a fact.

---

## Filling silence

**Pattern:** Rephrasing or multiple-choice rescue before the person finishes thinking.

**Rewrite toward:** Ask once. Count to five silently. Let them fill the space.

---

## Answering your own question

**Pattern:** Listing candidate answers inside the ask.

**Rewrite toward:** "How does your team communicate?"

---

## Why barrage

**Pattern:** Stacked "why" that feels interrogatory.

**Rewrite toward:**

- "What led you to that decision?"
- "Help me understand the thinking behind that."
- "What was happening that made that feel right?"

---

## Hypothetical future behavior

**Pattern:** Predicting unpaid future use or spend.

**Rewrite toward past behavior:**

- "Have you ever paid for something like this?"
- "Tell me about the last time you needed this."

---

## Feature fishing

**Pattern:** Asking whether a specific solution would help.

**Rewrite toward problems and jobs:**

- "What's frustrating about how this works now?"
- "What would make your workflow easier?"

---

## Stack-ranking demands

**Pattern:** Forcing a single artificial #1.

**Rewrite toward constraints:**

- "What's driving the urgency here?"
- "Which of these could you live without for six months?"

---

## Jargon assumptions

**Pattern:** Unexplained acronyms or insider terms.

**Rewrite toward plain language:**

- "How satisfied would you say your customers are overall?"
- "How often do you release new versions—daily, weekly, monthly?"
