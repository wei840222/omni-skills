# Sweden Domain Knowledge

## Schengen Entry Policy

Sweden applies the Schengen short-stay framework. Whether a visitor needs a visa depends on nationality and purpose of travel; short stays are generally limited to 90 days in any 180-day period across the Schengen Area. Check the official EU and Swedish sources immediately before advice because entry systems and national guidance can change.

## EES and ETIAS

The EU Entry/Exit System (EES) digitizes records for eligible non-EU short-stay travelers. ETIAS is a separate future travel-authorisation system for visa-exempt visitors; verify its live operational status through the official EU portal before presenting it as a pre-travel requirement.
