# Proposal Writing

## Structure That Converts

1. **Hook** — Reference something specific from the job post
2. **Credibility** — 1–2 relevant portfolio examples, not the whole history
3. **Approach** — Brief outline for this project only
4. **CTA** — Concrete next step: "15-min call to clarify scope?"

## What Kills Proposals

- "Dear Hiring Manager" or other generic openers
- Copy-pasted templates without customization
- Listing every skill instead of the relevant ones
- Asking questions already answered in the job post
- Pricing without understanding scope

## Pricing Strategy

| Scenario | Approach |
|----------|----------|
| Competitive job (50+ proposals) | Slightly below usual rate to enter, upsell later |
| Direct invitation | Premium rate — they chose you |
| Complex scope | "Happy to discuss after a quick call" |
| Unclear budget | Propose a range, ask clarifying questions |

Before locking a bid, read the **Freelancer Service Fee** shown on that proposal
screen (0–15% per contract) and fold it into the take-home math. See
`platform.md`.

## Connects Efficiency

- Best Match jobs early after posting often convert better
- Direct invitations do not spend Connects — respond fast
- Skip jobs with 50+ proposals unless the fit is unusually strong
- Boost only when the job matches a strong, relevant profile history
- Confirm the Connects cost printed on the job before applying; it can change
  while the post is live
