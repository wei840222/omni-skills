# Legal & Compliance

## Out-of-scope / ToS boundaries

Treat these as outside the skill’s task boundary:

- Automated login or session management
- Auto-sending proposals without human review
- Scraping job posts or client data
- Multiple accounts per person
- Payments outside Upwork (direct bank, crypto, PayPal, etc.)
- Misrepresenting who does the work

## What is allowed

- AI assistance for drafting when the human reviews before sending
- Research and analysis of public job posts the user pasted in
- Templates customized for each proposal
- CRM tracking outside Upwork that never stores Upwork credentials
- Productivity tools that do not access the Upwork account

## Consequences of violations

| Violation | Typical consequence |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Chargebacks >1% | Warning → suspension |
| Off-platform payment | Permanent ban risk |
| Multiple accounts | All accounts banned |
| Fake reviews | Account terminated |
| ToS circumvention | Funds frozen + ban |

## User responsibilities

1. **Disclosure**: If a client prohibits AI assistance, respect it or decline.
2. **Confidentiality**: Keep client NDA data out of third-party AI prompts.
3. **Accuracy**: Claim only capabilities the user actually has.
4. **Jurisdiction**: Client location can change privacy and tax duties (for
   example GDPR when the client is in the EU).

## Safe practices

- Keep negotiation and agreements in Upwork chat
- Use funded milestones for fixed-price protection
- Document scope in writing before starting
- Save important conversations from the Upwork thread
- Keep Upwork credentials private — never paste them into the agent session

Cross-check scam patterns with Upwork’s own guidance:
[Recognize red flags and avoid scams](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/35088484250003-Recognize-red-flags-and-avoid-scams).
