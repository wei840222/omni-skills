# Upwork Platform Mechanics

Mutable fee and Connect numbers change. Confirm the live values on the job or
offer screen and in `sources.md` before quoting them as current.

## Fees Structure

- **Freelancer Service Fee**: Per-contract rate between **0% and 15%**. Upwork
  shows the exact percentage when you submit a proposal or receive an offer; once
  the contract starts the rate is locked for that contract.
  Source: [Learn about the Freelancer Service Fee](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee).
- **Direct Contracts exception**: Bringing your own client via Direct Contracts
  uses a reduced freelancer fee; Freelancer Plus can drop that Direct Contract
  fee to **0%**. Marketplace contracts still use the per-contract 0–15% band.
  Source: [What is Freelancer Plus?](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062888-About-the-Freelancer-Plus-plan).
- **Client Marketplace Fee**: Clients on the free Basic plan pay **up to 7.99%**
  on payments to freelancers. Qualifying U.S. bank-account payers can get a
  reduced **3%** rate. Business Plus uses a higher marketplace fee schedule.
  Source: [What is the Client Marketplace Fee?](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4660220468499-What-is-the-Client-Marketplace-Fee).
- **Contract Initiation Fee**: Clients pay a one-time **$0.99–$14.99 USD** fee
  per new Marketplace or Project Catalog contract. Amount varies by contract type
  and account history.
  Source: [Contract Initiation Fee](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/26106318334611-What-is-the-Contract-Initiation-Fee-on-Upwork).

## Job Success Score (JSS)

Factors commonly reflected in JSS:

- Client feedback (public + private)
- Long-term client relationships
- Contract outcomes (completed vs cancelled)
- Responsiveness

**To protect JSS:**

- Close inactive contracts (stale contracts hurt)
- Communicate clearly before ending any contract
- Ask for private feedback before public review
- Resolve issues before they become disputes

## Contract Types

| Type | When to Use | Risk |
|------|-------------|------|
| Fixed-price | Clear scope, defined deliverables | Scope creep |
| Hourly | Ongoing work, unclear scope | Time tracking scrutiny |
| Milestone-based | Large fixed projects | Protect with partial payments |

**Fixed-price protection:** Wait for a funded milestone before starting work.
**Hourly protection:** Use the Upwork desktop app for tracking, keep detailed
memos, stay inside weekly hour limits, and prefer clients with verified billing.

## Connects System

- Connects are tokens used to submit proposals, boost proposals, and power the
  Availability Badge.
- Purchase price: **$0.15 USD** per Connect (sold in bundles).
- Free monthly Connects: some freelancers receive **10** free Connects/month on
  Basic (eligibility varies; check Connects history). Freelancer Plus includes
  **100** Connects/month after the first month.
- Proposal cost varies per job and can change while the post is live; read the
  number shown on that job before applying instead of assuming a fixed range.
- Refunds happen in limited cases documented by Upwork (for example certain
  platform closures). Treat ordinary declines as non-refundable unless the live
  Help article says otherwise.
- Boosting spends extra Connects for priority placement; use only when the job
  is a strong profile fit.

Sources: [Understanding and using Connects](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects),
[Freelancer Plus](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062888-About-the-Freelancer-Plus-plan).

## Account Levels

| Level | Typical signals | Benefits |
|-------|-----------------|----------|
| Rising Talent | New, promising | Badge visibility; Connects reward on earning the badge |
| Top Rated | Sustained strong outcomes | Search placement and faster support |
| Top Rated Plus | Sustained excellence | Appears in “I hire Top Rated” style client filters |

Exact badge thresholds change; confirm current criteria on Upwork Help before
telling a user they “qualify.”
