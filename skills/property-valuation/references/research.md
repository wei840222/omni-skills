# Research Notes — Property Valuation

Load this reference when explaining appraisal-regulated work, selecting comparable-sales evidence, or discussing a tax-related valuation. Confirm local law and current market data before presenting jurisdiction-specific advice.

## Appraisal scope and independence

- **Federal financial-institutions appraisal rules** — <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-I/part-34/subpart-G>
  - Appraisal requirements can apply to federally related transactions; a conversational estimate is not a substitute for the applicable appraisal process.
- **FDIC real-estate appraisal standards** — <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-323/subpart-A>
  - Regulated institutions must use written real-estate appraisals or evaluations under the rule's conditions; keep regulated-purpose conclusions within a qualified professional's scope.

## Comparable-sales evidence

- **Fannie Mae Selling Guide — Appraisal report forms and exhibits** — <https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b4-1.2-01/appraisal-report-forms-and-exhibits>
  - For lending-oriented appraisal work, applicable report requirements govern comparable-sale reporting; use this as a source for process context rather than a universal adjustment schedule.

## Tax context

- **IRS Topic No. 701, Sale of your home** — <https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc701>
  - A home-sale tax result depends on facts and applicable tax rules; route tax consequences to qualified tax guidance instead of deriving them from an informal market estimate.
