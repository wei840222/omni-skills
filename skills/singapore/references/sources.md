# Official Sources & Technical Specifications

This document records the official governmental, immigration, and labor authority portals for Singapore.

## Official Singapore Government Portals

- **Ministry of Manpower (MOM) - Passes & Permits**: [https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits)
  - Work pass eligibility criteria, Employment Pass COMPASS (Complementarity Assessment Framework) points criteria, ONE Pass, S Pass, and Dependant's Pass.
- **Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA)**: [https://www.ica.gov.sg/](https://www.ica.gov.sg/)
  - SG Arrival Card electronic declaration, entry visa requirements, automated lane eligibility, and Permanent Resident (PR) application procedures.
- **Singapore Tourism Board (VisitSingapore)**: [https://www.visitsingapore.com/en/](https://www.visitsingapore.com/en/)
  - Official national travel guide, neighborhood profiles, heritage precincts, licensed tour operators, and public event schedules.
- **Central Provident Fund (CPF) Board**: [https://www.cpf.gov.sg/](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/)
  - Social security contribution rules for citizens and permanent residents, Ordinary Account housing usage, and healthcare Medisave guidelines.
