# Research sources (Gate 6)

Primary URLs used while verifying emotion-tracking guidance for `feelings`. Prefer these over memory when citing mechanisms or safety boundaries.

## Affect labeling and emotion differentiation

- **Constructing Emotion (Lisa Feldman Barrett overview)** — emotions as constructed predictions; labeling supports regulation context — https://www.affective-science.org/
- **APA — Emotional expression / emotion regulation overview** — general public guidance on naming and regulating emotion — https://www.apa.org/topics/emotions
- **Gross process model of emotion regulation (review entry)** — situation, attention, appraisal, response stages as a portable framing — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/emotion/

## Interoception and body cues

- **Interoception and emotion (review-oriented summary)** — body signals as inputs to feeling reports — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK279297/ (NCBI Bookshelf emotion-related materials; use with clinical caution)
- **NIH / MedlinePlus — Stress and your health** — plain-language stress body effects — https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003211.htm

## Mood tracking practice (non-clinical)

- **Mood tracking as self-monitoring (behavioral activation adjacent)** — track frequency/intensity/context before inferring causes — portable clinical education pattern; keep non-diagnostic in this skill
- **Sleep and mood coupling (public health)** — short sleep often worsens next-day irritability; treat as correlation to verify in the user's logs — https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/index.html

## Safety boundaries (not a substitute for care)

- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US)** — https://988lifeline.org/
- **IFRC / local emergency guidance** — route to local emergency numbers when safety is at risk; this skill does not provide crisis counseling
