# Legal Compliance

Orientation only. Confirm controlling statutes with `sources.md` and local counsel before acting on thresholds or penalties.

## Documentation Requirements

### Performance Issues
- Minimum 3 documented conversations before PIP
- Each conversation: date, attendees, specific issues, agreed actions
- Manager signature or email confirmation

### Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)
- Specific, measurable metrics
- Clear timeline (typically 30-60 days)
- Explicit consequences if not met
- Employee acknowledgment signature

### Terminations
- Written reason code (performance, conduct, RIF, etc.)
- Documentation history attached
- Witness present during conversation
- Exit interview offered

### Accommodations
- Log every request within 24 hours
- Respond with next steps within 5 business days
- Document interactive-process conversations (ADA orientation: see `sources.md`)
- Final decision with rationale

## Red Flags — Escalate to Legal

Immediate escalation:
- Termination request for employee who filed a complaint in the last 90 days
- Pregnant employee or anyone on medical/family leave at risk
- Same manager terminating 3+ employees of a protected class without clear non-discriminatory documentation
- Mass layoff that may trigger WARN-style notice duties (U.S. WARN overview in `sources.md`)
- Any pattern that looks like quiet firing after protected activity

## Jurisdiction Rules

| Issue | US (orientation) | EU (orientation) | UK (orientation) |
|-------|------------------|------------------|------------------|
| At-will termination | Often available, still constrained by statute/contract | Cause / justified grounds more typical | Unfair dismissal and contract limits common |
| Notice period | State/contract dependent | Often longer contractual/statutory notice | Statutory + contract |
| Automated HR decisions | Sector/city rules may apply (e.g. NYC AEDT) | GDPR Art. 22-style safeguards may apply | Similar fairness/data expectations |
| Non-competes | State-by-state and changing | Often limited | Must be reasonable in scope/duration |

**Multi-jurisdiction principle:** Apply the MOST restrictive workable rule set, then confirm with counsel.

## Compliance Calendar

Track and alert (jurisdiction-specific; verify on agency pages):
- I-9 / work-authorization expirations (90/60/30 day warnings) — https://www.uscis.gov/i-9
- OSHA logs where applicable
- EEO-1 filing windows for covered U.S. employers — EEOC EEO-1 page in `sources.md`
- Benefits enrollment windows
- COBRA / continuation-coverage notice deadlines — DOL COBRA page in `sources.md`
- State-specific training requirements (e.g., CA harassment training)
- Annual policy acknowledgment re-signing

## Contractor vs Employee

Red flags for misclassification risk (FLSA / wage-hour orientation — see DOL FLSA in `sources.md`):
- Engagement longer than a pure project window with no other clients
- Schedule controlled primarily by the company
- Uses company equipment exclusively
- Paid like wages rather than a true project rate
- Duties identical to employees on the same team

**Cost of error:** audits and assessments are case-specific; do not quote a single global dollar figure. Escalate classification questions early.

## Separation Agreements

**Draft conditionally.** Provide checklist for legal review first:
- Consideration amount
- Release scope
- Non-disparagement clause
- Confidentiality terms
- Return of property
- Age-40+ timing/consideration requirements under ADEA-oriented practice (see EEOC ADEA source)
