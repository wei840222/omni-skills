# Research sources (Gate 6)

Primary orientation sources for `chro`. Re-check jurisdiction pages before repeating statutory deadlines, filing thresholds, or penalty figures. This package does **not** assert a single global misclassification fine amount.

## Equal employment and hiring

- **U.S. EEOC — Prohibited Employment Policies/Practices** — illegal interview topics and discriminatory practices orientation (U.S.-centric; localize elsewhere) — https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices
- **U.S. EEOC — Enforcement Guidance on Reasonable Accommodation and Undue Hardship under the ADA** — interactive process and accommodation documentation expectations — https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/enforcement-guidance-reasonable-accommodation-and-undue-hardship-under-ada
- **U.S. EEOC — Age Discrimination in Employment Act of 1967 (ADEA)** — age-40+ protections relevant to separation agreements and consideration periods — https://www.eeoc.gov/statutes/age-discrimination-employment-act-1967
- **U.S. EEOC — EEO-1 data collection** — EEO-1 filing context for larger U.S. employers — https://www.eeoc.gov/data/eeo-1-data-collection

## Wage/hour, classification, leave

- **U.S. DOL Wage and Hour Division — Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)** — employee vs independent contractor / wage-hour baseline — https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa
- **U.S. DOL — Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)** — covered leave escalation path — https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fmla
- **U.S. DOL EBSA — COBRA continuation coverage** — post-termination notice orientation — https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ebsa/laws-and-regulations/laws/cobra
- **U.S. DOL ETA — Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN)** — mass-layoff notice framework (U.S.) — https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/layoffs/warn

## Work authorization and onboarding records

- **U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services — Form I-9** — employment eligibility verification process — https://www.uscis.gov/i-9

## Pay transparency

- **California DIR / DLSE — Pay Transparency** — California pay-transparency obligations orientation (example of state-level salary-range disclosure rules; other jurisdictions differ) — https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/Pay-Transparency.htm
- **Colorado Department of Labor and Employment — Equal Pay for Equal Work Act overview** — another U.S. pay-range posting example — https://cdle.colorado.gov/equalpay

## AI / automated employment tools

- **NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection — Automated Employment Decision Tools (Local Law 144)** — bias audit and notice expectations for covered AEDTs in NYC — https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/automated-employment-decision-tools.page
- **EUR-Lex — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act)** — EU AI Act official text; check applicability before treating HR tooling as high-risk — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj

## Practice framing (secondary)

- **CIPD — Performance management factsheets hub** — contemporary HR performance-practice framing — https://www.cipd.org/en/knowledge/factsheets/performance-factsheet/
- **ACAS (UK) — Managing performance** — fairness-oriented performance management guidance (UK-centric secondary check) — https://www.acas.org.uk/managing-performance

## Evidence limits

- No universal dollar amount for misclassification penalties is asserted here; IRS/DOL outcomes are case-specific.
- Salary bands, headcount benchmarks, and turnover targets in operational checklists are workflow heuristics, not cited market statistics.
- Always prefer local counsel and the controlling statute/agency page for the employer’s jurisdictions.
