# Betting Domain Knowledge

## Kelly Criterion

In finance and betting, the Kelly criterion sizes a sequence of bets to maximize the expected logarithm of wealth. Full Kelly can be aggressive when the estimated edge is wrong, so many bettors use fractional Kelly (for example half or quarter Kelly) to reduce variance and bankroll drawdowns.

## Implied Probability

Implied probability converts bookmaker odds into a break-even win rate, including the bookmaker margin (vig).

- Decimal odds: `1 / decimal_odds`
- Positive American odds (+X): `100 / (X + 100)`
- Negative American odds (-X): `X / (X + 100)`
