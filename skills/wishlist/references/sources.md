## Practice notes derived for this skill

Stable-domain practices retained from the skill design (not vendor price claims):

1. **Capture before checkout** — writing the want down reduces pure impulse buys and preserves the "why".
2. **Target price as decision anchor** — compare observed quotes to a user-set target rather than to marketing "was" prices.
3. **History beats single snapshots** — a one-day "sale" label is weak without a prior observed price.
4. **Cool-down on emotional purchases** — when the user signals stress shopping, prefer a short wait or explicit must-have justification before buy help.
5. **Credentials stay out of notes** — wishlist files are not a password or card vault.

## Out of scope claims
