## Notes for agents

1. Visa exemption length, DTV fees/funds proof, retirement bank-balance rules, and LTR income floors change. Never copy a number from this package into a filing packet without the current official HTML/PDF for that nationality and date.
2. Since 2024, Thai tax residency and foreign-income remittance rules are a separate track from immigration status — open Revenue Department guidance before any plan that crosses ~180 days/year.
3. Rail fares and line maps move with new monorail/MRT extensions; prefer operator pages over memory when the user is buying a multi-day pass.
4. Cannabis, alcohol, and nightlife enforcement have shifted multiple times since 2022 — treat package notes as orientation and verify current practice before advising carry or purchase.
5. US State Department and some Tourism Authority pages may block automated fetches; still prefer those live human-opened pages when available for advisory wording.
