# Research sources (Gate 6)

Primary references used to keep Taiwan logistics current. Prefer these over stale secondary blogs when facts drift.

## Transport

- **Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR)** — west-coast HSR routes, stations, booking rules via https://www.thsrc.com.tw/en
- **Taiwan Railways Administration (TRA)** — conventional rail including east-coast lines via https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip
- **Taoyuan Airport MRT / access** — airport rail context via https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/en/_pages/

## Tourism & weather risk

- **Tourism Administration, MOTC** — official destination framing and regional guidance via https://eng.taiwan.net.tw/
- **Central Weather Administration** — typhoon, rain, and heat risk checks before mountain/coast days via https://www.cwa.gov.tw/eng/

## Practical traveler friction

- **National Immigration Agency / border context** — entry rules change; verify current policy rather than memorizing visa lore via https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5475/
- City night-market and cash-first stall norms remain operational heuristics; always re-check market open days locally rather than treating famous markets as always-on.
