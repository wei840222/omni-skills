# Price Research Sources

Use these primary references when verifying price history, sale legitimacy, total-cost framing, or consumer-protection claims. Prefer live retailer/product pages for the exact SKU under review; the links below are baselines, not live quotes.

## Price History and Trackers

- **CamelCamelCamel** — Amazon price-history charts and alerts via https://camelcamelcamel.com/
- **Keepa** — Amazon price tracking, charts, and historical graphs via https://keepa.com/
- **Google Flights** — flight fare exploration and date flexibility via https://www.google.com/travel/flights
- **Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI** — official U.S. consumer price index context via https://www.bls.gov/cpi/

## Consumer Protection and Manipulation Context

- **FTC consumer guidance** — spotting fake sales, dark patterns, and deceptive pricing claims via https://consumer.ftc.gov/
- **FTC Business Guidance on deceptive pricing** — retailer comparison/discount framing rules via https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance
- **CMA guidance on drip pricing / online choice architecture** — total-price transparency expectations via https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/competition-and-markets-authority

## Cross-Retailer Verification Habits

- Confirm identical SKU / model number before comparing "same" products across retailers.
- Treat "was / now" labels as claims until corroborated by an independent history tool or archived listing.
- Always recompute total cost (shipping, tax, fees, required add-ons) before a buy/wait verdict.
