# Planning Guide

## Start with the decision

Classify the request before selecting recommendations:

1. **Visit:** dates, party size, accessibility needs, lodging area, budget, and planned activities.
2. **Move or housing:** work or study location, commute tolerance, household needs, budget, parking, and lease timeline.
3. **Local mobility:** origin, destination, time of day, luggage or mobility needs, and willingness to drive, ride-share, walk, or use transit.
4. **Work or study:** institution or workplace, schedule, compensation or budget constraints, and preferred housing radius.

Load only the relevant topic references, then load `current-information.md` whenever the recommendation depends on a live price, schedule, regulation, availability, weather condition, safety alert, or event status.

## Guardrails

- Frame neighborhood and safety advice as location- and time-specific; encourage checking the exact route, building, and current conditions.
- Present budgets, commute durations, salaries, and prices as estimates unless verified for the user’s date and circumstances.
- For emergencies or active hazards, prioritize official alerts and emergency services over this skill’s background guidance.
- When a visitor’s itinerary crosses distant areas, group activities geographically and verify travel time on the day of travel.
