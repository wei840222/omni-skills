# Domain Knowledge: Streaming & Content Tracking

## JustWatch and Streaming Aggregators
JustWatch is a website and application that provides information on the availability of films and TV shows on various streaming platforms (such as Netflix, HBO Max, Disney+, Hulu, Peacock, Fandango, Apple TV, and Amazon Prime Video). Since content frequently moves between streaming platforms due to licensing agreements, availability data must be treated as volatile. Always suggest users verify current availability on streaming aggregators like JustWatch if they need to find where to watch a specific title.
