# Domain Knowledge

## Iterative Writing Process
The core of effective writing involves structured phases: planning (briefing and outlining), drafting (executing the plan), and refining (auditing and editing). Separation of these concerns prevents premature optimization.

## Version Control for Content
Maintaining a history of edits allows writers to experiment safely. It provides the ability to revert changes, compare drafts, and understand the evolution of a piece.

## Quality Audits
Objective audits evaluate content against predefined criteria (e.g., tone, clarity, structure). This ensures the final output meets the desired standards and aligns with the original brief.
