## Notes

- Housing rent/sale bands in `cost.md` and neighborhood files are planning ranges labeled early 2026, not live MLS quotes; re-check Visit Las Vegas, local listings, and county data before hard financial advice.
- Nevada residency is multi-factor (home, license, voter, vehicle, life footprint); there is no single automatic day-count rule that always defeats a former-state challenge.
- Strip vs Downtown gaming odds and table minimums change by property and time; treat casino figures as directional, not guarantees.
