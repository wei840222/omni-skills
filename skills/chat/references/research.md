# Communication-preference research

Use this reference when explaining why the skill distinguishes explicit feedback, transparent application, and confirmation before persistent personalization.

## Human-AI interaction

- **Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction — Microsoft Research**
  https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/
  The publication proposes 18 generally applicable guidelines for human-AI interaction and reports evaluation with design practitioners. For this skill, its durable implication is that user-facing AI behavior should be understandable and controllable; it does not prescribe the three-strike policy.
