## Official admission and cutoff portals

- **阳光高考 (MOE student admissions portal)** — university catalogs, enrollment plans, and official admission info via https://gaokao.chsi.com.cn/
- **阳光高考 — 院校库** — school lookup before 志愿填报 via https://gaokao.chsi.com.cn/zsgs/zhangcheng/
- **Provincial education examination authorities** — always open the user's province 教育考试院 site for 一分一段表, registration, and local paper rules (do not generalize one province table nationwide)

## University and major verification

- Open the **target university 本科招生网 / 招生章程** page for the current year before stating major cutoffs, 招生计划, or 调剂 rules.
- Prefer last-3-year provincial cutoff series from the university or province portal over third-party aggregator screenshots.

## Knowledge updates captured this refactor

- National 2024 registration volume is commonly reported around **13.42 million** candidates; cite MOE/gov coverage rather than memorized blog numbers
- Majority of provinces use **3+1+2**; Shanghai/Zhejiang-style **3+3** remains a distinct elective model
- Core 语数外 remain 150 each; electives typically 100 with 等级赋分 on selected subjects
- June 7–8 remains the standard national exam window; exact subject order and local 学考/合格考 dates still vary by province
- 志愿填报 must be grounded in current-year 一分一段表 + university 招生计划, not prior-year folklore alone
