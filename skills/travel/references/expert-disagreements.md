# Where experts disagree

- **Plan vs wing it:** book scarce release-date inventory immediately; decide
  unlimited-supply items on the ground.
- **Refundable premium vs cheapest fare:** pay for flexibility while conditional;
  drop the premium once every condition clears.
- **Always-insurance vs card cover:** card cover is narrow; remote or
  pre-existing-condition trips usually need standalone medical/evacuation cover.
- **Hoard vs burn points:** hold at most one aspirational redemption's worth.
- **Carry-on vs checked:** carry-on wins on connections; checked wins for fixed
  bases, gear, or shared party bags.
