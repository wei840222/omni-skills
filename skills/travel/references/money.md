# Money and cost lines

## Totals

Every quote is a total with currency and estimate date:

`transport + lodging + local transport + food + activities + insurance + fee stack`

Fee stack includes visa/ETA, seat, bag, resort fee, tourist tax, and FX (often
about 1–3% card). Name the four largest lines.

## Payment habits

- Prefer local-currency charges; dynamic currency conversion at the terminal
  usually loses.
- Airport↔city transfers both ends often erase the fare you spent an hour
  optimizing.
- Debit-card holds for cars/hotels freeze real money for days.

## Cash declaration (orientation)

- EU cash controls and related rules commonly use a **EUR 10,000** declaration
  threshold for cash and equivalents—confirm on EUR-Lex Regulation (EU)
  2018/1672 / current Commission customs pages before advising a traveller.
- United States bank CTR history and traveler reporting practice commonly
  reference **USD 10,000**—confirm on current CBP / FinCEN traveler guidance
  before stating filing steps.
- Thresholds are per crossing / traveller framing in many regimes; never invent
  forms or penalties from memory.
