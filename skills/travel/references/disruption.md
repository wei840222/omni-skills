# Disruption and claims

Order of operations: stabilize the trip, then claim.

1. Rebook or re-accommodate with the operating carrier / property.
2. Keep receipts, delay notices, and bag tags.
3. File claims under the regime that applies to the ticket and departure
   jurisdiction; do not invent compensation amounts.
4. Stolen passport / visa / residence card: police report first (insurers and
   embassies usually require it), then the nearest consulate.
