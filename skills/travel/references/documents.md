# Documents and entry rules

Orientation only. Re-open the destination authority and
`references/sources.md` before any non-refundable booking.

## Passport validity windows

- Many airlines and destinations refuse boarding or entry when remaining
  validity is under about six months. Diary renewal around expiry − 9 months.
- Schengen short-stay practice commonly also requires that the passport was
  issued within the previous 10 years and remains valid at least three months
  beyond the intended departure from the Schengen area. An early renewal can
  fail on issue date while still showing calendar validity—verify on EC
  border-crossing / visa-policy pages.
- Several countries still ask for blank visa pages (often two facing pages).

## Rolling day allowances

- Count backwards from the intended day of entry, not by calendar year.
- Schengen orientation: 90 days in any rolling 180-day window across the area
  together. Example: entering 10 March with 62 days already inside the previous
  180 leaves 28; a 30-day plan fails at the border.
- Use the European Commission short-stay calculator when the math is close.

## Authorizations and other gates

- ETA / ESTA / ETIAS-style pre-travel authorization is separate from a visa.
  Apply days ahead; silent rejection surfaces at check-in.
- Onward or return tickets may be required by the carrier even when the border
  would negotiate.
- Visas attach to a passport booklet—renewal can orphan a visa; carry both or
  transfer.
- Yellow fever certificates and other health docs: see `references/health.md`.
- Currency declaration thresholds: see `references/money.md`.

## Procedure

1. Read `passport_countries` from config; if unset, say entry rules are unverified.
2. Open destination immigration / MFA page and record check date in the place file.
3. Only then propose non-refundable dates.
