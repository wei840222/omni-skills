# Travel day

## Connection buffers (orientation)

- One ticket: published minimum connection time is the floor; the operating
  carrier owns the miss.
- Separate tickets: the traveller owns the miss. Budget generous self-transfer
  time (orientation ≥3 h international / ≥2 h domestic) and treat the last
  departure of the day as a hotel night risk rather than a connection.
- Airport arrival orientation: ~2 h international / ~90 min domestic, plus
  whatever the place file recorded last time. Scale with `risk_posture`.

## Crossing

- Know what cannot enter and what must be declared (`references/money.md`,
  destination customs page).
- Transit visas can apply even for airside connections—check for
  `passport_countries`, not only the final destination.
