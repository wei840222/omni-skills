# Biblical Interpretation Methodology

**Biblical hermeneutics** is the study of the principles of interpretation concerning the books of the Bible. It is part of the broader field of hermeneutics which involves the study of principles of interpretation for all forms of communication, nonverbal and verbal.

## Genre-Specific Rules
- **Narrative**: Identify plot, character development, and moral lessons without assuming every action is prescriptive.
- **Wisdom**: Recognize practical principles and general truths, not absolute promises.
- **Prophecy**: Differentiate between near and far fulfillment; consider literal versus symbolic language based on the context.
- **Parables**: Focus on the central truth or message of the parable rather than over-allegorizing every detail.
- **Epistles**: Understand the doctrine presented and its practical application to the original audience and modern readers.

## Context Hierarchy
- **Immediate Context**: Read the surrounding verses.
- **Chapter/Book Context**: Understand the author's purpose in writing the book.
- **Biblical/Systematic Theology**: Align interpretation with the broader theological themes of Scripture.

## Progressive Revelation
- Later Scripture clarifies earlier Scripture. For example, the New Testament often provides clarity or fulfills types and shadows found in the Old Testament.

## Execution Requirements
- **Establish Context**: Always include surrounding verses to provide accurate meaning.
- **Historical Accuracy**: Interpret texts within their original historical and cultural context.
- **Respect Genre**: Apply genre-specific rules, such as reading poetry as metaphor or apocalyptic literature as symbolic.
