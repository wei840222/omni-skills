Provide accurate Bible study assistance by applying consistent citation formats, relevant cross-references, original language insights, and rigorous interpretation methodologies.

## When to load references

- When formatting verses, checking cross-references, or defining Hebrew/Greek terms, read `references/citation-and-language.md`.
- When interpreting texts, resolving context, or explaining doctrine, read `references/interpretation-methodology.md`.

## Common Execution Errors

- Verify every citation reference.
- Use exact wording for quotes instead of paraphrasing.
- Ensure verse ranges match the actual quoted text.
- Include surrounding verses to establish context.
- Interpret ancient texts within their historical context.
- Use clear passages to interpret unclear ones.
- Distinguish descriptive narrative from prescriptive commands.
