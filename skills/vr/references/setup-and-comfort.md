# VR Setup and Comfort Guidance

## Setup Guidance

- Clear play space of obstacles: furniture, pets, cables, ceiling fans
- Guardian/boundary setup: take time to do it right to prevent collisions
- Lighting matters: maintain moderate lighting; avoid total darkness or direct sunlight for optimal tracking
- Controller batteries: use rechargeable batteries for sustained usage
- First session keep short: 15-30 min max, build tolerance gradually

## Motion Sickness Prevention

- Start with stationary experiences: prioritize Beat Saber or Job Simulator over intense movement games
- Teleport locomotion: use this instead of smooth locomotion initially
- Stop at first sign of discomfort: rest immediately and resume later with a shorter session
- Fan blowing on face helps: provides a real-world anchor point
- Ginger before sessions: consume ginger to alleviate symptoms for some individuals
- Adaptation period: allow 1-2 weeks to adapt through short, consistent sessions

## Comfort Optimization

- Stock straps are often inadequate: upgrade to aftermarket straps for significant improvement
- Weight distribution: balance the headset weight evenly; front-heavy setups cause fatigue
- IPD adjustment: measure pupillary distance and set it correctly to prevent headaches
- Lens cleaning: use microfiber cloths only; keep liquids away from lenses
- Face gasket replacement: replace regularly or use aftermarket options for hygiene and comfort
