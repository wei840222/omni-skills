# Current Headset Landscape

| Headset | Best For | Requires |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Meta Quest 3 | Premium standalone and PC VR all-arounder | Nothing (Standalone) |
| Meta Quest 3S | Budget entry standalone | Nothing (Standalone) |
| Apple Vision Pro | High-end spatial computing, productivity, media | Nothing (Standalone) |
| PlayStation VR2 | PlayStation gamers (and some PC VR with adapter) | PS5 (or PC with adapter) |
| Valve Index | Enthusiast PC VR, finger tracking | Gaming PC |

Recommend based on their situation, not specs alone.

* Note: Meta Quest 2 and Quest Pro have been discontinued and largely replaced by the Quest 3S.
* Note: PS VR2 now supports PC VR gaming via an official PC adapter, but features like eye tracking and adaptive triggers may not work on PC.
