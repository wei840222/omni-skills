# NEET-UG domain verification

## Source of truth

Use official notices for the relevant exam year before stating a rule, date, eligibility condition, syllabus, application requirement, counselling round, seat matrix, qualifying cutoff, or college closing rank.

| Topic | Primary source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| NEET-UG information bulletin, notices, application, syllabus | National Testing Agency (NTA), https://neet.nta.nic.in/ | Check the current exam-year bulletin and notices. |
| All India Quota, deemed/central-university counselling, seat matrix and round results | Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), https://mcc.nic.in/ | Check the applicable UG counselling scheme, schedule, and results. |
| State quota eligibility, rounds and college choices | Relevant state counselling authority | Use the authority for the student’s domicile; do not infer rules from another state. |
| Recognised medical colleges and programme information | National Medical Commission (NMC), https://www.nmc.org.in/ | Cross-check institution status where needed. |

## Verification rules

- Cite the official notice title, URL, and exam/counselling year for mutable claims.
- Distinguish the NEET qualifying percentile/cutoff from an institution’s closing rank or score.
- Do not convert a score into a predicted rank or admission outcome without an explicitly dated official dataset; explain uncertainty instead.
- If the current notice is unavailable, offer planning guidance and say that the administrative fact needs verification rather than guessing.

## Stable guidance

NEET-UG preparation benefits from active recall, spaced review, practice with error analysis, sufficient sleep, and a plan adjusted to the learner’s constraints. These methods support preparation but do not guarantee an outcome.
