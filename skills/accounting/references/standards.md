# Accounting Standards

## International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)
International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) are accounting standards issued by the IFRS Foundation and the International Accounting Standards Board.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Financial_Reporting_Standards

## Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (United States)
The Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) are the set of default accounting standards used by companies based in the United States.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generally_Accepted_Accounting_Principles_(United_States)
