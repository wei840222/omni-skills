# Platform norms

Rewrite every cross-post for the destination culture. The same announcement can
be a short Discord embed-style update, a Slack channel summary with owners, a
Telegram concise blast, or a forum post with a stable title.

## Discord

- Voice and text cultures differ by server; read `#rules` and recent channel
  history before posting.
- Prefer clear channel purpose, slow-mode, and role-gated admin actions over
  ad-hoc bans in public.
- For safety policy questions, open Discord’s Community Guidelines, Safety
  Center, and Safety Library rather than paraphrasing from memory.
- Automod / safety tooling changes; confirm current controls in Discord’s
  support / safety surfaces before recommending a specific toggle name.

## Slack

- Channels are work-shaped: state the decision, owner, and due date early.
- Threads keep channels scannable; put durable decisions in the top-level post
  or a linked doc.
- Permission and guest models vary by workspace plan—verify current Slack Help
  Center guidance before promising admin capabilities.
- Community-style Slack spaces still need clearer norms than casual Discord
  hangouts; avoid meme-first tone unless the workspace already uses it.

## Telegram

- Groups and channels behave differently: channels broadcast; groups converse.
- Keep messages short; link out for long briefs.
- Bot-driven moderation or keyboards belong to `telegram-bot-api`; this skill
  stays on community ops and copy.
- Confirm community / FAQ guidance on telegram.org when policy questions arise.

## Forums and Reddit-like spaces

- Title quality and searchability matter more than chat speed.
- Follow subreddit / forum reddiquette and local moderation wikis.
- Prefer evergreen posts plus scheduled refreshes over constant bumping.

## Adaptation checklist before publish

1. Read the last day of the target channel or thread.
2. Match length, emoji density, and CTA to local norms.
3. Replace cross-platform jargon (“slash command”, “thread”, “topic”) with the
   destination’s vocabulary.
4. Update `<state_root>/content/calendar.md` with the adapted variant, not a
   single identical blob marked for every network.
