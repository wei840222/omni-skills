# Engagement tactics

## Mix that sustains conversation

Aim for roughly:

- 40% value (answers, resources, how-tos)
- 30% engagement (questions, polls, reply threads)
- 20% announcements
- 10% fun / social glue

Adjust per community culture; document the chosen mix in the community file.

## Reply discipline

1. Post, then stay for the first wave of replies.
2. Answer at least a handful of comments before leaving the thread.
3. Ask one concrete follow-up that invites a next message.
4. Thank critics when the feedback is usable; move policy disputes to the
   documented process instead of arguing in public.

## Rituals that create return visits

- Weekly open office / AMA windows during peak hours
- New-member spotlight when volume is low enough to stay personal
- Recurring “wins of the week” threads that reward contribution
- Seasonal campaigns that already appear on
  `<state_root>/content/calendar.md`

## Measurement loop

Update weekly metrics in the community file. Prefer active members and
engagement rate over follower vanity. If joins rise while engagement falls,
treat activation—not top-of-funnel ads—as the next lever and consider handing
acquisition-loop diagnosis to `growth`.

## Cross-skill handoffs

- Brand voice conflicts → `branding`
- Editorial programs outside community stewardship → `content-marketing`
- Budget / channel portfolio decisions → `cmo`
