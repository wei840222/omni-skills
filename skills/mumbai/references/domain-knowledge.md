# Mumbai Domain Knowledge

## Overview
Mumbai, also known as Bombay, is the capital city of the Indian state of Maharashtra. It is the financial capital and the most populous city proper of India, with a population of approximately 12.5 million. The larger Mumbai Metropolitan Region has a population of over 23 million.

## Geography and Economy
- It is located on the Konkan coast in western India, featuring a deep natural harbor.
- Mumbai generates about 6.16% of India's GDP, 25% of the nation's industrial output, and 70% of maritime trade and capital transactions.
- The city is home to the Bombay Stock Exchange and numerous financial institutions.
- It has the highest number of billionaires in Asia.

## Entertainment
- The city is a major center for both Marathi and Hindi film industries, the latter commonly known as Bollywood, which is one of the largest film industries globally.

## Transportation and Living
- Mumbai is highly reliant on its extensive local train network.
- The monsoon season significantly affects daily life, requiring specific preparedness.
