# Sources — Mumbai

Back to [Mumbai Skill Overview](../SKILL.md).

Use these verified primary sources when commuting, monsoon readiness, municipal civic services, or neighborhood housing rules apply in Mumbai.

## Municipal Governance & Public Services

- **Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC / MCGM)** — https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/
  - Greater Mumbai civic administrative authority overseeing urban infrastructure, property tax, water supply, disaster management warnings, and health sanitation.

## Rail Transit & Urban Mobility

- **Indian Railways (Suburban Railway Network)** — https://indianrailways.gov.in/
  - Backbone of Mumbai transit comprising the Western Line, Central Line, and Harbour Line. Key source for suburban train zones, peak-hour frequency, security advisories, and women-only coaches (Ladies Compartments).
- **Reliance Mumbai Metro One** — https://www.reliancemumbaimetro.com/
  - Modern rapid transit link connecting Versova, Andheri, and Ghatkopar (Line 1). Provides automated smart card ticketing, contactless gate entry, security checks, and feeder connectivity.

## Monsoon & Weather Hazards

- **India Meteorological Department (IMD)** — https://mausam.imd.gov.in/
  - National meteorological authority publishing real-time radar, cyclone tracking, and severe rainfall alerts (Yellow, Orange, Red alerts) for Mumbai coastal and inland flood risk during the southwest monsoon (June to September).
