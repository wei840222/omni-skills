# Research sources (Gate 6)

Orientation sources for `real-estate-skill`. Re-open the live page before stating a current statute, disclosure duty, lending rule, or fair-housing interpretation as fact. This package teaches **process heuristics**, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Fair housing and discrimination (US federal orientation)

- **U.S. HUD — Fair Housing Act overview** — protected classes and housing discrimination orientation — https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/fair_housing_equal_opp/fair_housing_act_overview
- **U.S. HUD — Fair Housing** — program hub for complaints and guidance — https://www.hud.gov/fairhousing
- **U.S. DOJ — Fair Housing Act** — enforcement orientation — https://www.justice.gov/crt/fair-housing-act-1

## Consumer / homebuying process

- **CFPB — Buying a House** — consumer process and mortgage shopping orientation — https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/
- **CFPB — Mortgage tools** — loan estimate / closing disclosure orientation — https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/mortgages/
- **FTC — Buying and owning a home** — consumer protection orientation for purchases and scams — https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-and-owning-home

## Investment education (not personalized advice)

- **SEC — Real estate investment risks (investor.gov)** — securities and real-estate investment product risk orientation — https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/real-estate-investment-trusts-reits
- **IRS — Publication 527 Residential Rental Property** — US tax concepts for residential rentals; open current year PDF from IRS before quoting rules — https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527

## Local law and licensing

- State real-estate commission / licensing board pages (search the state name + “real estate commission”) — agent licensing and disclosure baselines
- Local bar association or housing court self-help pages — lease, eviction, and contract procedure orientation

## Evidence limits

- No universal “always offer X% below ask” rule is asserted.
- Cap rate, 1% rule, GRM, and CoC are screening heuristics only; they are not proof a deal is sound.
- Fair-housing protected classes and landlord-tenant rules vary by state and city; federal pages are orientation, not a substitute for local counsel.
- Wire-fraud guidance: never invent or verify wire instructions inside chat; always route to the user’s closing attorney or title company on a verified channel.
