# Seller and landlord orientation

Educational process notes only. Local statute, lease forms, and eviction procedure control; load `legal-compliance.md` for disclaimers and fair-housing limits.

## Sellers

High-level flow:

```text
Pricing → Prep → List → Showings → Negotiate → Close
```

### Pricing

- Prefer a CMA (comparable market analysis) over anchoring solely on purchase basis or asking neighbors.
- Track days-on-market and prior price cuts as leverage signals.
- Disclose known material defects per local law; when unsure, recommend the listing agent and attorney path.

### Prep and list

- Repair ROI: prioritize safety, systems, and financing blockers (FHA/VA items) over pure cosmetics.
- Listing copy stays factual; avoid protected-class or neighborhood-composition framing (see `legal-compliance.md`).
- Photos and access logistics are operational; keep lockboxes and showing instructions on verified channels.

### Negotiate and close

- Load `negotiation.md` for counters, repair requests, and concession framing.
- Title, survey, and HOA document timelines are jurisdiction-specific—confirm with the closing team.
- Route wire and funds handling exclusively through the closing attorney or title company.

## Landlords

High-level flow:

```text
Screen → Lease → Manage → Maintain → Renew / Terminate
```

### Screening (fair-housing safe)

- Apply the **same written criteria** to every applicant (income multiple, credit threshold band, rental history, criminal-history policy only if locally lawful and consistently applied).
- Document objective reasons; avoid ad-hoc exceptions that create disparate treatment.
- Protected-class and source-of-income rules differ by city—re-check local guidance before designing criteria.

### Lease essentials (orientation)

Typical topics to cover with local counsel / forms:

- parties, premises, term, rent, deposits, utilities
- entry notice, maintenance duties, subletting
- early termination, notices, attorney review of addenda

Educational outlines only—do not issue a binding lease from this skill.

### Manage and maintain

- Separate emergency vs routine maintenance SLAs.
- Keep habitability obligations front-and-center; local housing codes win over informal agreements.
- Recordkeeping: rent ledger, notices, repair logs—store only what the landlord already lawfully holds.

### Renew / terminate

- Notice periods and just-cause / no-fault rules are highly local.
- Eviction is a court process in most jurisdictions; this skill explains concepts and directs users to local housing court / attorney resources rather than DIY lockout steps.

## Agent assist (light)

When the user is an agent drafting client-facing text:

- listing descriptions: features, condition, logistics—no demographic steering
- market updates: comps and DOM, not “who lives there”
- negotiation prep: load `negotiation.md`
