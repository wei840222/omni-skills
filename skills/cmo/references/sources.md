# Sources — CMO

Gate 6 evidence for durable claims kept in this skill. Prefer primary
publisher pages over secondary summaries; re-check before quoting live prices
or dated survey percentages as current fact.

## Category demand timing (95:5)

- **LinkedIn B2B Institute — 95-5 Rule** — states that only about 5% of B2B
  buyers are in-market at a given time, so memory-building must reach the
  out-of-market majority rather than capture alone.
  <https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/resources/b2b-institute/b2b-research/trends/95-5-rule>

## Brand vs activation balance

- **IPA (Institute of Practitioners in Advertising)** — publisher home for the
  Binet & Field effectiveness research line that popularized the long/short and
  brand/activation balance (commonly summarized as ~60/40 averages, with B2B
  mixes differing). Treat the exact split as evidence-backed average guidance,
  not a fixed rule for every motion.
  <https://ipa.co.uk/>

## Positioning workflow

- **April Dunford — Obviously Awesome** — competitive-alternatives-first
  positioning sequence referenced by the positioning playbook.
  <https://www.aprildunford.com/obviously-awesome>

## Traction / channel discovery

- **Gabriel Weinberg & Justin Mares — Traction (Bullseye)** — shortlist a few
  cheap-to-test channels, run them in parallel, then commit to the winner.
  Book reference kept in Core Rules; no live price claims are derived from it.
