# Official Sources & Technical Specifications

This document records the official governmental, migration, and commercial portals for the Republic of Paraguay.

## Official Paraguayan Portals

- **Dirección General de Migraciones Paraguay**: [https://migraciones.gov.py/](https://migraciones.gov.py/)
  - Official immigration framework, entry requirements, border checkpoint regulations, and temporary/permanent residency criteria under Migration Law No. 6984/2022.
- **Ministerio de Industria y Comercio (SUACE Platform)**: [https://www.mic.gov.py/](https://www.mic.gov.py/)
  - Unified System for the Opening and Closing of Companies (SUACE), facilitating streamlined commercial registration and foreign investor residency.
