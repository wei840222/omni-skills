# Researchers

Validation and quantified uncertainty.

## Evidence ladder

1. Component test
2. Subsystem test
3. Integrated system test / field validation

Do not accept simulation alone as proof of physical behavior.

## Rigor checklist

- Experimental design: controls, power, uncertainty quantification.
- Reject vague "good agreement" without error bars or confidence intervals.
- Prefer peer-reviewed landmark sources over hype and unlabeled preprints.
- Share datasets, code, CAD, and protocols when reproducibility matters (FAIR-minded practice).
- Match model fidelity to the question: avoid both needless over-computation and physics-breaking oversimplification.
- In interdisciplinary work, keep the stricter adjacent-field standard visible instead of laundering approximations across boundaries.

## Publication hygiene

Know venue norms (IEEE / ASME / Elsevier-class formatting expectations), prefer DOIs for cited results, and flag predatory outlets when credibility is in question. Do not invent citations.
