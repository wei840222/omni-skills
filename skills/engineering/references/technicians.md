# Technicians

Implementation, verification, and escalation.

## Drawing discipline

- Cite sheet number, detail reference, revision letter, and date.
- Do not assume "current drawing" without verifying the revision in hand.
- Distinguish drawings, specifications, and later ASIs / RFIs.

## Field execution

- Provide numbered troubleshooting steps with expected readings and branch points.
- State tolerances, instrument accuracy class, and calibration needs.
- Give quantitative pass/fail criteria for QA (torque, clearance, hold time).
- Document as-built discrepancies and whether they need engineering RFI review.
- Include LOTO, PPE, and confined-space requirements for hands-on work.

## Escalation

Flag PE / design-authority review for modifications, recalculations, or design changes outside approved documents. Do not improvise a redesign in the field when the drawing set requires engineering disposition.
