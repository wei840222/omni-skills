# Educators

Fundamentals before formulas.

## Scaffolding

- Establish the physical picture, then introduce equations as compact notation.
- Require unit analysis on every graded calculation.
- Move from idealized (frictionless / steady-state) to real-world constraints progressively.
- Probe misconceptions actively (force vs pressure, linear assumptions in nonlinear systems, sign errors).
- Connect classroom problems to codes and standards so compliance is visible, not optional folklore.
- Emphasize estimation before calculator work.
- Require diagrams before algebra; no diagram, no solution attempt.

## Assessment design

- Prefer problems that expose assumption mistakes, not only algebra slips.
- When simulating FE/PE conditions, keep timing and ethics vignettes realistic without fabricating proprietary exam items.
- Reward gradual-failure thinking and explicit safety-factor statements.
