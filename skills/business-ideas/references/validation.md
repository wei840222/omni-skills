# Idea Validation Methods

Validate before building. Most ideas fail validation, which is the point.

## Quick Validation (1-2 hours)

### Search Trend & Keyword Check
- Search problem/solution keywords via Google Trends, Ahrefs (Free Keyword Generator), or AnswerThePublic.
- Look for upward trend or stable demand.
- Identify the search intent (informational vs. transactional).

### Competition & Market Scan
- Search "best [solution] for [audience]".
- Check software review sites (G2, Capterra) for feature gaps and negative reviews.
- Note: No competitors often means no market, not a blue ocean.

### Community Pulse (Reddit/X/Discord)
- Search problem keywords in relevant niche communities.
- Measure pain intensity (are people asking for solutions or just complaining?).
- Find discussions about current inadequate workarounds.

### Willingness to Pay Signal
- Look for pricing questions or posts like "I'd pay for X".
- Check if competitors are actively running search ads (indicates commercial viability).

## Medium Validation (1-2 days)

### Painted Door (Landing Page) Test
1. Create a high-converting landing page (Carrd, Typedream, Framer) explaining the unique value proposition.
2. Add a clear call-to-action (waitlist or early access email capture).
3. Drive 100-500 targeted visitors via niche communities or paid ads ($50-100).
4. Measure conversion rate (>5-10% indicates strong interest).

### Pre-sale or Deposit Attempt
1. Offer a lifetime deal or early-bird pricing for early believers.
2. Ask for a refundable deposit or actual payment (via Stripe payment links).
3. Refund immediately if validation fails.

### Problem Discovery Interviews
1. Conduct 5-10 "Mom Test" style interviews with potential customers.
2. Focus on their past behavior, not future promises ("When was the last time you dealt with this?").
3. Listen to how they currently solve the problem and how much they spend before pitching the idea.

## Deep Validation (1-2 weeks)

### Concierge MVP
1. Deliver the service manually (behind the scenes) to 3-5 customers.
2. Charge real money to validate value delivery.
3. Optimize the process manually before writing code to automate it.

### Productized Service Pivot
1. If software takes too long to build, validate the core value by offering it as a service first.
2. Use no-code tools (Airtable, Zapier, Make) to simulate the backend.

### B2B Letter of Intent (LOI) / Design Partnerships
1. Approach potential B2B customers with mockups or a slide deck.
2. Negotiate a design partnership or secure signed LOIs contingent on delivering the promised spec.
3. Multiple LOIs from ICPs (Ideal Customer Profiles) validate the B2B market.

## Validation Scorecard

After validation, score each dimension:

| Signal | Score 1-10 |
|--------|-----------|
| People searching for solution | |

| **Total (out of 50)** | |

**Interpretation:**
- 40+ → Strong proceed (Build MVP)
- 30-39 → Proceed with caution
- 20-29 → Major pivot needed
- <20 → Archive idea and move on

## Red Flags (Stop Signals)

- "Interesting idea" without payment signal
- Only friends/family show interest
- Target customer hard to reach
- Competition with infinite funding
- Requires behavior change + new category

## Green Flags (Proceed Signals)

- Strangers pay for early access
- Target customers easy to find and reach
- Existing budget for this problem
- Competition exists but is beatable
- Clear path to first 10 customers

## Validation Budget Guide

| Stage | Budget | Time | Goal |
|-------|--------|------|------|
| Quick | $0-50 | 2h | Filter out obviously bad ideas |
| Medium | $50-500 | 2d | Confirm demand exists |
| Deep | $500-2000 | 2w | Confirm people will pay |

**Rule:** Ensure you spend at least $200 on validation before investing time into building.
