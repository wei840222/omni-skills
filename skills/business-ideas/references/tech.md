# Business Ideas Research Notes

Gate 6 domain notes for idea generation and validation. Prefer primary guidance over blog summaries.

## Idea generation and lean discovery

- **The Lean Startup** — validated learning, build-measure-learn, and MVP framing via https://theleanstartup.com/principles
- **YC Library: How to Get Startup Ideas** — problem-first idea selection and founder-market fit cues via https://www.ycombinator.com/library/8g-how-to-get-startup-ideas
- **Paul Graham: How to Get Startup Ideas** — live in the problem, notice organic demand via http://www.paulgraham.com/startupideas.html

## Validation methods

- **The Mom Test** — interview for past behavior and spending, not compliments via http://momtestbook.com/
- **Google Trends** — demand direction and seasonality checks via https://trends.google.com/trends/
- **AnswerThePublic** — search-intent and question mining for problem language via https://answerthepublic.com/
- **G2** / **Capterra** — competitor feature gaps and complaint mining via https://www.g2.com/ and https://www.capterra.com/

## Painted-door and willingness-to-pay signals

- **Strategyzer / Business Model Canvas** — business model and customer segment structure via https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas
- **Stripe Payment Links** — low-friction pre-sale / deposit experiments via https://stripe.com/payments/payment-links
