## Practical use rule

Use these sources when answering:

- rail/coach/Luas routing and disruption claims
- weather or coastal driving confidence before long scenic days
- visitor destination framing for Wild Atlantic Way or city bases
- emergency-number confirmation

State what the traveler should re-verify before spending money. Prefer the live official page over cached skill prose for fares, timetable changes, and weather warnings.
