# CBO domain anchors

## Core capabilities

1. **Business development** — pipeline quality, relationship mapping, conversion
2. **Strategic partnerships** — partner ID, deal structure, shared success metrics
3. **Market expansion** — demand validation, entry mode, localization checklist
4. **Deal-making** — BATNA, term sheets, due-diligence coordination
5. **Revenue diversification** — channel mix, concentration caps, pricing ops
6. **Competitive intelligence** — win/loss themes, positioning, competitor offers
7. **Cross-functional handoffs** — sales, product feedback, marketing co-motion

## Decision checklist

Before committing BD resources:

- [ ] Company stage? (seed / growth / enterprise)
- [ ] Current revenue mix and top-customer share?
- [ ] Partnership capacity (named owners, not side projects)?
- [ ] Market readiness (validated demand vs assumption)?
- [ ] Strategic fit with core product and brand?

## Critical rules

- **Strategic fit before terms** — economics cannot rescue misalignment
- **Start small, prove value** — pilots with review gates beat blanket exclusivity
- **Walk-away point defined** — set before negotiation starts
- **Pipeline quality over quantity** — 10 qualified leads beat 100 cold contacts
- **Exit clauses matter** — term, notice, wind-down, IP, data return
- **Customer concentration is risk** — no single customer should threaten survival

## By company stage

| Stage | BD focus |
|-------|----------|
| **Seed** | Demand proof, credibility partnerships, referral networks |
| **Series A** | Repeatable BD process, first channel partners, competitive positioning |
| **Series B** | Enterprise deals, international beachheads, selective M&A scouting |
| **Series C+** | Platform partnerships, category leadership, acquisition integration |

## Failure modes

| Symptom | Likely cause | Recovery |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| Many "partners," no revenue | Vague collab, no owner/metrics | Kill or convert to pilot with shared KPI |
| Expansion CAC doubles core | Copy-paste GTM, weak localization | Pause spend; re-validate beachhead |
| One customer >30% revenue | Over-service + thin pipeline | Secure renewal length; force multi-account plan |
| Deals stall at legal | Scope/IP/exit undefined early | Term sheet essentials before long prose |
| Channel conflict | No segmentation or deal registration | Write rules by segment/geo; protect accounts |

## Handoff boundaries

- Live counterparty thread with hard limits → `negotiate`
- List price / packaging → `pricing`
- Pipeline CRM hygiene → `crm`
- Growth loops / experiments → `growth` or `cgo`
