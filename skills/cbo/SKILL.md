## When to use

- Partnership identification, scoring, outreach, launch, and QBR cadence
- Geographic, vertical, upmarket, or downmarket expansion decisions
- Term-sheet economics, scope, duration, protection, and exit clauses
- Revenue mix, channel conflict, concentration risk, and price-increase playbooks
- Stage-appropriate BD focus (seed → Series C+) with walk-away points defined

Route away when the work is mainly:

- Live counterparty negotiation with principal hard limits → `negotiate`
- List-price / packaging design without a live deal → `pricing`
- CRM rescue, forecast hygiene, or contact dedupe → `crm`
- Acquisition loops, CAC experiments, north-star metrics → `growth` / `cgo`
- Founder weekly priorities spanning product/hiring/runway → `startup` / `founder`
- Company strategy or CEO/board narrative → `strategy` / `ceo`

## Stateless

This skill is knowledge-only. It does not create a local persistent state tree.
Do not write runtime notes into this package. Durable pipeline records belong in
`crm` (or the host CRM); live counters belong in `negotiate`.

## Quick workflow

1. **Lock stage and concentration** — company stage, top-customer share, partnership capacity.
2. **Classify the bet** — partnership, expansion, deal, or revenue-mix problem (one primary lane).
3. **Load one reference lane** from the table below; do not dump every file.
4. **Name BATNA and walk-away** before terms; pilots before scale.
5. **Default one path** with success metrics, owner, review cadence, and exit clause.
6. **Hand off** live counters to `negotiate`, price books to `pricing`, and pipeline writes to `crm`.

## Inline anchors

- **Strategic fit before terms** — misaligned partnerships fail regardless of economics.
- **Start small, prove value** — pilots reduce risk; expand after shared metrics clear.
- **Walk-away defined first** — know limits before the room; desperation is visible.
- **Pipeline quality over quantity** — few qualified leads beat a cold-contact pile.
- **Exit clauses matter** — partnerships end; plan wind-down and IP up front.
- **Customer concentration is risk** — treat top-customer share >30% as critical.
