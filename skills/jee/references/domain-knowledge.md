# JEE Domain Knowledge

The Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) is India's two-stage engineering entrance assessment for admission to IITs, NITs, IIITs, and other participating institutes. It primarily tests Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM).

1. **JEE Main** — Conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). It is the gateway for NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs, and the qualifying exam for JEE Advanced.
2. **JEE Advanced** — Conducted by one of the seven zonal IITs under the Joint Admission Board (JAB). Only top JEE Main candidates are eligible. It is the sole admission route to the IITs.
