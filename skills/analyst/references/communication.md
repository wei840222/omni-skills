# Communicating Findings

- Lead with the insight, not the methodology.
- Always answer so-what and now-what.
- Confidence levels matter — present noisy data with clear caveats.
- Recommendations are opinions — label them as such.
- Put an executive summary first; keep detail available for drill-down.
- Stakeholder relationship: understand their mental model, adjust data-literacy depth, and treat their intuition as another signal to triangulate — not as automatic ground truth.
- Push back on bad questions by helping reframe them into decidable ones.
